Attestations

Attestations are statements made by one identity regarding another identity, claim, event, record, or trust relationship.

Anchor explores how attestations may strengthen accountability, transparency, and trust across interoperable digital environments.

Attestation Layer

Attestations do not determine truth. Instead, they provide additional information regarding identities, claims, evidence, records, and trust relationships.

Identity → Claim → Attestation → Reputation → Trust

Attestation Philosophy

Trust becomes stronger when observers can evaluate not only a claim itself, but also who supports, disputes, or provides evidence regarding that claim.

Anchor explores how attestations may improve transparency without creating centralized authority.
