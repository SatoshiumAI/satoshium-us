Current Scope

Anchor currently governs Integrity References and the institutional processes required to create, validate, verify, publish, version, correct, and maintain them.

Anchor does not operate an identity network, reputation system, trust-scoring network, or certification authority. Identity, claims, attestations, reputation, and trust remain bounded reference domains rather than Anchor-owned authority.

Authority Boundary

Authority answers who owns the record.
Integrity answers whether the referenced representation remains consistent with the anchored representation.

Reference does not transfer authority.
