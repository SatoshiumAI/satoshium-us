Anchor Framework

Initial Anchor Structure

Anchor is currently being established as a framework for identity, trust, claims, attestations, governance, reputation, standards, and accountability.

Future development may expand identity systems, claim models, trust frameworks, reputation mechanisms, governance structures, interoperability standards, and integration with Certifier, Registry, Chronicle, Beacon, and Attestor.

Anchor Workflow

Anchor follows a simple trust sequence: identity, claim, attestation, reputation, and trust. This progression helps participants understand who is making claims, what support exists, and why trust may be justified.

Atlas creates intelligence. Certifier reviews it. Registry catalogs it. Chronicle remembers it. Anchor preserves trust. Attestor verifies it.
Satoshium Anchor is being built as an identity and trust layer — one relationship at a time.
