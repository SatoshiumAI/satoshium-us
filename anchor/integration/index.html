Satoshium Anchor Integration

Anchor Integration defines how Anchor connects with Certifier, Registry, Chronicle, and the broader Satoshium Suite to preserve the integrity of important artifacts.

Integration answers a simple question: what becomes anchored?

Purpose

Anchor Integration establishes the boundary between systems that create, catalog, or remember records and the system that preserves cryptographic integrity references for those records.

Core Distinction

Certifier creates certification artifacts. Registry catalogs certified records. Chronicle preserves certification events. Anchor preserves integrity references.

Not a Certification System

Anchor does not certify subjects, evaluate evidence, assign scores, issue receipts, or determine certification status. Those responsibilities remain with Certifier.

Integrity Layer

Anchor provides an integrity layer by preserving hashes, timestamps, signatures, and related references that help demonstrate whether artifacts have changed.

Integration Relationship

Anchor integrates with the Suite by receiving or referencing artifacts whose integrity should be independently preserved. Anchor does not replace those artifacts. It preserves integrity evidence about them.

Certifier creates authority.
Registry preserves discoverability.
Chronicle preserves history.
Anchor preserves integrity.

Certification Package

A Certification Package may be anchored so its contents can be referenced against a preserved integrity value.

SCPR

A Satoshium Certification Public Report may be anchored to preserve a verifiable reference to the public certification report.

SCR

A Satoshium Certification Receipt may be anchored to preserve a verifiable reference to the issued receipt.

SCRD

A Satoshium Certified Record may be anchored so Registry, Chronicle, Attestor, and other systems can reference its preserved integrity state.

What Anchor Preserves

Anchor preserves integrity metadata and references. It does not need to own the full record in order to preserve evidence that the record existed in a particular form at a particular time.

Hashes · Timestamps · Signatures · Integrity References · Artifact Links · Verification Metadata

Relationship to Certifier

Certifier remains the authority for certification artifacts and certification decisions. Anchor may preserve integrity references for Certification Packages, SCPRs, SCRs, SCRDs, and related outputs without becoming the certification authority.

Certifier answers whether an artifact was certified. Anchor helps answer whether an artifact has changed.

Relationship to Registry and Chronicle

Registry may reference anchored artifacts as part of public cataloging. Chronicle may reference anchoring events as part of historical preservation. Anchor supports both systems by preserving integrity evidence.

Registry catalogs the record. Chronicle remembers the event. Anchor preserves the integrity reference.

Authority Boundaries

Anchor does not decide what a record means. It does not determine whether a certification is valid, whether a Registry Entry is active, or whether a historical event should be interpreted in a particular way.

Anchor preserves cryptographic and temporal references that allow other systems and reviewers to verify integrity.

Integration Philosophy

Anchor should preserve integrity without absorbing the responsibilities of the systems it supports. Its role is not to certify, catalog, remember, discover, or attest. Its role is to preserve evidence that an artifact existed in a specific form.

Records explain what exists. Events explain what happened. Anchors help show whether artifacts changed.
