Authority Boundary

Publication makes Anchor authoritative for the published Integrity Reference and its Anchor-owned metadata.

It does not transfer authority over the referenced Source Artifact.

Anchor Identifier → Anchor authority
Source-System Identifier → Source Institution authority

Reference does not transfer authority.
