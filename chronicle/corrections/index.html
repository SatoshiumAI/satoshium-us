Satoshium Chronicle Corrections

Corrections improve Chronicle records while preserving transparency, historical context, and traceability whenever practical.

Chronicle recognizes that new evidence emerges, sources become available, and understanding evolves over time. Corrections help improve the historical record without unnecessarily obscuring its history.

Why Corrections Exist

Historical records are rarely perfect. New information, additional evidence, improved sourcing, or expanded context may justify updates to existing Chronicle entries.

Transparency

Corrections should remain visible and understandable. Historical reviewers should be able to identify what changed and why the correction occurred.

Historical Context

Chronicle seeks to preserve historical continuity whenever practical. Corrections should improve accuracy without unnecessarily removing important historical context.

Accuracy

The primary objective of a correction is to improve the quality, reliability, and usefulness of the historical record.

Evidence Updates

Corrections may result from newly discovered evidence, improved documentation, or additional information that was not available when the original record was created.

Source Updates

Corrections may involve improved source attribution, additional references, archival discoveries, or clarification regarding the origin of information.

Correction Workflow

Chronicle follows a structured correction process designed to balance historical preservation with improved accuracy.

Issue Identified → Review → Evidence Assessment → Correction → Documentation → Publication

Minor and Major Corrections

Minor corrections may include typographical errors, metadata updates, source additions, formatting improvements, or clarification notes.

Major corrections may include revised timelines, updated event descriptions, new evidence, source disputes, or significant changes to historical interpretation.

The greater the historical impact, the more important transparency, evidence, and documentation become.

Correction Philosophy

Chronicle does not treat corrections as a mechanism for erasing history. Corrections become part of the historical record itself.

The goal is not to create a perfect history. The goal is to preserve how understanding evolved over time.
