## Overview

Satoshium Chronicle is the historical-preservation institution of the Satoshium Suite.

Chronicle preserves qualifying historical Occurrences through canonical **Chronicle Entries** while maintaining durable references to authoritative Suite records, Sources, Evidence, Provenance, Relationships, Corrections, Versions, Verification state, Publication state, and preservation lineage.

Chronicle is operational and has completed its transition from pre-operational architecture into production operation.

## Canonical Object

The canonical Chronicle object is the **Chronicle Entry**.

The Occurrence is what happened.

The Chronicle Entry is Chronicle's structured historical-preservation record representing that qualifying Occurrence.

```text
Occurrence
→ Preservation Eligibility
→ Chronicle Entry
```

No separate canonical "Chronicle Event" object exists.

## Preservation Eligibility

Chronicle does not preserve every activity.

An Occurrence must satisfy **Preservation Eligibility** before becoming a Chronicle Entry.

Preservation Eligibility asks:

> Should Chronicle preserve this Occurrence?

The model was exercised in production when the July 5, 2026 issuance of `SC-CERT-2026-0001` was determined eligible for historical preservation and became the Occurrence represented by `CHR-2026-0001`.

```text
Eligibility
≠ Verification

Eligibility
≠ Validation

Eligibility
≠ Publication
```

## Verification and Validation

Chronicle Verification and Validation remain distinct.

```text
Verification
→ reviews Chronicle's historical representation

Validation
→ determines whether the Chronicle Entry satisfies
  applicable structural and procedural requirements
```

Verification does not re-adjudicate a determination owned by another Suite institution.

Validation does not establish source truth or source authority.

## Authority Boundary

Chronicle is authoritative for its own:

- Chronicle Entry identity;
- historical-preservation representation;
- Chronicle Provenance;
- Chronicle Relationships;
- Chronicle Verification state;
- Chronicle Corrections;
- Chronicle Version lineage;
- Chronicle Publication state; and
- preservation lineage.

Other Suite institutions remain authoritative for their own canonical objects and determinations.

```text
Historical Preservation
≠ Source Authority

Reference
≠ Authority Transfer

≠ Mutation
```
