Satoshium Chronicle Definitions

Definitions establishes the canonical terminology used throughout Chronicle documentation, schemas, records, procedures, and public historical-preservation interfaces.

Consistent terminology protects architectural clarity. Terms should retain their defined meaning across Chronicle and remain compatible with broader Satoshium Suite standards.

Core Chronicle Model

Occurrence → Preservation Eligibility → Chronicle Entry

The Occurrence is what happened. The Chronicle Entry is Chronicle's canonical structured historical-preservation record representing that qualifying Occurrence.

Chronicle

The historical-preservation institution of the Satoshium Suite. Chronicle is authoritative for its own Entries and historical-preservation state while referenced Suite systems retain authority for their own objects.

Chronicle Entry

The canonical historical-preservation object of Chronicle. A Chronicle Entry represents one qualifying historical Occurrence.

Occurrence

An event, action, change, decision, publication, milestone, lifecycle transition, governance development, or other thing that happened independently of Chronicle.

Event Type

A controlled classification describing the kind of Occurrence represented by a Chronicle Entry. Event Type does not create a second canonical object.

Event-Type Profile

A specialization of the Chronicle Base Schema that adds Event-Type-specific requirements without creating a new canonical record class.

Preservation Eligibility

Chronicle's admission rule for historical preservation. It answers: “Should Chronicle preserve this occurrence?”

Historical Significance

The substantive reason an Occurrence is important enough to preserve. It answers: “Why is this occurrence worth preserving?”

Record

An architectural umbrella term for structured information created, maintained, or governed within Chronicle. Record is not a competing canonical object.

Supporting Record

A Chronicle-owned record serving a distinct function in support of Chronicle Entries, such as Source, Evidence, Verification, Correction, Provenance, Relationship, or Version records.

Authoritative Record

A record whose institutional authority comes from the system responsible for creating or governing it. Chronicle may reference it without assuming its authority.

Source, Evidence, and Provenance

Source = Where information came from.
Evidence = What bears on the Entry or claim.
Provenance = How the information originated, moved, and entered Chronicle.

Source

The origin of information referenced by Chronicle. A Source does not automatically establish truth or Chronicle authority.

Source Record

A supporting Chronicle-owned record documenting source attribution, temporal context, location, provenance, archival state, limitations, and relationships.

Source Type

A controlled classification describing what a Source is, such as a webpage, document, archive, database, institutional record, repository, dataset, or statement.

Source Role

A controlled classification describing how a Source functions in relation to a Chronicle Entry or Evidence Record.

Evidence

Material that supports, challenges, contradicts, clarifies, corroborates, contextualizes, or limits confidence in a Chronicle Entry or claim.

Evidence Record

A supporting Chronicle-owned record describing the role, origin, integrity, limitations, provenance, and relationships of Evidence.

Provenance

The documented origin and movement of information, Sources, Evidence, records, or artifacts into Chronicle.

Attribution

Identification of the creator, publisher, institution, system, person, or Source responsible for originating or distributing information.

Authority

Institutional responsibility assigned to a Suite system for its own objects, determinations, actions, and lifecycle.

Verification

Review of Chronicle's own historical representation using authoritative references, Sources, Evidence, Provenance, temporal information, relationships, and limitations.

Validation

Determination that a Chronicle-owned record conforms to applicable structural and procedural requirements.

Correction

A documented change to a Chronicle-owned record intended to improve accuracy, clarity, context, provenance, references, relationships, or completeness.

Version

A preserved state within the lineage of a Chronicle-owned record. Record Version is distinct from Schema Version.

Schema Version

The version of the schema governing the structure of a Chronicle-owned record.

Controlled Value

An approved enumerated term used where consistency, interoperability, validation, or machine readability requires a governed vocabulary.

Relationship

A structured connection between a Chronicle Entry, supporting record, Authoritative Record, or other referenced object.

Lifecycle

The defined sequence of institutional states or procedures through which a Chronicle-owned record progresses over time.

Publication

The institutional process by which a Chronicle-owned record is approved and made publicly available.

Preservation State

The current archival or preservation condition of a Chronicle-owned record, Source, Evidence item, or referenced material.

Retrospective Preservation

Preservation of an Occurrence after it happened when later context establishes Preservation Eligibility.

Historical Continuity

Preservation of relationships, context, provenance, corrections, versions, and temporal order across Chronicle history.

Timeline

A downstream discovery view that organizes published Chronicle Entries chronologically. Timeline is not a separate canonical historical object.

Institutional Memory

Durable preservation of historically significant context, relationships, decisions, milestones, records, and changes needed to understand Satoshium over time.

Traceability

The ability to reconstruct relationships among Chronicle Entries, authoritative records, Sources, Evidence, Provenance, Verification, Corrections, Versions, and publication history.

State Separation

Chronicle uses several distinct institutional states. These concepts should not be collapsed into one generalized status field.

Verification State ≠ Validation State ≠ Publication State ≠ Preservation State ≠ Lifecycle State

Authority Principle

Chronicle may reference authoritative objects from across the Satoshium Suite, but the originating system retains institutional authority.

Reference does not transfer authority.

Definitions Summary

Chronicle terminology exists to preserve architectural precision across documentation, records, schemas, validation, publication, and historical review.

Events happen. Suite systems establish authority. Chronicle preserves qualifying historical memory.
