## Licensing Philosophy

Satoshium Chronicle is designed as an open historical-preservation framework within the Satoshium Suite.

Its institutional purpose is to preserve qualifying historical occurrences through canonical **Chronicle Entries** while maintaining durable context, provenance, evidence relationships, authoritative references, corrections, versions, and historical continuity.

The decision to release Chronicle under the MIT License reflects the belief that historical-preservation frameworks, schemas, methodologies, documentation structures, reference models, and interoperability patterns should remain broadly accessible for study, implementation, adaptation, and improvement.

Open licensing can support:

* Transparency
* Collaboration
* Research
* Education
* Preservation
* Interoperability
* Reproducibility
* Long-term stewardship
* Historical continuity

## Source, Evidence, and Provenance Materials

Chronicle distinguishes among:

* **Source** — where information originated

## Verification and Validation

Chronicle distinguishes between Verification and Validation.

### Verification

Verification reviews Chronicle's historical representation, including matters such as:

* Source consistency
* Evidence relationships
* Provenance
* Temporal consistency
* Authoritative references
* Relationship integrity
* Known limitations

### Validation

Validation determines whether a Chronicle-owned record conforms to applicable:

* Schemas
* Required fields
* Controlled values
* Identifier rules
* Relationship rules
* Provenance requirements
* Versioning requirements
* Publication requirements

Neither Verification nor Validation should be interpreted as transferring authority from the originating system to Chronicle.
