# Chronicle

## Overview

Chronicle is the historical preservation layer of the Satoshium ecosystem.

Its purpose is to record, preserve, organize, and maintain historical information in a structured and transparent manner.

Chronicle documents events, entries, sources, evidence, verification activities, and corrections so that information can be reviewed and understood within its historical context.

Chronicle does not determine what people should believe.

Chronicle preserves the record.

---

## Mission

The mission of Chronicle is to preserve historical accountability through transparent recordkeeping.

Chronicle seeks to:

* Preserve significant events
* Document historical context
* Associate evidence with records
* Maintain source attribution
* Support verification efforts
* Record corrections transparently
* Improve long-term institutional memory
* Support future review and research

---

## Core Principles

### Preservation

Historical information should remain available whenever practical.

### Transparency

Records should be understandable and reviewable.

### Traceability

Relationships between records should remain visible.

### Evidence-Based Review

Claims should be evaluated using available evidence and sources.

### Historical Continuity

The evolution of information should remain visible over time.

---

## What Chronicle Records

Chronicle may preserve:

### Entries

Historical events, observations, decisions, publications, and milestones.

### Sources

The origins of information associated with Chronicle records.

### Evidence

Materials that support, challenge, or contextualize historical claims.

### Verification

Confidence assessments and review activities.

### Corrections

Changes that improve the accuracy and transparency of the historical record.

---

## Architecture

Chronicle is organized around a simple historical record model.

```text
Chronicle
└── Records
    ├── Entries
    ├── Sources
    ├── Evidence
    ├── Verification
    └── Corrections
```

Each component serves a distinct purpose while contributing to a broader historical record.

## Long-Term Vision

The long-term vision of Chronicle is a durable historical record system capable of preserving information across generations.

Future implementations may support:

* Historical archives
* Evidence repositories
* Source preservation
* Verification records
* Correction histories
* Distributed storage
* Digital attestations
* Cryptographic integrity verification

Technologies may evolve.

The preservation mission remains unchanged.
