Satoshium Chronicle

Satoshium Chronicle is the historical-preservation institution of the Satoshium Suite. It preserves qualifying historical occurrences through canonical Chronicle Entries while maintaining authoritative references, Sources, Evidence, Provenance, Verification, Validation, Corrections, Versions, and historical continuity.

Chronicle does not preserve every activity and does not replace the authority of the Suite systems it references. It preserves qualifying historical memory in a structured, transparent, traceable, and institutionally bounded form.

Chronicle Workflow

Chronicle follows an institutional preservation sequence that begins with an Occurrence and Preservation Eligibility, then proceeds through Chronicle Entry creation, authoritative references, Sources, Evidence, Provenance, Verification, Validation, Publication, Versioning, and long-term preservation.

Occurrence → Preservation Eligibility → Chronicle Entry → Authoritative References → Sources / Evidence / Provenance → Verification → Validation → Publication → Versioned Historical Preservation
Events happen. Suite systems establish authority. Chronicle preserves qualifying historical memory.
