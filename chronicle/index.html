Satoshium Chronicle

Satoshium Chronicle is the historical preservation layer of the Satoshium ecosystem. It exists to record, organize, preserve, and maintain historical information across records, entries, sources, evidence, verification activities, and corrections.

Chronicle does not determine what people should believe. It preserves what was recorded, what evidence existed, what sources were available, and how understanding evolved over time.

Status

Chronicle is currently in the specification and design phase. Initial documentation, schemas, and preservation concepts have been established in preparation for future development.

Open Status →
Chronicle begins as a framework for preserving historical records. Future development may expand archival capabilities, evidence repositories, verification workflows, correction histories, distributed preservation, and interoperability with Certifier, Registry, Anchor, Beacon, and Attestor.

Chronicle Workflow

Chronicle follows a simple historical preservation sequence: event, source, evidence, verification, preservation, and historical continuity. This workflow helps ensure that meaningful events remain understandable across time.

Events preserve history. Sources identify origins. Evidence supports understanding. Verification evaluates confidence. Chronicle preserves institutional memory.
Satoshium Chronicle is being built as a historical preservation layer — one record at a time.
