Chronicle · Schemas

Satoshium Chronicle Schemas

Schemas provide the structural foundation for Chronicle records. They define how historical information is organized, represented, related, and preserved across the system.

Consistent schemas improve transparency, interoperability, traceability, and long-term historical preservation.

Consistency

Schemas provide common structures for similar record types, making historical information easier to understand and review.

Traceability

Structured relationships help connect entries, sources, evidence, corrections, and verification activities.

Interoperability

Well-defined schemas improve compatibility between systems, repositories, tools, and future implementations.

Longevity

Historical records become more valuable when they remain understandable across changing technologies and generations.

Validation

Schemas help ensure that records contain the information necessary for preservation, review, and historical context.

Relationships

Structured references allow Chronicle records to remain connected and understandable within a broader historical network.

Schema Philosophy

Schemas should support preservation without dictating interpretation. Their purpose is to organize information, not determine conclusions.

The goal is not complexity. The goal is clarity.
