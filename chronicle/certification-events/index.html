Satoshium Chronicle Certification Events

Certification Events define the certification-related historical occurrences that Chronicle may preserve as part of the historical record.

Chronicle does not create, replace, or reinterpret certifications. Certifier remains authoritative for certification records and lifecycle actions. Chronicle preserves the historical occurrence and context of certification activity by referencing those authoritative records.

Purpose

Certification Events provide Chronicle with a clear historical model for preserving qualifying moments in the life of a certification while maintaining the authority boundaries established by the Satoshium Suite.

Core Distinction

Certifier is authoritative for Certification Packages and certification lifecycle actions. Registry is authoritative for SREG Registry Entries. Chronicle is responsible for preserving qualifying historical occurrences and their context without assuming the authority of either system.

Suite Alignment

Certification Events follow Satoshium Suite standards for stable terminology, reference-based interoperability, clear institutional boundaries, structured records, and repeatable procedures. Chronicle references authoritative Suite objects rather than duplicating or replacing them.

Historical Meaning

A preserved certification occurrence records what happened, when it happened, the authoritative record that establishes the occurrence, and the historical context needed to understand its place within the broader certification history.

Record References

Certification Events reference the authoritative Certification Package produced by Certifier and may also reference related public outputs, Registry Entries, receipts, attestations, integrity references, public pages, or other supporting Suite records. Referenced records remain authoritative within the systems that created them.

Certification Event Types

Chronicle may preserve several certification-related event types when the underlying occurrence satisfies Chronicle preservation rules and can be tied to an authoritative certification record.

Certification Created → Certification Renewed → Certification Suspended → Certification Revoked → Certification Expired

Certification Created

A Certification Created event records the historical occurrence in which Certifier produced a new authoritative Certification Package. Chronicle references that package; it does not recreate the certification determination.

Certification Renewed

A Certification Renewed event records the historical occurrence in which Certifier authoritatively renewed a certification according to the applicable certification rules and lifecycle model.

Certification Suspended

A Certification Suspended event records the historical occurrence in which Certifier authoritatively placed a certification into a suspended state according to the applicable certification status model.

Certification Revoked

A Certification Revoked event records the historical occurrence in which Certifier authoritatively revoked a certification according to the applicable certification rules and lifecycle model.

Certification Expired

A Certification Expired event records the historical occurrence in which a certification reached an expired state under the applicable Certifier rules and certification lifecycle model.

Future Event Types

Additional certification event types may be defined only when they correspond to authoritative certification lifecycle actions or states recognized by Certifier. Anchor and Attestor activity should be preserved through their own Chronicle event-type profiles rather than being reclassified as certification authority.

What an Event Preserves

A Certification Event should preserve enough context to make the historical occurrence understandable without duplicating, replacing, or reinterpreting the authoritative certification record.

Event Type · Event Date · Originating System · Authoritative Certification Package · Related Registry Entry · Public References · Historical Context · Supporting Records

What an Event Does Not Do

A Certification Event does not certify, renew, suspend, revoke, expire, or otherwise determine the state of a certification. Those actions and determinations remain within Certifier’s authority and applicable certification rules.

Chronicle preserves that the occurrence happened and references the authority that established it. Chronicle does not create, replace, or reinterpret that authority.

Relationship to Registry

Registry creates and maintains independent SREG Registry Entries that catalog certified records over time. Chronicle may reference those Registry Entries when preserving certification-related occurrences, but Registry remains authoritative for the SREG and its Registry lifecycle state.

Registry owns the catalog record. Chronicle preserves the historical occurrence and its context.

Event Philosophy

Certification Events help Chronicle preserve how certifications changed over time while honoring Suite-wide standards for stable objects, clear authority boundaries, durable references, and repeatable workflows. Chronicle provides historical continuity without replacing the systems that create, catalog, anchor, discover, attest, or orchestrate authoritative Suite records.
