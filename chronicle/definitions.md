# Chronicle Definitions

## Purpose

This document defines key terms used throughout Chronicle documentation, schemas, records, and related materials.

These definitions exist to promote consistency, clarity, and shared understanding across the Chronicle system.

---

## Chronicle

Chronicle is the historical preservation layer of the Satoshium ecosystem.

Its purpose is to preserve records, sources, evidence, verification activities, and corrections in a structured and transparent manner.

---

## Record

A preserved unit of information maintained within Chronicle.

Records may represent entries, sources, evidence, verification activities, corrections, or related historical information.

---

## Entry

A historical record documenting an event, observation, decision, publication, milestone, investigation, or other significant occurrence.

Entries serve as the primary records within Chronicle.

---

## Source

The origin of information associated with a Chronicle record.

Sources identify where information came from and help establish attribution, context, and traceability.

A source answers:

> Where did the information originate?

---

## Evidence

Information, materials, artifacts, documentation, or records that support, challenge, clarify, or contextualize a claim or historical record.

Evidence answers:

> What supports or challenges the claim?

---

## Verification

The process of evaluating confidence in a record using available evidence, sources, context, and review procedures.

Verification does not determine absolute truth.

It documents the current level of confidence.

---

## Verification Status

A confidence assessment assigned to a record following review.

Examples may include:

* Unverified
* Under Review
* Partially Verified
* Verified
* Disputed
* Superseded

---

## Correction

A documented modification to a Chronicle record intended to improve accuracy, clarity, context, or completeness.

Corrections become part of the historical record rather than replacing it.

---

## Historical Record

A preserved representation of information associated with a particular event, observation, decision, publication, or period in time.

---

## Historical Context

Information that helps explain the circumstances surrounding a historical record.

Context may include:

* Time
* Location
* Participants
* Conditions
* Related events

---

## Attribution

The identification of the origin, creator, publisher, or source of information.

---

## Corroboration

The process of comparing multiple independent sources or evidence records to determine whether they support a common conclusion.

---

## Provenance

The documented history of a record, source, evidence item, or artifact.

Provenance helps establish origin, custody, authenticity, and historical context.

---

## Authenticity

The degree to which a record, source, or evidence item is believed to be genuine and unaltered.

---

## Integrity

The preservation of a record in a manner that maintains its consistency, completeness, and reliability over time.

---

## Preservation

The process of maintaining historical information for future access, review, and understanding.

---

## Traceability

The ability to identify relationships between records, sources, evidence, verification activities, and corrections.

Traceability helps preserve historical continuity.

---

## Transparency

The principle that records, sources, evidence, verification activities, and corrections should be understandable and reviewable whenever practical.

---

## Confidence

The degree of certainty associated with a record based upon available evidence, sources, context, and review.

Confidence may change as new information becomes available.

---

## Reviewer

An individual, organization, system, or process responsible for evaluating information within Chronicle.

---

## Repository

A collection of records, documentation, schemas, or supporting materials maintained within Chronicle.

---

## Schema

A structured definition describing how a particular type of Chronicle record is organized and represented.

---

## Relationship

A documented connection between two or more Chronicle records.

Examples include:

* Entry-to-source relationships
* Entry-to-evidence relationships
* Entry-to-correction relationships
* Source-to-evidence relationships

---

## Lifecycle

The sequence of stages through which a record progresses over time.

Examples may include:

* Creation
* Review
* Verification
* Correction
* Preservation
* Archival

---

## Historical Continuity

The preservation of relationships, context, and record history over time.

Historical continuity allows future reviewers to understand how knowledge evolved.

---

## Guiding Principle

Chronicle seeks to preserve:

* What was recorded
* What evidence existed
* What sources were available
* How understanding evolved

The objective is not perfect certainty.

The objective is transparent historical preservation.
