Satoshium Chronicle Sources

Sources identify where information originated. They provide attribution, context, and traceability for the records preserved within Chronicle.

Understanding the origin of information is essential to historical review. Sources help explain who created, published, recorded, or preserved the information being referenced.

Attribution

Sources help establish who created, published, or recorded information associated with a Chronicle record.

Transparency

Historical records become more understandable when reviewers can identify where information originated and how it was obtained.

Context

Sources often provide important historical context that helps explain events, decisions, observations, and conclusions.

Traceability

Chronicle seeks to maintain clear connections between records and their sources so information can be independently reviewed.

Verification Support

Verification activities frequently begin by examining source quality, origin, consistency, and corroboration.

Preservation

Chronicle seeks to preserve source information whenever practical, even when original materials become unavailable or archived.

Source Categories

Chronicle may preserve many forms of source information, depending on the nature of the historical record.

Documents → Publications → Archives → Databases → Public Records → Interviews → Websites

Sources and Evidence

Sources and evidence serve different roles within Chronicle.

A source identifies where information came from. Evidence represents the materials that support, challenge, or contextualize a claim or historical record.

Source = Origin of information.
Evidence = Support for a claim.

Source Philosophy

Recording a source does not imply endorsement. Chronicle preserves sources to improve transparency and historical understanding.

The goal is not to preserve conclusions. The goal is to preserve where information originated.
