# Chronicle Scope

## Purpose

This document defines the intended scope of Chronicle.

Chronicle exists to preserve historical records, evidence, sources, verification activities, and corrections in a structured and transparent manner.

The purpose of scope is to establish clear boundaries regarding what Chronicle is designed to do and what falls outside its responsibilities.

---

## In Scope

Chronicle may support:

### Historical Entries

Recording events, observations, decisions, publications, milestones, investigations, and other historically relevant information.

### Record Preservation

Maintaining historical records in a structured and accessible format.

### Source Attribution

Documenting where information originated.

### Evidence Preservation

Preserving materials that support, challenge, clarify, or contextualize historical records.

### Verification Support

Recording confidence assessments, reviews, and verification activities.

### Corrections

Documenting updates and improvements to historical records while preserving transparency.

### Historical Relationships

Maintaining connections between entries, sources, evidence, verification records, and corrections.

### Long-Term Preservation

Supporting durable storage and preservation of historical information.

---

## Out of Scope

Chronicle is not intended to:

### Determine Absolute Truth

Chronicle preserves records and supports review.

It does not establish universal truth.

### Replace Human Judgment

Historical interpretation remains the responsibility of reviewers, researchers, and readers.

### Act as a Legal Authority

Chronicle does not grant legal status, legal standing, or legal authority.

### Function as a Certification System

Certifications, attestations, and trust assertions belong within Certifier.

### Function as a Registry

Structured record identification and classification belong within Registry.

### Moderate Opinion

Chronicle may preserve records containing differing viewpoints without attempting to resolve all disagreements.

### Guarantee Permanence

Chronicle seeks long-term preservation but cannot guarantee perpetual availability of all records.

---

## Relationship to Other Systems

### Certifier

Certifier provides attestations regarding records, claims, evidence, or information.

Chronicle preserves the historical record surrounding those attestations.

### Registry

Registry organizes and identifies records.

Chronicle preserves historical context associated with those records.

### Chronicle

Chronicle preserves events, sources, evidence, verification activities, and corrections.

---

## Guiding Principle

Chronicle answers:

> What was recorded?

> What evidence existed?

> What sources were available?

> How did understanding evolve?

Chronicle does not answer:

> What must everyone believe?

---

## Future Scope

Future Chronicle implementations may expand to include:

* Historical archives
* Evidence repositories
* Verification workflows
* Preservation systems
* Distributed storage
* Public historical exploration
* Integrity verification mechanisms

Such expansions should remain consistent with Chronicle's preservation mission.

---

## Scope Summary

Chronicle preserves history.

It records events, sources, evidence, verification activities, and corrections so that future reviewers can understand not only what happened, but how knowledge evolved over time.

The objective is preservation, transparency, and historical continuity.
