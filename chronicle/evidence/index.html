Satoshium Chronicle Evidence

Evidence provides support, context, and transparency for Chronicle records. It helps reviewers understand what information was available and how historical conclusions were formed.

Evidence does not determine truth by itself. Instead, it contributes to the historical record and supports independent review, verification, and analysis.

Supporting Information

Evidence helps support, challenge, clarify, or contextualize historical entries, observations, and claims preserved within Chronicle.

Transparency

Preserving evidence allows future reviewers to examine the underlying materials rather than relying solely on conclusions or interpretations.

Documentation

Evidence may include documents, records, screenshots, photographs, reports, archives, logs, and other supporting materials.

Historical Context

Evidence often provides valuable context that helps explain why events occurred and how they were understood at the time.

Verification Support

Verification efforts rely heavily on evidence when assessing confidence in historical records and associated claims.

Preservation

Chronicle seeks to preserve evidence whenever practical so future generations can independently review historical records.

Evidence Categories

Evidence may take many forms depending on the nature of the historical record being preserved.

Documents → Images → Audio → Video → Logs → Archives → Artifacts

Evidence and Sources

Sources identify where information originated.

Evidence represents the materials that support, challenge, or contextualize a claim, event, or historical record.

Source = Where information came from.
Evidence = What supports or challenges the claim.

Evidence Philosophy

Chronicle preserves evidence so historical records can be reviewed, examined, and reevaluated over time.

The goal is not to preserve conclusions. The goal is to preserve the information that informed them.
