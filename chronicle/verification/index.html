Chronicle Verification Procedure

Verification is Chronicle's structured review of its own historical representation. It examines whether a Chronicle Entry is adequately supported, internally coherent, traceable to authoritative references, Sources, Evidence, and Provenance, temporally consistent, relationship-aware, and appropriately limited where uncertainty remains.

Verification does not re-adjudicate certifications, registrations, attestations, anchors, discovery signals, workflows, Atlas records, or other determinations owned by another Satoshium institution.

Verification Question

Is Chronicle's historical representation adequately supported, internally coherent, traceable, and appropriately limited by the authoritative references, Sources, Evidence, Provenance, Relationships, dates, and information available?

Verification reviews Chronicle. It does not replace the authority of the systems Chronicle references.

Entry Identity

Confirm that the Entry under review is clearly identified and that the reviewed materials, Sources, Evidence, Relationships, and authoritative references belong to the intended Chronicle Entry Version. Identifier syntax and formal conformance are tested during Validation.

Source Record Existence

Confirm that required Source Records or external Source references exist, are identifiable, and correspond to the Sources cited by the Entry.

Authoritative References

Confirm that referenced authoritative Suite objects are identifiable and that Chronicle represents their role without assuming their institutional authority.

Event-Date Consistency

Confirm that Event Date and other temporal information are internally consistent and are not confused with Entry creation, retrieval, publication, Correction, or Version dates.

Relationship Consistency

Confirm that material Relationships use the correct direction and meaning and are consistent with the referenced records and historical sequence.

Evidence Availability

Confirm that required Evidence is available or properly referenced and that known limitations, unavailable items, or disputed material remain visible.

Provenance Consistency

Confirm that origin, access method, retrieval date, Source references, transformations, and known provenance limitations are coherent.

Historical Context

Confirm that Chronicle's historical context does not overstate what the Sources, Evidence, authoritative records, or Relationships support.

Verification Does Not Re-Adjudicate Authority

Chronicle may verify that it accurately references an authoritative record. Chronicle must not re-decide the determination owned by the originating system.

Chronicle may verify: “This Entry correctly references SC-CERT-2026-0001.”
Chronicle must not independently re-certify what Certifier already determined.
Reference does not transfer authority.

Verification Sequence

Entry Version Identified → Authoritative References Reviewed → Sources Reviewed → Evidence Reviewed → Dates Reviewed → Relationships Reviewed → Provenance Reviewed → Historical Context Reviewed → Limitations Reviewed → Verification State Assigned → Entry Proceeds to Validation

1. Identify the Entry

Confirm the record under review and the applicable Chronicle Entry Version, Event Type, and governing schema or profile context.

2. Confirm Review Scope

Confirm the exact Entry Version being reviewed and ensure that all materials considered during Verification correspond to that Entry. Formal identifier syntax, uniqueness, and schema conformance are handled by Validation.

3. Check Sources

Confirm that required Source Records and external references exist and are sufficiently identifiable for later review.

4. Check Authority

Confirm that authoritative external records are correctly identified and that Chronicle has not converted a reference into an authority claim.

5. Check Time

Compare Event Date and other relevant dates against the cited records, Source context, and Provenance.

6. Check Relationships

Review directional and non-directional Relationships for semantic accuracy, target correctness, historical consistency, and unsupported implications.

7. Check Evidence

Confirm required Evidence exists or is referenced, Evidence Type is appropriate, and Evidence limitations are visible.

8. Check Provenance

Review the documented information path and confirm that Chronicle can explain how the supporting information entered the historical record.

9. Review Historical Context

Confirm that Chronicle's narrative and contextual representation does not overstate what the authoritative references, Sources, Evidence, Relationships, dates, or Provenance support.

10. Review Limitations

Confirm that unresolved conflicts, missing Sources, incomplete Provenance, unavailable Evidence, uncertain dates, or other material limitations are disclosed.

11. Assign Verification State

Assign the approved Verification State that accurately reflects the current result of Chronicle's review.

Controlled Verification States

Verification State should use the Chronicle Controlled Values Registry.

Not Reviewed · In Review · Verified · Verified with Limitations · Unresolved

Not Reviewed

The Chronicle Entry has not yet undergone the applicable Verification procedure.

In Review

Verification is actively underway and no final Verification State has yet been assigned.

Verified

Chronicle completed the applicable review and found the historical representation sufficiently supported and internally consistent under current Chronicle rules.

Verified with Limitations

Chronicle completed the review, but material limitations remain and are explicitly preserved in the Entry or supporting records.

Unresolved

A material issue remains open, such as conflicting Evidence, incomplete Provenance, uncertain dates, unresolved Relationships, or another condition preventing a fully resolved Verification outcome.

State Is Reviewable

Verification State may change when new Sources, Evidence, Corrections, Provenance, or authoritative records become available.

Verification and Evidence

Verification reviews Evidence but does not convert Evidence into authority.

Evidence supports review.
Verification evaluates Chronicle's representation.
Authority remains where it originated.

Verification and Provenance

Verification checks whether the recorded provenance path is sufficient and internally consistent. It does not manufacture missing Provenance.

Unknown does not become known through review.

Verification and Validation

Verification and Validation are sequential but distinct institutional functions.

Verification = Is Chronicle's historical representation adequately supported and reviewable?
Validation = Does the current Entry Version conform to the institutional and machine-readable requirements that govern it?

Verification State is not a Validation result. Validation applies the CHR-VAL-001 through CHR-VAL-014 rule set and produces the authoritative conformance outcome:

PASS → Entry may proceed to the separate Publication Gate.
FAIL → Entry returns for correction, completion, or review.

Schema defines structure. Verification reviews the representation. Validation tests conformance. Publication determines public production state.

Verification and the Publication Gate

Verification is a prerequisite to publication, but Verification alone does not make an Entry publication-ready.

Required Verification State + Validation PASS + Required Authoritative References + Publication Prerequisites = Eligible to Proceed to the Publication Gate

The required Verification State may depend on Chronicle publication rules and material limitations. Validation confirms whether that prerequisite has been satisfied; the Publication Gate then makes the separate institutional approval decision, and Publication performs the later state-changing action.

Reverification

Reverification may be required when material new information changes the basis on which Chronicle previously reviewed the Entry.

New Evidence · New Source · Provenance Correction · Relationship Change · Event-Date Correction · Authoritative Reference Change · Historical Context Change · Material Entry Version

When a change affects requirements previously validated, Reverification should be followed by targeted or full Revalidation as governed by the Chronicle Validation Sequence.

Verification Traceability

Verification results should remain traceable to the exact Entry Version reviewed, the authoritative references, Sources, Evidence, Provenance, Relationships, limitations considered, and the Verification State assigned.

Chronicle does not presently require a separate canonical Verification Record for every Entry merely for architectural symmetry. Verification information should be preserved through the Entry, supporting operational artifacts, or other lightweight institutional documentation sufficient to reconstruct the review.

Preserve the review. Do not create a new canonical object unless operations justify one.
