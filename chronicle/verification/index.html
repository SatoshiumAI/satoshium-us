Chronicle · Verification

Satoshium Chronicle Verification

Verification provides a structured process for evaluating confidence in Chronicle records. It helps reviewers assess evidence, sources, authenticity, consistency, and historical context.

Verification does not determine absolute truth. Instead, it documents the current level of confidence based on the information available at the time of review.

Evidence-Based Review

Verification relies on evidence, documentation, and supporting materials rather than assumptions or unsupported conclusions.

Transparency

Verification activities should be understandable and reviewable, allowing others to see how confidence assessments were reached.

Source Evaluation

Sources play an important role in verification by helping reviewers assess origin, reliability, consistency, and context.

Corroboration

Confidence may increase when multiple independent sources and evidence records support the same conclusion.

Historical Context

Verification considers the broader historical circumstances surrounding an event, claim, observation, or record.

Continuous Review

Verification is not permanent. New evidence and new sources may strengthen, weaken, or revise previous assessments.

Verification Outcomes

Chronicle verification focuses on confidence assessment rather than declarations of absolute certainty.

Unverified → Under Review → Partially Verified → Verified → Disputed

Verification Workflow

Verification follows a structured review process that seeks to balance transparency, evidence, and historical context.

Record Identified → Sources Reviewed → Evidence Reviewed → Assessment → Confidence Assigned

Verification Philosophy

Verification should answer a simple question:

How confident are we based on the information currently available?

Chronicle preserves evidence and records. Verification evaluates confidence. Historical understanding remains open to future review.
