Satoshium Chronicle Integration

Chronicle Integration defines how Chronicle relates to Certifier, Registry, and the broader Satoshium Suite by preserving certification events rather than certification records.

Integration answers a simple question: what history should Chronicle preserve?

Purpose

Chronicle Integration establishes the boundary between operational records and historical events. Chronicle does not store certifications. Chronicle preserves the history of certification activity.

Core Distinction

Certifier creates certification records. Registry catalogs certified records. Chronicle preserves certification events.

Not a Certification Store

Chronicle does not become the home of Certification Packages, SCRDs, receipts, or Registry Entries. Those records remain with the systems responsible for creating or cataloging them.

Historical Layer

Chronicle serves as the historical layer that records when meaningful certification-related events occurred and why they matter within the broader Satoshium ecosystem.

Integration Relationship

Chronicle integrates with Certifier and Registry by observing or referencing significant certification activity. It preserves the event history without assuming authority over the certification or the Registry Entry.

Certifier creates certification records.
Registry catalogs certified records.

Certifier Relationship

Certifier remains the authority for certification outcomes, evidence review, scoring, certification status, Certification Packages, SCRDs, and receipts.

Registry Relationship

Registry remains the public catalog for certified records and their Registry lifecycle state through SREG Registry Entries.

Chronicle Relationship

Chronicle preserves the historical occurrence of certification events, such as creation, renewal, suspension, revocation, expiration, or other significant certification milestones.

Suite Relationship

Chronicle may support future interoperability by preserving historical context that can be referenced by Anchor, Attestor, Beacon, Navigator, Registry, and other Suite systems.

Certification Events

Chronicle preserves events that describe meaningful moments in the life of a certification. These events provide historical context without duplicating the certification record itself.

Certification Created → Certification Renewed → Certification Suspended → Certification Revoked → Certification Expired

Why Chronicle Preserves Events

Certifications may change over time. A certification may be created, renewed, suspended, revoked, superseded, or allowed to expire. The certification record documents the operational state. Chronicle documents the historical event.

Records explain what exists. Events explain what happened.

Authority Boundaries

Chronicle does not determine whether a certification is valid, active, suspended, revoked, or expired. Those meanings belong to Certifier and, where applicable, Registry’s cataloging lifecycle.

Chronicle records that an event occurred, preserves the context of that event, and references the systems or records that establish its authority.

Integration Philosophy

Chronicle should preserve history without absorbing the responsibilities of other systems. Its role is not to certify, catalog, attest, anchor, or discover. Its role is to remember.

Chronicle preserves the historical memory of the Suite.
