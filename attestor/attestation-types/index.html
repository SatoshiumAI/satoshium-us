Attestation Types

Attestation Types classify governed assertions within Satoshium Attestor so that each Attestation can be interpreted, validated, and evaluated according to the kind of statement it expresses.

Attestor's operational architecture establishes six controlled Attestation Types. These values classify the governed assertion expressed by an Attestation; they do not redefine the identity, type, or authority of any referenced Suite or external source object.

Classification principle: An Attestation Type classifies the Attestor assertion. It does not redefine the type or authority of a referenced Suite object.

Identity

Identity Attestations express bounded assertions involving identity, continuity, participation, association, or another identity-related matter. The Attestation preserves applicable source authority and does not become the underlying identity authority.

Controlled value: identity
Adopted

Evidence

Evidence Attestations express bounded assertions concerning the presence, relevance, relationship, or governed use of evidence. Evidence remains distinct from the Attestation and retains its own provenance and authority context.

Controlled value: evidence
Adopted

Source & Provenance

Source / Provenance Attestations express bounded assertions concerning source relationships, attribution, origin, authorship, provenance, or the connection between a source and the subject of an Attestation.

Controlled value: source-provenance
Adopted

Verification-Related

Verification-Related Attestations express bounded assertions that reference validation, verification, review, or certification outcomes when relevant. The originating process retains its authority; Attestor does not reproduce that authority as its own.

Controlled value: verification-related
Adopted

Relationship & Condition

Relationship / Condition Attestations express bounded assertions concerning a relationship, condition, status, qualification, participation, or other governed circumstance within the Attestation's defined scope.

Controlled value: relationship-condition
Adopted

Correction & Supersession

Correction / Supersession Attestations express bounded assertions concerning correction, supersession, or governed change where an Attestation is the appropriate object. Correction itself remains a governed activity, and lifecycle or versioning rules determine the effect on canonical identity and state.

Controlled value: correction-supersession
Adopted

Controlled Vocabulary

Attestation Types form a governed controlled vocabulary rather than an open-ended collection of labels. The adopted values are identity, evidence, source-provenance, verification-related, relationship-condition, and correction-supersession. Additional values require governed architectural change rather than ad hoc creation.

Type → Meaning → Eligibility → Required Relationships → Validation

Type Does Not Transfer Authority

A type name must not imply that Attestor owns the underlying authority. For example, a verification-related Attestation does not make Attestor the Certifier; an identity-related Attestation does not make Attestor the identity authority; and an evidence-related Attestation does not make Attestor the source of the evidence.

Reference does not transfer authority.

Reputation Is Not an Attestation Type

The pre-Suite model included Reputation Attestations. That category is not part of the adopted Attestation Type vocabulary. Attestor does not establish a generic reputation framework, reputation score, or canonical reputation object.

Attestor produces Trust Statements. It does not presently own or generate a canonical reputation model.
