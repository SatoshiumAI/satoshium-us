Attestation Types

Attestation types define the major categories of statements that may be documented within Attestor.

These categories help organize identity statements, evidence statements, source statements, corrections, verification context, reputation signals, and trust-related observations.

Identity Attestations

Statements regarding identity, continuity, participation, organizational association, or identity-related context.

Defined

Evidence Attestations

Statements regarding evidence, supporting materials, documents, records, datasets, media, or other information relevant to a subject.

Defined

Source Attestations

Statements regarding sources, attribution, provenance, relevance, origin, authorship, or source relationships.

Defined

Verification Attestations

Statements referencing verification outcomes, certifications, validation activities, or evidence review context.

Conceptual

Reputation Attestations

Statements related to accumulated trust signals, historical behavior, participation, accountability, or reputation context.

Conceptual

Correction Attestations

Statements documenting corrections, amendments, clarifications, evidence updates, attribution updates, or retractions.

Defined

Attestation Categories

Attestation types help distinguish what kind of statement is being made and what kind of trust-related context it may provide.

Identity → Evidence → Source → Verification → Reputation → Correction

Attestation Philosophy

Attestation types provide structure for statements. They do not determine whether a statement is true, verified, or trustworthy.

Their value comes from preserving attribution, transparency, traceability, and context.

An attestation records that a statement was made. Trust requires context.
