# Anchor Record

## Purpose

The Anchor Record serves as the trust-related reference connecting Attestor to identity information maintained within Anchor.

Attestor focuses on trust.

Anchor focuses on identity.

The Anchor Record provides a structured method for referencing identities while preserving clear separation between identity management and trust evaluation.

---

## Why Anchor Records Exist

Trust-related information often depends upon understanding:

* Who made a statement
* Who is being referenced
* Who provided evidence
* Who issued an attestation
* Who performed a verification

These questions require identity context.

Anchor Records provide a consistent mechanism for referencing identity information maintained by Anchor.

---

## Relationship to Anchor

Anchor serves as the identity layer of the Satoshium ecosystem.

A simplified relationship may be represented as:

```text id="u8w5tr"
Anchor → Identity
Attestor → Trust
```

Anchor helps establish who is involved.

Attestor helps document trust-related information associated with those identities.

---

## What an Anchor Record Contains

An Anchor Record may contain references to:

* Identity identifiers
* Identity status
* Identity type
* Identity relationships
* Identity metadata
* Related attestations

The record exists primarily as a reference mechanism.

Identity authority remains with Anchor.

## Identity Types

Anchor Records may reference identities such as:

### Individual

A human participant.

### Organization

A company, institution, association, or entity.

### Project

A defined initiative, protocol, or system.

### System

A software or automated system.

### Community

A group or collective identity.

Additional identity classes may emerge over time.

---

## Identity References

Anchor Records do not create identities.

Anchor Records reference identities maintained elsewhere.

A simplified relationship may be represented as:

```text id="n5v4zp"
Anchor Identity
        ↓
   Anchor Record
        ↓
Trust Context
```

This preserves separation of responsibilities.

## Guiding Principles

### Attribution

Identity references should remain clear.

### Traceability

Relationships should support review.

### Consistency

Identity references should remain standardized whenever practical.

### Transparency

Identity context should remain understandable.

### Separation of Responsibilities

Identity management remains the responsibility of Anchor.

---

## Long-Term Vision

As Attestor evolves, Anchor Records may become foundational components of identity-linked trust frameworks, helping connect attestations, evidence, reputation signals, verification outcomes, and historical records through persistent identity references.

The objective is not to merge identity and trust.

The objective is to connect them responsibly.
