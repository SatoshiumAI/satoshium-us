# External Record

## Purpose

The External Record serves as the trust-related reference connecting Attestor to information originating outside the Satoshium ecosystem.

Trust-related evaluations often depend upon information from external sources, organizations, institutions, jurisdictions, publications, and systems.

External Records help preserve those relationships while maintaining attribution, context, and traceability.

---

## Why External Records Exist

Not all information originates within Satoshium.

Users frequently rely upon:

* Government records
* Public databases
* Academic research
* Media publications
* Corporate disclosures
* Regulatory filings
* Industry reports
* Independent archives

External Records help document how those sources contribute to trust-related understanding.

---

## What an External Record Contains

An External Record may contain references to:

* External sources
* Public records
* Research publications
* Regulatory documents
* Government records
* Independent certifications
* Third-party attestations
* External evidence

External Records function primarily as attribution and context references.

Authority remains with the originating source.

## Guiding Principles

### Attribution Matters

External information should remain connected to its source.

### Transparency Matters

Users should understand where information originated.

### Traceability Matters

Relationships should support review and investigation.

### Context Matters

External information should remain connected to relevant records.

### Authority Remains External

External Records reference information.

They do not transfer authority from the originating source.

---

## Long-Term Vision

As Attestor evolves, External Records may become foundational components for documenting how outside information contributes to trust evaluations, verification activities, reputation development, and accountability across digital ecosystems.

The goal is not to absorb external information.

The goal is to preserve its relationship to trust.
