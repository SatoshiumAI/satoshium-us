# Beacon Record

## Purpose

The Beacon Record serves as the trust-related reference connecting Attestor to discovery information maintained within Beacon.

Beacon helps users discover information.

Attestor helps users understand trust-related context associated with that information.

Beacon Records preserve relationships between discovery events, signals, results, sources, and trust-related records.

---

## Why Beacon Records Exist

Discovery often precedes trust evaluation.

Users frequently encounter information through discovery systems before determining:

* Whether the information is reliable
* What evidence supports it
* What trust signals exist
* What historical context is available

Beacon Records help preserve the connection between discovery and trust.

---

## Relationship to Beacon

Beacon serves as the discovery layer of the Satoshium ecosystem.

A simplified relationship may be represented as:

```text
Beacon → Discovery
Attestor → Trust
```

Beacon helps users find information.

Attestor helps preserve trust-related context associated with discovered information.

---

## What a Beacon Record Contains

A Beacon Record may contain references to:

* Discovery events
* Discovery results
* Search outcomes
* Signals
* Sources
* Query references
* Related trust records
* Associated attestations

Beacon Records primarily function as discovery-context references.

Authority over discovery processes remains with Beacon.

Beacon Records may reference identities maintained by Anchor.

---

## Guiding Principles

### Discovery Should Remain Traceable

Users should understand how information was discovered.

### Sources Should Remain Visible

Discovery should preserve source attribution whenever practical.

### Context Should Be Preserved

Discovery results should remain connected to supporting information.

### Transparency Matters

Users should be able to understand discovery pathways.

### Separation of Responsibilities

Beacon discovers information.

Attestor preserves trust context.

---

## Long-Term Vision

As Attestor evolves, Beacon Records may become foundational components for documenting how information was discovered, how trust-related evaluations emerged, and how evidence, sources, and signals contributed to trust-related conclusions.

The goal is not merely to preserve results.

The goal is to preserve discovery context.

---

## Guiding Statement

The purpose of a Beacon Record may be summarized as:

```text
Discovery reveals information.

Beacon Records preserve how it was found.
```

---

## Status

Beacon Record standards are currently conceptual and may evolve as Beacon and Attestor continue to mature.
