# Atlas Reference Profile

## Purpose
Describes how Attestor may reference **Atlas Authoritative Intelligence** when relevant and eligible for an Attestation or Trust Statement evaluation.

## Canonical Boundary
**Atlas → Authoritative Intelligence**  
**Attestor → Trust Statement**

Attestor does not create an “Atlas Record” that replaces or duplicates Atlas authority.

## Potential Context
Depending on the authoritative Atlas object and future eligibility rules, Attestor may reference jurisdictional, geographic, regulatory, media, or other Atlas intelligence.

## Reference Requirements
An Atlas reference should preserve enough information to identify the authoritative Atlas object, its source identifier or resolvable reference, relevant state/version where applicable, provenance, scope and relevance, relationship to the Attestation/evaluation, and Atlas authority.

## Governing Principle
> **Reference does not transfer authority.**

## Status
Foundational reference profile. Exact fields, eligibility rules, schemas, validation, and source-state handling remain advanced architecture.
