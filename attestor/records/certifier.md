# Certifier Record

## Purpose

The Certifier Record serves as the trust-related reference connecting Attestor to verification and certification information maintained within Certifier.

Certifier evaluates evidence.

Attestor preserves trust context.

Certifier Records help document how verification outcomes, certifications, evidence reviews, and validation activities contribute to trust-related information.

---

## Why Certifier Records Exist

Trust often depends upon more than statements alone.

Users frequently want to know:

* Has this information been reviewed?
* Was supporting evidence evaluated?
* Has a certification been issued?
* What verification outcomes exist?
* What level of confidence may be warranted?

Certifier Records help preserve these relationships.

---

## Relationship to Certifier

Certifier serves as the verification layer of the Satoshium ecosystem.

A simplified relationship may be represented as:

```text id="h8t3xm"
Certifier → Verification
Attestor → Trust
```

Certifier helps determine whether support exists.

Attestor helps preserve the trust-related context associated with those findings.

---

## What a Certifier Record Contains

A Certifier Record may contain references to:

* Certification records
* Verification outcomes
* Evidence reviews
* Verification reports
* Validation findings
* Confidence indicators
* Associated trust records

Certifier Records function primarily as verification-context references.

Authority over certification and verification remains with Certifier.

## Certifier Records and Attestations

Verification and attestation are related but distinct.

A simplified distinction may be represented as:

```text id="j6r2fk"
Attestation → Statement
Verification → Evaluation
```

Attestations document claims.

Verification evaluates support for claims.

Certifier Records help preserve the relationship between the two.

## Guiding Principles

### Verification Should Remain Visible

Users should understand whether verification occurred.

### Evidence Matters

Verification context should remain connected to supporting evidence.

### Transparency Matters

Verification outcomes should remain understandable.

### Traceability Matters

Relationships should support review and investigation.

### Separation of Responsibilities

Certifier verifies.

Attestor preserves trust context.

---

## Long-Term Vision

As Attestor evolves, Certifier Records may become foundational components for documenting how verification activities influence trust, reputation, accountability, and confidence across digital ecosystems.

The objective is not to replace verification.

The objective is to preserve verification context.
