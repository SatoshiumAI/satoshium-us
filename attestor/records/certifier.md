# Certifier Reference Profile

## Purpose
Describes how Attestor may reference a **Certifier Certification Package** or other recognized Certifier-governed certification context when relevant and eligible.

## Canonical Boundary
**Certifier → Certification Package**  
**Attestor → Trust Statement**

Certification remains authoritative within Certifier. Attestor evaluates the relevance of eligible certification context to its own bounded conclusion.

## No Automatic Trust Effect
A certification outcome does not automatically become an Attestation, Trust Statement, trust score, reputation indicator, or favorable conclusion.

## Reference Requirements
A Certifier reference should preserve enough information to identify the Certification Package or recognized Certifier object, Certifier identifier, certification status and relevant state, provenance, applicable scope, relationship to the Attestation/evaluation, and Certifier authority.

## Historical Terms
Earlier Attestor material referenced verification reports, evidence reviews, validation findings, confidence indicators, and other Certifier artifacts. Such artifacts should be referenced only where current Certifier architecture recognizes them and Attestor has a defined reason to reference them directly.

## Governing Principle
> **Reference does not transfer authority.**

## Status
Foundational reference profile. Exact eligibility, schemas, validation, and certification-reference rules remain advanced architecture.
