# Chronicle Record

## Purpose

The Chronicle Record serves as the trust-related reference connecting Attestor to historical information preserved within Chronicle.

Chronicle preserves history.

Attestor preserves trust context.

Chronicle Records help document how historical events, timelines, records, corrections, and evidence contribute to trust-related understanding.

---

## Why Chronicle Records Exist

Trust often develops over time.

Users frequently want to understand:

* What happened previously?
* What historical evidence exists?
* Have similar events occurred before?
* How has an identity behaved over time?
* What corrections or changes have occurred?

These questions require historical context.

Chronicle Records help preserve the connection between history and trust.

---

## Relationship to Chronicle

Chronicle serves as the historical layer of the Satoshium ecosystem.

A simplified relationship may be represented as:

```text id="h4t8qn"
Chronicle → History
Attestor → Trust
```

Chronicle helps preserve what happened.

Attestor helps preserve why that history may matter from a trust perspective.

---

## What a Chronicle Record Contains

A Chronicle Record may contain references to:

* Historical entries
* Events
* Timelines
* Historical evidence
* Corrections
* Milestones
* Related attestations
* Related trust signals

Chronicle Records function primarily as historical-context references.

Authority over historical preservation remains with Chronicle.
