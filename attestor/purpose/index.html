Attestor · Purpose

Purpose

Attestor exists to explore, preserve, and organize trust-related information through attestations, evidence, trust signals, accountability records, and historical context.

Its purpose is not to determine truth, but to document information relevant to trust.

Preserve Attestations

Attestor provides a framework for documenting statements made by individuals, organizations, institutions, and systems regarding claims, events, identities, relationships, and records.

Preserve Evidence Context

Trust-related information becomes more useful when supporting evidence remains connected to the statements it informs.

Document Trust Signals

Trust signals provide contextual information that may influence how observers evaluate credibility, accountability, and reputation.

Support Accountability

Trust grows when actions, statements, corrections, and historical records remain visible and reviewable.

Preserve History

Historical context often influences trust. Attestor seeks to preserve relationships between trust-related information and historical records.

Enable Interoperability

Trust information should remain portable across identities, records, verification systems, and future digital ecosystems.

Core Philosophy

Trust is rarely the result of a single event. It often emerges through a combination of statements, evidence, actions, accountability, history, and transparency.

Attestation → Evidence → Trust Signal → Reputation → Trust

The Role of Attestor

Within the Satoshium ecosystem, Attestor serves as the trust layer connecting identity, verification, records, discovery, and history.
