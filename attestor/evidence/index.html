Attestor · Evidence

Evidence

Evidence provides supporting information, records, sources, and context connected to attestations, claims, trust signals, and future trust evaluations.

Evidence helps explain why a statement may deserve attention, but evidence does not automatically establish truth.

Evidence Records

Future evidence records may document the materials, sources, documents, media, datasets, or references associated with an attestation.

Future

Supporting Context

Evidence may provide context that supports, challenges, clarifies, or complicates a claim, record, event, or attestation.

Conceptual

Evidence Attestations

Evidence attestations may document statements about evidence, including whether it supports, partially supports, contradicts, or contextualizes a subject.

Planned

Source References

Evidence should remain connected to its sources whenever practical so origin, attribution, and traceability remain visible.

Defined

Evidence Relationship

Evidence supports interpretation. Attestations document statements. Trust emerges only after context, evidence, history, and judgment are considered together.

Evidence → Attestation → Trust Context

Evidence Philosophy

Evidence should remain transparent, attributable, traceable, and reviewable whenever practical.

Attestor does not treat evidence as automatic proof. It treats evidence as information that helps users understand why trust may or may not exist.
