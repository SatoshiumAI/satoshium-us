Attestor · Scope

Scope

The scope of Attestor defines the institutional responsibility, authority boundaries, and limitations within which Attestations are evaluated and Trust Statements are produced.

Attestor may reference governed information across the Satoshium Suite, but it does not absorb the canonical responsibilities of the institutions that produce or preserve those inputs.

Canonical responsibility: Attestor → Trust Statement
Governing principle: Reference does not transfer authority.

Within Scope

Attestor's scope includes governed Attestations, evaluation of eligible evidence and authoritative references, preservation of provenance and scope, production of Trust Statements, and governed correction or lifecycle treatment of Attestor-owned objects.

Assertion Scope

Attestor must preserve the boundary within which an Attestation applies. The subject, assertion, conditions, relevant time or context, and other applicable limitations should remain distinguishable wherever required by the governing model.

Evaluation Scope

Attestor evaluates only the question supported by its eligible inputs and applicable rules. An evaluation should not silently expand beyond the evidence, provenance, authority, status, or conditions available to support it.

Trust Statement Scope

A Trust Statement is a bounded Attestor conclusion. Its scope and limitations must remain visible so that a conclusion about one subject, condition, period, or purpose is not interpreted as a universal judgment.

Certifier Context

Attestor may reference a Certifier Certification Package as an authoritative input. Certifier retains authority over the certification and its canonical package. Attestor does not certify or recertify the subject.

Registry Context

Attestor may reference a Satoshium Registry record. Registry retains authority over the registered record, its identifier, status, and lifecycle. Attestor does not register or redefine the record.

Chronicle Context

Attestor may reference a Chronicle Entry as historical or provenance-bearing context. Chronicle retains authority over the preserved event record. Attestor does not rewrite Chronicle history.

Anchor Context

Attestor may reference an Anchor Integrity Reference when integrity context is relevant. Anchor retains authority over that integrity reference. Attestor does not establish Anchor's integrity determination.

Beacon Context

Attestor may reference Beacon Discovery Signals or Discovery Metadata when discovery context is relevant. Beacon retains authority over those discovery objects. Attestor does not create or redefine Beacon signals.

Atlas Context

Attestor may reference authoritative Atlas intelligence when relevant to an evaluation. Atlas retains authority over its intelligence and structural record. Attestor does not become the originating intelligence authority.

Navigator Context

Attestor may participate in workflows defined or orchestrated through Navigator. Navigator retains responsibility for workflow definition and orchestration; Attestor retains responsibility for its own governed evaluation and Trust Statement.

Other Governed Sources

Attestor may eventually accept eligible external or otherwise governed sources when advanced rules permit them. Eligibility does not transfer source authority to Attestor.

Outside Scope

Attestor does not determine universal truth, issue another institution's canonical objects, alter authoritative source records, create a generic reputation system, produce an automatic trust score, or replace independent human or institutional judgment beyond the meaning of its own governed Trust Statement.

Certification Package ≠ Trust Statement
Registry Record ≠ Trust Statement
Chronicle Entry ≠ Trust Statement
Integrity Reference ≠ Trust Statement
Discovery Signal ≠ Trust Statement
Attestor evaluates governed inputs to produce its own bounded Trust Statement.

Institutional Position

Attestor operates as a distinct Satoshium Suite institution with a bounded canonical responsibility:

Atlas → Authoritative Intelligence
Navigator → Workflow Definition / Orchestration
Certifier → Certification Package
Registry → Satoshium Registry Record
Chronicle → Chronicle Entry
Anchor → Integrity Reference
Beacon → Discovery Signal / Discovery Metadata
Attestor → Trust Statement

Interoperability connects institutional objects. It does not collapse their authority boundaries.
