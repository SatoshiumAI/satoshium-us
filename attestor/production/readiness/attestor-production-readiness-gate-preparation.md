## Critical Boundary

Templates prove that the institution has a controlled place to record a production fact. They do not prove that the future production fact is true.

Accordingly:

> Template readiness ≠ Production proof.

The formal Gate may determine whether these controls are sufficient to begin the first operation. The first operation itself must populate them with real governed evidence.
