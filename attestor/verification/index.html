Verification

Verification and trust are related but distinct concepts. Verification evaluates information. Trust evaluates confidence in information, people, organizations, identities, and records.

Verification may contribute trust signals, but verification alone does not determine trust.

Verification activities may produce certifications, findings, reports, validations, or other evidence that contribute to trust-related understanding.

Verification vs Trust

Verification seeks to evaluate information against evidence and defined standards.

Trust incorporates verification but also considers transparency, accountability, reputation, consistency, and historical behavior.
