Satoshium Attestor

Satoshium Attestor is the Satoshium Suite institution responsible for producing structured, attributable, evidence-linked Trust Statements. It expresses bounded trust conclusions while preserving the authority, provenance, status, and meaning of the records and systems it references.

Attestor does not determine universal truth, replace certification, redefine registered records, rewrite history, establish integrity, or perform discovery. It produces trust statements from governed inputs while maintaining explicit authority boundaries across the Suite.

Governing principle: Reference does not transfer authority.

Canonical Responsibility

Within the Satoshium Suite, Attestor owns the creation and lifecycle of Trust Statements. A Trust Statement may reference certifications, registry records, Chronicle events, Anchor integrity references, Beacon discovery metadata, Atlas intelligence, Navigator workflow context, or other governed Suite inputs, but those references remain authoritative within their originating institutions.

Attestor → Trust Statement

Authority Boundary

Attestor interprets governed inputs for a trust-specific purpose. It does not become the source of truth for the objects it references and does not silently inherit the authority of another Suite institution.

Certifier owns certification.
Registry owns registered record identity and state.
Chronicle owns preserved event history.
Anchor owns integrity references.
Beacon owns discovery signals and discovery metadata.
Attestor owns Trust Statements.

Suite Method

Attestor follows the Suite's evidence-first, authority-bounded method: identify the subject and applicable authority, reference eligible evidence and canonical records, evaluate the attestation under Attestor rules, preserve provenance and limitations, and produce a scoped Trust Statement.
