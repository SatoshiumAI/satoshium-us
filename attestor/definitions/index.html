Attestor · Definitions

Definitions

Definitions establish the shared vocabulary used throughout Attestor, helping preserve consistency, transparency, and interoperability across trust-related records and systems.

Trust depends not only on information, but on a common understanding of language.

Attestation

A statement made by an attestor regarding a subject, claim, event, record, identity, relationship, or observation.

Defined

Evidence

Information, documentation, records, or supporting materials that may contribute context to an attestation or trust evaluation.

Defined

Trust Signal

Information that may influence how an observer evaluates credibility, reliability, reputation, or trust-related context.

Defined

Correction

A revision, clarification, amendment, or retraction that improves understanding while preserving historical transparency.

Defined

Shared Vocabulary

Definitions help create consistent meaning across attestations, records, evidence, trust signals, and historical references.

Future Attestor standards may expand this vocabulary as additional trust-related concepts emerge.

Language → Meaning → Understanding → Trust

Definition Philosophy

Definitions should clarify concepts rather than impose conclusions. Shared understanding helps improve communication, transparency, and interoperability.
