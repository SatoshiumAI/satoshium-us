Attestor · Operational Definitions

Definitions

Definitions establish the foundational vocabulary used throughout Satoshium Attestor so that assertions, evidence, evaluation, provenance, lifecycle relationships, and Trust Statements can be interpreted consistently.

These definitions describe Attestor concepts within their institutional scope. They do not redefine canonical objects or terminology governed by other Satoshium Suite institutions.

Governing principle: Reference does not transfer authority.

Attestation

A governed, attributable assertion used by Attestor to express a bounded statement about a subject, record, relationship, condition, event, or other trust-relevant matter.

Established

Evidence

Supporting material or an authoritative reference used by Attestor to evaluate an Attestation and support a Trust Statement. Evidence retains its own source, provenance, status, relevance, and authority.

Established

Trust Statement

Attestor's canonical institutional output: a governed, attributable, bounded conclusion produced through Rule-Constrained Evaluation of an Attestation against eligible governed inputs while preserving provenance, scope, limitations, relevant state, and authority boundaries.

Canonical

Trust-Relevant Context

Information or an indicator that may be relevant to evaluating an Attestation or supporting a Trust Statement. The earlier term “trust signal” remains descriptive only and is not presently a separate canonical Attestor object.

Descriptive

Correction

A governed activity addressing an identified error or required change in an Attestor-owned object while preserving prior state, traceability, and the applicable Lifecycle and Versioning rules.

Established

Provenance

Information sufficient to understand the origin, attribution, custody, relationship, or relevant history of an input, assertion, evaluation, or Attestor output.

Established

Scope

The defined boundary within which an assertion, evidence relationship, evaluation, or Trust Statement applies. Scope prevents a bounded conclusion from silently becoming a universal one.

Established

Authority Boundary

The limit of Attestor's institutional authority in relation to an object, source, assertion, or conclusion. Referenced objects remain governed by their originating authorities.

Established

Established Supporting Terms

The operational architecture establishes the supporting vocabulary needed to interpret Attestor objects and operations consistently.

Attesting Authority · Eligible Governed Input · Eligibility · Evaluation · Evaluation Outcome · Lifecycle State · Publication State · Relationship · Version Identity · Validation · Conformance

Attesting Authority

The authority attributable to the governed assertion expressed by an Attestation. Attesting Authority remains distinct from Referenced Authority and Attestor Authority.

Established

Eligible Governed Input

A governed source object, reference, evidence item, Attestation, or other admissible input that has satisfied the applicable eligibility determination for a particular Attestor evaluation.

Established

Eligibility

A governed admission determination for a particular Attestor evaluation. Availability, authority, and reference do not by themselves establish eligibility.

Established

Evaluation

The rule-constrained institutional process through which Attestor considers an Attestation and eligible governed inputs to determine an Evaluation Outcome and form a bounded conclusion.

Established

Evaluation Outcome

A controlled result of Rule-Constrained Evaluation: supported, partially-supported, not-supported, contradicted, or indeterminate. An Evaluation Outcome is not itself the bounded conclusion or Trust Statement identity.

Established

Lifecycle State

The governed state of an Attestor object: draft, active, superseded, withdrawn, or retired. Review and correction are activities rather than lifecycle states.

Established

Publication State

The governed public-representation state of an Attestor object: unpublished or published. Canonical creation, lifecycle activation, and publication remain distinct.

Established

Relationship

A governed directional connection between Attestor objects or referenced objects using the adopted relationship vocabulary without implying identity or authority transfer.

Established

Version Identity

The identity of a governed state of a canonical object. Canonical identity answers which object; version identity answers which governed state of that object.

Established

Validation

The determination of whether an Attestor object satisfies the normative structural and rule requirements applicable to that object. Validation is distinct from substantive Evaluation.

Operational

Conformance

A determination that a declared target satisfies an applicable requirements set using the required validation and evidence. Validation supports Conformance but does not equal it.

Operational

Relevant State

The material temporal, lifecycle, publication, or source-state context applicable to an Attestation, governed input, Evaluation, or Trust Statement at the time it is interpreted or evaluated.

Established

Suite Vocabulary Boundary

Attestor may reference Certification Packages, Satoshium Registry records, Chronicle Entries, Anchor Integrity References, Beacon Discovery Signals or Discovery Metadata, Atlas intelligence, Navigator workflow context, and other governed information.

Attestor definitions describe how those references participate in Attestor evaluation. They do not replace the definitions, schemas, identifiers, lifecycle rules, or authority established by the originating institution.

Shared interoperability does not require shared ownership of meaning.
