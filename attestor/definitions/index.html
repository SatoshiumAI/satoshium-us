Attestor · Definitions

Definitions

Definitions establish the foundational vocabulary used throughout Satoshium Attestor so that assertions, evidence, evaluation, provenance, lifecycle relationships, and Trust Statements can be interpreted consistently.

These definitions describe Attestor concepts within their institutional scope. They do not redefine canonical objects or terminology governed by other Satoshium Suite institutions.

Governing principle: Reference does not transfer authority.

Attestation

A governed, attributable assertion used by Attestor to express a bounded statement about a subject, record, relationship, condition, event, or other trust-relevant matter.

Foundational

Evidence

Supporting material or an authoritative reference used by Attestor to evaluate an Attestation and support a Trust Statement. Evidence retains its own source, provenance, status, relevance, and authority.

Foundational

Trust Statement

Attestor's canonical institutional output: a governed, attributable, bounded conclusion produced through Attestor evaluation from eligible inputs while preserving provenance, scope, limitations, status, and authority boundaries.

Canonical

Trust-Relevant Context

Information or an indicator that may be relevant to evaluating an Attestation or supporting a Trust Statement. The earlier term “trust signal” remains descriptive only and is not presently a separate canonical Attestor object.

Descriptive

Correction

A governed change addressing an identified error in an Attestor assertion or Trust Statement while preserving the relationship between the prior and corrected state.

Foundational

Provenance

Information sufficient to understand the origin, attribution, custody, relationship, or relevant history of an input, assertion, evaluation, or Attestor output.

Foundational

Scope

The defined boundary within which an assertion, evidence relationship, evaluation, or Trust Statement applies. Scope prevents a bounded conclusion from silently becoming a universal one.

Foundational

Authority Boundary

The limit of Attestor's institutional authority in relation to an object, source, assertion, or conclusion. Referenced objects remain governed by their originating authorities.

Foundational

Supporting Terms

Advanced Attestor architecture will require additional controlled terminology for concepts such as subject, attesting authority, source, status, lifecycle, clarification, withdrawal, supersession, versioning, eligibility, validation, and evaluation.

Those terms should be defined when their architecture is established rather than prematurely assigning machine meanings during foundational reconciliation.

Concept → Definition → Governed Use → Validation

Suite Vocabulary Boundary

Attestor may reference Certification Packages, Satoshium Registry records, Chronicle Entries, Anchor Integrity References, Beacon Discovery Signals or Discovery Metadata, Atlas intelligence, Navigator workflow context, and other governed information.

Attestor definitions describe how those references participate in Attestor evaluation. They do not replace the definitions, schemas, identifiers, lifecycle rules, or authority established by the originating institution.

Shared interoperability does not require shared ownership of meaning.

Definition Governance

Foundational definitions should remain stable enough to support coherent architecture while allowing advanced Attestor work to refine terms where necessary. Machine vocabulary, controlled values, and schema-level semantics should be adopted only when their governing structures are established.
