ATTESTOR · ATTESTATION GENERATION

Satoshium Attestor Attestation Generation

Attestation Generation defines how Attestor produces attestations from certifications, records, events, anchors, and supporting references.

Attestor does not duplicate Certifier. It transforms referenced authority into accountable trust statements.

Purpose

Attestation Generation establishes the process by which authoritative records become structured attestations without transferring authority away from the originating system.

Core Relationship

Certification may support an attestation, but the attestation is not the certification. It is a trust statement that references certification.

Reference-Based

Attestations should reference Certification Packages, SCRDs, SREGs, events, anchors, and public records rather than copying their contents.

Trust Statement Output

The output of attestation generation is a structured trust statement that identifies what is being attested, what supports it, and where authority originates.

Generation Sequence

Attestor follows a structured process for producing attestations from authoritative references.

Certification Referenced → Evidence Reviewed → Source Records Identified → Trust Statement Drafted → Attestation Produced → Public Reference Preserved

Certification Referenced

Attestor may begin with a certification output such as a Certification Package, SCPR, SCR, SCRD, or related public certification record.

Evidence Reviewed

Supporting evidence, metadata, public references, Registry entries, Chronicle events, and Anchor references may be reviewed for context.

Source Records Identified

Attestor identifies the source systems and records that support the attestation so authority remains traceable.

Trust Statement Drafted

A trust statement is drafted to express what is being attested and which authoritative records support the statement.

Attestation Produced

The attestation is produced as an accountable statement referencing the records, systems, identities, or artifacts that support it.

Public Reference Preserved

The generated attestation may be published, referenced, indexed, discovered, anchored, or cataloged by other Suite systems.

What Attestation Generation Uses

Attestation Generation may rely on multiple Suite objects and references without taking ownership of them.

Certification Package · SCPR · SCR · SCRD · SREG · Chronicle Event · Anchor Reference · Beacon Signal

What Attestation Generation Does Not Do

Attestation Generation does not certify a subject, issue a certification, create Registry status, preserve historical events, anchor artifacts, or publish discovery signals.

Certifier certifies. Attestor attests.

Generation Philosophy

A strong attestation is valuable because it is accountable, traceable, and supported by references to authoritative records.

The goal is not to recreate the certification. The goal is to produce a clear trust statement that can be understood, reviewed, and connected to its supporting authority.
