Certifier · Attestations

Attestations

Attestations support independent verification of Satoshium certification records, receipts, evidence references, integrity references, and Suite-ready outputs generated from a canonical Certification Package.

Within the Suite architecture, Certifier issues certification artifacts while Attestor may later produce attestation records that verify selected claims, references, hashes, Registry entries, and preserved certification outputs.

Attestation reference associated with SC-CERT-2026-0001 for the Atlas Jurisdiction Record — El Salvador. The reference supports future verification of the certification package, public receipt, certified record, evidence references, and registry-ready outputs.

Attestation Record Template

Defined Structure

The attestation template defines how future verification records may identify the certification object, referenced artifacts, evidence links, integrity references, verification status, and reviewer or system statements.

Verification Scope

Bounded

Attestations should verify specific certification facts or artifact relationships. They do not replace the Certification Package, reopen the certification decision, or independently redefine Suite Standards or Suite Methodology.

Suite Integration

Downstream Support

Attestation records may reference SCR receipts, SCRD certified records, SREG Registry entries, ANCH integrity references, SCHR historical records, and supporting SEV evidence records.

Attestation Flow

Certifier produces the certification record. Registry catalogs it. Chronicle preserves its historical context. Anchor preserves integrity references. Attestor supports verification of selected certification facts, evidence references, and generated outputs.

Attestation Record

Attestation Role

Attestations are not the certification authority. They are verification-support records that help preserve confidence in the relationship between a certification decision and the artifacts, evidence, hashes, receipts, certified records, and Registry references that support it.

Standards define expectations. Methodology defines the process. Certifier issues the certification. Attestor supports future verification of what was issued, referenced, preserved, and cataloged.
