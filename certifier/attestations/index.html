Attestations

Attestations support independent verification of Satoshium certification decisions, Certified Records, receipts, evidence references, integrity references, Registry entries, and other Suite-ready outputs generated from a canonical Certification Package.

Within the Suite architecture, Certifier issues certification artifacts while Attestor may later produce Attestation Records that verify selected claims, references, hashes, Registry entries, preserved outputs, and relationships among certification records.

Attestation Record Template

Defined Structure

The Attestation Record template defines how future verification records may identify the referenced certification, selected claims, related artifacts, evidence links, integrity references, Registry entries, verification status, and reviewer or system statements.

Verification Scope

Bounded

Attestations verify specific certification claims, facts, references, or artifact relationships. They do not replace the Certification Package, reopen or alter the certification decision, or independently redefine Suite Standards or Suite Methodology.

Suite Integration

Downstream Support

Attestation records may reference SCR receipts, SCRD certified records, SREG Registry entries, ANCH integrity references, SCHR historical records, and supporting SEV evidence records.

Attestation Flow

Certifier issues the certification and its generated artifacts. Registry catalogs the Certified Record. Chronicle preserves certification history. Anchor preserves integrity references. Attestor may then verify selected claims, evidence references, record relationships, and preserved outputs.

Attestation Role

Attestation Records are not certification decisions and Attestor is not the certification authority. They preserve verifiable statements about selected claims, references, hashes, evidence, certified records, Registry entries, and other artifacts connected to an issued certification.

Standards define expectations. Methodology defines the process. Certifier issues the certification. Registry catalogs it. Chronicle preserves its history. Anchor preserves its integrity references. Attestor verifies selected claims and relationships without replacing the original certification authority.
