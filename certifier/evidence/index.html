Certifier · Evidence

Evidence

Evidence is the factual foundation of Satoshium Certifier. It supports reviews, findings, reports, receipts, certification records, and future verification.

Every certification determination should be traceable to reviewable evidence available at the time of certification.

Screenshots

Screenshots preserve visual proof of pages, interfaces, workflows, public records, and certification outputs at a specific point in time.

Evidence Type

Hashes

Hashes provide integrity references that help determine whether an artifact has changed after review.

Integrity

Notes

Notes preserve observations, assumptions, limitations, reviewer comments, and reasoning that may not fit inside structured records.

Context

Archived Evidence

Archived evidence preserves superseded, expired, revoked, or historical materials so past certification activity remains reviewable.

Preservation

Evidence Role

Evidence connects certification claims to reviewable materials. Standards define what should be evaluated; evidence shows what was examined.

Certifier favors preservation over deletion. Evidence that supported a decision may remain historically important even after a certification expires, changes, or is revoked.
Evidence turns certification from assertion into reviewable record.
