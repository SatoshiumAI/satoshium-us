Methodology

Methodology explains how Satoshium Certifier applies the Suite Methodology to perform evidence-based certification reviews, create Certification Packages, issue decisions, and generate reviewable certification artifacts.

Suite Standards define the expectations. Suite Methodology defines the process. Certifier executes that process as the Suite’s operational certification implementation.

Methodology Role

Methodology is no longer framed as a Certifier-owned rule system. It is a Suite-level process that Certifier applies through operational review, evidence organization, documented reasoning, certification decisions, and artifact generation.

Standards define what must be true. Methodology defines how review is performed. Certifier preserves the result as a reviewable certification record.

Operational Boundary

Certifier does not independently redefine the Suite Methodology. It implements the methodology by applying it to certification subjects and producing Certification Packages, reports, receipts, certified records, and Suite-ready references.

This distinction keeps the institutional architecture clear: Suite Methodology governs the process; Certifier performs the certification work.
Consistent methodology makes certification repeatable; preserved records make it reviewable.
