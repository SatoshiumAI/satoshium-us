Methodology

Methodology explains the repeatable process used by Satoshium Certifier to evaluate certification targets using standards, evidence, documented findings, and transparent reasoning.

Every certification should follow the same review methodology so that determinations remain consistent, reproducible, and reviewable over time.

1. Define Scope

Identify the certification target, applicable standards, assumptions, and review boundaries.

2. Collect Evidence

4. Issue Determination

Methodology Role

Methodology is the procedural layer of Certifier. Standards define what should be evaluated; methodology defines how that evaluation is performed.

A consistent methodology improves transparency, repeatability, and confidence in every certification issued by Satoshium Certifier.
Consistent methodology builds consistent trust.
