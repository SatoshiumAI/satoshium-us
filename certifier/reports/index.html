Certification Reports

Certification Reports are official generated review artifacts produced by Satoshium Certifier from a canonical Certification Package.

Reports preserve scope, evidence, applicable Suite Standards, applicable Suite Methodology, findings, limitations, assessment, and certification decision in a reviewable human-readable form.

SCPR — Certification Process Report
The Certification Package preserves the canonical record. The Certification Process Report preserves the review reasoning. The Certification Receipt summarizes the outcome. The Certified Record preserves the public and machine-readable certification result.

Report Role

Certification Reports make certification reasoning visible. They connect evidence to findings, findings to assessment, and assessment to certification decision while preserving the governing Suite context behind each review.

Evidence provides facts. Suite Standards define expectations. Suite Methodology defines the review process. Certifier executes the review and produces the report as a generated record of certification reasoning.
