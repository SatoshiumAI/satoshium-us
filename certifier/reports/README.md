# Certification Reports

This directory contains Certification Reports generated by the Satoshium Certifier framework.

Certification Reports serve as the primary review record supporting certification activities.

A Certification Report documents:

* What was reviewed
* Why it was reviewed
* What standards were applied
* What evidence was considered
* What findings were reached
* What determination was made

Reports are the authoritative record of a certification event.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the reports directory is to preserve detailed certification reviews in a structured, transparent, and durable format.

Certification Reports provide the foundation upon which certification determinations are based.

Reports help answer questions such as:

* What certification activity occurred?
* What evidence supported the review?
* What findings were documented?
* Why was certification granted or denied?
* Can the determination be understood years later?

Reports provide the reasoning layer of Certifier.

---

# Report Philosophy

Certifier distinguishes between:

```text id="mdxqsw"
Evidence
     ↓
Report
     ↓
Receipt
```

Evidence supports review.

Reports document review.

Receipts summarize review.

The report is the central record connecting evidence to certification outcomes.

# Relationship to Certification Records

Certification Records capture structured metadata describing a certification event.

Certification Reports provide the narrative and supporting details behind that record.

Typical relationship:

```text id="78n6jo"

          ↓
```

```text id="qz5s76"
Evidence
     ↓
```

# Relationship to Receipts

Certification Receipts summarize Certification Reports.

Typical relationship:

```text id="72lzri"
Certification Report
          ↓
Certification Receipt
```

Reports preserve detail.

Receipts preserve proof.

Future reviewers should be able to trace every receipt back to a corresponding report.

# Preservation Philosophy

Certification Reports should generally be preserved indefinitely whenever practical.

Reports contain:

* Findings
* Reasoning
* Context
* Determinations

These records may remain valuable long after certification events have concluded.

Historical reports should be archived rather than deleted whenever possible.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The reports directory serves as the institutional memory of Certifier.

Years after a certification event occurs, the report should still allow a future reviewer to understand:

* What happened
* Why it happened
* What evidence existed
* What standards were applied
* What determination was reached

The report therefore becomes more than documentation.

It becomes preserved reasoning.

As the Satoshium ecosystem expands, Certification Reports may become some of the most important records generated by Certifier.
