Certifier · Governance

Governance

Governance explains how Satoshium Certifier is maintained as the Suite’s operational certification implementation while remaining aligned with Suite Standards, Suite Methodology, and the broader Satoshium certification ecosystem.

Certifier governance does not define Suite-wide certification rules by itself. It preserves operational consistency, version control, record integrity, and responsible maintenance for the certification artifacts Certifier produces.

Suite Standards → Suite Methodology → Certifier Workflow →

Governance Purpose

Certifier governance keeps certification operations consistent, transparent, evidence-based, and reviewable across time while preserving the authority of Suite-level standards and methodology.

Operational

Suite Alignment

Suite Standards define certification expectations and Suite Methodology defines the certification process. Certifier governance ensures that Certifier pages, records, templates, and outputs remain aligned with those governing layers.

Suite-Governed

Revision Control

Certification pages, schemas, templates, artifact formats, evidence models, and operational references should be versioned or preserved when substantive changes occur.

Required

Record Preservation

Certifier favors preservation over deletion. Superseded, expired, revoked, archived, or replaced certification materials should remain reviewable whenever practical.

Principle

Decision Integrity

Certification decisions should be traceable to certification subject, scope, governing standards, methodology, evidence, findings, assessment, and the canonical Certification Package.

Core Rule

Artifact Stewardship

Governance maintains the relationship between the Certification Package and generated artifacts, including SCPR, SCR, SCRD HTML, SCRD JSON, SREG, SCHR, ANCH, SATR, and SEV references.

Preserved

Governance Flow

Governance protects the operational path through which Certifier applies Suite rules and produces durable certification records.

Governance maintains the integrity of the certification system. Standards define expectations. Methodology defines process. Certifier governance keeps implementation, artifacts, pages, and preservation practices coherent over time.

Governance Role

Governance is the operational stewardship layer of Certifier. It does not replace Suite Standards, Suite Methodology, evidence, reports, receipts, Registry entries, or attestations. It explains how Certifier’s implementation should remain coherent, maintainable, and historically reviewable.

Certifier may evolve, but its records should remain understandable. Governance protects that continuity by preserving the relationship between decisions, evidence, artifacts, and Suite references.
Governance preserves coherence so certification records can outlast the moment that created them.
