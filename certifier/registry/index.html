Certifier · Registry

Registry

Registry describes how Satoshium Certifier prepares certification records for cataloging, discovery, preservation, and Suite integration after a certification decision is issued.

Certifier produces registry-ready references from the canonical Certification Package. The Satoshium Registry catalogs those references as SREG entries while preserving the distinction between certification authority and record discovery.

SREG-2026-000001

Registry-Ready Reference

The inaugural registry-ready reference corresponds to SC-CERT-2026-0001, the Atlas Jurisdiction Record — El Salvador certification. The Certification Package remains the canonical certification object; the SREG entry provides cataloging and discovery context.

Registry Entry Model

SREG

A Satoshium Registry Entry catalogs certification identifiers, subjects, statuses, artifact references, evidence references, integrity references, and related Suite records without replacing the underlying Certification Package.

Certification Package Source

Canonical Object

Registry references are derived from Certification Packages, not from informal summaries. This preserves traceability from SREG back to SCPR, SCR, SCRD HTML, SCRD JSON, evidence records, and the certification decision.

Discoverability

Catalog Layer

Registry makes certified records discoverable across the Suite by organizing certification outputs into structured, queryable, and historically stable references.

Suite Integration

Interoperable

Registry entries connect Certifier outputs with Chronicle history, Anchor integrity references, Beacon discovery metadata, Attestor trust statements, and Navigator workflows.

Authority Boundary

Separated Roles

Certifier certifies. Registry catalogs. Registry does not issue certification decisions, alter determinations, or replace the Certification Package as the authoritative source.

Registry Handoff Flow

The registry handoff begins after Certifier has established a certification subject, reviewed evidence, created the canonical Certification Package, issued a decision, and generated public and machine-readable certification artifacts.

Registry Role

Certifier creates certification records. Registry organizes and exposes those records for long-term discovery across the Satoshium ecosystem. This page preserves the Certifier-side handoff between operational certification and Registry cataloging.
