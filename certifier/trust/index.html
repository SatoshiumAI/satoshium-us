Certifier · Trust Model

Trust Model

Satoshium Certifier does not ask users to trust certification outcomes because they are asserted. It builds trust by preserving standards, evidence, methodology, reasoning, records, receipts, and future verification pathways.

The Trust Model explains why Certifier records can be reviewed, understood, challenged, preserved, and verified across time.

Published Standards

Trust begins with visible rules. Certification determinations should reference published standards that define what was evaluated and what requirements applied.

Rules

Reviewable Evidence

Evidence supports certification findings. Screenshots, URLs, notes, hashes, reports, datasets, and other materials allow future reviewers to understand what was examined.

Evidence

Transparent Reasoning

Certification Reports preserve the reasoning behind determinations, including scope, methodology, findings, limitations, assumptions, and reviewer judgment.

Reasoning

Public Receipts

Certification Receipts provide concise public proof that certification occurred and direct users to the supporting report, record, standard, and registry reference.

Proof

Registry Discovery

Registry entries make certification records discoverable, referenceable, and easier to manage across the broader Satoshium ecosystem.

Catalog

Future Attestation

Attestations may independently review certification records, receipts, reports, evidence, hashes, and Registry entries to strengthen confidence in Certifier outputs.

Verification

Trust Model Role

The Trust Model is the confidence architecture of Certifier. It explains how standards, evidence, methodology, reports, receipts, Registry entries, preservation references, and attestations combine to support reviewable trust.

Trust is not declared. Trust is constructed through transparent rules, preserved evidence, documented reasoning, public records, and independent verification.
Trust is not assumed. It is documented, reviewed, preserved, and verified.
