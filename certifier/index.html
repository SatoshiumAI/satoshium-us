Satoshium Certifier

Satoshium Certifier is the Operational Certification institution of the Satoshium Suite. It applies applicable Suite Standards and certification-specific Suite Methodology to evidence-supported certification while preserving the authority of every other Suite institution.

Certifier evaluates certification subjects by organizing governed evidence, applying applicable standards and certification methodology, reaching certification determinations, and preserving the resulting Certification Package. Generated reports, receipts, certified records, machine-readable records, and Suite-ready references remain Certifier artifacts derived from that Package.

Institutional Position

Certifier is the Suite's Operational Certification institution. Its canonical operational object is the Certification Package. Certifier applies applicable Suite Standards and certification-specific Suite Methodology through its own evidence architecture, workflow, Certification Classes, schema profiles, generated outputs, and interoperability practices.

Governance Boundary

Certifier governs certification operations within its settled institutional authority. Routine and institution-owned material certification changes remain with Certifier, subject to Suite coherence review where applicable. Changes to institutional role, canonical ownership, Suite membership, authority allocation, or constitutional position require Suite Governance / Architecture Reconciliation.

Operational Certifier Structure

Certifier is established as the Suite’s operational implementation for evidence-based certification. Its operational structure centers on the Certification Package as Certifier's canonical operational object, from which reports, receipts, certified records, machine-readable records, and Suite references are generated. Certifier exists to transform evidence into reviewable, repeatable, and independently verifiable certification records for the Satoshium Suite.

The inaugural operational certification, SC-CERT-2026-0001, established the Atlas Jurisdiction Record — El Salvador as Certifier's first production subject and proved the end-to-end certification workflow from evidence review through canonical Certification Package and downstream Suite references.
The SCRD JSON record SCRD-SC-CERT-2026-0001 also became the Source Artifact for ANCH-2026-0001, the first published Satoshium Anchor Integrity Reference. This demonstrates the Suite boundary in production: Certifier owns the certification record; Anchor owns the Integrity Reference that preserves integrity context for its defined representation.
The canonical Certification Package SC-CERT-2026-0001 subsequently became the primary authoritative source for BEAC-2026-0001, Satoshium Beacon's first published production Discovery Signal. Beacon owns the discovery representation; Certifier continues to own the certification decision, class, lifecycle, and status. Reference does not transfer authority.
