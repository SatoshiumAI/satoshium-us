Certifier · Operational Implementation

Satoshium Certifier

Satoshium Certifier is the operational certification implementation of the Satoshium Suite. It applies Suite Standards and Suite Methodology to review digital artifacts, workflows, services, reports, datasets, tools, and records through evidence-supported certification.

Certifier evaluates certification subjects by organizing evidence, applying Suite Standards and Suite Methodology, reaching certification decisions, and preserving the resulting Certification Package. Reports, receipts, certified records, machine-readable records, and Suite-ready references are generated from that Package.

Institutional Position

Certifier operates beneath Suite Standards and Suite Methodology and serves as the Suite's operational certification implementation. It applies those shared foundations through its own Evidence architecture, Workflow, Certification Classes, schema, Certification Package, generated outputs, trust framework, and interoperability rules.

Operational Certifier Structure

Certifier is established as the Suite’s reference implementation for evidence-based certification. Its operational structure centers on the Certification Package as Certifier's canonical operational object, from which reports, receipts, certified records, machine-readable records, and Suite references are generated. Certifier exists to transform evidence into reviewable, repeatable, and independently verifiable certification records for the Satoshium Suite.

The inaugural operational certification, SC-CERT-2026-0001, established the Atlas Jurisdiction Record — El Salvador as Certifier's first production subject and proved the end-to-end certification workflow from evidence review through canonical Certification Package and downstream Suite references.

Certifier Workflow

Certifier follows a repeatable workflow in which a certification subject is reviewed against Suite Standards and Suite Methodology, evidence is organized, a canonical Certification Package is created, and generated certification artifacts are produced for review, public verification, cataloging, preservation, integrity protection, discovery, and future attestation across the Satoshium Suite.
