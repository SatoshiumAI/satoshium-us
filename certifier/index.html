Satoshium Certifier

Satoshium Certifier is the trust and certification layer of the Satoshium ecosystem. It reviews digital artifacts, workflows, services, reports, datasets, and tools against defined standards and preserves evidence-supported certification records.

Certifier begins as a static public interface for standards, certified items, reports, receipts, registry references, and future attestations.

Standards

Certification standards define the requirements, criteria, evidence expectations, and determination rules used by Certifier.

Certified Items

Certified Items will list artifacts, services, workflows, datasets, tools, and pages that have completed review under Certifier.
