Satoshium Certifier

Satoshium Certifier is the trust and certification layer of the Satoshium ecosystem. It reviews digital artifacts, workflows, services, reports, datasets, and tools against defined standards and preserves evidence-supported certification records.

Certifier begins as a public interface for standards, certified items, reports, receipts, registry references, and future attestations. Its first major certification target is expected to be the Satoshium Atlas Initial Build Phase.

Initial Certifier Structure

Certifier is currently being established as a standards-based framework for review, evidence preservation, certification reporting, public receipts, Registry integration, and future attestation support.

The first major certification target is expected to be the Atlas Initial Build Phase. This will establish Certifier’s first operational certification record, report, receipt, and Registry-ready reference.

Certifier Workflow

Certifier follows a simple review sequence: input, review, evidence, certification, receipt, and Registry. This workflow transforms digital artifacts into documented, reviewable trust records.

Atlas creates intelligence. Certifier reviews it. Registry catalogs it. Chronicle remembers it. Anchor preserves it. Attestor verifies it.
Satoshium Certifier is being built as a trust layer — one certification record at a time.
