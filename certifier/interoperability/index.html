Certifier applies Suite Standards and Suite Methodology to produce Certification Packages, reports, receipts, certified records, machine-readable outputs, and Suite-ready references that can be cataloged, preserved, anchored, discovered, attested, and orchestrated by other systems.

Interoperability Role

Interoperability ensures that Certifier is not an isolated review surface. It turns certification outputs into structured, traceable, reusable records that can participate in the Satoshium Suite without collapsing the distinct responsibilities of each system.

Certifier certifies. Registry catalogs. Chronicle preserves history. Anchor preserves integrity. Beacon publishes discovery. Attestor supports verification. Navigator orchestrates use.

Boundary Principle

Interoperability does not mean that every Suite system performs certification. Certifier remains the operational certification implementation. Other Suite systems consume, preserve, reference, verify, discover, or route certification outputs according to their own institutional roles.

The Certification Package remains the canonical source. Suite references extend its usability; they do not replace the certification decision.
Interoperability turns certification records into Suite infrastructure.
