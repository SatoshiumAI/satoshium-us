# Overview

Satoshium Certifier is a standards-based review and certification framework designed to evaluate digital artifacts, intelligence outputs, services, workflows, datasets, and other qualifying records against defined criteria.

Its purpose is not to create information, but to assess information and determine whether it satisfies an established certification standard.

Certifier exists to provide structured evidence that a review occurred, that evaluation criteria were applied, and that the resulting determination was documented in a repeatable and transparent manner.

Within the Satoshium ecosystem, Certifier serves as the trust and verification layer between creation and long-term reliance.

---

# Mission Statement

The mission of Satoshium Certifier is to establish a repeatable framework for evaluating digital artifacts and producing verifiable records of review, certification, and evidence.

Certifier seeks to increase transparency, consistency, accountability, and trust by documenting how determinations are made and preserving the supporting evidence behind those determinations.

---

# Purpose

Digital systems increasingly generate information at a scale that exceeds the practical ability of individuals to manually review every output.

Artificial intelligence systems, automation platforms, decentralized networks, and collaborative publishing environments can create large volumes of content, records, reports, and operational artifacts.

The existence of information alone does not establish reliability.

Certifier exists to answer fundamental questions:

* Was this reviewed?
* Against what standard was it reviewed?
* What evidence was considered?
* What conclusions were reached?
* Who or what performed the review?
* When did the review occur?
* Can the process be independently understood later?

By creating structured certification records, Certifier transforms review activities into durable, inspectable evidence.

# What Certifier Is Not

Satoshium Certifier is not:

* A legal authority.
* A regulatory authority.
* A licensing authority.
* A governmental body.
* A court.
* A replacement for independent verification.
* A guarantee of truth.
* A guarantee of accuracy.
* A guarantee of future performance.
* A substitute for professional judgment.
* A substitute for legal, financial, medical, engineering, or regulatory review.

Certification indicates that an artifact was reviewed according to a defined certification standard at a particular point in time.

Certification does not imply perfection, permanence, endorsement, or immunity from future revision.

# Foundational Principles

Certifier is guided by the following principles:

## Transparency

Certification processes should be understandable and reviewable.

## Repeatability

Equivalent reviews should produce equivalent outcomes when evaluated under the same standards.

## Evidence-Based Evaluation

Certification decisions should be supported by documented evidence.

## Traceability

Certification records should preserve sufficient information to reconstruct how a determination was reached.

## Neutrality

Certification standards should be applied consistently regardless of origin, author, or affiliation.

## Preservation

Certification history should remain available for future review whenever practical.

## Interoperability

Certification records should be capable of integration with other systems and future Satoshium subsystems.
