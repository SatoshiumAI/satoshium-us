Definition of Completion

Satoshium Certifier is complete for the July 2026 milestone when it is public, coherent, operational, repeatable, and evidence-ready. Completion does not require full automation, external attestations, or mature API infrastructure. It requires a functioning certification implementation that can issue, preserve, and explain certification artifacts under the Suite's governing framework.

July completion means Certifier is ready as the Suite's first operational certification implementation. Later months may strengthen automation, Registry maturity, external verification, and workflow orchestration.
Certification is completed through alignment, evidence, artifacts, and preservation.
