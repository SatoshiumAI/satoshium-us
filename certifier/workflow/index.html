The workflow defines the operational path followed by every certification within Satoshium Certifier, from initial review through permanent preservation.

Each stage produces structured records that support transparency, repeatability, and long-term trust.

Every certification should follow the same workflow regardless of the certification target, ensuring consistency across reports, receipts, and records.

Lifecycle Integration

The workflow incorporates lifecycle states from Draft through Archived, allowing certifications to evolve while preserving historical context.

Integrated

Ecosystem Integration

The workflow connects Atlas, Certifier, Registry, Chronicle, Anchor, Beacon, Attestor, and Navigator into a unified certification ecosystem.

Growing

Workflow Role

The workflow is the operational backbone of Certifier. Methodology explains how reviews are performed; the workflow defines how information moves through the certification system.

A consistent workflow ensures that every certification can be reproduced, understood, audited, and preserved years after it is issued.
Repeatable workflow produces repeatable trust.
