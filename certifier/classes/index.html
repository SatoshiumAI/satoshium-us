Certification Classes

Certification Classes communicate the level, scope, and confidence posture of a certification issued by Satoshium Certifier under the governing Suite Standards and Suite Methodology.

Certification Classes do not create certification authority. They communicate the outcome of a completed certification by describing the confidence posture established through preserved Evidence Records, applicable Suite Standards, applied Suite Methodology, and the resulting Certification Package.

Suite Standards → Suite Methodology → Certifier Workflow →

Informational

Informational identifies certification subjects whose purpose is documentation, observation, inventory, or factual preservation rather than operational certification.

Evidence may be complete and reviewable, but the certification objective is to preserve institutional knowledge rather than certify operational capability.

Foundation

Operational

Operational identifies certification subjects that satisfy the applicable Suite Standards and Suite Methodology for their intended operational purpose.

The assigned class communicates that the certification subject has successfully completed the defined certification workflow and is certified within its documented operational scope.

Operational

Verified

Verified represents the highest current Certification Class. It indicates that evidence, review, methodology application, preservation, and generated certification artifacts collectively support the strongest available certification confidence.

Verified certifications typically include stronger evidence preservation, integrity references, Registry integration, and future Attestation support.

Highest Class

Class Assignment Flow

A Certification Class is assigned only after the Certification Subject has completed evidence review, application of Suite Standards, application of Suite Methodology, evaluation, and certification decision. The assigned class becomes part of the canonical Certification Package and is propagated to all generated certification artifacts.

Artifact Reflection

Certification Class should remain consistent wherever the certification decision is represented, including the Certification Package, SCPR, SCR, SCRD HTML, SCRD JSON, Registry references, and future Suite integrations.

Required Consistency

Not a Value Judgment

Certification Classes communicate the level and purpose of review. They do not measure commercial value, importance, popularity, ownership, or general superiority of a certification subject.

Boundary

Future Evolution

Additional Certification Classes may be introduced as Certifier evolves. Historical certifications should always remain traceable to the class definitions that existed when each certification was issued.

Versioned

Certification Class Role

Certification Classes communicate the outcome of certification. Suite Standards define expectations. Suite Methodology defines implementation. Evidence supports evaluation. Certifier performs certification. The assigned Certification Class summarizes the confidence posture established by the completed certification record.

A Certification Class should never be interpreted independently. It derives its meaning from the Certification Package, preserved Evidence Records, applicable Suite Standards, applied Suite Methodology, and the generated certification artifacts that document the certification decision.
Certification Classes communicate the depth, scope, and confidence posture of review.
