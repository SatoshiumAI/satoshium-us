Certification Classes

Certification Classes communicate the level, scope, and confidence posture of a certification issued by Satoshium Certifier under the governing Suite Standards and Suite Methodology.

Classes do not create certification authority by themselves. They describe the type of review performed, the intended use of the certification result, and the relationship between evidence, methodology, and the resulting Certification Package.

Suite Standards → Suite Methodology → Certifier Workflow →

Informational

Informational certifications document observations, facts, records, structures, or conditions without asserting operational readiness or comprehensive verification.

This class is useful when the purpose is to preserve reviewable information, identify evidence, or describe a certification subject without issuing a stronger operational conclusion.

Foundation

Operational

Operational certifications indicate that a certification subject has been reviewed against applicable Suite Standards and Suite Methodology and is functioning within its defined operational scope.

This class is appropriate when evidence, scope, assessment, and decision support a repeatable operational certification result.

Operational

Verified

Verified certifications represent the highest initial class and indicate that evidence, standards, methodology, review, and supporting references justify a high-confidence certification determination.

Verified status may require stronger evidence preservation, integrity references, attestation support, and downstream Suite verification readiness.

Highest Class

Class Assignment Flow

A Certification Class is assigned during the certification workflow after scope, evidence, applicable standards, methodology, evaluation, assessment, and decision have been documented. The class becomes part of the canonical Certification Package and is reflected in generated artifacts.

Current Class Use

The inaugural operational certification, SC-CERT-2026-0001 for the Atlas Jurisdiction Record — El Salvador, demonstrates the Operational class as Certifier’s first active certification posture.

Active Use

Artifact Reflection

The assigned class should appear consistently in the Certification Package, Certification Process Report, Certification Receipt, SCRD HTML, SCRD JSON, and Suite-ready reference records.

Required Consistency

Not a Value Judgment

Certification Classes communicate the level and purpose of review. They do not measure commercial value, importance, popularity, ownership, or general superiority of a certification subject.

Boundary

Future Evolution

Classes may expand as the Suite matures, but historical certification records should remain understandable, backward-compatible, and traceable to the class definitions in effect when issued.

Versioned

Certification Class Role

Certification Classes are the classification layer of Certifier. Standards define expectations, Methodology defines the process, evidence supports review, and Certifier assigns a class as part of the documented certification decision.

A class should always be read together with the Certification Package, the applicable Suite Standards, the applicable Suite Methodology, the evidence inventory, and the generated certification artifacts.
Certification Classes communicate the depth, scope, and confidence posture of review.
