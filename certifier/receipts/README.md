# Certification Receipts

This directory contains Certification Receipts generated by the Satoshium Certifier framework.

A Certification Receipt is a concise, public-facing record that documents the outcome of a certification event.

Receipts provide a durable and easily understandable summary of certification activities while referencing the more detailed records that support them.

Certification Receipts serve as the public proof layer of Certifier.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the receipts directory is to preserve certification summaries that can be easily referenced, shared, published, and reviewed.

Receipts answer fundamental questions such as:

* What was certified?
* What certification was granted?
* What was the determination?
* When was certification issued?
* What standard was applied?
* Where is the supporting report?

Receipts provide a simplified view of certification outcomes without requiring review of the full certification report.

---

# Receipt Philosophy

Certifier distinguishes between:

```text
Evidence
     ↓
Report
     ↓
Receipt
```

Evidence supports findings.

Reports explain findings.

Receipts summarize findings.

The receipt is intended to provide clear and durable proof that a certification event occurred.

---

# What Belongs Here

Examples of Certification Receipts include:

* Atlas certification receipts
* Workflow certification receipts
* Service certification receipts
* Dataset certification receipts
* Tool certification receipts
* Future subsystem certification receipts

Every receipt should reference a corresponding Certification Report.

* Certification Target

# Relationship to Reports

Certification Reports and Certification Receipts serve different purposes.

| Artifact | Purpose                      |
| -------- | ---------------------------- |
| Report   | Detailed review record       |
| Receipt  | Public certification summary |

Relationship:

```text
Review
     ↓
Certification Report
     ↓
Certification Receipt
```

The report contains detailed findings.

The receipt communicates the outcome.

---

# Relationship to Evidence

Receipts summarize certification activity but do not replace evidence.

Evidence remains the foundation of certification.

Typical relationship:

```text
Evidence
     ↓
Report
     ↓
Receipt
```

Future reviewers should be able to trace a receipt back to supporting reports and evidence.

---

# Relationship to Registry

Certification Receipts are expected to become one of the primary record types cataloged by Registry.

Example workflow:

```text
Certification Receipt
        ↓
Registry Entry
        ↓
Public Discovery
```

The receipt provides the summary layer that Registry users are most likely to encounter first.

# Relationship to Chronicle

Significant Certification Receipts may represent historical milestones.

Examples:

* First Atlas certification
* First Verified certification
* Certifier launch certification
* Major subsystem certifications

Such events may later be recorded within Chronicle.

```text

     ↓
Hash
     ↓
Anchor Preservation
```

This would allow receipt integrity to be independently verified over time.

```text
receipt-YYYY-NNNNNN.md
```

Example:

```text
receipt-2026-000001.md
```

---

# Receipt Identifier Standard

Recommended identifier format:

```text
SCR-YYYY-NNNNNN
```

Where:

```text
SCR
=
Satoshium Certification Receipt

YYYY
=
Year

NNNNNN
=
Sequential Identifier
```

Example:

```text
SCR-2026-000001
```

# Example Lifecycle

A typical receipt lifecycle may follow:

```text
Generated
     ↓
Published
     ↓
Referenced
     ↓
Archived
```

Historical receipts should generally be preserved rather than deleted.

---

# Preservation Philosophy

Receipts represent historical certification events.

Whenever practical:

* Preserve receipts.
* Archive receipts when necessary.
* Maintain identifier continuity.
* Retain references to supporting reports.

Historical receipts help establish long-term trust and transparency.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The receipts directory serves as the public record layer of Certifier.

A future user should be able to review a receipt years after issuance and quickly understand:

* What was certified
* What determination was reached
* What certification class was granted
* Where supporting documentation can be found

Receipts are intentionally concise, but they provide the entry point into the broader certification record system.

As Certifier evolves, receipts may become one of the most widely referenced artifacts across Registry, Chronicle, Anchor, Attestor, and other future Satoshium subsystems.
