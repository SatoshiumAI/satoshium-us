Atlas Certification Framework

The Atlas Certification Framework defines how Atlas records participate within the Satoshium Suite certification ecosystem. Rather than establishing independent certification standards, this Framework applies the existing Suite Standards and Suite Methodology to Atlas intelligence records through a consistent, transparent, and repeatable certification architecture.

PURPOSE

Purpose

The Atlas Certification Framework serves as the constitutional bridge between Atlas and the Satoshium Suite certification ecosystem. It defines how Atlas records become eligible for evaluation while preserving the authority of the Suite Standards and Suite Methodology.

AUTHORITY

Authority

The Framework derives its authority entirely from the Satoshium Suite. It does not establish independent standards or methodologies. Instead, it applies existing Suite governance specifically to Atlas certification subjects.

APPLICABILITY

Applicability

This Framework applies to Atlas records submitted for certification, including jurisdiction intelligence records, supporting metadata, evidence, trust dimensions, signals, change histories, and other canonical Atlas artifacts.

IMPLEMENTATION

Framework Role

Suite Standards define what certification requires. Suite Methodology defines how certification is performed. The Atlas Certification Framework applies both consistently to Atlas records, creating a repeatable certification implementation without introducing new governing rules.

PHILOSOPHY

Framework Philosophy

Standards establish expectations. Methodology establishes execution. The Atlas Certification Framework applies both consistently to Atlas records, allowing them to participate as authoritative certification subjects throughout the Satoshium Suite.

The Framework connects Atlas to the certification ecosystem without replacing the constitutional authority of the Suite Standards or Suite Methodology.
