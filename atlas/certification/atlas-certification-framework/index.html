🧭 Atlas Certification Framework

The Atlas Certification Framework defines how Atlas records participate within the Satoshium Suite certification ecosystem.

Rather than establishing independent certification standards, this Framework applies the existing Suite Standards and Suite Methodology to Atlas intelligence records through a consistent, transparent, and repeatable certification architecture.

Purpose

The Atlas Certification Framework serves as the constitutional bridge between Atlas and the Satoshium Suite certification ecosystem.

It defines how Atlas records become eligible for evaluation while preserving the authority of the Suite Standards and Suite Methodology.

The Framework connects Atlas to certification without creating a separate standards authority.

Authority

The Framework derives its authority entirely from the Satoshium Suite. It does not establish independent standards or methodologies.

Instead, it applies existing Suite governance specifically to Atlas certification subjects.

Suite Standards define expectations. Suite Methodology defines execution. This Framework applies both to Atlas.

Applicability

This Framework applies to Atlas records submitted for certification, including canonical intelligence records and their supporting artifacts.

  • Jurisdiction Records
  • Jurisdiction Intelligence Engine outputs
  • Metadata
  • Evidence
  • Signals
  • Trust Dimensions
  • Change histories

Framework Role

The Framework does not redefine Atlas records. Atlas remains the authority for its own intelligence objects, files, metadata, signals, and jurisdiction artifacts.

The Framework defines how those Atlas records become certification subjects within the Suite.

Framework Versioning

Boundary Rules

The Framework preserves institutional boundaries across the Suite.

  • Atlas owns the intelligence records.
  • Certifier owns the certification process.
  • Registry catalogs certified records.
  • Chronicle preserves certification events.
  • Anchor preserves integrity references.
  • Beacon publishes discovery signals.
  • Attestor produces Trust Statements.
  • Navigator orchestrates workflows.
The Framework connects responsibilities. It does not erase boundaries.

Framework Philosophy

Standards establish expectations. Methodology establishes execution.

The Atlas Certification Framework applies both consistently to Atlas records, allowing them to participate as authoritative certification subjects throughout the Satoshium Suite.

The Framework connects Atlas to the certification ecosystem without replacing the constitutional authority of the Suite Standards or Suite Methodology.
Intelligence becomes durable when it can be evaluated, referenced, and preserved.
