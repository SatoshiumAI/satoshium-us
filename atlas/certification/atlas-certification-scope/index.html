ATLAS · CERTIFICATION SCOPE

🧭 Atlas Certification Scope

The Atlas Certification Scope defines the boundaries of Atlas certification by identifying which Atlas artifacts are eligible for certification, what is evaluated, and what remains outside the certification process.

Return to Atlas → Open Framework → Open Profile →

Purpose

Defines the certification boundaries for Atlas records so every evaluation is consistent, transparent, and repeatable.

Authority

The Scope derives its authority from the Suite Standards, Suite Methodology, and Atlas Certification Framework.

Included

  • Jurisdiction Intelligence Engine (JIE) records
  • Country and State intelligence records
  • Evidence
  • Metadata
  • Trust Dimensions
  • Signals
  • Change Logs

Excluded

  • Registry Entries (SREG)
  • Chronicle Events
  • Anchor Integrity References
  • Beacon Discovery Metadata
  • Attestor Trust Statements
  • Navigator Workflows

Scope Philosophy

Atlas certification evaluates Atlas. It does not certify the operational records created by the rest of the Satoshium Suite. Each institutional component remains responsible for its own authoritative objects.

A clear certification boundary preserves institutional responsibility while enabling interoperability across the Suite.
