---

## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Alaska as an Arctic strategic access continuity jurisdiction positioned within the two-state Non-Contiguous Strategic Layer cluster shared exclusively with Hawaii. The evidence layer is retained from non-contiguous Foundation-and-Completion continuity with Hawaii and Pacific-edge adjacency with Washington and Oregon, rather than from land-border adjacency or independent state-sourced research. The signals layer reflects four structural coordination signals: Arctic strategic access continuity, Pacific-to-Arctic transition continuity, Pacific strategic adjacency continuity, and institutional adjacency continuity across the Pacific and Arctic frame, with no independent establishment of air-cargo, seaport, military-base, pipeline, or Arctic shipping-route inventories beyond the retained corridor anchors.
