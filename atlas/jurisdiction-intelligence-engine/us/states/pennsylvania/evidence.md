# Pennsylvania — Evidence Layer

## Scope

This file records only corridor-relevant structural anchors documented for Pennsylvania that are sufficient to support Atlas signal derivation.

It does not perform an open-ended statewide infrastructure survey and does not assign trust posture, routing role, coordination tier, or Atlas surfaces.

## Great Lakes port continuity

- The Erie-Western Pennsylvania Port Authority states Erie is Pennsylvania's only commercial lake port and the region's import and export link to the world through the St. Lawrence Seaway.
- The Port of Erie commercial materials state the port has efficient links to rail and Interstates 79 and 90.
- The same materials document drydocking, ship repair, dockage, warehousing, rail connection, and bulk-material handling at corridor-relevant Erie terminals.

Sources:
- Erie-Western Pennsylvania Port Authority, "Commercial" — https://www.porterie.org/commercial/

## Inland river logistics system

- The Port of Pittsburgh Commission states it promotes the commercial use and development of inland waterways for the residents and industries of southwestern Pennsylvania.
- Port of Pittsburgh materials identify the Allegheny, Monongahela, and Ohio rivers as the core inland-waterway structure, noting eight locks and dams on the Allegheny, Monongahela gateway continuity from West Virginia into Pennsylvania, and the Ohio River confluence in downtown Pittsburgh.
- The Pittsburgh Intermodal Terminals page documents an Ohio River site in Ambridge Industrial Park handling dry bulk, heavy lifts, lumber, steel, and palletized goods with substantial open and covered storage.

Sources:
- Port of Pittsburgh Commission — https://www.portofpittsburgh.com/
- Port of Pittsburgh Commission, "Pittsburgh Intermodal Terminals" — https://www.portofpittsburgh.com/terminals/pittsburgh-intermodal-terminals

## Class I rail and intermodal visibility

- The Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission states it regulates approximately 65 railroad companies operating in the state and that Pennsylvania has nearly 5,600 miles of track.
- The Pennsylvania PUC railroad-company list includes both CSX Transportation Inc. and Norfolk Southern Railway Company as operating railroad companies in Pennsylvania.
- The Pittsburgh Intermodal Terminals page documents Class I Norfolk Southern rail access at an Ohio River terminal.

Sources:
- Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission, "Rail" — https://www.puc.pa.gov/rail/
- Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission, "Railroad Companies" — https://www.puc.pa.gov/rail/railroad-companies/
- Port of Pittsburgh Commission, "Pittsburgh Intermodal Terminals" — https://www.portofpittsburgh.com/terminals/pittsburgh-intermodal-terminals

## Interstate corridor convergence

- PennDOT's 2045 Freight Movement Plan materials describe the Port of Pittsburgh district as served by CSX and Norfolk Southern and by interstate corridors including I-79, I-76 / PA Turnpike, and I-80.
- PennDOT's public statewide rail-map, National Highway System, and highway-statistics materials provide direct corridor visibility for I-76, I-79, I-80, I-81, and I-95 in Pennsylvania's freight environment.
- The Port of Erie commercial materials add direct corridor linkage to I-79 and I-90 at the Great Lakes edge of the state.

Sources:

## Pittsburgh air cargo logistics visibility

- PIT Cargo states Pittsburgh International Airport provides a faster, smarter, more efficient cargo gateway with domestic and international access.
- PIT Cargo states the airport has a strategic location between New York and Chicago for east-west distribution.
- PIT Cargo states the airport is within 500 miles of 50% of the U.S. population and 54% of U.S. buying power, with no congestion and easy ground access.

Sources:
- Pittsburgh International Airport, "PIT Cargo" — https://flypittsburgh.com/acaa-corporate/partner-with-us/pit-cargo/

## Research-institution engineering and research visibility

- Carnegie Mellon engineering materials document cross-disciplinary robotics research spanning soft and wearable robotics, legged and aerial robots, medical robotics, micro and nano robotics, underwater robots, and IoT robots.
- Carnegie Mellon materials also document the opening of the Robotics Innovation Center for research and partners.
- University of Pittsburgh research materials state Pitt spends more than $1 billion in research expenditures each year and generated $2.4 billion in Pennsylvania economic impact in fiscal year 2023.

Sources:
- Carnegie Mellon University College of Engineering, "Robotics" — https://engineering.cmu.edu/research/thrusts/robotics.html
- University of Pittsburgh, "Research" — https://www.pitt.edu/research

## PJM transmission participation

- PJM states it coordinates the movement of electricity through all or parts of Pennsylvania and other Mid-Atlantic and Midwest jurisdictions.
- PJM states its public transmission maps include infrastructure from 69 kV up to 765 kV.

Sources:
- PJM, "Territory Served" — https://www.pjm.com/about-pjm/who-we-are/territory-served.aspx

## Pennsylvania BEAD broadband coordination

- The Pennsylvania Broadband Development Authority states Pennsylvania will receive more than $1.16 billion in federal BEAD funding.
- PBDA states Pennsylvania completed a BEAD 5-Year Action Plan approved in October 2023.
- PBDA states approved BEAD proposal materials cover community anchor institutions, local coordination efforts, implementation activities, and related broadband deployment planning.

Sources:
- Pennsylvania Broadband Development Authority, "Broadband Equity, Access, and Deployment (BEAD) Program" — https://broadband.pa.gov/funding/broadband-equity-access-and-deployment-bead-program/

## Corridor-linked industrial persistence

- Port of Erie commercial materials document shipbuilding, dry-docking, cargo management, material handling, and rail-connected bulk-terminal operations on Lake Erie.
- Port of Pittsburgh materials describe the Monongahela as a river once home to many of Pittsburgh's steel mills, and the Pittsburgh Intermodal Terminals page documents steel among the commodities handled on the Ohio River.
- Pennsylvania manufacturing materials state manufacturing employs 1 in 10 residents, contributes more than $111 billion annually to the state economy, and includes metallurgy and metal manufacturing that strengthen robotics, technology, and energy.

Sources:
- Erie-Western Pennsylvania Port Authority, "Commercial" — https://www.porterie.org/commercial/
- Port of Pittsburgh Commission — https://www.portofpittsburgh.com/
- Port of Pittsburgh Commission, "Pittsburgh Intermodal Terminals" — https://www.portofpittsburgh.com/terminals/pittsburgh-intermodal-terminals
- Pennsylvania Department of Community and Economic Development, "Manufacturing" — https://dced.pa.gov/pennsylvanias-top-industries/advanced-manufacturing/
