## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Pennsylvania as a Northeast Institutional Mesh jurisdiction under the jurisdiction lens "Great Lakes industrial + inland river logistics + Mid-Atlantic transition corridor," with canonical scope limited to corridor-layer anchors across nine evidence subsections. The signals layer reflects nine structural coordination signals anchored in directly documented primary sources: a Great Lakes port-continuity signal through Erie's documented St. Lawrence Seaway linkage, rail connectivity, and I-79 and I-90 access; an inland-river logistics-system signal through the Allegheny, Monongahela, and Ohio river structure and Ohio River intermodal terminal activity; a Class I rail and intermodal visibility signal through documented CSX and Norfolk Southern operating presence and substantial statewide rail mileage; an interstate freight corridor convergence signal through documented I-76, I-79, I-80, I-81, and I-95 visibility; a Pittsburgh air cargo logistics signal through PIT Cargo's documented cargo specialization and midpoint positioning between New York and Chicago; a research-institution engineering visibility signal through Carnegie Mellon robotics research and University of Pittsburgh documented research expenditure scale; a PJM transmission participation signal through documented PJM coordination of electricity movement through Pennsylvania; a Pennsylvania BEAD broadband coordination signal through documented BEAD funding exceeding $1.16 billion; and a corridor-linked industrial persistence signal through Erie shipbuilding, Pittsburgh steel and inland waterway continuity, and documented manufacturing employment and output.
