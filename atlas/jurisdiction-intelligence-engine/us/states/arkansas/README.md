## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Arkansas as a jurisdiction where evidence absence is preserved as the normalized outcome. The evidence layer records that no Arkansas-specific normalized documentary evidence entries have been established in the current package beyond placeholder layer files, and that absence of evidence is a valid structural outcome under Atlas normalization rules. Topology metadata is attached — placing Arkansas in the Mississippi Valley Transit & Distribution Corridor under a Gulf Corridor Layer foundation resolving into the Central Interior Completion Layer — but that placement does not substitute for evidence-layer claims. No signals, trust dimensions, profile characterization, or builder-mode scenario scaffolding are established beyond the structural record of evidence absence.
