## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Minnesota as an Upper Midwest Continuity jurisdiction representing the Atlas's first three-way cross-cluster placement — positioned in the Upper Midwest Continuity Corridor under the Great Lakes Industrial Core Foundation Layer and attached to the Northern Interior Completion Layer — under the jurisdiction lens "Upper Mississippi + western Great Lakes logistics continuity corridor." The signals layer reflects nine structural coordination signals anchored in directly documented primary sources: a Lake Superior continuity signal through Duluth/Superior port activity and Great Lakes-Seaway access; an Upper Mississippi freight continuity signal through documented river tonnage and southbound waterway access toward the Gulf; an inland bulk-cargo structure signal through commodity handling on both the Mississippi and Lake Superior systems; a Class I rail visibility signal through documented BNSF, Canadian National, CPKC, and Union Pacific presence; an interstate freight corridor continuity signal through National Highway Freight Network visibility at I-35, I-90, and I-94; an intermodal freight structure signal through MnDOT statewide intermodal mapping and optimization tooling; a University of Minnesota engineering and applied research visibility signal; a MISO transmission participation signal through documented Minnesota Tranche One transmission projects; and a broadband coordination signal through Office of Broadband Development BEAD and Digital Equity program coordination.
