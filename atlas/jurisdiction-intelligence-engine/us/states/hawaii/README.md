## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Hawaii as a Pacific strategic projection environment positioned within the two-state Non-Contiguous Strategic Layer cluster shared exclusively with Alaska. The evidence layer is retained from non-contiguous Foundation-and-Completion continuity with Alaska's already-retained evidence and Pacific-edge adjacency with Washington and Oregon, rather than from land-border adjacency or independent state-sourced research. The signals layer reflects four structural coordination signals: Pacific strategic projection continuity, Pacific strategic adjacency continuity, Pacific-to-Arctic adjacency continuity, and institutional adjacency continuity across the Pacific strategic frame, with no independent establishment of west-coast-to-Pacific projection continuity as a standalone anchor, Pacific perimeter continuity, island-chain projection continuity, energy-grid participation visibility, BEAD coordination visibility, or research participation beyond institutional adjacency under the current corridor-bounded scope.
