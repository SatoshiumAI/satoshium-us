## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records North Dakota as a Northern Plains Sparse-Node jurisdiction and canonical member of the three-state Northern Plains Sparse-Node Corridor cluster with Montana and South Dakota, under the jurisdiction lens "Northern plains sparse-node continuity within northern interior completion." The evidence layer is retained from corridor continuity between adjacent Atlas states — Montana, South Dakota, and Minnesota — plus a narrow Wyoming-facing western frame visible transitively through Montana, with all citations at metadata depth, rather than from independent state-sourced research. The signals layer reflects four structural coordination signals: a northern plains sparse-node continuity signal through retained placement on the Northern Plains Sparse-Node Layer and Northern Interior Completion Layer across the three-state frame; an east-west northern interior continuity signal between Montana-facing sparse-node structure and Minnesota-facing northern-interior continuity; an institutional adjacency signal across the Montana, South Dakota, and Minnesota-facing frame; and an interior adjacency toward the Wyoming-facing western frame through the visible Montana–Wyoming structure, with no independent establishment of north-south plains continuity toward a Canada-facing frame, freight or logistics continuity, rail visibility, energy-grid participation, BEAD coordination, or inland-waterway continuity.
