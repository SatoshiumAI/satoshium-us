## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Ohio as a Great Lakes Industrial Core jurisdiction under the jurisdiction lens "Great Lakes industrial + inland freight convergence corridor," with canonical scope limited to corridor-layer anchors across eleven evidence subsections. The signals layer reflects ten structural coordination signals anchored in directly documented primary sources: a Great Lakes manufacturing continuity signal through documented Cleveland and Toledo port structure and Great Lakes and Seaway linkage; an Ohio River logistics continuity signal through the Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky inland-port jurisdiction and documented active terminal density; a Midwest inland freight convergence signal through statewide transportation-system scale and multimodal freight anchors; an interstate freight convergence signal through documented visibility of I-70, I-71, I-75, I-77, I-80, and I-90; a rail intermodal corridor visibility signal through state-approved intermodal facilities, Rickenbacker coast-to-coast access, Cleveland Class I rail service, and Toledo freight-rail concentration; an air cargo logistics visibility signal through Rickenbacker cargo specialization and international carrier access; a steel-belt manufacturing continuity signal through documented steel-primary cargo at Cleveland and Toledo direct-reduction steel production; a research-institution engineering visibility signal through Ohio State engineering research in mobility and manufacturing; a regional transmission participation signal through documented PJM membership for Ohio's six PUCO-regulated electric utilities; and a broadband expansion initiative signal through documented BEAD funding and BroadbandOhio coordination activity.
