## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records California as a Pacific Coastal Hyperscale jurisdiction with documented evidence across privacy statutory and enforcement structure, AI research and platform ecosystem concentration, responsible AI governance and municipal experimentation, and digital asset ecosystem visibility. The signals layer reflects a privacy-governance density signal through the CCPA, CPRA, and California Privacy Protection Agency; a concentration signal for frontier-model developers, AI infrastructure providers, cloud platform operators, and venture-capital density tied to AI-native infrastructure; an active responsible AI policy momentum signal through algorithmic transparency and automated decision accountability frameworks; a digital asset ecosystem density signal through infrastructure startups, Bitcoin tooling companies, and custody-adjacent security firms; and a DAO legal-structure absence signal reflecting the current non-establishment of statutory recognition for decentralized autonomous organizations. The evidence layer records these as documented structural anchors rather than routing-authority or readiness determinations.
