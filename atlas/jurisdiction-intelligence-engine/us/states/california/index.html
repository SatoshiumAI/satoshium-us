Privacy Governance Structure

The strongest documented governance pattern in the California package is the combined presence of the CCPA, the CPRA, and the CPPA. The evidence supports privacy rights, data-minimization expectations, and independent enforcement visibility as part of the ordinary structure of the jurisdiction.

Pattern
Documented privacy-governance structure
Named structures
CCPA · CPRA · CPPA
Stability characteristic
Named statutes and enforcement body
CCPA: California Consumer Privacy Act, Cal. Civ. Code §1798.100 et seq., established consumer rights regarding access to collected personal data, disclosure of data collection practices, opt-out rights for data sale, and data deletion requests.
CPRA: California Privacy Rights Act, Proposition 24 (2020), expanded the CCPA framework by introducing sensitive personal data classification, data minimization requirements, purpose limitation requirements, and enhanced enforcement authority.
CPPA: The California Privacy Protection Agency was created under CPRA as an independent enforcement authority responsible for privacy rulemaking, compliance oversight, consumer protection enforcement, and data governance supervision.
Stability-characteristic evaluation only. This section does not assign routing role, coordination tier, Atlas surface, or national significance, and does not forecast future enforcement actions.
