## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records New Mexico as a Southwest Transition Infrastructure jurisdiction with documented evidence organized around a federal research laboratory environment anchored by NNSA, Los Alamos National Laboratory, Sandia National Laboratories, and the DOE Center for Integrated Nanotechnologies; a nuclear engineering and secure compute environment through classified and mixed-use high-performance computing at Los Alamos; a defense-adjacent engineering and aerospace corridor through AFRL Directed Energy Directorate activity at Kirtland, White Sands Missile Range, and Spaceport America; an energy research and grid experimentation environment through Sandia storage and DOE Grid Modernization work; a university research and federal-lab interface environment centered on UNM and selected AFRL-linked partnerships; and a sparse statewide distribution with corridor-dependent institutional topology concentrated in Albuquerque, Los Alamos, and the southern White Sands and Spaceport corridor. The signals layer reflects those same thematic clusters, with no direct signal established for cross-state infrastructure adjacency effects, major IX concentration, private hyperscale siting, or uniform statewide digital-corridor coverage.
