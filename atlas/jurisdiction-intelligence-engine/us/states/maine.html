Maine had not enacted dedicated state-level AI legislation as of April 2026. Privacy / Data Handling

Maine enacted the Maine Consumer Data Privacy Act (MCDPA), which took effect July 1, 2024. The MCDPA establishes consumer rights including access, correction, deletion, and opt-out of targeted advertising and automated decision-making profiling. Enforcement is administered through the Maine Attorney General. Maine also maintains an ISP-specific privacy statute (LD 946 / Title 35-A §9301) prohibiting internet service providers from using, selling, or distributing subscriber personal information without consent — a surface that conditions broadband-adjacent infrastructure deployment within the corridor.

Biometrics / Identity

Maine treats biometric data as a regulated category under the MCDPA's sensitive data provisions. Biometric information — including fingerprints, retinal scans, facial geometry, and voice print data — requires opt-in consent prior to processing. Maine does not maintain a standalone biometric-specific statute comparable to Illinois' BIPA, but the MCDPA sensitive data framework conditions a structurally cautious posture for biometric-proximate deployments within this corridor.

Energy / Mining / Compute Posture

Bitcoin mining operates within Maine's legal framework with no specific prohibition as of 2026. Electricity costs within Maine are moderate relative to the continental US upper band, and the regulatory posture does not reflect the environmental skepticism toward proof-of-work that conditions California or northeastern urban corridor environments.
