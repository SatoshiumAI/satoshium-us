## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Wisconsin as a Great Lakes Industrial Core jurisdiction under the jurisdiction lens "Great Lakes industrial + Upper Midwest manufacturing continuity corridor," with canonical scope limited to corridor-layer anchors across nine evidence subsections. The signals layer reflects nine structural coordination signals anchored in directly documented primary sources: a Port Milwaukee Great Lakes continuity signal through documented Great Lakes and Mississippi River inland-waterway access, Union Pacific and CPKC rail service, and I-94 and I-794 direct port access; a Port of Green Bay Great Lakes logistics visibility signal through documented two-million-ton annual cargo movement and Canadian National and Fox Valley rail access; a Mississippi River freight boundary continuity signal through Wisconsin's documented position between federally designated waterborne corridors M-35 and M-90; a Class I rail visibility signal through documented BNSF, Canadian National, CPKC, and Union Pacific operating presence; an interstate corridor continuity signal through documented freight-program visibility for I-90, I-94, I-43, and I-41; a University of Wisconsin-Madison engineering research visibility signal; a MISO transmission participation signal through documented Grid Forward Central Wisconsin Tranche 1 project and MISO long-range transmission planning activity; a Wisconsin BEAD broadband coordination signal through documented BEAD administration by the Public Service Commission; and a corridor-linked industrial persistence signal through documented national-ranking concentration in fabricated metal products, paper manufacturing, and machinery manufacturing.
