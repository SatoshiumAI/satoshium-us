## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Louisiana as a Gulf Port and Energy Export jurisdiction with documented evidence across maritime and inland freight continuity, petrochemical and refinery and offshore and export infrastructure, coastal resilience and levee and flood-control coordination, oil and gas and utility and transmission governance, research and institutional ecosystem, shipbuilding and fabrication continuity, broadband and digital connectivity, and digital-asset statutory signals. The signals layer reflects a river and Gulf freight continuity signal through the Port of South Louisiana, Port of New Orleans, and Port of Greater Baton Rouge with documented Mississippi River waterway, Class I rail, and Gulf Intracoastal Waterway connectivity; a petrochemical and export infrastructure signal through documented LOOP deepwater crude terminal, Port Fourchon offshore support, and Baton Rouge petrochemical corridor visibility; a coastal resilience and flood-control coordination signal through CPRA, USACE New Orleans District, and documented levee and floodway structures; an oil and gas and utility governance signal through Louisiana DNR and LPSC oversight and MISO-linked utility participation; a research and institutional ecosystem signal through LSU Center for River Studies, Tulane ByWater Institute, and University of Louisiana System; a shipbuilding and fabrication signal through Bollinger Shipyards Gulf-access facilities; a broadband expansion signal through ConnectLA; and a digital-asset statutory signal through the Virtual Currency Businesses Act and the Blockchain Basics Act.
