## Coastal and flood-risk stability characteristics

## Utility and transmission stability characteristics

The evidence shows continuity through named utility-governance structures, especially the Louisiana Public Service Commission's jurisdiction over electric, natural gas, certain telecommunications services, and certain intrastate pipelines. This supports continuity visibility, but not a full statewide transmission-topology conclusion.
