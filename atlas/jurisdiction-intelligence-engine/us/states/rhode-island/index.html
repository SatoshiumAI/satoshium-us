Bitcoin / Digital Asset Policy

Rhode Island administers digital asset activity through the Division of Banking's money transmission licensing framework. Virtual currency businesses operating within the corridor are required to hold a Money Transmitter License (MTL) under the Rhode Island Money Transmission Act. The posture is regulatory rather than prohibitive, and the corridor does not operate a dedicated digital asset licensing regime equivalent to the NYDFS BitLicense or California's DFAL.

Privacy / Data Handling

Rhode Island's Data Transparency and Privacy Protection Act (RIDTPPA) became effective January 1, 2026, establishing the state's primary consumer data privacy enforcement surface. The regime is enforced exclusively by the Attorney General with no private right of action, and applies to entities controlling or processing personal data of 35,000 or more Rhode Island residents, or 10,000 or more residents where data sales constitute more than twenty percent of gross revenue.

Biometrics / Identity

Rhode Island does not operate a standalone biometric privacy statute as of 2026. Biometric identifiers are classified as sensitive data under RIDTPPA, subjecting their processing to data protection assessment requirements and opt-in consent obligations for certain uses. The corridor does not carry the elevated biometric restriction posture present in the Massachusetts or Connecticut governance surfaces, but RIDTPPA's sensitive data framework conditions a baseline level of deployment scrutiny.
