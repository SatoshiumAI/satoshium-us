Privacy / Data Handling

Vermont operates a developing privacy regulatory surface with two active compliance anchors: a consumer data privacy framework (H.121, enacted 2024) and a data broker registration requirement (enacted 2018). Enforcement is administered through the Vermont Attorney General's office rather than a dedicated privacy agency. The regulatory posture is moderate and developing, without California-scale enforcement infrastructure or penalty exposure, but the obligation landscape is genuine and expanding.

Biometrics / Identity

Vermont does not maintain a dedicated statewide biometrics law as of April 2026. Biometric data collected from Vermont residents interfaces with general consumer data protection frameworks under the Vermont CDPA's sensitive data classification provisions, but no dedicated biometric consent statute creates a distinct compliance layer within this corridor. The regulatory surface is limited rather than absent, and the national legislative discussion around biometrics is likely to condition Vermont's small-state legislative cycle going forward.
