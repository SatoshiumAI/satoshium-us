# Illinois — Evidence Layer

## Scope

This file records only evidence-supported characteristics documented for Illinois that are relevant to Atlas normalization. It does not assign trust posture, routing role, coordination tier, or Atlas surfaces.

## Freight rail and multimodal logistics infrastructure

- CMAP describes northeastern Illinois as a major freight hub and states that about a quarter of all U.S. rail freight originates, terminates, or passes through the region.
- CMAP states the northeastern Illinois freight network includes highway, rail, air, water, and pipeline assets, and references CREATE rail coordination work, truck-routing studies, and O'Hare-area freight planning.
- IDOT states Illinois is at the center of the nation’s rail network, with approximately 9,982 miles of railroad track and 41 operating railroads.

Sources:
- CMAP, "Freight and Rail" — https://cmap.illinois.gov/focus-areas/transportation/freight-and-rail/
- IDOT, "Public Transportation" — https://idot.illinois.gov/travel-and-maps/public-transportation.html

## Waterways, Port of Chicago, and river-basin / Great Lakes continuity

- IDOT states Illinois has 1,095 miles of navigable waterways that border or pass through the state.
- IDOT states those waterways connect Illinois to the Atlantic Ocean through the St. Lawrence Seaway and the Great Lakes, and to the Gulf of Mexico via the Mississippi River.
- IDOT states the Port of Chicago handles ocean vessels, lake vessels, and barges, and that the Lake Michigan port is served by 12 railroads with direct access to Interstates 90 and 94.
- IDOT's Illinois Port Facilities Capital Investment Grant program states that the Illinois Marine Transportation System includes ports on the Mississippi River, Illinois River, Chicago Area Waterway System, Kaskaskia River, Ohio River, and Lake Michigan.

Sources:
- IDOT, "Waterways" — https://idot.illinois.gov/travel-and-maps/waterways.html
- IDOT, "Illinois Port Facilities Capital Investment Grant Program" — https://idot.illinois.gov/programs-and-projects/grants/illinois-port-facilities-capital-investment-grant-program.html

## Air cargo infrastructure

- FlyChicago states Chicago O'Hare International Airport processes over two million metric tonnes of cargo worth more than $200 billion per year.
- FlyChicago states O'Hare ranks in the top 20 globally for cargo and is number one in the Americas by freight value.
- FlyChicago states O'Hare's northeast cargo campus can handle 50 percent more cargo traffic and freighter aircraft than before, and that the airport has approximately two million square feet of airside cargo facilities plus another two million square feet landside.

Sources:
- FlyChicago, "Cargo" — https://www.flychicago.com/business/cargo/pages/default.aspx

## Highway corridor visibility

- An official IDOT highway-system listing states Illinois is at the heart of the country’s interstate highway system and specifically highlights I-80, I-90, and I-70.
- CMAP states the northeastern Illinois freight network includes 10 interstate highways.
- IDOT directly documents I-90 and I-94 access at the Lake Michigan port serving the Port of Chicago complex.

## Higher-education, federal-lab, and research infrastructure

- The University of Chicago states that one of the resources enabling its scientific innovation is affiliation with two U.S. Department of Energy laboratories: Argonne National Laboratory and Fermilab.
- The University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign describes itself as one of the nation’s leading research institutions.
- Northwestern University's official site describes Northwestern as one of the country’s leading private research and teaching universities in Evanston, Illinois.
- Argonne's official site description identifies Argonne as a Department of Energy science and engineering research laboratory.
- Fermilab official materials identify its 6,800-acre site in Batavia, Illinois and state that it is managed for the U.S. Department of Energy Office of Science.

## Quantum ecosystem visibility

- The Illinois Quantum Information Science and Technology Center (IQUIST) states that it brings together physicists, engineers, computer scientists, and mathematicians to advance quantum information science and train a quantum-ready workforce through partnerships spanning academia, government, and industry.
- UChicago's quantum program states that it has strong research partnerships with academia, industry, and national labs, and that two of the ten national quantum research centers are located at the University of Chicago.

## Energy transmission and nuclear-generation visibility

- An Illinois executive-order page states that PJM and the Midcontinent Independent System Operator (MISO) are the regional transmission organizations in Illinois.
- Illinois Commerce Commission materials state that the Illinois bulk-power system is managed by two different system operators, PJM and MISO.
- Ready Illinois states that the Illinois Emergency Management Agency's Division of Nuclear Safety monitors 11 nuclear power reactors at six nuclear power stations licensed to generate electricity in Illinois.
- The Illinois Clean Energy Dashboard states that, as of 2024, Illinois generates 53.62% of its electricity from nuclear power and describes Illinois as the nation’s top nuclear power generator.

## Broadband and digital infrastructure visibility

- DCEO states that since 2019 the Illinois Office of Broadband has served as a single source for broadband matters in Illinois.
- DCEO states the Illinois Office of Broadband partners with the Illinois Broadband Lab, which is overseen by the University of Illinois System.
- DCEO states Connect Illinois began in 2019 as the state's inaugural broadband investment program, with $400 million originally appropriated for competitive broadband grants.
- DCEO states Connect Illinois also included $20 million for upgrades to the 100 GB Illinois Century Network, a 2,000-mile open-access institutional fiber network serving more than 3,400 K-12, higher-education, and library locations.
- DCEO states ARPA and IIJA included over $1.3 billion in federal support for the Connect Illinois Broadband Grant Program and related digital-equity initiatives.
- DCEO states the Governor-appointed Illinois Broadband Advisory Council convened in August 2019 as a 25-member council with ISP, state-agency, legislative, and stakeholder representation.
- DCEO maintains a data-center investment program that provides tax exemptions and credits for qualifying Illinois data centers.
- An official DCEO annual-report listing states Illinois has been positioned as one of the top five data-center markets in the United States and that the Chicago region is among the most active and fastest-growing; the underlying report PDF was not machine-readable during this collection pass, so this evidence is kept narrow.

## Digital-asset and blockchain statutory signals

- The Illinois General Assembly maintains 205 ILCS 725, the Blockchain Business Development Act.
- The Illinois General Assembly maintains 205 ILCS 730, the Blockchain Technology Act.
- A cited section of 205 ILCS 730 states that local governments may not impose taxes or fees on the use of a blockchain or smart contract, require a local certificate, license, or permit to use a blockchain or smart contract, or impose other local requirements relating to such use.
- The Illinois General Assembly maintains 205 ILCS 731, the Digital Assets and Consumer Protection Act.
- IDFPR states that on August 18, 2025, Governor Pritzker signed the Digital Assets and Consumer Protection Act (205 ILCS 731) and the Digital Assets Kiosk Act (205 ILCS 732), and that IDFPR is working on administrative rules to implement them.
- IDFPR business-resources materials state that the Division of Banking issued notice to digital-asset businesses and digital-asset kiosk operators in Illinois regarding DACPA and DAKA and the provisions that took immediate effect.
