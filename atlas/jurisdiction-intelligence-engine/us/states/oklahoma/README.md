# Oklahoma

## Atlas Jurisdiction Package

Oklahoma is a jurisdiction package within the Satoshium Atlas jurisdiction intelligence framework.

This package provides a structured jurisdiction record designed to support research, analysis, orientation, comparison, discovery, and future interoperability across the broader Atlas ecosystem.

---

## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Oklahoma as a Central Interior Logistics Spine jurisdiction with documented evidence organized around Oklahoma City and Tulsa corridor concentration, inland freight and multimodal logistics visibility through the Turner Turnpike and Tulsa Port of Catoosa, power governance and selected generation and transmission visibility through Oklahoma Corporation Commission and GRDA, an aerospace and defense and sustainment and weather-research institutional environment anchored by Tinker Air Force Base, the Mike Monroney Aeronautical Center, NOAA NSSL, and the National Weather Center, research university continuity through the University of Oklahoma, data-center and digital-infrastructure visibility through Google Mayes County, RACK59, and the Core Scientific Muskogee high-performance computing project, industrial and manufacturing continuity through the Tulsa Port of Catoosa and MidAmerica Industrial Park, and municipal asymmetry with differentiated visibility across Oklahoma City, Norman, Tulsa, Catoosa, and northeast Oklahoma nodes. The signals layer reflects those same thematic clusters, with no direct signal established for Texas or other cross-state spillover effects or central-U.S. corridor significance as a routing classification.
