## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Indiana as a Great Lakes Industrial Core jurisdiction under the jurisdiction lens "Inland logistics + industrial continuity corridor," with canonical scope limited to corridor-layer anchors across nine evidence subsections. The signals layer reflects nine structural coordination signals: Midwest inland freight continuity through documented statewide freight volume and multimodal integration; an Indianapolis logistics hub signal through documented FedEx scale and national cargo-facility ranking at IND; Ohio River transport adjacency through Jeffersonville and Mount Vernon inland-waterway continuity; rail intermodal corridor density through documented Class I rail access across all three Ports of Indiana facilities; interstate freight convergence across I-65, I-69, I-70, and I-74; manufacturing belt continuity through steel-belt and agricultural-heartland adjacency at Burns Harbor and Jeffersonville; Purdue engineering research visibility; regional energy transmission participation across MISO and PJM; and broadband expansion through documented BEAD approval and Indiana Connectivity Program activity.
