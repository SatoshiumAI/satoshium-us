## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Virginia as a Federal Interface Governance jurisdiction with documented evidence across hyperscale data-center concentration in Northern Virginia and the Ashburn corridor, federal-adjacent infrastructure environment through Washington D.C. proximity and federal contractor and cloud ecosystem adjacency, routing and interconnection concentration through the Ashburn exchange ecosystem and dense international traffic concentration, submarine cable landing visibility through Virginia Beach transatlantic cable infrastructure, grid and power constraint visibility through PJM participation and documented electricity-demand pressure from AI-scale compute environments, and financial and enterprise hosting support through cloud availability zones supporting enterprise and federal financial systems. The signals layer reflects a hyperscale concentration signal through Loudoun County and Ashburn corridor density; a federal-adjacent hosting signal; a routing and interconnection centrality signal through the Ashburn exchange ecosystem and heavy international traffic; a transatlantic landing signal through Virginia Beach submarine cable infrastructure; a power-constrained compute growth signal through PJM participation and power-allocation negotiations; and an enterprise and financial hosting support signal. The canonical package records Virginia as a concentrated digital-infrastructure environment where hyperscale compute, interconnection density, federal-adjacent hosting, and submarine-cable connectivity are tightly coupled in Northern Virginia, with no established custody-regime or digital-asset-finance jurisdiction signals.
