Bitcoin / Digital Asset Policy

West Virginia's digital asset posture is structured by the state Money Transmitters Act, administered by the Division of Financial Institutions (DFI). The framework does not impose Bitcoin-specific prohibitions and the state has not enacted cryptocurrency licensing tiers beyond the MSB registration framework. Federal Bank Secrecy Act and FinCEN registration requirements represent the primary compliance gate for custodial and transmission-oriented operators within this corridor. The energy infrastructure profile of the corridor has supported favorable conditions for mining-oriented operations.

Energy / Mining / Compute Posture

West Virginia operates as one of the most structurally favorable US corridors for energy-intensive deployment strategies. Appalachian Basin natural gas production from Marcellus and Utica shale formations, coal-fired and natural-gas baseload generation capacity, and historically lower electricity rate structures combine to condition a deployment environment that is favorable for proof-of-work mining, high-density compute, and energy-arbitrage strategies. No Bitcoin mining prohibition or proof-of-work restriction operates within this corridor as of 2026.
