## Documented signals

### Digital asset licensing signal

New York shows a documented licensing-and-supervision environment for digital asset businesses through BitLicense, NYDFS-regulated custody approvals, and recurring supervisory authority over licensed virtual-currency activity.

### Institutional custody and settlement signal

New York shows a documented concentration of regulated financial institutions, custodians, exchanges, clearinghouses, and asset managers that supports institutional digital-asset integration through supervised channels.

### Stablecoin oversight signal

New York shows a documented stablecoin oversight structure through NYDFS guidance addressing reserve transparency, redemption assurance, and audit expectations.

### Compliance-density signal

New York shows a documented compliance-dense operating environment through AML, identity-verification, examination, and enforcement expectations applied to licensed financial institutions and digital-asset operators.

### Enterprise blockchain signal

New York shows a documented pattern of enterprise blockchain experimentation within major financial institutions across settlement, custody, and verification workflows.

### Financial-sector AI oversight signal

New York shows documented monitoring and governance attention to automated decision systems in lending, trading, and risk-modeling contexts within the financial sector.

### Governance-wrapper absence signal

The evidence shows that New York does not currently have a statutory DAO recognition framework.
