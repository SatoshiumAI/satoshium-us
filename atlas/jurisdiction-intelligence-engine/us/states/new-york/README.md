## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records New York as an Atlantic Financial and Global Gateway jurisdiction with documented evidence across digital asset licensing and supervision through the NYDFS BitLicense framework, supervised custody approvals, and stablecoin issuance guidance; institutional financial concentration through major global investment banks, exchanges, clearinghouses, asset managers, and regulated custodians; financial-sector AI oversight visibility through monitoring of automated decision systems in lending, trading, and risk-modeling contexts; and a governance-wrapper absence signal reflecting non-establishment of statutory DAO recognition. The signals layer reflects a licensing-and-supervision signal through BitLicense and recurring NYDFS examination and enforcement authority; an institutional custody and settlement signal; a stablecoin oversight signal; a compliance-density signal through AML, identity-verification, and audit expectations; an enterprise blockchain signal through experimentation inside regulated financial channels; a financial-sector AI oversight signal; and a governance-wrapper absence signal. The canonical package records New York as a supervised financial-infrastructure environment where institutional legitimacy, compliance structures, and regulated integration pathways are more visible than open experimentation flexibility.
