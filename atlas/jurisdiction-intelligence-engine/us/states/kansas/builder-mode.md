# Kansas — Builder Mode

Builder-mode derivation constraint: builder-mode content derives strictly from normalized jurisdiction layers.


## corridor_interpretation

Kansas should be interpreted as a corridor-structured environment where inland transport and logistics visibility is organized around documented turnpike and multimodal freight systems rather than a uniformly described statewide mesh.

## municipal_structure_interpretation

Kansas should be interpreted as a node-concentrated municipal environment with clearer structure in Wichita, Kansas-side Kansas City corridor nodes, and Topeka-centered/statewide governance visibility, with less complete structure outside those areas.

## governance_visibility_interpretation

Kansas should be interpreted as having visible statewide governance structure for transport and utility domains through documented policy coordination, rail-program administration, utility oversight, and turnpike operations.

## logistics_structure_interpretation

Kansas should be interpreted as an inland logistics and freight environment with documented rail, intermodal, highway, and freight-policy coordination structures, while preserving that full statewide freight-node hierarchy detail is incomplete.

## research_environment_interpretation

Kansas should be interpreted as a multi-node research environment anchored in KU, K-State, K-State Olathe, and Wichita State/NIAR systems, with visible university and technical-institution continuity in the currently documented set.

## energy_environment_interpretation

Kansas should be interpreted as an energy and transmission environment with visible utility-governance and transmission-process structure, while statewide resilience and full generation-delivery transparency remain incompletely documented.

## digital_infrastructure_interpretation

Kansas should be interpreted as a selective digital-infrastructure environment with documented facility, backbone, and broadband-program visibility at specific nodes and programs, without a complete statewide interconnection and private-facility inventory.

## agricultural_and_industrial_interpretation

Kansas should be interpreted as an agricultural-industrial and manufacturing environment with documented continuity across agricultural production systems, manufacturing subsectors, and corridor-linked logistics integration.

## cross_state_adjacency_interpretation

Kansas should be interpreted with limited cross-state adjacency visibility in the current record, where directly documented references are concentrated on Oklahoma-border corridor continuity and Oklahoma City-to-Newton passenger-rail evaluation context.
