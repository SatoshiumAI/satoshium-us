# Kansas — Evidence Layer

## Wichita aviation and manufacturing concentration

Kansas has documented aviation and manufacturing concentration in the Wichita corridor.

The current Kansas package preserves evidence of:

- Kansas Department of Commerce aerospace materials describing Wichita as a concentration point for aerospace and defense research, development, and manufacturing activity
- Wichita State University National Institute for Aviation Research (NIAR) materials describing applied aerospace and manufacturing R&D, certification, testing, training, sustainment, and advanced-manufacturing capabilities
- NIAR location materials documenting multiple Wichita-area sites, including ATLAS, HAMR, and other aerospace-testing and manufacturing-support facilities
- Kansas Department of Commerce manufacturing materials describing statewide manufacturing activity that includes aviation-related subsectors

These materials document Wichita-centered aerospace and manufacturing infrastructure without establishing uniform statewide aviation concentration.
