# Kansas

## Atlas Jurisdiction Package

Kansas is a jurisdiction package within the Satoshium Atlas jurisdiction intelligence framework.

This package provides a structured jurisdiction record designed to support research, analysis, orientation, comparison, discovery, and future interoperability across the broader Atlas ecosystem.

---

## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Kansas as a Central Interior Logistics Spine jurisdiction with documented evidence organized around Wichita aviation and manufacturing concentration, Kansas City Kansas-side infrastructure visibility, Topeka governance visibility, inland freight and rail and highway and logistics structure, energy and transmission and utility environment, research-university continuity across KU, K-State, K-State Olathe, and Wichita State, data-center and digital-infrastructure visibility, agricultural-industrial and manufacturing continuity, municipal node concentration with asymmetric statewide coverage, and limited central-U.S. and cross-state adjacency evidence. The signals layer reflects those same thematic clusters as distinct signals — including a Wichita-centered aerospace and advanced-manufacturing concentration signal, a Kansas-side Kansas City corridor infrastructure signal through Logistics Park Kansas City and Lenexa digital-facility visibility, a statewide governance and transport-coordination signal through KDOT and KCC, an inland freight and logistics-structure signal, an energy-transmission and utility-environment signal, a research-university continuity signal, a digital-infrastructure visibility signal through KanREN backbone connectivity and middle-mile broadband programs, an agricultural-and-manufacturing continuity signal, and a municipal node-concentration signal reflecting stronger documentary coverage in Wichita, Topeka-governance, and Kansas-side Kansas City corridor environments.
