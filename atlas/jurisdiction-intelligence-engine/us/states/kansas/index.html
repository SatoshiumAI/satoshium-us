3. Evidence Summary

The evidence layer documents the following for Kansas.

Wichita aviation and manufacturing concentration

The evidence layer records that Kansas has documented aviation and manufacturing concentration in the Wichita corridor.

The evidence layer records that the current Kansas package preserves evidence of:

  • Kansas Department of Commerce aerospace materials describing Wichita as a concentration point for aerospace and defense research, development, and manufacturing activity
  • Wichita State University National Institute for Aviation Research (NIAR) materials describing applied aerospace and manufacturing R&D, certification, testing, training, sustainment, and advanced-manufacturing capabilities
  • NIAR location materials documenting multiple Wichita-area sites, including ATLAS, HAMR, and other aerospace-testing and manufacturing-support facilities
  • Kansas Department of Commerce manufacturing materials describing statewide manufacturing activity that includes aviation-related subsectors

The evidence layer records that these materials document Wichita-centered aerospace and manufacturing infrastructure without establishing uniform statewide aviation concentration.

Kansas City (Kansas-side) infrastructure visibility

The evidence layer records that Kansas has documented infrastructure visibility on the Kansas side of the Kansas City corridor.

The evidence layer records that the current Kansas package preserves evidence of:

  • Kansas Department of Commerce logistics materials describing Logistics Park Kansas City in Edgerton, Kansas as a large inland distribution and intermodal environment
  • logistics materials describing Kansas-side corridor references where I-35 and I-70 converge in the Kansas City area
  • K-State Olathe materials describing research and partnership activity in the greater Kansas City area on the Kansas side
  • DataBank materials describing digital-infrastructure facilities in Lenexa, Kansas

The evidence layer records that these materials document Kansas-side Kansas City corridor infrastructure without attributing Missouri-side assets to Kansas.

Topeka governance visibility

The evidence layer records that Kansas has documented statewide governance and regulatory visibility with Topeka-centered state institutions.

The evidence layer records that the current Kansas package preserves evidence of:

  • Kansas Department of Transportation (KDOT) freight and rail materials documenting a state unit that develops and coordinates multimodal freight and rail policy, updates statewide rail and freight plans, and administers rail-improvement programs
  • Kansas Corporation Commission (KCC) electric and transmission-distribution materials documenting utility-rate and utility-oversight functions for electricity and related infrastructure domains
  • Kansas Turnpike Authority materials documenting a user-fee roadway system and statewide tolling operations

The evidence layer records that these materials document state governance visibility and transportation and utility oversight functions relevant to infrastructure coordination.

Inland freight, rail, highway, and logistics visibility

The evidence layer records that Kansas has documented inland freight, rail, highway, and logistics structure.

The evidence layer records that the current Kansas package preserves evidence of:

  • KDOT freight and rail materials documenting state freight and rail policy coordination, rail-improvement programming, and updates to statewide multimodal freight and rail plans
  • Kansas Department of Commerce logistics materials describing Class I and short-line rail participation, multimodal logistics framing, and intermodal logistics assets in Kansas
  • Kansas Turnpike Authority materials describing a 236-mile roadway from the Oklahoma border to Kansas City
  • KDOT passenger-rail materials documenting evaluation of extension from Oklahoma City to Newton, Kansas for connection to Amtrak service

The evidence layer records that these materials document visible corridor and logistics structure without establishing national freight-hub classification.

Energy, power, grid, and transmission environment

The evidence layer records that Kansas has documented energy and utility-governance visibility in the current source set.

The evidence layer records that the current Kansas package preserves evidence of:

  • KCC electric and transmission-distribution materials documenting oversight and regulatory functions for electric utilities and related infrastructure
  • KCC transmission references describing statutory treatment for larger transmission-line siting categories in Kansas
  • Evergy company-facts and transmission-project materials documenting utility service footprint, transmission and distribution mileage, substation visibility, and active transmission-project workflow

The evidence layer records that these materials document utility and transmission visibility but do not provide a complete statewide power-resilience or generation-delivery map.

Research-university continuity

The evidence layer records that Kansas has documented research-university continuity through major public-university systems.

The evidence layer records that the current Kansas package preserves evidence of:

  • University of Kansas Office of Research materials documenting research support systems, centers and labs, and continuing research expenditures
  • Kansas State University research materials documenting research-development structures, sponsored-program support, and research-center continuity
  • K-State Olathe materials documenting research and industry partnership activity in the Kansas City-side environment
  • Wichita State / NIAR materials documenting sustained aerospace and advanced-manufacturing research institutions

The evidence layer records that these materials document continuity across KU, K-State, K-State Olathe, and Wichita State-linked research institutions.

Data-center and digital-infrastructure visibility

The evidence layer records that Kansas has documented but uneven digital-infrastructure visibility in the current source set.

The evidence layer records that the current Kansas package preserves evidence of:

  • DataBank materials documenting a Lenexa, Kansas data-center campus with colocation and connectivity features
  • KanREN materials documenting a statewide research-and-education network backbone with public-university and Kansas City-area points of presence
  • KanREN materials documenting 100G and 400G backbone connectivity and linkage to research-network platforms
  • IdeaTek and Kansas state broadband-project materials documenting middle-mile and rural-serving broadband expansion initiatives

The evidence layer records that these materials document visible facilities and network programs but do not establish complete statewide IX density or private-facility coverage.

Agricultural-industrial or manufacturing continuity

The evidence layer records that Kansas has documented agricultural-industrial and advanced-manufacturing continuity.

The evidence layer records that the current Kansas package preserves evidence of:

  • Kansas Department of Commerce agriculture materials describing broad agricultural production and agriculture-related industrial activity
  • Kansas Department of Commerce manufacturing materials documenting large manufacturing employment and firm presence across multiple subsectors
  • logistics and manufacturing materials connecting agricultural, food-processing, and industrial operations to multimodal freight and corridor infrastructure

The evidence layer records that these materials document continuity in agricultural and manufacturing systems without classifying Kansas as a national coordination environment.

Municipal asymmetry and incomplete statewide coverage

The evidence layer records that Kansas has documented node concentration with incomplete statewide documentary coverage.

The evidence layer records that the current Kansas package preserves evidence of:

  • strong concentration of documentary visibility in Wichita (aviation and manufacturing institutions), Kansas-side Kansas City corridor nodes (logistics, research, and digital facilities), and statewide governance bodies
  • thinner direct documentary coverage for municipal variation outside the best-documented Wichita, Topeka-governance, and Kansas-side Kansas City corridor environments
  • digital and utility evidence that is stronger at specific nodes and programs than as a full statewide inventory

The evidence layer records that these materials document municipal asymmetry and incomplete statewide infrastructure transparency.

Limited central-U.S. and cross-state adjacency evidence

The evidence layer records that Kansas has limited but directly documented adjacency evidence in the current source set.

The evidence layer records that the current Kansas package preserves evidence of:

  • Kansas Turnpike materials documenting corridor linkage from the Oklahoma border to Kansas City
  • KDOT passenger-rail materials documenting an evaluated Oklahoma City to Newton, Kansas rail extension concept

The evidence layer records that the current source set does not provide enough normalized documentary coverage to characterize:

  • Missouri adjacency effects as infrastructure consequences beyond Kansas-side corridor references
  • Nebraska, Colorado, or Texas adjacency effects as normalized infrastructure consequences
  • national routing position or national freight significance as a classification
