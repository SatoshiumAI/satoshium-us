AI Policy

Mississippi operates without a comprehensive AI governance statute as of Q2 2026. The Joint Legislative Committee on AI, formed in 2024, maintains a review and study posture without binding enforcement authority. The Mississippi Department of Information Technology Services (ITS) governs state technology procurement through general frameworks without AI-specific attestation or incident reporting requirements. The operating environment does not impose documentation burdens or model-governance obligations on teams deploying within this corridor.

Bitcoin / Digital Asset Policy

Mississippi's digital asset posture is among the more permissive in the southeastern US. HB 1430 (2024) enables state agencies to accept cryptocurrency for tax and fee payment interfaces. SB 2901 (2024) explicitly excludes virtual currency miners from money transmitter classification under state law and provides express protections for residential mining operations. No state-level digital asset licensing regime equivalent to California's DFAL framework is active. AML and BSA obligations continue to apply through federal frameworks at the applicable deployment layer.
