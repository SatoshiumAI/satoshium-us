---

## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Mississippi as the sole canonical member of both the Lower Mississippi River Corridor and the Gulf Interior Completion Layer, under the jurisdiction lens "Lower Mississippi transition continuity between Southeast interior foundation and Gulf interior completion." The evidence layer is retained from corridor continuity between adjacent Atlas states — Arkansas, Tennessee, Alabama, and Louisiana — with Louisiana cited at evidence-layer depth for lower-river continuity, rather than from independent state-sourced research. The signals layer reflects five structural coordination signals: a lower Mississippi continuity signal through retained placement between the Arkansas-facing valley-transit frame and the Louisiana-facing lower-river frame; a Southeast interior continuity signal through shared Southeast Interior Completion Layer placement with Tennessee and Alabama; a north-south transition signal through retained placement resolving from Southeast-interior foundation into Gulf-interior completion; an inland Arkansas-to-Alabama continuity signal; and a narrow corridor-visible freight and inland-waterway signal, with no independent establishment of rail visibility, energy-grid participation, BEAD coordination, or research participation beyond interior adjacency.
