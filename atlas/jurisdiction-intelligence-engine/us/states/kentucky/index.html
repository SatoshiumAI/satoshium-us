Bitcoin / Digital Asset Policy

Kentucky has established meaningful legislative protections for Bitcoin infrastructure and digital asset rights. HB 701 (2022) extended legal protections for Bitcoin mining operations, affirmed node-operation rights, and created favorable treatment for self-custodied Bitcoin holdings. HB 230 (2022) established electricity rate classification for mining operations. The state operates as a structurally supportive surface for Bitcoin miners and infrastructure operators within the national deployment map.
