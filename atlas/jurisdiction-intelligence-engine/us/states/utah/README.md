## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Utah as a Mountain Interior Continuity jurisdiction with documented evidence organized around Wasatch Front corridor concentration, Salt Lake City and Provo and Ogden institutional and infrastructure visibility, data center and colocation visibility through Novva and EdgeConneX Salt Lake Valley facilities, direct interconnection and fiber-facility visibility through the Salt Lake Internet eXchange and Salt Lake City telecommunications right-of-way structures, inland logistics and transport corridor structure through Salt Lake City International Airport and FrontRunner commuter rail, mountain-corridor geography and infrastructure concentration through Wasatch Front planning and Central Wasatch mountain-transportation initiatives, research university continuity through University of Utah cybersecurity governance and Utah State University Space Dynamics Laboratory defense and space research, a defense and aerospace and simulation and cyber-adjacent institutional environment anchored by Hill Air Force Base and Ogden Air Logistics Complex, energy planning and power-availability conditions through Utah Office of Energy Development and facility-level substation visibility, and municipal asymmetry with corridor-concentrated visibility outside which statewide documentary coverage is sparse. The signals layer reflects those same thematic clusters with no direct signal established for statewide backbone density, major IX concentration beyond SLIX, or uniform statewide data-center distribution.
