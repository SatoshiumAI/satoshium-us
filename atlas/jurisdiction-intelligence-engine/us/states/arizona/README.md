# Arizona

## Atlas Jurisdiction Package

Arizona is a jurisdiction package within the Satoshium Atlas jurisdiction intelligence framework.

This package provides a structured jurisdiction record designed to support research, analysis, orientation, comparison, discovery, and future interoperability across the broader Atlas ecosystem.

---

## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Arizona as a Southwest Transition Infrastructure jurisdiction with documented evidence across corporate formation structures, digital asset legislation and supervised experimentation, compute infrastructure and industrial capacity, AI research and university research surfaces, and routing and interconnection visibility. The signals layer reflects a regulatory sandbox environment expanded to include digital assets via the Financial Technology, Digital Assets and Blockchain Sandbox Program, TSMC Arizona CHIPS Act investment supporting advanced semiconductor fabrication in Phoenix, a statutory data center tax-relief program administered by the Arizona Commerce Authority, and an interconnection ecosystem anchored by DE-CIX Phoenix and middle-mile fiber connectivity via the Arizona Department of Transportation. The evidence layer records these as documented structural anchors rather than routing-authority or readiness determinations.
