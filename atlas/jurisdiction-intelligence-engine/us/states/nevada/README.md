## Atlas Jurisdiction Package

Nevada is a jurisdiction package within the Satoshium Atlas jurisdiction intelligence framework.

This package provides a structured jurisdiction record designed to support research, analysis, orientation, comparison, discovery, and future interoperability across the broader Atlas ecosystem.

---

## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Nevada as a Basin and Range Interior Transition jurisdiction with documented evidence organized around Las Vegas and Reno corridor concentration, data center and colocation infrastructure visibility, logistics and transport corridor structure, renewable energy and power-availability environment, desert and high-desert siting suitability, university and research continuity, gaming and cyber-adjacent technical environment, southern and northern municipal asymmetry with sparse statewide distribution, and limited intermountain and western adjacency evidence. The signals layer reflects those same thematic clusters as distinct signals — including a corridor-concentration signal centered on Las Vegas, Henderson, Reno, Sparks, and Storey County; a data-center visibility signal through documented Google and Switch facilities; a transport-corridor signal through documented I-11 planning and I-80 Reno-Sparks corridor activity; a renewable-energy and power-availability signal through documented GOE project approvals and site-level power conditions; a desert and high-desert siting-environment signal; a university and research continuity signal through documented UNLV and UNR R1 status, NSHE system positioning, and Desert Research Institute presence; a gaming and cyber-adjacent institutional signal; a municipal-asymmetry signal reflecting differentiated southern and northern Nevada visibility; and a sparse-distribution signal reflecting thinner statewide documentary coverage outside the primary corridor nodes.
