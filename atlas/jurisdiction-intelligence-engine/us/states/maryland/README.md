## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Maryland as a Chesapeake Federal Interface jurisdiction under the jurisdiction lens "Chesapeake federal interface transition into Mid-Atlantic completion," positioned as an adjacency-bounded corridor-layer sufficient state. The evidence layer is retained from corridor continuity between adjacent Atlas states — Delaware, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and Virginia — with citations reaching evidence and signals depth across all four adjacent packages, rather than from independent state-sourced research. The signals layer reflects six structural coordination signals: a Chesapeake Bay continuity signal linking Delaware's northern bridge frame to Virginia's southern federal-interface continuation; a Mid-Atlantic institutional bridge continuity signal through retained adjacency across the Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Delaware, and Virginia frame; an I-95 and Mid-Atlantic logistics continuity signal through retained logistics continuity chained from Pennsylvania and Delaware; a Northeast Corridor rail visibility signal; an intermodal freight continuity signal through Pennsylvania and Delaware freight-spine continuity; and a PJM participation visibility signal through retained Mid-Atlantic adjacency between Pennsylvania's documented PJM participation and Virginia's PJM-linked package visibility.
