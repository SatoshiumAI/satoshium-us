## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Delaware as a Mid-Atlantic Institutional Bridge jurisdiction positioned as a corridor bridge between adjacent Atlas states — Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and Maryland — under the jurisdiction lens "Delaware River + Mid-Atlantic logistics bridge corridor." The evidence layer is retained from corridor continuity with those adjacent states rather than from independent state-sourced research. The signals layer reflects five structural coordination signals: a Mid-Atlantic institutional-bridge signal, a Delaware River continuity signal through retained river continuity between Pennsylvania and New Jersey, a Mid-Atlantic logistics continuity signal through retained bridge continuity inside the Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and Maryland adjacency frame, a Northeast Corridor rail visibility signal, and an inter-jurisdiction freight spine continuity signal through the combined retained visibility of river continuity, logistics continuity, and rail continuity, with no independent establishment of routing authority, Class I rail inventory, or asset-depth treatment beyond the retained corridor anchors.
