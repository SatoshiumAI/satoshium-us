Bitcoin / Digital Asset Policy

Idaho's digital asset environment is structured around general Money Transmitter License (MTL) requirements administered through the Idaho Department of Finance. No cryptocurrency-specific prohibition or dedicated digital asset licensing regime is in place as of 2026. The state has enacted limited but meaningful protections for digital asset holders: SB 1223 (2023) established that virtual currency held in custodial accounts is not subject to Idaho's unclaimed property (escheatment) provisions, reducing a structural friction point for custodial operators.

Privacy / Data Handling

Idaho enacted the Idaho Consumer Protection of Personal Information Act (SB 1325, effective July 1, 2024), establishing a limited consumer data privacy framework. The regime is narrower in scope and enforcement posture than California's CPRA: it applies only to controllers processing personal data of 100,000 or more Idaho consumers, or 25,000 or more consumers where the controller derives more than 50 percent of revenue from personal data sale. Enforcement rests exclusively with the Idaho Attorney General; no private right of action exists.

The identity regulatory surface is defined by the absence of a structured biometric framework rather than by active restrictions.
