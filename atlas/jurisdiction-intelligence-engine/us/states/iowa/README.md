## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Iowa as a Central Interior Agricultural Continuity jurisdiction under the jurisdiction lens "Central interior agricultural continuity within central plains completion," positioned as an adjacency-bounded corridor-layer sufficient state. The evidence layer is retained from corridor continuity across five adjacent Atlas states — Minnesota, Wisconsin, Illinois, Missouri, and Nebraska — plus a narrow Mississippi River interface anchor retained from the evidence layers of Illinois, Minnesota, and Wisconsin, rather than from independent state-sourced research. The signals layer reflects six structural coordination signals: central interior structural continuity through shared Central Interior Continuity Layer placement alongside Nebraska and Missouri; upper-midwest connector continuity between the Minnesota-facing northern-interior frame and the Wisconsin and Illinois Great Lakes-facing frame; north-south interior continuity from the Minnesota-facing northern frame into the Missouri-facing central frame; east-west interior continuity across the Nebraska–Illinois frame; institutional adjacency continuity across the full five-state surrounding frame; and a Mississippi River interface continuity signal through retained corridor-visible inland-waterway continuity at the Illinois, Minnesota, and Wisconsin-facing river edge.
