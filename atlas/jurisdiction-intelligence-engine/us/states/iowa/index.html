Privacy / Data Handling

Iowa enacted SF 262 (Iowa Consumer Data Protection Act, ICDPA), effective January 1, 2025. The ICDPA is modeled on the Virginia VCDPA framework and establishes consumer rights covering access, deletion, correction, portability, and opt-out of targeted advertising and profiling. The enforcement surface is administered exclusively through the Iowa Attorney General with no private right of action. Applicability thresholds engage at 100,000 consumer records or 25,000 consumer records where data sales constitute the primary revenue source.
