---

## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Colorado as a Mountain West Research jurisdiction with documented evidence across consumer data privacy framework, automated decision-system governance visibility, rulemaking and implementation infrastructure, cryptocurrency and fintech compatibility visibility, institutional alignment visibility, and research and innovation environment. The signals layer reflects a privacy-governance signal through the Colorado Privacy Act; an automated decision-system accountability signal through documented consumer-protection treatment of automated processing and profiling; a rulemaking-operationalization signal through structured implementation timelines and public-facing compliance documentation; a regulated innovation compatibility signal through the coexistence of privacy governance with research and experimentation environments; a limited cryptocurrency-compatibility signal through pilot public-payment acceptance and a non-restrictive posture toward regulated fintech; an interstate governance-alignment signal through participation in multi-state privacy governance patterns; and a research and applied data-science signal through academic and technical institutions contributing to AI-adjacent research. The evidence layer records these as documented structural anchors rather than routing-authority or readiness determinations.
