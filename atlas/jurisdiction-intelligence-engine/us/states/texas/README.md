## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Texas as a National Energy and Logistics Spine jurisdiction with documented evidence across ERCOT grid architecture and energy-market structure, compute and verification infrastructure visibility, renewable energy visibility, and infrastructure policy visibility. The signals layer reflects an ERCOT grid-architecture signal through documented self-contained grid structure with real-time wholesale pricing, interruptible-load participation, merchant-generation flexibility, and transmission expansion; a grid-responsive mining signal through documented industrial Bitcoin mining participation in curtailment, demand-response, ancillary-services, and reserve-balancing activities; a verification infrastructure concentration signal through documented concentration of proof-of-work infrastructure; a renewable-aligned compute signal through documented wind generation leadership, expanding utility-scale solar deployment, and compute placement near generation corridors; a hyperscale and AI infrastructure signal through documented expansion of cloud campuses, AI training clusters, and storage infrastructure; an energy-compute co-location signal through documented locating of infrastructure near wind, solar, and natural-gas generation environments; and an infrastructure-policy support signal through documented policy visibility treating mining and compute as compatible with grid participation and long-term investment. The canonical package records no established signals for DAO-recognition, custody-framework, or monetary-law innovation structures.
