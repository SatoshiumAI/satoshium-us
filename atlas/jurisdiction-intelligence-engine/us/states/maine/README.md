## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Maine as the sole canonical member of both the Northeast Terminal Edge Corridor and the Northeast Terminal Completion Layer — the terminal state of the Northeast Completion Layer cluster — under the jurisdiction lens "Northeast terminal-edge transition into terminal completion." The evidence layer is retained from corridor continuity between adjacent Atlas states — New Hampshire, Vermont, Massachusetts, and New York — with New Hampshire cited at metadata, evidence, and signals depth, rather than from independent state-sourced research. The signals layer reflects four structural coordination signals: a Northeast terminal-edge continuity signal through retained terminal placement beyond the New Hampshire and Vermont support frame; a terminal completion continuity signal through retained continuity between the northern Northeast support frame and Maine's terminal completion structure; a continuation-from-New-Hampshire-support-continuity signal; and a terminal-position institutional adjacency signal, with no independent establishment of coastal adjacency continuity within the Gulf of Maine frame, Class I rail visibility, National Highway Freight Network visibility, ISO-NE participation, or BEAD coordination.
