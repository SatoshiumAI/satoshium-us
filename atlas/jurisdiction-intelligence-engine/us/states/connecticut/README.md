## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Connecticut as a Northeast Institutional Mesh jurisdiction positioned as a corridor-visible institutional bridge between adjacent Atlas states — New York, Massachusetts, and Rhode Island — under the jurisdiction lens "Long Island Sound + Northeast Corridor institutional mesh continuity corridor." The evidence layer is retained from corridor continuity with those adjacent states rather than from independent state-sourced research. The signals layer reflects three structural coordination signals: an institutional-mesh bridge signal through retained Northeast institutional placement, a Long Island Sound continuity signal through retained coastal continuity between New York and Rhode Island, and a Northeast Corridor rail visibility signal through retained rail continuity between New York and Rhode Island and Massachusetts, with no independent establishment of Class I rail continuity, National Highway Freight Network visibility, ISO-NE participation, or BEAD coordination beyond the retained corridor anchors.
