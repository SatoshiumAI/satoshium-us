## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Wyoming as an Interior Western Statutory Flex jurisdiction with documented evidence across digital asset property recognition, Special Purpose Depository Institution framework authorization, DAO legal recognition within the LLC structure, blockchain recordkeeping acceptance for corporate governance, utility token statutory clarification, legislative continuity through a standing blockchain and digital innovation committee, low state-layer identity and AI expansion signals through documented absence of broad biometric and AI licensing frameworks, federal interaction boundaries through continuing FinCEN and SEC and CFTC applicability, and institutional density constraint through documented limits in major clearing institutions, national exchanges, and Tier-1 venture capital concentration. The signals layer reflects a digital-asset statutory clarity signal, a DAO coordination compatibility signal, a legislative continuity signal, a low state-layer identity expansion signal, a low state-layer AI licensing signal, a federal-boundary sensitivity signal, and an institutional density constraint signal. The canonical package characterizes Wyoming as a statute-forward jurisdiction where legal clarity is more strongly documented than physical infrastructure concentration.
