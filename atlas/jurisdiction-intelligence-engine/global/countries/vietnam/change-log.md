# Vietnam — Change Log

## Change-Log Scope

- This file documents normalization and interpretation decisions only for the Vietnam canonical package.
- No rankings, trust scores, readiness levels, or comparative status assignments are made here.
- No deployment guidance, deployment approval, or deployment avoidance guidance exists here.
- This file is a renderer-safe audit layer recording structural scope, interpretation safeguards, decomposition boundaries, and evidence constraints.

## Canonical Package Composition

- `evidence.md` records evidence-bounded infrastructure, institutional, and operational source material.
- `signals.md` records structural signals derived strictly from the evidence layer.
- `trust-dimensions.md` records operational continuity, coordination, interoperability, and dependency interpretations derived strictly from the evidence and signals layers.
- `metadata.md` records descriptor-oriented structural classification derived strictly from the evidence, signals, and trust-dimensions layers.
- `profile.md` records structural jurisdiction characterization derived strictly from the evidence, signals, trust-dimensions, and metadata layers.
- `builder-mode.md` records builder-facing structural interpretation derived strictly from the evidence, signals, trust-dimensions, metadata, and profile layers.

## Evidence-Layer Normalization Decisions

- The evidence layer was normalized using an infrastructure-first structure covering administrative systems, identity, payments, telecommunications, data infrastructure, internet governance, research networks, energy coordination, logistics, cybersecurity, and regional interfaces.
- Evidence statements were kept institution-specific and source-bounded rather than translated into strategic, comparative, or evaluative claims.
- Promotional wording was normalized into descriptive institutional phrasing where required for cross-country consistency.
- Operational metrics and planning references were preserved only where they materially supported structural characterization, without converting them into performance ranking or superiority framing.
- Institutional decomposition discipline was maintained so that payments, telecom, cyber, logistics, data, identity, and governance functions remained separately evidenced before later-layer interpretation.
- Evidence limitations, partial visibility, and legacy-source access constraints were preserved explicitly rather than normalized away.

## Signals-Layer Interpretation Decisions

- Signals were treated as evidence-derived operational behaviors rather than as strategic assessments or capability claims.
- Administrative, payment, telecom, data, internet-governance, research, energy, logistics, cyber, and regional signals were framed as coordination, continuity, interoperability, and interaction patterns only.
- No hidden-capability interpretation, readiness logic, ranking logic, or Atlas surface assignment was introduced in the signals layer.
- Interoperability was interpreted in bounded structural terms, especially for payments, DNS participation, research-network linkage, and international connectivity.
- Signal phrasing preserved absence-of-signal validity so that unmentioned signals were not treated as negative evidence.

## Trust-Dimensions Interpretation Decisions

- Trust dimensions were normalized as operational continuity and coordination characteristics rather than as trust certification or deployment qualification.
- Persistence, continuity, interoperability, and resilience were framed as descriptive structural properties only.
- A non-certification posture was maintained throughout, with explicit exclusion of safety, trustworthiness, readiness, and deployment-approval language.
- Trust interpretation remained bounded by the evidence and signals layers and was not extended into legal, commercial, or geopolitical judgment.
- Dependency and visibility constraints were retained inside the trust-dimensions layer rather than collapsed into implied negative conclusions.

## Metadata Normalization Decisions

- Metadata was normalized as descriptor-oriented classification rather than evaluative classification.
- The metadata layer used a non-ranking structural format focused on jurisdiction type, infrastructure orientation, continuity characteristics, interoperability characteristics, and bounded dependency characteristics.
- Operational characterization remained topology-oriented and renderer-safe, without strategic corridor assignment or deployment interpretation.
- Topology framing was kept bounded to documented coastal, regional, maritime-facing, and cross-system characteristics already supported by prior layers.
- Decomposition discipline was maintained so metadata did not collapse into profile or builder-mode language.

## Profile-Layer Characterization Decisions

- The profile layer was normalized as infrastructure-centered structural characterization rather than as strategic profile or capability profile.
- Operational synthesis remained bounded by prior layers and did not introduce new infrastructure claims or unstated institutional roles.
- Continuity-oriented interpretation was preserved across administrative, payment, telecom, data, governance, research, energy, logistics, cyber, and regional sections.
- Regional connectedness was described only through documented interface behavior such as cross-border QR links, international DNS presence, research linkage, cable planning, and gateway-port planning.
- Characterization logic remained bounded, descriptive, and non-comparative throughout.

## Builder-Mode Interpretation Decisions

- Builder mode was normalized as an operational interaction layer using a builder-facing but non-prescriptive reading of the completed package.
- The layer emphasized infrastructure interaction, continuity relationships, interoperability surfaces, and visibility boundaries rather than deployment choice or strategic advice.
- The recurring builder-mode methodology treated systems as coordinated environments, not as certified or approved operating surfaces.
- Each builder-mode section remained evidence-derived and renderer-safe, with no translation into trust score, readiness level, or strategic recommendation.
- Anti-deployment safeguards were preserved explicitly so builder interpretation did not become deployment guidance.

## Evidence & Visibility Constraints

- Full visibility into Vietnam’s high-value settlement architecture was not available in the accessible evidence set, so financial interpretation remained more complete for retail-payment interoperability than for full settlement depth.
- Research-network characterization remained bounded by partial accessibility of some legacy MOST and VinaREN materials.
- Internet-governance characterization remained bounded by incomplete visibility into real-time peering, exchange traffic, and broader exchange-centrality conditions.
- Data infrastructure visibility remained partial rather than a complete inventory of public, private, or foreign-operated compute and hosting capacity.
- Telecommunications characterization remained bounded by incomplete market-share and full operator service-quality visibility.
- Cybersecurity characterization remained stronger for public-sector coordination than for all-sector certification, audit, and private-sector resilience structures.
- Logistics characterization remained bounded beyond documented seaport, postal, and transport-node references and did not constitute a complete multimodal operational map.

## Renderer-Safety & Normalization Safeguards

- Explicit constraints sections were preserved across layers to prevent semantic drift into ranking, readiness, trust, or deployment language.
- Absence of signal, dimension, descriptor, or characteristic was treated as valid and non-negative.
- Anti-speculative phrasing was maintained throughout the package.
- Bounded interpretation was preserved at each layer so later layers did not overrun the evidence perimeter.
- Decomposition discipline was maintained so layer roles remained distinct and renderer-safe.
- Infrastructure-first methodology was preserved across normalization to keep claims anchored to documented systems and institutions.
- Anti-amplification normalization was applied so planning targets, operational metrics, and institutional roles were not translated into superiority, strategic leverage, or approval language.

## Cross-Layer Consistency Notes

- `evidence.md` remained distinct from `signals.md` by preserving raw documented conditions rather than inferred behaviors.
- `signals.md` remained distinct from `trust-dimensions.md` by preserving structural behavior interpretation separately from continuity and dependency interpretation.
- `metadata.md` remained distinct from `profile.md` by preserving descriptor-oriented classification separately from broader structural characterization.
- `profile.md` remained distinct from `builder-mode.md` by preserving jurisdiction characterization separately from builder-facing interaction interpretation.
- Layer separation was maintained to prevent semantic collapse, prevent repetition from functioning as escalation, and preserve bounded interpretation depth across the package.
