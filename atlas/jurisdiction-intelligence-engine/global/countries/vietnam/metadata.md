# Vietnam — Metadata Layer

## Metadata Scope

- This metadata layer derives strictly from `evidence.md`, `signals.md`, and `trust-dimensions.md` for Vietnam.
- No rankings, trust scores, readiness levels, deployment approvals, or certifications are assigned here.
- The metadata below is structural only and describes observed operational, interoperability, continuity, and coordination characteristics.
- This file is intended as a renderer-safe jurisdiction descriptor and topology-oriented classification surface.

- research-network characterization remains bounded by partial access to legacy MOST and VinaREN materials

- metadata remains limited to documented operational characteristics and does not provide an exhaustive national infrastructure census

## Metadata Constraints & Interpretation Limits

- This metadata is not a score, ranking, trust designation, or readiness classification.

- No geopolitical interpretation, military inference, intelligence inference, or hidden-capability inference is implied or supported.
- Absence of metadata is valid and should not be interpreted as absence of the underlying infrastructure, institution, or capability.
- All metadata remains bounded by the evidence, signals, and trust-dimensions layers and should not be extended into unsupported strategic or predictive conclusions.
