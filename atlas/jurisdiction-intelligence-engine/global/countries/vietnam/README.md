## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Vietnam as a coordinated national infrastructure jurisdiction combining digital administration, central-bank-directed retail-payment interoperability, telecommunications modernization, mixed public and commercial data infrastructure, registry and DNS governance, research-network participation, grid coordination, maritime logistics planning, public-sector cyber coordination, and ASEAN-linked regional integration, spanning digital governance and procedural digitization, central-bank retail payment modernization, telecommunications and connectivity, data infrastructure and digital continuity, registry and DNS governance, research network and scientific collaboration, energy grid and industrial coordination, maritime logistics and trade connectivity, cybersecurity and digital resilience, and ASEAN and regional integration frameworks. Primary coordination cities are Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, and Da Nang. The evidence layer documents the National Public Service Portal and VNeID digital-governance continuity with Scheme 06 population database and e-KYC integration; State Bank of Vietnam direction over NAPAS with NAPAS 247 fast-transfer and cross-border QR-payment connectivity to Singapore, Thailand, Cambodia, and Lao PDR with coordination activity toward China, Republic of Korea, and Japan; national data-centre policy targeting at least three national multi-purpose data centres alongside commercial buildout including Tan Thuan CMC; VNNIC management of the .VN ccTLD with eight DNS server clusters and more than 100 points of presence, IPv6, DNSSEC, and Anycast deployment, and VNIX-linked reporting; Viettel and VNPT 5G licensing under CST regulation as Vietnam's first successful spectrum allocation in 15 years; VinaREN research-network participation with TEIN-linked international connectivity; EVN National Load Dispatch Center grid coordination; seaport master plan-linked maritime gateway development; and VNCERT/CC and the National Cyber Security Centre public-sector cyber coordination. The evidence layer applies bounded-visibility constraints throughout rather than negative signals.
