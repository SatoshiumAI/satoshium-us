# Vietnam — Builder Mode

## Builder-Mode Scope

- This builder-mode layer derives strictly from `evidence.md`, `signals.md`, `trust-dimensions.md`, `metadata.md`, and `profile.md` for Vietnam.

## Administrative & Service Environment

- The current package documents a unified service-access environment centered on the National Public Service Portal rather than a fully fragmented administrative-service perimeter.
- Administrative procedures, online public services, fee-payment functions, feedback handling, and cross-provincial access pathways document coordinated procedural systems operating through a shared service layer.
- Reusable records and linked access to connected business, tax, and insurance datasets document cross-system administrative interoperability within the current source set.
- Administrative continuity appears through standing central access, monitoring, evaluation, and procedural coordination functions rather than isolated transaction-by-transaction service handling.
- For builder interpretation, the administrative and service environment appears as a coordinated procedural-access surface with linked service integration across multiple public-service systems.
