## Jurisdiction Normalization Summary

- South Africa normalized as a Pretoria-centered administrative environment with distributed territorial continuity rather than a single-node administrative model.
- It normalized as a layered transport, payment, digital, and energy continuity environment rather than a single-sector jurisdictional model.
- It normalized as a regulated telecommunications continuity environment through ICASA, `.za` naming governance, ZADNA, and NAPAfrica rather than a purely operator-defined communications environment.
- It normalized as an electricity-coordination continuity environment through Eskom, NTCSA, balancing visibility, and Southern African Power Pool interaction rather than a fully self-contained electricity perimeter.
- It normalized as a maritime continuity environment through TNPA and visible multi-port continuity rather than one-port concentration.
- It normalized as a research-network-supported continuity environment through SANReN, TENET, NICIS, eduroam, and related research-data infrastructure rather than a commercial-connectivity-only environment.
- It normalized as a regional interconnection continuity environment through payment settlement, electricity interaction, peering visibility, research-network linkage, aviation continuity, maritime continuity, and naming-governance continuity rather than geopolitical framing.
- It normalized as a bounded-observability environment in which continuity is visible across multiple public layers while deeper private, cyber-operational, logistics, and commercial topology remains only partly visible.
