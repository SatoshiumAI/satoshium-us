# South Africa

## Atlas Jurisdiction Package

South Africa is a jurisdiction package within the Satoshium Atlas jurisdiction intelligence framework.

This package provides a structured jurisdiction record designed to support research, analysis, orientation, comparison, discovery, and future interoperability across the broader Atlas ecosystem.

---

## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records South Africa as a Pretoria-centered administrative coordination jurisdiction with Johannesburg as a commercial and financial concentration node, combining distributed nine-province territorial continuity, central-bank settlement governance, state-entity infrastructure coordination, telecommunications and exchange-layer infrastructure, research-network participation, multi-port maritime logistics, energy-grid operational coordination, and cybersecurity management under bounded observability conditions. The evidence layer documents SITA State Information Technology Agency government-ICT coordination and e-government infrastructure; South African Reserve Bank SARB SAMOS RTGS and TCPB and NPS settlement systems; Eskom national electricity generation and transmission and distribution infrastructure with Transnet Freight Rail and Transnet Port Terminals and Transnet National Ports Authority multi-port maritime coordination; NEMISA and ZADNA and ZA-IX exchange-layer and domain-governance infrastructure; TENET and SANReN and NREN research-network federation; CSIR and information-technology research coordination; Government Communication and Information System GCIS and SAPS and State Security Agency and CSIRT-vGov cybersecurity and civil-coordination structures; and ports of Durban, Cape Town, Port Elizabeth, Richards Bay, East London, and Saldanha as distributed maritime-logistics anchors. The canonical package records bounded observability throughout and holds all economic-power, continental-anchor, investment-destination, regional-leadership, and political-complexity framings strictly outside the evidence layer.
