- The evidence indicates a signal of Pretoria-centered administrative coordination operating through national departments, specialist institutions, and distributed provincial administration rather than through a single consolidated public-service stack.
- The coexistence of national departments with nine provincial-government surfaces indicates a signal of national-and-provincial continuity rather than capital-only administrative relevance.
- The coexistence of DHA, SARB, ICASA, ZADNA, SACAA, SANRAL, TNPA, NDMC, and CSIR indicates a signal of regulator-operator separation paired with recurring multi-agency continuity rather than one-institution administrative control.
- The overall administrative pattern indicates centralized coordination with distributed service delivery and infrastructure operation rather than centralized administration with fully centralized execution.
- These signals remain operational only and do not imply governance ranking, political interpretation, or state-capacity comparison.
