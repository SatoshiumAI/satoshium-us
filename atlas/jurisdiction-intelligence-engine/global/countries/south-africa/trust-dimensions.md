## Administrative Continuity Characteristics

- The evidence and signals support a trust dimension of Pretoria-centered administrative continuity through nationally visible departments, specialist institutions, and distributed provincial administration rather than through a single consolidated public-service stack.
- The coexistence of national departments and nine provincial-government surfaces supports a trust dimension of national-and-provincial continuity rather than capital-only administrative relevance.
- DHA, SARB, ICASA, ZADNA, SACAA, SANRAL, TNPA, NDMC, and CSIR support a trust dimension of regulator-operator separation paired with recurring multi-agency continuity rather than one-institution administrative control.
- The overall administrative pattern supports a trust dimension of centralized coordination with distributed execution, because service delivery and infrastructure operation remain distributed even where coordination is visibly concentrated.
- These characteristics remain descriptive only and do not imply governance-quality ranking, political interpretation, or state-capacity comparison.
