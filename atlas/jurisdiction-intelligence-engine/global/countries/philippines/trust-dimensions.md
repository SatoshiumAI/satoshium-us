- Public visibility remains uneven across ports, cable systems, grid assets, telecom deployments, and private backbone arrangements, which constrains how fully continuity can be characterized from open sources.

- Metropolitan concentration in Luzon, with secondary concentration in Cebu and Davao, indicates that continuity characteristics are distributed but not evenly distributed.
- These dimensions therefore describe observable operational dependencies and constraints only, not a complete inventory of all national infrastructure conditions.
