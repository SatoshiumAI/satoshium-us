## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Sweden as a Stockholm-centered digital-governance coordination environment, a Sunet-linked national research-network federation surface, a Netnod-centered exchange-neutrality infrastructure environment, a Linköping-based EuroHPC Arrhenius compute participation site, and a Skellefteå battery-industrial anchor location, aligned across the ScanMed freight corridor, Nordic electricity-market integration, EU digital identity interoperability, NORDUnet research network cooperation, CBSS Baltic, and EU DIGITAL programme frameworks. The evidence layer documents DIGG Ena digital-governance coordination with Sweden Connect and eIDAS interoperability; Riksbank RIX-RTGS, RIX-INST, and TIPS linkage settlement infrastructure with e-krona CBDC research programme; Ericsson telecommunications standards participation and PTS spectrum governance; Sunet, SunetC encrypted backbone, NORDUnet, NAISS compute coordination, and EuroHPC Arrhenius hosting; Netnod exchange neutrality and .se registry governance; Svenska kraftnät transmission coordination with Aurora Line and Konti-Skan Connect cross-border interconnectors; MSB civil-contingency coordination and CERT-SE national CSIRT role; and Northvolt Ett battery-industrial infrastructure and BASE battery research alliance. The signals layer reflects distributed Nordic coordination spanning digital governance, standards participation, research networking, exchange neutrality, battery-industrial anchoring, and grid-interconnection structures embedded within EU, Nordic, and Baltic frameworks.
