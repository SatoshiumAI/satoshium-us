## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Japan as a sovereign nation-state in the Indo-Pacific region using a per-domain anchor-classification metadata structure across national compute coordination, research federation, semiconductor ecosystem, exchange and backbone, government digital infrastructure, energy coordination, regulatory and cybersecurity, defense and simulation, space infrastructure, identity federation, and cross-border participation domains. The evidence layer documents national compute coordination through RIKEN's Fugaku system, HPCI coordination, University of Tokyo Wisteria/BDEC-01, and JAXA JSS3; a federated research landscape spanning RIKEN, AIST, NII, JAXA, QST, major universities, JST, and JSPS; a semiconductor ecosystem anchored by the JASM Kumamoto manufacturing surface with TSMC, Sony, DENSO, and Toyota investment, Sony Semiconductor Solutions' nationwide Japan network, Renesas, and Kioxia; and documented multilateral participation across economic, semiconductor, and technology coordination frameworks. The canonical package carries a persistent topology-placement refusal recorded across all source layers: the evidence does not support corridor routing role assignment or topology completion-layer placement, superseding Phase 3 scaffolding values.
