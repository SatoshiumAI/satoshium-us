# Japan — Signals Layer

It does not assign routing role, corridor membership, readiness tier, interoperability posture, trust ranking, topology placement, or comparative status.

- The evidence supports national HPC coordination presence, federated compute orchestration signals, and research-linked compute continuity signals, but does not support a PRACE-equivalent participation signal or a sovereign compute autonomy classification.
