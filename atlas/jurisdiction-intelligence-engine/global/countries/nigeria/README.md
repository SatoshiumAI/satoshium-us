## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Nigeria as an Abuja-centered federal coordination jurisdiction with Lagos-linked operational reinforcement and distributed multi-node territorial continuity across thirty-six states, combining central-bank payment-settlement interoperability, telecommunications and exchange-layer governance, electricity coordination, maritime and aviation logistics, research-network participation, and cybersecurity management under bounded observability conditions. The evidence layer documents CBN Central Bank of Nigeria NIBSS RTGS-S4 and Nigeria Central Switch payment-settlement infrastructure; NCC Nigeria Communications Commission, NiRA .ng registry, and IXPN Internet Exchange Point Nigeria telecommunications and exchange governance; TCN Transmission Company of Nigeria, NERC Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission, and NBET Nigerian Bulk Electricity Trading electricity coordination; NPA Nigerian Ports Authority and NIWA National Inland Waterway Authority and NIMASA Nigerian Maritime Administration and Safety Agency maritime infrastructure; NgREN Nigerian Research and Education Network research-network participation; and national cybersecurity and civil-coordination structures. The canonical package records Abuja as the federal administrative anchor and Lagos as the documented commercial and operational reinforcement node, with neither treated as the exclusive national center — preserving distributed multi-node continuity as a canonical structural feature. All population-scale, oil-and-gas, fintech, mobile-money, Lagos-dominance, West Africa leadership, gateway-to-Africa, security-conflict, and economic-power framings are held strictly inside negation and exclusion boundaries.
