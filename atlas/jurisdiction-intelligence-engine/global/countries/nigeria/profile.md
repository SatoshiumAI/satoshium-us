## Jurisdiction Overview

- Nigeria presents as an Abuja-centered federal coordination environment with distributed state continuity and multi-node territorial continuity rather than a single-node national operating model.
- The visible operational environment is reinforced by payment-settlement interoperability, telecom-domain-exchange continuity, electricity coordination, maritime-support continuity, research-network-supported continuity, regional interconnection, and bounded observability.
- Public continuity is visible through overlapping administrative, identity, payment, telecommunications, electricity, transportation, aviation, maritime, emergency, cyber/data, and research-network layers rather than through one consolidated institutional stack.
- Lagos appears as an important operational reinforcement layer inside the wider federal and territorial environment, but the profile does not normalize Nigeria as Lagos-only any more than it normalizes it as Abuja-only.
- This overview remains strictly operational and does not adopt population-size, oil-resource, economic-power, or regional-leadership framing.

## Administrative Environment

- Nigeria presents as an Abuja-centered administrative environment whose visible continuity depends on federal ministries, departments, agencies, service portals, and regulator-operator relationships rather than on a single consolidated public-service stack.
- Administrative coordination is visibly concentrated through Abuja-facing federal institutions including the Office of the Secretary to the Government of the Federation and wider federal service surfaces, while execution remains distributed across a thirty-six-state environment and multiple sectoral institutions.
- OSGF continuity, federal service access surfaces, and publicly visible supervised agencies indicate recurring federal coordination continuity rather than capital-only symbolic presence.
- Ministries, departments, agencies, regulators, and service portals together indicate multi-agency public-service continuity rather than one-institution service control.
- The resulting administrative environment is one of centralized coordination with distributed execution, federal administrative continuity, state-level continuity, and distributed public-service operation.
- This section remains descriptive only and does not imply governance-quality ranking, political interpretation, or state-capacity comparison.
