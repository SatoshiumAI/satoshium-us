## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records France as a hybrid national–European institutional coordination jurisdiction with a multi-agency coordinated infrastructure organization pattern. The evidence layer documents national research and HPC infrastructure through GENCI, IDRIS, TGCC, CINES, and the Jules Verne EuroHPC exascale consortium with Jean Zay, Joliot-Curie, and Adastra systems; a research federation spanning CNRS, CEA, Inria, Inserm, ONERA, Université PSL, Sorbonne, and Université Paris-Saclay within the European Research Area; semiconductor and advanced manufacturing through the Crolles-Grenoble ecosystem anchored by STMicroelectronics, GlobalFoundries, CEA-Leti, and Soitec within France 2030 and Electronique 2030 frameworks; a certification-mediated sovereign cloud qualification framework; an ENTSO-E synchronized transmission interoperability environment; ESA-linked launcher coordination; FranceConnect-centered administrative identity federation; and EU-embedded digital governance alignment through GDPR and the AI Act. The canonical metadata records France using its own per-domain field structure covering compute, research, semiconductor, exchange, cloud, energy, defense, space, identity, regulatory, and cross-border integration domains rather than a corridor-triad format.
