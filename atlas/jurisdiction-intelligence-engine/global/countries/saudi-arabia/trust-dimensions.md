# Saudi Arabia - Trust Dimensions

Trust derivation constraint: trust dimensions derive strictly from `evidence.md` and `signals.md`. This file does not introduce new infrastructure claims, assign routing authority, assign readiness tiers, rank jurisdictions, infer deployment eligibility, or extend beyond documented anchors.

## Institutional Continuity Dimension

- The source layers indicate institutional continuity across energy, industrial systems, digital governance, payment systems, telecommunications, logistics, cybersecurity, and cross-border institutional embedding through centralized state-coordinated structures rather than fragmented operator-led continuity.
- Vision 2030 and NIDLP indicate continuity in industrial and logistics coordination through a standing national programmatic layer tied to infrastructure development.
- Aramco, National Grid SA, Mawani, and GACA indicate continuity across hydrocarbons, transmission, export, port, and aviation systems through named national infrastructure institutions.
- SDAIA and NIC indicate continuity in digital-governance and data-platform coordination through the National Data Bank, government cloud(s), the Government Secure Network, and the Government Service Bus.
- SAMA indicates continuity in central-bank-led payment-system governance, while CST and SaudiNIC indicate continuity in telecommunications regulation, gateway-linked connectivity, and naming-layer stewardship.
- NCA indicates continuity in national cyber coordination through regulatory, control-framework, and incident-response functions.
- GCCIA and OPEC participation indicate that Saudi institutional continuity also remains embedded in repeated GCC and global coordination structures rather than a wholly closed national perimeter.
- The documented trust characteristic is institutional continuity across multiple critical infrastructure layers under strong centralized coordination, combined with durable participation in wider regional and global systems.

## Digital Governance and Identity Dimension

- The source layers indicate centralized digital-governance continuity across identity-adjacent systems, public-service delivery, and data-governance frameworks under coordinated state control.
- SDAIA and NIC indicate continuity through national oversight of data and AI governance, National Data Bank operation, government cloud functions, and interagency service infrastructure.
- GOV.SA indicates continuity through a national service-access layer that concentrates public digital-service access within a common state platform.
- Absher, National Single Sign-On, Nafath, and Tawakkalna indicate continuity across identity, authentication, and public-service interaction through named and repeatedly referenced national platforms.
- The documented integration of e-identification, trust services, secure payments, e-notifications, e-invoicing, and interoperability frameworks indicates that digital-service continuity is carried through interconnected public-service building blocks rather than isolated applications.
- NDMO indicates continuity in formal data-governance stewardship through frameworks for classification, sharing, privacy, freedom of information, and open data.
- The documented trust characteristic is centralized digital-governance continuity carried through state-coordinated identity-adjacent systems, service-delivery platforms, and data-governance controls.

## Settlement Infrastructure Integration Dimension

- The source layers indicate central-bank-led payment-system continuity with layered domestic settlement infrastructure and controlled cross-border experimentation without independent global settlement authority.
- SAMA indicates continuity through central-bank and rulebook coordination over domestic banking and payment-system governance.
- SARIE indicates continuity through a national payment and settlement mechanism linking the banking system inside a common domestic settlement layer.
- The coexistence of SARIE, instant-payment infrastructure, mada, SADAD, and Saudi Payments back-end operations indicates layered payment-rail continuity rather than dependence on a single national payment mechanism.
- Real-time instant-payment functionality indicates standing domestic real-time settlement capability within the broader SAMA-governed payment environment.
- Project Aber indicates continuity in controlled cross-border experimentation with the Central Bank of the UAE, while the same source layers bound trust claims away from independent global settlement authority.
- The documented trust characteristic is continuity of layered domestic payment infrastructure under central-bank coordination, combined with bounded cross-border experimentation rather than sovereign settlement independence.

## Data and AI Governance Dimension

- The source layers indicate state-coordinated data and AI governance continuity supported by national data platforms and hybrid cloud infrastructure integrating government-operated and global provider systems.
- SDAIA and the National Strategy for Data & AI indicate continuity through a centralized national authority and strategy layer for data leadership, digital transformation, and AI development.
- NDMO indicates continuity through formal governance rules for data classification, sharing, privacy, freedom of information, and open data.
- NIC and the National Data Bank indicate continuity through nationally coordinated data-platform operation tied to government cloud(s), the Government Secure Network, and the Government Service Bus.
- Government Cloud Deem indicates a government-operated cloud continuity layer inside the broader public-sector digital environment.
- Google Cloud, Oracle, planned Microsoft region availability, Aramco-linked cloud development, MCIT mega-data-center initiatives, and center3 infrastructure indicate that Saudi data and AI continuity is carried through a hybrid cloud environment rather than an exclusively state-operated compute perimeter.
- The same source layers do not document a fully sovereign hyperscale compute stack independent of global provider and partner participation, which bounds the trust claim accordingly.
- The documented trust characteristic is centralized governance continuity across national data platforms and AI policy, combined with hybrid cloud infrastructure attachment rather than fully sovereign compute independence.

## Research and Compute Dimension

- The source layers indicate institutionally anchored compute capability with high-performance research systems, without evidence of a fully sovereign national compute stack.
- KAUST indicates continuity through Shaheen III and related supercomputing infrastructure inside a named national research institution.
- KACST indicates continuity through its role as national science agency, national laboratory, and research and innovation coordination institution.
- Together, KAUST and KACST indicate continuity across research compute and national scientific coordination rather than a purely university-local compute environment.
- The same source layers provide stronger support for institutional compute and research coordination than for a fully articulated national research-network backbone spanning all regions.
- The documented trust characteristic is continuity of high-performance research compute and institutional research coordination, bounded away from claims of fully sovereign national compute-stack autonomy.

## Exchange and Naming Infrastructure Dimension

- The source layers indicate nationally coordinated internet gateway and exchange continuity combined with stable naming-layer governance under institutional control without global exchange authority classification.
- stc indicates continuity through the Saudi Arabian Internet Gateway, international internet backbone, international internet connectivity, landing-station access, and cable-station-linked infrastructure.
- center3 indicates continuity through published internet-exchange, data-center, and subsea-connectivity infrastructure across multiple Saudi regions.
- SaudiNIC indicates continuity through registration-authority functions, TLD management, registrar licensing, registry operations, and DNSSEC-related statistics under a dedicated national naming institution.
- The evidence more directly supports gateway and exchange continuity through stc and center3 than through a currently retrievable official SAIX-branded surface, which bounds this dimension to the documented current institutional anchors.
- The documented trust characteristic is continuity of gateway, exchange, and naming-layer governance under institutional control, while the same source layers do not support broader exchange-authority classification.

## Telecommunications and Digital Infrastructure Dimension

- The source layers indicate state-regulated telecommunications continuity with integrated national and international connectivity infrastructure supporting digital-service and data systems.
- CST indicates continuity through national regulation, sector enablement, 5G coordination, and spectrum-allocation activity involving major telecom operators.
- STC, Mobily, and Zain participation in CST’s 5G and spectrum surfaces indicates continuity through regulated multi-operator telecommunications development rather than an unstructured market environment.
- stc’s backbone, gateway, landing-station, and international connectivity functions indicate continuity between domestic telecommunications infrastructure and wider international network attachment.
- center3 indicates additional continuity through carrier-neutral data-center, subsea, and exchange infrastructure integrated into the national telecom environment.
- The documented use of GOV.SA, Nafath, Absher, and Tawakkalna alongside telecom reporting indicates that telecommunications continuity and digital-service continuity are operating as interdependent system layers.
- The documented trust characteristic is continuity of state-regulated telecommunications and digital infrastructure spanning domestic service delivery and international connectivity attachment.

- More broadly, the source layers do not support routing authority, readiness tiers, jurisdiction rankings, or deployment-eligibility conclusions.

## Trust Dimensions Summary Statement

Saudi Arabia is documented as a centralized, state-coordinated infrastructure jurisdiction spanning energy, digital governance, payments, telecommunications, data systems, logistics, and cybersecurity, operating within global financial, cloud, logistics, and energy networks. The documented trust dimensions indicate continuity across institutional coordination, digital-governance and identity systems, central-bank-led settlement infrastructure, data and AI governance, research compute, gateway and naming governance, telecommunications, energy and industrial systems, logistics, cybersecurity, and GCC/global institutional integration without assigning readiness tiers, routing authority, or deployment eligibility.
