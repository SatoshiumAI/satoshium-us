## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Saudi Arabia as a Riyadh-centered centralized state-coordinated infrastructure jurisdiction spanning GCC energy and grid integration, global hydrocarbon export and industrial coordination, GCC and global financial system integration, regional telecommunications and international connectivity, global logistics and maritime and aviation connectivity, national data and AI governance, and GCC and global cybersecurity coordination frameworks, with primary coordination cities of Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dhahran. The evidence layer documents SDAIA, NIC, GOV.SA, Absher, Nafath, Tawakkalna, National Data Bank, and NDMO digital-governance coordination; SAMA, SARIE RTGS, instant-payment infrastructure, mada, SADAD, Saudi Payments, and Project Aber cross-border CBDC experimentation; SDAIA national data and AI authority, government cloud Deem, Google, Oracle, and Microsoft hyperscaler regions, center3 data-center infrastructure, and MCIT data-center initiatives; KAUST Shaheen III and KACST research-compute environments; stc internet gateway, center3 internet exchange, and SaudiNIC .sa registry governance; CST regulatory authority with STC, Mobily, and Zain operators and 5G deployment; Saudi Aramco hydrocarbon infrastructure, National Grid SA transmission, renewable-energy and hydrogen initiatives through NEOM and ENOWA; Mawani ports, GACA aviation infrastructure, and Vision 2030 and NIDLP logistics coordination; NCA national cybersecurity authority and Essential Cybersecurity Controls; and GCCIA grid interconnection, OPEC participation, and Project Aber regional institutional embedding. The signals layer reflects centralized institutional continuity organized through state structures across energy, digital governance, payments, telecommunications, data systems, logistics, and cybersecurity, embedded within wider global financial, cloud, logistics, telecom, and energy networks.
