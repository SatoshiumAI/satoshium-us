# Indonesia

## Atlas Jurisdiction Package

Indonesia is a jurisdiction package within the Satoshium Atlas jurisdiction intelligence framework.

This package provides a structured jurisdiction record designed to support research, analysis, orientation, comparison, discovery, and future interoperability across the broader Atlas ecosystem.

---

## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Indonesia as a Jakarta-centered large-scale archipelagic infrastructure jurisdiction combining digital identity systems, public-service integration, central-bank-operated settlement and payment modernization, inter-island telecommunications, submarine-cable connectivity, public-sector cloud continuity, registry governance, research-network participation, maritime logistics modernization, cyber coordination, and ASEAN and global integration interfaces, spanning archipelagic inter-island connectivity, digital governance and procedural digitization, central-bank payment modernization, submarine cable and maritime connectivity, public-sector cloud and digital continuity, registry and domestic exchange governance, research network and scientific collaboration, energy grid and industrial coordination, cybersecurity and digital resilience, ASEAN and global integration, and maritime logistics and trade connectivity frameworks. The evidence layer documents Dukcapil, IKD, SPBE, INA Digital, Satu Data Indonesia, and Komdigi digital-governance coordination; Bank Indonesia BI-RTGS, SKNBI, BI-FAST, QRIS, GPN, digital-payment interoperability, and QRIS Antarnegara cross-border QR-payment systems; PDN systems including PDN Cikarang and the national API backbone with AWS Jakarta, Google Cloud Jakarta, and Azure Indonesia Central mixed-provider cloud environments; PANDI, .ID registry governance, APJII, IIX, domestic interconnection, and DNS governance; Palapa Ring, BAKTI BTS 4G, Telkom Indonesia, Telkom neuCentrIX, Telin, and Bifrost submarine-cable systems; iDREN, TEIN research-network connectivity, BRIN-linked scientific infrastructure, and ASEAN-Korea HPC collaboration; PLN grid modernization with smart-grid and inter-island transmission; Pelindo, PTOS-M, Phinnisi, and the National Logistics Ecosystem with port digitization and maritime trade infrastructure; and BSSN and Gov-CSIRT government-sector incident-response and digital-resilience coordination. Primary coordination cities are Jakarta, Surabaya, Batam, Bandung, and Makassar.
