# Finland

## Atlas Jurisdiction Package

Finland is a jurisdiction package within the Satoshium Atlas jurisdiction intelligence framework.

This package provides a structured jurisdiction record designed to support research, analysis, orientation, comparison, discovery, and future interoperability across the broader Atlas ecosystem.

## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Finland as a Helsinki-centered digital-governance coordination environment, a CSC- and Kajaani-linked EuroHPC LUMI compute-hosting environment, a Funet-linked research-network federation surface, a Fingrid-linked Nordic synchronous-area transmission participant, a FICIX-centered exchange-layer neutrality environment, and a CBSS-, TEN-T-, and euro-area institutional coordination participant, aligned across Nordic electricity-market integration, North Sea–Baltic TEN-T corridor, euro-area monetary infrastructure, EU digital identity interoperability, NORDUnet research network cooperation, and CBSS Baltic coordination frameworks. The evidence layer documents DVV population-system governance, Suomi.fi e-Identification, Messages, e-Authorization, Data Exchange Layer, Finnish Authenticator, and eIDAS interoperability; Bank of Finland TARGET2, TIPS, T2S settlement participation and digital euro preparation-phase participation; CSC, Funet, NORDUnet, EuroHPC LUMI hosting in Kajaani, and HELMI quantum-computing linkage; FICIX exchange-layer neutrality and Traficom .fi registry governance; Nokia Oulu Bell Labs, Hexa-X-II leadership, and 6G flagship programme telecommunications standards participation; Fingrid Nordic synchronous-area participation with Fenno-Skan, EstLink, and Aurora Line interconnectors; NCSC-FI cyber situational-awareness, CERT activity, and National Emergency Supply Agency preparedness; and euro-area, CBSS Baltic, and TEN-T North Sea–Baltic institutional participation. The signals layer reflects a multi-layer national coordination environment spanning digital governance, EuroHPC compute hosting, telecommunications standards participation, settlement modernization, research-network federation, exchange neutrality, grid interconnection, and preparedness coordination embedded within EU, Nordic, and Baltic institutional systems.
