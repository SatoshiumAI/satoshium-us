# Georgia

## Atlas Jurisdiction Package

Georgia is a jurisdiction package within the Satoshium Atlas jurisdiction intelligence framework.

This package provides a structured jurisdiction record designed to support research, analysis, orientation, comparison, discovery, and future interoperability across the broader Atlas ecosystem.

---

## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Georgia as a Tbilisi-centered South Caucasus coordination jurisdiction with Poti as a secondary maritime node, operating under a bounded-observability topology framework that applies as a first-class structural condition throughout the canonical package. The evidence layer documents the my.gov.ge portal and Public Service Hall network and e-signature and e-cabinet and e-procurement and State Register digital-governance infrastructure; National Bank of Georgia RTGS and IRTS and card-processing settlement systems; GRENA research-network infrastructure with eduGAIN federation — canonically distinguished from GÉANT, which is not documented for Georgia; NIC.GE .ge registry governance; Georgian State Electrosystem GSE transmission coordination; GOGC gas transmission and South Caucasus Pipeline corridor; Tbilisi and Kutaisi and Batumi airports under Airports of the World and TbilAvia; Poti and Batumi ports within the Black Sea maritime framework; CERT.GOV.GE and national PSCC 112 emergency coordination; and Rustavi, Batumi, Kutaisi as distributed secondary urban coordination nodes. The canonical package introduces bounded observability as a first-class topology framework, applying observability-constraint language rather than standard negative classifications where canonical documentation is limited — a structural distinction from the European cluster that uses GÉANT and from Armenia which documents GÉANT access via ASNET-AM.
