The canonical package records Armenia as a Yerevan-centered landlocked South Caucasus coordination jurisdiction with Gyumri as a documented secondary urban coordination node, combining digital-government interoperability, central-bank payment settlement, research-network federation with both GÉANT and eduGAIN surfaces, telecommunications governance, aviation continuity, energy interconnection, and bounded observability across documented infrastructure surfaces.
