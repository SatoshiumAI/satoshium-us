## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records South Korea as an East Asia national jurisdiction using a per-domain visibility-classification metadata structure across structural institutional compute, research, infrastructure, semiconductor, identity, regulatory, and coordination environment domains. The evidence layer documents national compute coordination through KISTI's National Supercomputing Center and Nurion supercomputer, ETRI's Future Computing Research Division and AI Computing Research Laboratory, and KAIST and Seoul National University AI research surfaces; a federated research landscape spanning NRF, KISTI, ETRI, KIST, KRISS, KIER, KIMM, KAIST, Seoul National University, and POSTECH; a semiconductor ecosystem anchored by Samsung Foundry manufacturing sites at Giheung, Hwaseong, and Pyeongtaek and SK hynix campus and manufacturing surfaces at Icheon, Bundang, Cheongju, and the Yongin semiconductor cluster; and documented multilateral participation across economic and technology coordination frameworks. The canonical package carries a persistent topology-placement refusal recorded across all source layers — corridor alignment, readiness tier, and topology completion layer are explicitly not assigned — superseding Phase 3 scaffolding values.
