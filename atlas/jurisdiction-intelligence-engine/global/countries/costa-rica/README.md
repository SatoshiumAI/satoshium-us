## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Costa Rica as a San José-centered administrative jurisdiction whose national continuity depends on distributed territorial coordination across administrative, identity, payment, telecommunications, electricity, transport, aviation, maritime, emergency-coordination, cyber-governance, and research-network layers rather than any single system, with Pacific and Caribbean maritime continuity through distinct port-administration environments, spanning San José-centered administrative concentration, distributed territorial continuity, layered transport, payment, digital, and energy continuity, regulated telecommunications, electricity coordination, Pacific–Caribbean maritime continuity, research-network continuity, regional interconnection, disaster-response and cyber-coordination, and bounded observability frameworks. The evidence layer documents Portal Pura Vida Digital, MICITT, and RACSA-coordinated public-service access with Tribunal Supremo de Elecciones cédula and Identidad Digital Costarricense identity continuity and DIMEX non-citizen administrative identity; Banco Central de Costa Rica payment administration through SINPE and SINPE Móvil with Liquidación de Mercados securities settlement under delivery-versus-payment logic; SUTEL telecommunications regulation alongside FONATEL-backed Espacios Públicos Conectados and NIC Costa Rica .cr naming administration; ICE operation-and-control with CNFL distribution and ARESEP regulation and Ente Operador Regional-linked regional electricity interaction; CONAVI national road administration with INCOFER passenger rail; DGAC civil-aviation administration with Juan Santamaría and Daniel Oduber Quirós international airports; INCOP-administered Puerto Caldera on the Pacific and JAPDEVA-administered Limón-Moín on the Caribbean; CNE national alert and multi-hazard coordination; MICITT and CSIRT-CR cyber coordination; and RedCONARE, CONARE, RedCLARA, and eduroam research-network continuity. The canonical package explicitly excludes eco-tourism, biodiversity, sustainability, democracy-ranking, happiness-ranking, regional-leadership, and environmental-symbolism interpretations as negated-only constructions. Primary coordination city is San José.
