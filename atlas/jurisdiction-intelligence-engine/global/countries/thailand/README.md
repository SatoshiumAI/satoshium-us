## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Thailand as a Bangkok-centered multimodal continuity jurisdiction with documented eastern seaboard coupling, organized around layered aviation, metropolitan and commuter rail, payment interoperability, internet exchange, international gateway connectivity, cloud-region presence, and centrally coordinated power transmission, spanning Bangkok-centered multimodal continuity, eastern seaboard industrial and digital coupling, aviation and metropolitan rail coordination, PromptPay and cross-border QR interoperability, domestic exchange and international gateway connectivity, cloud-region and data-center coupling, centralized power transmission, identity and transaction governance, public warning and hydrological monitoring, research network and knowledge-network, and ASEAN and mainland regional integration frameworks. Primary coordination cities are Bangkok, Chonburi, Phuket, and Chiang Mai. The evidence layer documents Airports of Thailand's six-airport system anchored in Bangkok across Suvarnabhumi, Bangkok International, Phuket, Chiang Mai, Hat Yai, and Chiang Rai; MRTA multi-line Bangkok metropolitan rail and SRTET Commuter Train Red Line; EECO multimodal projects with U-Tapao Airport and Eastern Aviation City including High-Speed Rail Linking Three Airports and Laem Chabang and Map Ta Phut port expansion; Bank of Thailand direction over PromptPay with cross-border QR connectivity to Singapore, Lao PDR, Cambodia, Indonesia, Malaysia, and Vietnam under ASEAN Payment Connectivity; ETDA transaction governance and NDID DID Platform identity exchange with ThaIDF federation; BKNIX neutral internet exchange alongside NT international gateway and submarine-cable participation in seven systems; AWS Asia Pacific Thailand Region and Google Cloud Bangkok regions with NT Chonburi data-center EEC positioning; EGAT centrally coordinated transmission across 500, 230, 132, 115, and 69 kV lines with cross-border electricity interaction with Lao PDR and Malaysia; and TMD weather-warning and RID hydrological monitoring continuity.
