---

## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Israel within the Eastern Mediterranean Research and Compute Corridor, documented through institution-published materials spanning national academic research-network coordination, distributed university AI research, biometric identity-documentation and authentication federation, national registry and domestic exchange coordination, civilian cyber-defense coordination, central-bank settlement infrastructure, and central-bank exploratory CBDC research. The evidence layer documents research-network and HPC coordination through IUCC and ILAN with 100Gb/s access to GÉANT and EuroHPC Joint Undertaking participation; distributed university AI research surfaces across the Weizmann AI Hub for Scientific Discovery, Tel Aviv University Center for Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, and Technion Tech.AI; a biometric identity-documentation and government-identification federation through the National Biometric Database Authority and gov.il Government Identification System; internet registry and exchange infrastructure through ISOC-IL managing the .il and ישראל ccTLD registries and operating the Israel Internet Exchange; civilian cyber-defense coordination through the Israel National Cyber Directorate and CERT-IL; and payment-system and CBDC exploration through the Bank of Israel's ZAHAV RTGS system and ongoing digital shekel research programme.
