## Atlas Jurisdiction Package

Portugal is a jurisdiction package within the Satoshium Atlas jurisdiction intelligence framework.

This package provides a structured jurisdiction record designed to support research, analysis, orientation, comparison, discovery, and future interoperability across the broader Atlas ecosystem.

---

## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Portugal as a Lisbon-centered Atlantic-facing continuity jurisdiction with Sines as a secondary infrastructure node and island-inclusive continuity across the Azores and Madeira, combining Iberian interoperability, EU payment and settlement integration, transatlantic cable connectivity, research-network participation, civil-protection coordination, and Atlantic maritime logistics. The evidence layer documents Infraestruturas de Portugal rail infrastructure of 3,621.6 km and road network of 17,447 km; Rail Freight Corridor 4 connecting Sines and Setúbal and Lisbon and Aveiro and Leixões into Spain and France; ANA 10-airport network with Lisbon Airport and Oriente station; Port of Lisbon TEU container handling and Port of Sines deep-water infrastructure with ZILS logistics zone and LNG terminal; REN electricity and gas transmission with OMIE Iberian electricity market participation and Spain interconnections; ANACOM telecommunications governance with GigaPIX exchange in Lisbon and Porto; FCCN, RCTS, and CAM ring research-network infrastructure; EllaLink transatlantic cable connecting Sines to Fortaleza; Start Campus Sines data-campus; Banco de Portugal, SICOI, SEPA, and TARGET T2 and T2S and TIPS settlement participation; AMA, gov.pt, ePortugal, Chave Móvel Digital, and Citizen Card digital-governance and identity infrastructure; and ANEPC, IPMA, CNCS, and CERT.PT civil-protection and cybersecurity coordination. The canonical package records Portugal as not an Atlantic cable-dominance classification beyond documented landing and exchange infrastructure, and not a broader Iberian telecommunications-authority classification.
