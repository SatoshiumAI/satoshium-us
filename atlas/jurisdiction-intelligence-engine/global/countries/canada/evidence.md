# Canada — Evidence Layer

## Federal AI Governance Signals

- CIFAR states that the Pan-Canadian Artificial Intelligence Strategy launched in 2017 and identifies Amii in Edmonton, Mila in Montreal, and the Vector Institute in Toronto as Canada’s three National AI Institutes.
- The Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat states that the Directive on Automated Decision-Making applies to any automated decision system in production used to make an administrative decision or a related assessment about a client.
- The Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat states that responsible officials must complete, approve, and publish the final results of an Algorithmic Impact Assessment before the production use of an automated decision system.
- Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada states that Bill C-27, the Digital Charter Implementation Act, 2022, included the Artificial Intelligence and Data Act.
- ISED’s archived AIDA companion document describes the proposed federal AI framework as risk-based and states that Canada would align approaches with international partners including the European Union, the United Kingdom, and the United States.
- CIFAR operates as the visible national coordination layer for the Pan-Canadian AI Strategy and its tri-hub AI institute structure.

## Federal Privacy Governance Architecture

- The Office of the Privacy Commissioner states that the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act is Canada’s federal private-sector privacy law.
- The Office of the Privacy Commissioner states that PIPEDA sets the ground rules for how private-sector organizations collect, use, and disclose personal information in the course of commercial business.
- The Office of the Privacy Commissioner states that the Privacy Commissioner of Canada is an Agent of Parliament and that the OPC oversees compliance with both the Privacy Act and PIPEDA.
- ISED states that Bill C-27 was tabled as the Digital Charter Implementation Act, 2022.
- ISED’s bill summary states that Bill C-27 included the Consumer Privacy Protection Act, which would replace PIPEDA’s private-sector rules, create disposal and data-mobility rights, strengthen protections for children’s personal information, and provide stronger order-making and penalty tools.
- Federal privacy materials document an enacted PIPEDA accountability framework with OPC oversight together with formally tabled reform materials emphasizing consent, transparency, disposal, data mobility, and stronger enforcement.

## Digital Asset Oversight Signals

- The Canadian Securities Administrators state that the work of bringing crypto platforms into compliance with Canadian securities laws is ongoing.
- The CSA states that crypto asset trading platforms currently operating and in discussions with Canadian securities regulators must sign a pre-registration undertaking to continue operations while their registration applications are reviewed.
- The CSA states that certain unregistered crypto asset trading platforms filed enhanced pre-registration undertakings following Staff Notice 21-332, and that enhanced undertakings were required for continued operation in Canada while registration was pursued.
- The CSA states that registered platforms and platforms operating under pre-registration undertakings remain subject to terms and conditions related to value-referenced crypto assets, documenting a securities-law treatment tendency for stablecoin-like instruments unless specifically accommodated.
- FINTRAC states that money services businesses dealing in virtual currency must register with FINTRAC and comply with obligations under the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act.
- CIRO states that it is the pan-Canadian self-regulatory organization overseeing investment dealers, mutual fund dealers, and trading activity on Canada’s debt and equity marketplaces, and that it carries on the regulatory functions of IIROC and the MFDA.

## Compute Infrastructure Signals

- Statistics Canada states that hydroelectricity remained the largest contributor to Canada’s electricity generation in 2024 at 55.2 percent.
- Statistics Canada states that Quebec produced 50.2 percent of Canada’s hydroelectricity in 2024, and its reporting on hydroelectricity generation identifies Quebec, British Columbia, and Manitoba as major hydro-generation anchors.
- Natural Resources Canada states that the lowest electricity prices in Canada are found in British Columbia, Manitoba, and Quebec because these provinces have access to low-cost hydroelectricity from large-scale projects.
- CANARIE states that it is the federal partner in Canada’s National Research and Education Network, providing interprovincial and international connectivity, and that Canada’s NREN connects to a global web of more than 100 NRENs.
- The Digital Research Alliance of Canada states that it is funded by the Government of Canada and works with partners across the country to improve access, strengthen security, and build a skilled digital research infrastructure workforce.
- The Alliance states that it advances and maintains digital research infrastructure supporting national research computing, research data, and research software, and its investment materials reference contribution agreements with ISED to implement Canada’s national DRI Strategy.
- Shared Services Canada states that it has established four enterprise data centres that host data and applications used to enable the delivery of digital services to Canadians.
- CIFAR’s Pan-Canadian AI Strategy materials identify Vector, Mila, and Amii as the national AI institute layer supporting Canada’s AI research and compute-adjacent cluster structure.

## Telecom and Interconnection Signals

- The CRTC identifies Bell, Rogers, and TELUS as the top three mobile service providers in Canada by revenues and subscribers, and its telecommunications reporting documents their central role in the national communications market.
- Bell states that it operates a coast-to-coast fibre backbone with interconnection points across Canada, and Bell Wholesale states that its network extends across Canada and into the United States and Europe.
- Rogers states that it connects millions of Canadians coast to coast through its internet and 5G network infrastructure.
- TELUS states that its 5G network is built on a robust fibre network backbone and describes national-scale broadband expansion in Ontario and Quebec.
- ISED’s International Submarine Cable Licences Regulations materials state that the Minister of Innovation, Science and Economic Development may issue international submarine cable licences, documenting a federal landing and authorization layer for submarine cable infrastructure.
- TorIX states that the Toronto Internet Exchange directly connects Canadian business internet traffic using local network infrastructure.
- QIX states that its mission is to establish and operate internet exchange infrastructure serving Quebec, Canadian, and foreign network, content, and Internet service providers, with the objective of making Montreal a preferred peering location in the Canadian and North American market.

## Energy Topology Compatibility Signals

- Statistics Canada states that hydroelectricity was the largest source of electricity generation in Canada in 2024.
- Statistics Canada’s electricity reporting and Natural Resources Canada’s electricity materials document Quebec, British Columbia, and Manitoba as major hydro-linked generation environments within Canada’s national electricity structure.
- Shared Services Canada states that two of its four enterprise data centres use large cooling wheels to exchange outdoor cold air with indoor hot air, taking advantage of Canada’s climate to save energy.
- Natural Resources Canada’s Best Practice Guide for Canadian Data Centres treats cooling and free-cooling as central operational variables in Canadian data-centre efficiency management.
- Natural Resources Canada states that the North American Bulk Electric System includes 34 active transmission lines between Canada and the United States.
- The Canada Energy Regulator states that it regulates all electricity exported from Canada and currently regulates 86 international power lines.
- Natural Resources Canada states that the North American Renewable Integration Study provides a planning-to-operation perspective on the Canadian and United States power systems and examines expanded international transmission infrastructure in support of shared clean-energy and climate goals.

## Institutional Stability Signals

- The Supreme Court of Canada states that judicial independence means that the judiciary has the ability to make decisions based solely on fact and law, free of influence from government or outside parties.
- The Supreme Court of Canada states that an accord between the Chief Justice of Canada and the Minister of Justice was entered into in 2019 to strengthen the independence of the Supreme Court of Canada.
- The Department of Justice states that the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms protects rights and freedoms including freedom of expression and equality rights.
- The House of Commons states that Canada is a constitutional monarchy and a parliamentary democracy founded on the rule of law and respect for rights and freedoms.
- The House of Commons states that Parliament for Canada consists of the Crown, the Senate, and the House of Commons.
- The Department of Justice states that federal courts have the power to review decisions, orders, and other administrative actions of most federal boards, commissions, and tribunals, documenting a standing judicial-review layer for federal administration.
