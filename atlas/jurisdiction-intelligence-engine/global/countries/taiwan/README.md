## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Taiwan as a Taipei-centered advanced-semiconductor and digital-coordination jurisdiction with distributed municipal and county territorial continuity, combining semiconductor fabrication infrastructure, digital-government service delivery, central-bank settlement governance, telecommunications and exchange-layer infrastructure, research-network federation, high-performance compute coordination, energy grid management, aviation and maritime logistics, and cybersecurity management. The evidence layer documents TSMC Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company fabrication infrastructure as the canonical semiconductor anchor; FSC Financial Supervisory Commission and TWSE Taiwan Stock Exchange and financial-market coordination; Central Bank of the Republic of China Taiwan CBC and payment-settlement infrastructure; government digital-service and e-government platforms; TWNIC Taiwan Network Information Centre .tw registry governance; NCHC National Center for High-Performance Computing research and HPC infrastructure; TEIN and research-network participation; Taipower Taiwan Power Company electricity transmission and grid management; Taoyuan International Airport and other aviation infrastructure; Port of Kaohsiung and Port of Keelung and other maritime-logistics infrastructure; and national cybersecurity coordination structures. The canonical package records Taiwan under bounded observability for politically sensitive context throughout, holding all geopolitical, sovereignty, cross-strait, security, semiconductor-superpower, and strategic-location framings strictly inside negation and exclusion boundaries while preserving documented institutional and infrastructure surfaces as the canonical evidence record.
