## Administrative Continuity Characteristics

- The evidence and signals support a trust dimension of Taipei-centered administrative coordination through the Executive Yuan, ministries, agencies, regulators, and public-service institutions rather than through a single consolidated public-service stack.
- The coexistence of the Executive Yuan, the Ministry of the Interior, transport and communications bodies, digital-affairs bodies, and regulator-facing institutions supports a trust dimension of recurring central administrative continuity rather than capital-only symbolic presence.
- Public visibility of six special municipalities, thirteen counties, three autonomous municipalities, 198 county-administered townships and cities, and 170 districts supports a trust dimension of municipal, county, district, and township continuity rather than a single-node administrative model.
- The coexistence of central ministries with multi-level territorial administration supports a trust dimension of distributed public-service continuity rather than administration confined to Taipei ministries alone.
- The overall administrative pattern supports Taipei-centered coordination with distributed municipal and county continuity across territorial and public-service layers rather than centralized administration with fully centralized delivery.
- These characteristics remain descriptive only and do not imply political interpretation, sovereignty discussion, democracy narratives, or governance scoring.
