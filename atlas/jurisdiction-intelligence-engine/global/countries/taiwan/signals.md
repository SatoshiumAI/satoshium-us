- The coexistence of the Executive Yuan, the Ministry of the Interior, and ministry-level transport, communications, and digital-affairs bodies indicates a signal of recurring central administrative continuity rather than capital-only symbolic presence.
- Public visibility of six special municipalities, thirteen counties, three autonomous municipalities, 198 county-administered townships and cities, and 170 districts indicates a signal of recurring municipal, county, district, and township continuity rather than a single-node administrative model.
- The coexistence of central ministries with multi-level territorial administration indicates a signal of distributed public-service continuity rather than administration confined to Taipei ministries alone.

- These signals remain operational only and do not imply political interpretation, sovereignty discussion, democracy narratives, or governance scoring.
