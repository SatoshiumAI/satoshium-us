## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Norway as an Oslo-centered digital-governance coordination environment, a Statnett-linked Nordic electricity-interconnection jurisdiction, a Gassco-linked continental gas-delivery coordination environment, a Sikt-linked research-network federation surface, a NIX-centered exchange-layer neutrality environment, and an EEA and Nordic institutional coordination participant, aligned across Nordic electricity-market integration, North Sea energy infrastructure, EEA, Schengen, NORDUnet research network, and Nordic Council cooperation frameworks. The evidence layer documents Digdir, ID-porten, Altinn, NOBID interoperability, and EU Digital Identity Wallet pilot participation with eIDAS 2.0 preparation; Norges Bank NBO, NICS, SWIFT-linked settlement infrastructure with CBDC Phase 5 evaluation; Nkom telecommunications governance and 5G frequency programme; Sikt, Uninett, NORDUnet, Sigma2 HPC, and Norwegian AI Cloud coordination; NIX exchange-layer neutrality and Norid .no registry governance; Statnett transmission with 17 cross-border electricity interconnections including North Sea Link, NordLink, and NorNed; Gassco continental gas pipeline infrastructure and European receiving-terminal integration; NSM, NCSC Norway, NorCERT, DSB, and Nødnett responder-network preparedness; and EEA, Schengen, and Nordic Council institutional cooperation. The signals layer reflects a multi-layer national coordination environment anchored in digital governance, settlement modernization, transmission interconnection, continental gas-delivery continuity, research-network federation, exchange neutrality, and civil-preparedness coordination embedded within EEA and Nordic institutional frameworks.
