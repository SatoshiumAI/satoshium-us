## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records the Netherlands as a Rotterdam-centered maritime gateway, an Amsterdam-centered exchange and colocation environment, a Brainport Eindhoven semiconductor-equipment anchor region, and a Hague-centered international legal institutional host platform, with primary coordination cities of Rotterdam, Amsterdam, The Hague, and Veldhoven, aligned across the Rhine–Alpine, North Sea–Mediterranean, and North Sea–Baltic TEN-T corridors and the Benelux coordination framework. The evidence layer documents Rotterdam maritime gateway continuity through the Port of Rotterdam Authority and Schiphol cargo activity; AMS-IX exchange-layer interconnection and Amsterdam-area colocation including Equinix AM-series and Google Cloud Eemshaven regional presence; ASML semiconductor-equipment infrastructure concentration and Brainport Eindhoven cluster participation within European Chips Act alignment; DigiD and Logius digital identity infrastructure with eIDAS cross-border interoperability; DNB, AFM, and ECB supervisory participation; TenneT cross-border grid coordination and Hydrogen Network Netherlands energy-transition deployment; Delta Programme climate-adaptation governance; and international legal-institution hosting through the International Court of Justice, International Criminal Court, Permanent Court of Arbitration, Europol, and Eurojust. The signals layer reflects multi-node coordination spanning maritime logistics, neutral interconnection density, semiconductor-equipment concentration, legal-hosting persistence, and corridor-linked logistics integration embedded within EU and Benelux institutional frameworks.
