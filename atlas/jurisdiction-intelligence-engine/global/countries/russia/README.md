## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Russia as a Moscow-centered administrative coordination jurisdiction with Saint Petersburg as a documented secondary concentration node and distributed federal-subject territorial continuity across a large continental extent, combining digital-government service delivery, central-bank settlement and payment-system governance, telecommunications and exchange-layer infrastructure, research-network federation, energy-grid and pipeline coordination, aviation and maritime logistics, and cybersecurity management under bounded observability conditions and with extra sensitivity handling applied throughout. The evidence layer documents Gosuslugi portal and digital-government infrastructure; Bank of Russia RTGS and payment-system settlement governance; RNETS and telecommunications infrastructure coordination; RIPN .ru registry and RU-IX and MSK-IX exchange-layer infrastructure; RUNNet Russian University Network research-network federation; Rosseti and FGC UES national electricity-grid coordination; Gazprom and Transneft pipeline and energy-transit infrastructure; civil-aviation and maritime-logistics infrastructure; and NKCSS and national cybersecurity coordination structures. The canonical package receives extra sensitivity handling as noted in the build record, with bounded observability applied as a first-class framework, and all geopolitical, military, sanctions, conflict, great-power, energy-coercion, foreign-policy, authoritarian, and strategic-influence framings held strictly inside negation and exclusion boundaries throughout all canonical layers. China and Russia serve as the current structural reference pages for Atlas country template verification.
