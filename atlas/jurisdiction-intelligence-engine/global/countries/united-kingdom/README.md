## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records the United Kingdom within the London Financial Compute Corridor under a Financial Governance Anchor foundation with a Global Financial Infrastructure Coordination Node topology completion role. The evidence layer documents digital asset regulatory structure through the FCA AML/CTF cryptoasset registration regime and HM Treasury's phased perimeter-expansion path; financial market infrastructure through Bank of England supervision of recognised payment systems, central securities depositories, and central counterparties including the RTGS Renewal Programme and CHAPS modernization; the Digital Securities Sandbox as a live regulated environment for digital-securities activity jointly operated by the Bank of England and FCA; AI governance through a sector-based pro-innovation model coordinated by DSIT and the AI Safety Institute; Open Banking and Smart Data infrastructure through Open Banking Limited and the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025; a national compute investment framework through the UK Compute Roadmap; and international financial governance participation through G7, BIS Innovation Hub London Centre, FSB, IOSCO, and OECD.AI structures. The signals layer reflects ten documented signals spanning cryptoasset perimeter expansion, Digital Securities Sandbox deployment, financial market infrastructure modernization, AI governance coordination, regulatory coordination architecture through DRCF, Open Banking and Smart Data infrastructure, compute investment framework, international financial governance participation, financial innovation deployment surfaces, and legal classification infrastructure through the Law Commission digital-assets programme.
