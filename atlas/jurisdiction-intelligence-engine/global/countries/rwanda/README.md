## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Rwanda as a Kigali-centered East African coordination jurisdiction combining digital-government service delivery, central-bank payment settlement, telecommunications governance, electricity grid coordination, aviation logistics, research-network participation, and civil-coordination management under bounded observability conditions. The evidence layer documents Irembo citizen-service platform and RDB Rwanda Development Board digital-governance and service-delivery infrastructure; National Bank of Rwanda BNR and BKOFR payments settlement infrastructure; RURA Rwanda Utilities Regulatory Authority telecommunications governance; Rwanda Energy Group REG and EUCL electricity distribution and grid coordination; Kigali International Airport and RwandAir aviation infrastructure; MINICT Ministry of ICT and Innovation digital-policy coordination; and research-network and cybersecurity coordination structures. The canonical package records Rwanda as a compact city-state-like governance environment where Kigali concentration is the dominant structural pattern, with bounded observability applied as a first-class framework across evidence layers where documentation depth is limited. All economic-miracle, investment-destination, genocide-recovery, political-model, regional-tech-hub, and continental-governance-influence framings are held strictly inside negation and exclusion boundaries.
