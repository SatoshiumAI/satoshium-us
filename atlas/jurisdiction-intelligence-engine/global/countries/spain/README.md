## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Spain as a Madrid-centered administrative concentration jurisdiction with multi-node logistics continuity, Atlantic and Mediterranean corridor participation, Iberian interoperability, EU payment and settlement integration, energy interconnection continuity, shared-service administration, RedIRIS research-network federation, and mainland-plus-island territorial continuity. The evidence layer documents Red SARA shared government network with Cl@ve identity platform including PIN, Permanente, Mobile, and eIDAS node, DNIe, @firma, Valide, and Autentica; Banco de España participation in TARGET and TIPS; ADIF rail network of 11,672 km and 1,448 stations and 45 freight terminals with DaVinci traffic-management system; Aena 46-airport and 2-heliport network; Puertos del Estado with 46 ports and 28 port authorities; Atlantic Corridor linking Irun and Hendaye to Portugal with branches via Madrid and Algeciras and Mediterranean Corridor linking Portbou and Cerbère to Algeciras and Seville; Red Eléctrica electricity transmission with France, Portugal, and Morocco interconnections and Bay of Biscay link; Red.es digital-administration coordination, .es registry, and 5G deployment; RedIRIS and RedIRIS Nova 100 research-network infrastructure, MareNostrum 5 at BSC-CNS, and eduroam; INCIBE-CERT and CCN-CERT national cybersecurity coordination; and PERTE Chip semiconductor policy and IMB-CNM-CSIC research. The canonical package records Spain as operating as a layered multi-node coordination environment rather than a single-corridor or single-node system.
