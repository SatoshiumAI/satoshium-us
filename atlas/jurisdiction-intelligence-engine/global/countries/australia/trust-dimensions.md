## Institutional Continuity Dimension

- The source layers indicate institutional continuity supported by constitutional federal structures, separated powers, judicial independence protections, standing national court institutions, and durable multi-level governance systems.
- Australia’s federal constitutional structure and federation of states and self-governing territories indicate continuity through a durable multi-level national governance system rather than a single centralized administrative authority.
- The documented separation of legislative, executive, and judicial powers indicates continuity through formal checks and balances embedded in the constitutional order.
- Judicial independence protections, including removal only for proved misbehavior or incapacity and protection against remuneration reduction while in office, indicate continuity of court independence from ordinary political interference.
- The High Court’s role as final court of appeal and the Chapter III court structure indicate standing national judicial continuity through constitutionally anchored institutions.
- The documented trust characteristic is institutional continuity carried through constitutional federal structures, separated powers, judicial independence protections, and standing national court institutions.

## Privacy and Data Governance Dimension

- The source layers indicate national privacy-governance continuity supported by statutory privacy obligations, principles-based governance systems, accountability mechanisms, and mandatory breach-notification structures.
- The Privacy Act 1988 indicates continuity through a statute-backed privacy framework governing how Australian Government agencies and covered organizations handle personal information.
- The Australian Privacy Principles indicate continuity through a principles-based and technology-neutral governance structure spanning collection, use, disclosure, accountability, integrity, correction, and access rights.
- The principles-based and technology-neutral character of the APP framework indicates a privacy-governance environment designed to remain durable across changing technical conditions rather than being tied to narrow technology-specific rules.
- The Notifiable Data Breaches scheme indicates continuity through a formal accountability and notification structure requiring disclosure to affected individuals and the OAIC where serious harm is likely.
- The documented trust characteristic is national privacy-governance continuity supported by statutory privacy obligations, principles-based governance systems, accountability mechanisms, and mandatory breach-notification structures.
