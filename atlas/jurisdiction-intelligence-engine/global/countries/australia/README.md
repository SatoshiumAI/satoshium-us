## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Australia as a Canberra-, Sydney-, Melbourne-, and Perth-centered Indo-Pacific federal infrastructure jurisdiction combining research-network federation, submarine-connectivity governance, cyber-governance coordination, digital-asset compliance structures, privacy and institutional continuity systems, and regional institutional integration, spanning Indo-Pacific submarine connectivity, federal digital governance and privacy, research-network federation, critical-infrastructure cyber governance, commercial digital infrastructure integration, payment modernization and financial oversight, federated energy coordination, and international institutional integration frameworks. The evidence layer documents AI Ethics Principles, the Voluntary AI Safety Standard, the APS AI Plan 2025, and AS ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF alignment as AI governance surfaces; the Privacy Act 1988 with Australian Privacy Principles and Notifiable Data Breaches scheme; a split digital-asset oversight layer through ASIC INFO 225 for securities obligations and AUSTRAC AML/CTF-linked registration for digital currency exchange providers; NCI Tier-1 compute, NCRIS coordination, Pawsey supercomputing, CSIRO National AI Centre, and AARNet research-network federation; ACMA carrier governance with submarine cable protection zones; AEMC, AEMO, and AER federated energy coordination across the National Electricity Market with separate Western Australia and Northern Territory systems; the Security of Critical Infrastructure Act 2018 with Systems of National Significance and ASD-linked enhanced cyber obligations; commercially integrated hyperscale and colocation environments; and OECD, Australia-Singapore Digital Economy Agreement, Five Eyes, and AUKUS institutional embedding under federal constitutional governance with separation of powers.
