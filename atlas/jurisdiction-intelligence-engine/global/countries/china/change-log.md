- China was normalized as a Beijing-centered administrative coordination environment with distributed provincial and municipal continuity rather than a single-node national operating model.

- China was normalized as a payment-clearing-settlement interoperability environment through People’s Bank of China coordination, named payment rails, clearing continuity, settlement continuity, and cross-border payment visibility where evidenced rather than a fragmented payment model.
- China was normalized as a telecom-domain-connectivity continuity environment through Ministry of Industry and Information Technology continuity, international gateway-exchange continuity, CNNIC continuity, `.cn` and `.中国` continuity, and internet-resource continuity rather than a purely operator-defined communications environment.
- China was normalized as an electricity-grid continuity environment through State Grid continuity, China Southern Power Grid continuity, dispatch continuity, transmission continuity, and electricity-service continuity rather than a detached electricity model.
- China was normalized as a national multimodal transportation continuity environment through Ministry of Transport continuity, railway continuity, highway continuity, waterway continuity, airport continuity where publicly visible, postal continuity, and transport-system coordination rather than isolated modal systems.
- China was normalized as a distributed aviation continuity environment through Civil Aviation Administration of China continuity, airport-network continuity, aviation-regulation continuity, and aviation-service continuity rather than a single-airport model.
- China was normalized as a distributed maritime continuity environment through port continuity, maritime-network continuity, throughput continuity, and outward-facing route continuity where evidenced rather than one-port concentration.
- China was normalized as an emergency-coordination continuity environment through Ministry of Emergency Management continuity, monitoring continuity, alert continuity, emergency-response continuity, and central-local response continuity rather than event-only emergency visibility.
- China was normalized as a cyber/data governance continuity environment through the Data Security Law, Cyberspace Administration of China continuity, network-data regulation continuity, and public governance-surface continuity rather than unsupported hidden-capability interpretation.
- China was normalized as a research-network-supported continuity environment through CERNET continuity, regional-center continuity, campus-network continuity, distributed academic continuity, and international-link continuity where evidenced rather than a fully exposed academic-network topology.
- China was normalized as a regional and international interconnection continuity environment through CIPS continuity, gateway-exchange continuity, research-network links, maritime-route continuity where evidenced, immigration-service reach, and electricity interconnection where evidenced rather than geopolitical framing.
- China was normalized as a bounded-observability environment in which continuity is visible across multiple public layers while deeper private-network, cyber-operational, commercial-topology, infrastructure-dependency, telecom/private-peering, payment-provider topology, port-terminal, airport-operational, and provincial or local service detail remains only partly visible.
