- Public visibility of provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities directly under the central government, and special administrative regions supports a trust dimension of multi-level administrative continuity rather than a single-node administrative model.
- The coexistence of central ministries with distributed territorial units supports a trust dimension of public-service continuity beyond the capital node rather than administrative presence limited to Beijing institutions alone.
- The overall administrative pattern supports Beijing-centered administrative coordination with distributed provincial and municipal continuity.
- These characteristics remain descriptive only and do not imply governance scoring, political interpretation, or party-state narrative.
