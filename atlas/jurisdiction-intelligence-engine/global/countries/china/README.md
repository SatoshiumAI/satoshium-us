## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records China as a Beijing-centered federal-administrative coordination jurisdiction with Shanghai as a documented financial and commercial concentration node and Shenzhen as a technology-sector concentration node, combining digital-government infrastructure, central-bank settlement and payment-system governance, telecommunications and exchange-layer infrastructure, research-network federation, energy-grid coordination, aviation and maritime logistics, and cybersecurity management under bounded observability conditions. The evidence layer documents national digital-government platform and e-government service infrastructure; People's Bank of China PBOC CIPS Cross-Border Interbank Payment System and CNAPS China National Advanced Payment System settlement infrastructure; MIIT Ministry of Industry and Information Technology telecommunications governance; CNNIC China Internet Network Information Center .cn registry and CNIX exchange-layer infrastructure; CERNET China Education and Research Network research-network federation with GÉANT and regional connectivity; national electricity grid coordination spanning State Grid Corporation and Southern Power Grid; civil-aviation and maritime-logistics infrastructure across major ports and airports; and CNCERT national cybersecurity coordination. The canonical package records China under bounded observability throughout, with all geopolitical, great-power, surveillance, censorship, party-state, manufacturing-export, Belt-and-Road, technology-superpower, economic-power, logistics-power, and strategic-influence framings held strictly inside negation and exclusion boundaries — every such reference appears only inside exclusion clauses, with the surveillance-censorship-geopolitical-party-state-economic-power inference prohibition stated across all canonical layers. China and Russia serve as the current structural reference pages for Atlas country template verification.
