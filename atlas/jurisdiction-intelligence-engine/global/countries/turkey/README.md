## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Turkey as an Ankara-centered administrative coordination jurisdiction with Istanbul as a commercial and financial concentration node, operating as a layered dual-concentration coordination environment spanning digital-government interoperability, central-bank settlement and payment modernization, rail and aviation infrastructure, energy transmission and transit, research-network federation, telecommunications coordination, and cybersecurity management. The evidence layer documents e-Devlet digital-government platform, BTK telecommunications regulatory authority, and TRABİS domain-registry infrastructure; Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye EFT and FAST 7/24 instant-payment settlement systems; TCDD rail network and TCDD Taşımacılık operations with Marmaray Bosphorus crossing rendered as documented infrastructure; KGM road network of 68,517 km and 3,796 km of motorways and 28,297 km of divided roads as of 1 January 2026; DHMİ airport authority with Istanbul Airport, Sabiha Gökçen, and Esenboğa coordination; Mersin port; TEİAŞ electricity transmission with 817 HV substations and 15 cross-border interconnection lines as of April 2026; BOTAŞ gas transmission with TANAP and TurkStream and Tuz Gölü and Silivri and Marmara Ereğlisi and Dörtyol and Blue Stream and Baku-Tbilisi-Erzurum and Shah Deniz interfaces; TÜBİTAK ULAKBİM and ULAKNET research-network infrastructure with GÉANT connectivity; and AFAD and Disaster and Emergency Coordination Board and USOM coordination. The canonical package applies anti-symbolic-geography discipline throughout, recording Bosphorus crossing infrastructure without East-West bridge, Europe-Asia gateway, or bridge-between-continents framing; Turkey operates as a layered dual-concentration coordination environment rather than a single-corridor or single-node system.
