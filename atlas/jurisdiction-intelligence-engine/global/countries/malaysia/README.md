## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Malaysia as a Klang Valley-centered dual-region continuity jurisdiction whose national linkage spans Peninsular Malaysia and East Malaysia through maritime, aviation, digital, administrative, payment, and segmented energy systems rather than a continuous land corridor, spanning Klang Valley-centered concentration, Peninsular and East Malaysia dual-region continuity, maritime and aviation territorial linkage, federal and state port governance, PayNet and DuitNow payment interoperability, MyIX exchange and cloud-region coupling, segmented energy and utility coordination, portal-centered administration and identity, public warning and flood monitoring, and ASEAN and regional interoperability frameworks. Primary coordination cities are Kuala Lumpur and Klang Valley, Kuching, and Kota Kinabalu. The evidence layer documents seven major Federal ports including Port Klang, Port of Tanjung Pelepas, Johor Port, Kuantan, Penang, Bintulu, and Kemaman alongside separate Sabah and Sarawak state port jurisdictions; six international airports, 16 domestic airports, and 18 STOLports with Klang Valley Integrated Transit System KLIA Ekspres and KTM Komuter Port Klang line; PayNet national payment infrastructure with DuitNow instant transfers, DuitNow QR cross-border interoperability, and the National Addressing Database proxy management; JENDELA plan and Digital Nasional Berhad wholesale 5G coordination with MyIX domestic exchange, Telekom Malaysia Morib cable landing station, and AWS Malaysia Region; MyGovernment portal and MyDigital ID with NACSA, NC4, and CyberSecurity Malaysia cyber-governance; TNB National Grid Peninsular Malaysia transmission alongside separate SESB Sabah and Sarawak Energy arrangements with cross-border electricity links to Thailand and Singapore and PETRONAS Gas Peninsular pipeline network; and Public InfoBanjir and Portal Bencana public warning continuity. The package records segmented energy governance across three distinct territorial utility systems as a canonical structural anchor.
