## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Qatar as a Doha-centered centralized state-coordinated infrastructure jurisdiction anchored in LNG production and export, supported by digital-government and smart-infrastructure systems, and embedded across global energy, logistics, telecommunications, data, and research networks, spanning GCC energy and LNG export coordination, global LNG market integration, GCC and global financial system integration, regional telecommunications and international connectivity, global logistics and aviation connectivity, national data and smart infrastructure, and GCC and global cybersecurity coordination frameworks. The evidence layer documents MCIT, Digital Agenda 2030, TASMU Smart Qatar, Hukoomi, Tawtheeq, and Lusail smart-city digital-governance coordination; QCB, QPS RTGS, QMP mobile payment, and FAWRAN instant payment settlement infrastructure; MCIT Data Management Policy, TASMU Platform, MEEZA data-center infrastructure, and in-country Google Cloud and Microsoft cloud-region infrastructure; Qatar Foundation, HBKU, and QCRI research-compute environments; QIXP internet exchange and CRA-governed .qa registry; QNBN fiber backbone with Ooredoo and Vodafone Qatar 5G coverage; QatarEnergy LNG infrastructure, North Field expansion, and Kahramaa utility coordination; Hamad Port, Hamad International Airport, and Qatar Airways Cargo logistics infrastructure; and NCSA and NIA cybersecurity assurance and certification. The signals layer reflects centralized state-coordinated continuity across LNG production, digital governance, payments, data systems, telecommunications, logistics, and cybersecurity embedded within wider global energy, cloud, interconnection, logistics, and research networks.
