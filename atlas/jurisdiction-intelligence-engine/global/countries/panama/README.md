## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Panama as a Panama City-centered coordination jurisdiction with canal-administered operational continuity as the signature topology framework, combining dual-coast maritime infrastructure, central-bank settlement governance, telecommunications coordination, energy grid integration, aviation logistics, research-network participation, and regional integration interfaces, with the heaviest anti-mythology discipline of any jurisdiction in the program. The evidence layer documents the Panama Canal Authority ACP lock system across Gatun, Pedro Miguel, and Miraflores locks with notices-to-shipping, reservation, maintenance, and customer-service operational continuity rendered strictly as administered infrastructure; AMP-administered Atlantic maritime terminals at Manzanillo, Cristóbal-Panamá, Colon Container Terminal, and Panama Colon Container Port under construction; Pacific terminals at Balboa and PSA Panamá; SBP banking system and TELERED and Latin Clear payment and securities settlement infrastructure; PANNet government network and Universidad Tecnológica de Panamá UTP research-network participation; Panama-Costa Rica 230 kV interconnection between Río Claro and Progreso as the documented regional electrical interface; and Tocumen International Airport logistics infrastructure. The canonical package applies 27 explicit negation lines excluding canal-power, geopolitical-chokepoint, trade-dominance, logistics-superpower, gateway-state, tax-haven, financial-secrecy, bridge-of-the-world, global-crossroads, symbolic-geography, and strategic-trade-route classifications — the most heavily-negated page in the Atlas program — while preserving canal-administered operational continuity as a canonical structural anchor.
