## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Singapore within the Indo-Pacific Digital Governance and Interconnection Corridor as a sovereign city-state jurisdiction classified as an Indo-Pacific digital governance coordination hub with dense exchange-layer interconnection infrastructure, structured tokenisation supervision continuity, national AI governance experimentation capability, and energy-constrained compute-hosting expansion conditions. The evidence layer documents AI governance through Smart Nation's National AI Strategy 2.0, IMDA's A.I. Verify framework and AI Verify Foundation, and PDPC generative AI guidelines; privacy and data governance through the Personal Data Protection Act and PDPC supervision; digital asset and financial technology oversight through the Payment Services Act 2019 and its 2024 amendments expanding DPT service regulation, the MAS stablecoin framework, and Project Guardian cross-border tokenisation sandbox; compute and digital infrastructure through GovTech's Smart Nation programme and the Green DC Roadmap targeting at least 300 MW of additional data-centre capacity; telecommunications and submarine cable infrastructure through IMDA licensing, SGIX neutral exchange, and submarine cable permitting with a target to double landings within ten years; energy and sustainability constraints through Singapore's documented approximately 95 percent reliance on natural gas and land constraints limiting renewable deployment; and international governance participation through ASEAN Working Group on AI Governance chaired by IMDA, the Digital Economy Partnership Agreement, and Singapore–US AI governance framework crosswalk. The canonical metadata records Singapore as explicitly not classified as a semiconductor fabrication anchor, Five Eyes corridor, continental-scale hyperscale energy-export compute corridor, or supranational regulatory bloc jurisdiction.
