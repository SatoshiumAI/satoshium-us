## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Denmark as a Copenhagen-centered digital-governance coordination environment, a Danmarks Nationalbank-linked TARGET-integrated settlement participant, a DeiC-linked research-network federation surface, a DIX-centered exchange-layer neutrality environment, an Energinet-linked offshore-energy and grid-interconnection coordination environment, and a CFCS-linked cybersecurity coordination jurisdiction, aligned across Nordic electricity-market integration, North Sea offshore energy coordination, euro-area settlement integration through TARGET-linked participation, EU digital identity interoperability, NORDUnet research network cooperation, and Baltic-German energy interconnection frameworks. The evidence layer documents the Agency for Digital Government, borger.dk, MitID, NemLog-in, Digital Post, and Danish eID Gateway digital-governance and identity-interoperability infrastructure; Danmarks Nationalbank TARGET DKK, T2, TIPS, T2S settlement integration, fixed exchange rate linkage to euro-area systems, and digital euro monitoring; DeiC, Danish Research Network dual-router backbone, NORDUnet participation, EuroHPC access via LUMI, and Gefion AI compute coordination through DCAI; DIX exchange-layer neutrality and Punktum dk and DIFO .dk registry governance; Energinet transmission coordination, Bornholm Energy Island, 50Hertz cooperation, Nordic aFRR balancing, and DataHub electricity-system coordination; and CFCS national cybersecurity coordination with integrated GovCERT, MILCERT, and DKCERT research-network cybersecurity. The signals layer reflects a multi-layer national coordination environment spanning digital governance, pan-European settlement integration, research-network federation, exchange neutrality, offshore-energy and grid interconnection, and cybersecurity coordination embedded within EU, Nordic, and Baltic institutional frameworks.
