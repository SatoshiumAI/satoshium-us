## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Iceland as a Reykjavík- and Keflavík-centered North Atlantic connectivity and renewable-energy infrastructure jurisdiction combining submarine-cable continuity, distributed digital governance, renewable-powered digital infrastructure, domestic payment modernization, research-network participation, cybersecurity coordination, and Nordic and European institutional integration, spanning North Atlantic submarine connectivity, renewable-energy digital infrastructure, distributed digital-government and interoperability, Nordic and European institutional integration, research-network federation, domestic payment modernization and oversight, cybersecurity and digital-resilience coordination, and transatlantic aviation and connectivity frameworks. The evidence layer documents Digital Iceland, Ísland.is, My Pages, Digital Mailbox, Auðkenni, passkey-enabled identity systems, and Straumurinn X-Road distributed inter-agency data exchange digital-governance coordination; Central Bank of Iceland PFMI-aligned oversight with RTGS, EXP retail settlement, and payment-request modernization within the Icelandic-króna environment; Verne and the DataCenter Iceland ecosystem with renewable-powered compute hosting; Farice submarine systems including FARICE-1, DANICE, IRIS, and Greenland Connect-linked capacity; Míla backbone with Síminn, Nova, and Vodafone Iceland operator continuity and 5G deployment; ISNIC .is registry governance and RIX multi-site internet exchange; RHnet research network with NORDUnet integration; Landsvirkjun hydroelectric and geothermal generation with Landsnet transmission coordination; Keflavík cargo and east-west aviation continuity; NCC-IS cybersecurity coordination; and EEA and EFTA, NIIS, and North Atlantic institutional integration.
