# Czech Republic

## Atlas Jurisdiction Package

Czech Republic is a jurisdiction package within the Satoshium Atlas jurisdiction intelligence framework.

This package provides a structured jurisdiction record designed to support research, analysis, orientation, comparison, discovery, and future interoperability across the broader Atlas ecosystem.

---

## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records the Czech Republic as a Prague-centered coordination jurisdiction operating as a layered Central European coordination environment spanning digital-government interoperability, EU payment and settlement integration, rail and road infrastructure, energy transmission, research-network federation, internet exchange infrastructure, and cybersecurity coordination. The evidence layer documents Czech national digital-government infrastructure including the Citizen Portal, databox secure-messaging system, mojeID, and eObčanka national ID card with eIDAS interoperability; Czech National Bank participation in Eurosystem TARGET2 and TIPS settlement; Správa železnic rail infrastructure and Czech national highway network including the D1 motorway completed December 2022; Václav Havel Airport Prague with 16,353,522 passengers and 134,609 movements documented; ČEPS electricity transmission system with 4,188 km of 400 kV lines, 1,687 km of 220 kV lines, 45 substations, and 82 transformers; NET4GAS gas transmission network with more than 4,000 km of pipelines and 100 transfer stations; NIX.CZ neutral internet exchange with 1.5 Tbps DNS capacity; CESNET research-network infrastructure with GÉANT connectivity, Brno and Ostrava regional nodes, and Lanžhot cross-border connectivity; and NÚKIB national cybersecurity authority and CERTIS coordination. The canonical package records key figures including EU membership since 1 May 2004 and Schengen since 21 December 2007, and records the Czech Republic as operating as a layered Central European coordination environment rather than a single-corridor or single-node system.
