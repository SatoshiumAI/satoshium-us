## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Hong Kong as a dense urban district-structured coordination jurisdiction within the Pearl River Delta combining financial-market infrastructure, digital-government service delivery, payment and settlement systems, telecommunications and exchange-layer infrastructure, research-network participation, aviation and maritime logistics, and cybersecurity coordination under bounded observability conditions reflecting Hong Kong's distinctive constitutional status as a Special Administrative Region. The evidence layer documents SFC Securities and Futures Commission financial-market regulatory infrastructure; HKEX Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing financial-market exchange infrastructure; HKMA Hong Kong Monetary Authority and CHATS RTGS and FPS Faster Payment System settlement systems; government digital-service and e-government platforms; HKIX Hong Kong Internet Exchange exchange-layer infrastructure; HKJC and university and research-network participation; AMSRO and aviation coordination; Port of Hong Kong maritime logistics infrastructure; and cybersecurity coordination structures. The canonical package records Hong Kong under bounded observability for constitutional-status context throughout, holding all geopolitical, sovereignty, political-transition, financial-center-ranking, and strategic-location framings strictly inside negation and exclusion boundaries while preserving documented institutional and infrastructure surfaces as the canonical evidence record.
