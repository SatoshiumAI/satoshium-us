---

## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Italy as a Rome-centered administrative coordination jurisdiction with Milan as a secondary financial and exchange-layer node, operating as a layered multi-node coordination environment spanning digital-government interoperability, EU payment and settlement integration, rail and road infrastructure, port logistics, energy transmission, research-network federation, and cybersecurity coordination. The evidence layer documents SPID digital identity, CIE electronic identity card, pagoPA payment platform, and eIDAS interoperability digital-governance infrastructure; Banca d'Italia participation in Eurosystem TARGET2 and TIPS and T2S settlement; RFI rail network and ANAS road authority; port infrastructure including Genoa, Trieste, and Gioia Tauro within TEN-T Mediterranean Corridor and Rhine-Alpine Corridor alignment; Terna electricity transmission and Snam gas infrastructure with cross-border interconnections; MIX-IT neutral internet exchange in Milan; GARR research-network infrastructure with GÉANT connectivity; and national cybersecurity coordination through the Agenzia per la Cybersicurezza Nazionale. The canonical package records Italy as operating as a layered multi-node coordination environment rather than a single-corridor or single-node system, without assigning routing authority, readiness tiers, or comparative status.
