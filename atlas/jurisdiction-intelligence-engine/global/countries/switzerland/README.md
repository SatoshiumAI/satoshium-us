## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Switzerland within the Alpine Crypto-Legal Corridor under a Crypto-Legal Infrastructure Governance foundation with a Digital-Asset Regulatory Precedent Anchor topology completion role. The evidence layer documents crypto-legal infrastructure through FINMA DLT trading facility licensing, SIX Digital Exchange and BX Digital approvals, and Project Helvetia Phase III Swiss-franc wholesale CBDC settlement experimentation; international financial governance hosting through BIS head-office presence in Basel, Basel Committee supervisory cooperation, and FSB Secretariat presence hosted by the BIS; research and high-performance compute through ETH AI Center, EPFL participation in the Swiss National AI Institute, and CSCS Alps infrastructure distributed across Lugano, Lausanne, Villigen, and Bologna supporting AI and research virtual clusters; energy and grid interconnection through Swissgrid transmission ownership and cross-border coordination with European partners; neutral exchange and connectivity through SwissIX non-profit neutral exchange infrastructure; trans-Alpine infrastructure continuity through NRLA Gotthard, Lötschberg, and Ceneri base-tunnel freight rail; and data-governance legal structure through the FADP and FDPIC supervisory authority. The canonical metadata records structural absences for nationwide hyperscale concentration, semiconductor fabrication concentration, supranational legislative alignment authority, and centralized national governance uniformity.
