## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records El Salvador as a San Salvador-centered Central American coordination jurisdiction combining public-administration digitization, central-bank settlement governance, telecommunications coordination, electricity grid integration, aviation and port logistics, research-network participation, civil-protection coordination, and regional integration interfaces, operating under bounded observability with extensive Bitcoin-neutrality guardrails applied as a canonical evidence-method discipline throughout the package. The evidence layer documents government digital-service platforms and public-administration digitization infrastructure; Banco Central de Reserva BCR LBTR RTGS settlement system as the documented payment surface — with Bitcoin appearing in the canonical package only in negation phrases, never promotionally, as a canonical evidence boundary; SIEPAC Central American Electrical Interconnection System participation; CEPA multi-modal port and airport management authority; RAICES residency and migration systems; and ANTEL and Siget telecommunications governance. The canonical package applies the highest density of Bitcoin-neutrality guardrails of any jurisdiction in the program, with all Bitcoin-related claims held strictly to documented negation boundaries rather than promotional or institutional-affirmation framing, preserving BCR's LBTR system as the documented payment-settlement surface.
