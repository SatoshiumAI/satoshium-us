## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Chile as a Santiago- and San Antonio-centered Pacific-facing South American infrastructure jurisdiction combining digital-government interoperability, payment modernization, scientific-network federation, telecommunications continuity, renewable-energy-linked infrastructure, cybersecurity coordination, and Pacific institutional integration, spanning Pacific connectivity and telecommunications, digital governance and interoperability, research-network federation, cybersecurity and digital resilience, renewable-energy and grid coordination, scientific compute and academic infrastructure, logistics and Pacific trade connectivity, and global digital infrastructure integration frameworks. The evidence layer documents Gobierno Digital, ClaveÚnica, CasillaÚnica, DocDigital, CPAT, PISEE 1 and PISEE 2 interoperability systems under Law No. 21.180 digital-governance coordination; Banco Central de Chile LBTR RTGS and high-value settlement governance; DatosGob open data, Plan Nacional de Data Centers, Google Cloud Chile region, and AWS Chile renewable-energy-linked hosting infrastructure; REUNA, RedCLARA, AmLight, PIT Chile, NLHPC, and the Guacolda-Leftraru Epu scientific-compute environment; NIC Chile, .CL registry, and DNSSEC namespace governance; SUBTEL, Fibra Óptica Nacional, Fibra Óptica Austral, national fiber expansion, 5G deployment, and Pacific-facing submarine connectivity; the Coordinador Eléctrico Nacional and Sistema Eléctrico Nacional with diversified renewable-energy integration; San Antonio maritime cargo and Santiago International Airport with the FedEx Latin American logistics-hub environment; CSIRT Nacional and the Agencia Nacional de Ciberseguridad under Law No. 21.663; and Pacific Alliance, Asia-Pacific, RedCLARA and AmLight, and OECD benchmark-reference institutional integration.
