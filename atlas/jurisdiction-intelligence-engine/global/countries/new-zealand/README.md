## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records New Zealand as a Wellington-centered administrative coordination jurisdiction with Auckland as a commercial and population concentration node and distributed regional-council and territorial-authority continuity across 11 regional councils and 67 territorial authorities and Auckland Council local boards, combining digital-government interoperability, central-bank payment-settlement governance, telecommunications and exchange-layer infrastructure, research-network federation, electricity grid coordination, inter-island logistics, aviation, maritime, and emergency-management continuity. The evidence layer documents Public Service Commission and DIA and Govt.nz digital-government coordination; RealMe identity and citizenship and Births Deaths and Marriages and passports identity infrastructure; RBNZ Reserve Bank of New Zealand ESAS and Payments NZ BECS and CECS and HVCS and SBI settlement systems; Commerce Commission and Chorus and Northpower and Enable and Tuatahi fiber operators and InternetNZ .nz registry and APE and WIX exchange infrastructure telecommunications governance; Transpower National Grid and System Operator and Electricity Authority grid coordination; NZTA New Zealand Transport Agency and KiwiRail and Interislander Cook Strait ferry inter-island transport continuity; CAA Civil Aviation Authority and Airways New Zealand and Auckland and Wellington and Christchurch and Queenstown airports aviation infrastructure; Maritime NZ and Ports of Auckland, Tauranga, CentrePort, Lyttelton, and Port Otago maritime logistics; NEMA and NCMC and CIMS and GeoNet and MetService and Emergency Mobile Alert and 16 Civil Defence Emergency Management groups emergency coordination; and NCSC and CERT NZ and Privacy Commissioner and REANNZ and Tuakiri with 120-plus network members research and cybersecurity coordination. The canonical package records New Zealand as not a tourism classification, not a clean-green agricultural-export classification, not a disaster-resilience-defined classification, and not a geopolitical-strategic-location classification.
