# Brazil - Trust Dimensions

Trust derivation constraint: trust dimensions derive strictly from `evidence.md` and `signals.md`. This file does not introduce new infrastructure claims, assign routing authority, assign readiness tiers, rank jurisdictions, or infer deployment eligibility.

## Institutional Continuity Dimension

- The source layers indicate institutional continuity supported through layered federal coordination systems, interoperable public-service infrastructure, central-bank-operated settlement systems, nationally coordinated registry governance, telecommunications regulation, scientific-network federation, energy-grid coordination, and cybersecurity coordination structures rather than fragmented operator-by-operator continuity.
- GOV.BR, Conecta gov.br, and the Infraestrutura Nacional de Dados indicate continuity in federal digital-governance coordination through shared identity, interoperability, and administrative data-exchange systems.
- Banco Central do Brasil indicates continuity in payment-system governance through STR, Pix, SPI, and associated instant-payments regulation inside a central-bank-operated settlement environment.
- NIC.br, Registro.br, IX.br, and the implementation of CGI.br decisions indicate continuity in registry, numbering-resource, and exchange governance through established institutional internet-governance structures.
- RNP and LNCC indicate continuity in scientific-network federation and research-compute coordination through nationwide academic-network systems and a named Tier-0 HPC environment.
- Anatel and Telebras indicate continuity in telecommunications rollout and backbone infrastructure through regulation, 5G deployment, fiber-expansion commitments, and integrated terrestrial and satellite communications systems.
- ONS indicates continuity in energy-grid coordination through operation of the Sistema Interligado Nacional, while CTIR Gov and CERT.br indicate continuity in cyber coordination through federal incident-management and national internet-security capability structures.
- MERCOSUL participation and wider international logistics, research-network, and energy-interchange attachment indicate that Brazil’s institutional continuity also operates within Latin American and globally connected frameworks rather than within a closed domestic perimeter.
- The documented trust characteristic is institutional continuity carried through layered federal coordination mechanisms, nationally administered infrastructure institutions, and repeated attachment to regional and global systems.

## Digital Governance and Identity Dimension

- The source layers indicate digital-governance continuity supported through nationally coordinated identity systems, legal interoperability guarantees, electronic-signature infrastructure, and shared administrative data-exchange environments.
- GOV.BR and Conta GOV.BR indicate continuity through a common federal identity and authenticated service-access layer rather than fragmented agency-specific credential environments.
- The Carteira de Identidade Nacional indicates continuity through a national identification model using CPF as a unique standard and extending digital identity into an aligned public-administration framework.
- Conecta gov.br indicates continuity through automatic and secure interoperability between government systems, reducing repeated citizen-side document resubmission and linking identity to shared service execution.
- The Infraestrutura Nacional de Dados indicates continuity through a formal framework of rules, policies, standards, tools, and information assets governing strategic federal data exchange.
- GOV.BR electronic-signature services indicate continuity between digital identity, authenticated access, and document execution inside the same public digital-service environment.
- The documented trust characteristic is continuity of digital governance and identity coordination through nationally coordinated identity systems, legal interoperability guarantees, electronic-signature functionality, and shared administrative exchange environments.

## Financial Infrastructure and Settlement Dimension

- The source layers indicate payment-system continuity supported through RTGS settlement infrastructure, central-bank-operated instant-payments systems, layered retail-payment modernization mechanisms, and nationally coordinated financial interoperability structures.
- Banco Central do Brasil indicates continuity through central-bank leadership over Brazil’s payment and settlement environment rather than a decentralized settlement model.
- STR indicates continuity through a named RTGS infrastructure anchoring large-value funds-transfer settlement.
- Pix and SPI indicate continuity through central-bank-operated instant-payments systems supporting nationally integrated retail-payment modernization.
- The coexistence of STR, Pix, SPI, and Conta PI regulation indicates layered settlement and payment modernization rather than a single undifferentiated payment mechanism.
- The source layers more directly support domestic settlement and instant-payments continuity than broader claims about international settlement centrality beyond the Banco Central do Brasil perimeter.
- The documented trust characteristic is continuity of domestic payment and settlement governance under central-bank coordination, carried through RTGS and instant-payments infrastructure and bounded by the national scope of the documented financial systems.

## Data Infrastructure and Digital Continuity Dimension

- The source layers indicate public-sector digital continuity supported through interoperable federal systems, sovereign-administration data infrastructure, nationally coordinated public-service platforms, and sector-specific exchange environments.
- Conecta gov.br and the Infraestrutura Nacional de Dados indicate continuity through interoperable federal systems and shared administrative data-exchange mechanisms rather than isolated public-sector data silos.
- The Nuvem de Governo, operated with Serpro and Dataprev under direct federal responsibility and with data remaining in national territory, indicates continuity through sovereign-administration storage, legal conformity, interoperability, and continuity planning functions.
- RNDS indicates continuity through a national health-data interoperability environment linking public and private institutions across states, municipalities, care settings, and systems.
- The Portal Brasileiro de Dados Abertos indicates continuity through a nationally coordinated open-data publication and reuse surface rather than fragmented agency publication channels.
- The coexistence of shared federal interoperability systems, sovereign-administration cloud infrastructure, health-data exchange, and open-data publication indicates a layered public digital-continuity environment rather than a single-platform model.
- The documented trust characteristic is continuity of public-sector digital infrastructure through interoperable federal systems, national administrative data infrastructure, coordinated public-service platforms, and sector-specific exchange environments.

## Research Network and Scientific Compute Dimension

- The source layers indicate scientific-network federation continuity supported through nationwide academic-network interconnection, federated identity environments, international research-network participation, and nationally coordinated HPC infrastructure.
- RNP indicates continuity through PoPs in all 27 states and the Rede Ipê backbone, establishing a nationwide academic-network interconnection layer rather than geographically limited institutional networking.
- CAFe and eduroam Brasil indicate continuity through federated identity and access environments spanning multiple institutions within the research and education system.
- RNP’s documented global integration indicates that Brazil’s scientific-network continuity operates inside wider international research-network environments rather than a closed domestic academic-network perimeter.
- LNCC and Santos Dumont indicate continuity through a named Tier-0 scientific compute environment operating within SINAPAD rather than through only local institutional compute capacity.
- The documented trust characteristic is continuity of scientific-network federation and research-compute coordination through named national network and HPC systems, bounded by the source layers’ narrower sovereign compute evidence beyond the research environment.

## Internet Exchange and Registry Governance Dimension

- The source layers indicate registry and exchange continuity supported through institutionally administered namespace governance, distributed multi-city exchange infrastructure, nationally coordinated numbering-resource administration, and established internet-governance structures.
- NIC.br indicates continuity through administration of .br domain operations, IP-number distribution, ASN registration, and implementation of CGI.br decisions and projects.
- Registro.br indicates continuity through structured domain registration and IP and ASN resource distribution within a dedicated national institutional framework.
- IX.br indicates continuity through distributed multi-city exchange infrastructure rather than a single-node national exchange environment.
- The documented coexistence of NIC.br, Registro.br, IX.br, CERT.br, and CGI.br-linked governance indicates an established internet-governance environment spanning naming, numbering, exchange support, and internet-security capability-building.
- The documented trust characteristic is continuity of registry and exchange governance through institutionally administered namespace stewardship, distributed exchange infrastructure, numbering-resource coordination, and standing internet-governance structures.

## Telecommunications and Digital Connectivity Dimension

- The source layers indicate telecommunications continuity supported through nationwide 5G deployment, public backbone infrastructure, fiber-expansion obligations, integrated terrestrial and satellite communications systems, and coordinated multi-operator telecommunications environments.
- Anatel indicates continuity through national 5G deployment oversight, municipal rollout expansion, and the regulatory framework governing telecommunications modernization.
- The 5G auction’s PAIS-linked obligations indicate continuity through northern fiber-expansion commitments and federal administrative communications buildout rather than a mobile-only infrastructure strategy.
- Telebras indicates continuity through backbone infrastructure spanning more than 30,000 kilometers of optical fiber and through satellite solutions operating in synergy with the national backbone.
- The coexistence of Anatel-regulated 5G deployment, Telebras backbone infrastructure, fiber obligations, and multi-operator market participation indicates a coordinated telecommunications environment rather than a single self-contained operator stack.
- The documented trust characteristic is continuity of telecommunications and digital connectivity through regulated rollout, public backbone systems, integrated terrestrial and satellite infrastructure, and coordinated multi-operator telecommunications development.

- The coexistence of CTIR Gov and CERT.br indicates a dual coordination structure spanning the federal administrative perimeter and the broader Brazilian internet environment.
- The documented trust characteristic is continuity of cybersecurity and digital resilience through federal coordination, national internet-security capability structures, threat-information exchange, and organized cyber-response mechanisms.

## Constraint Boundary Dimension

- The source layers indicate constraint boundaries reflecting a globally integrated infrastructure environment dependent on mixed-provider compute ecosystems, distributed telecommunications environments, limited sovereign-scale compute infrastructure, and constrained evidence for broader global infrastructure centrality beyond the documented institutional perimeter.
- The Nuvem de Governo environment includes solutions from AWS, Google, Huawei, and Oracle, which indicates that part of Brazil’s digital continuity depends on mixed-provider compute ecosystems rather than a wholly sovereign hyperscale stack.
- RNP and LNCC indicate meaningful scientific-network and HPC continuity, but the source layers limit sovereign compute evidence largely to research systems rather than a broad sovereign compute estate.
- Programa Brasil Semicondutores indicates industrial-policy intent, but the source layers do not document a sovereign semiconductor fabrication stack in operation, which bounds industrial and compute autonomy claims.
- IX.br indicates strong distributed exchange continuity, but the source layers do not support broader claims of global exchange centrality beyond the documented national exchange environment.
- Anatel and Telebras indicate strong telecommunications continuity, while the same source layers describe a mixed regulatory, public, and multi-operator environment rather than a single self-contained telecom stack.
- MERCOSUL integration, international energy interchange, global research-network participation, international logistics connectivity, and mixed-provider infrastructure indicate that Brazil’s continuity environment remains structurally attached to cross-border and global systems rather than a fully closed domestic perimeter.
- The documented trust characteristic is continuity within a globally integrated infrastructure environment whose compute, telecommunications, and wider connectivity autonomy remain bounded by mixed-provider dependencies, research-weighted sovereign compute evidence, and limits on broader centrality claims beyond the documented institutional base.

## Trust Dimensions Summary Statement

Brazil is documented as a South American-scale infrastructure jurisdiction combining federal digital-government interoperability, RTGS and instant-payments continuity, internet-exchange and registry governance, scientific-network federation, telecommunications rollout, energy-grid coordination, logistics infrastructure, cyber coordination, and regional institutional integration. The source layers indicate continuity across institutional coordination, digital identity and interoperability, central-bank-led settlement infrastructure, public-sector data systems, research-network federation, registry and exchange governance, telecommunications connectivity, grid coordination, logistics systems, and MERCOSUL-linked global integration without assigning readiness tiers, routing authority, or deployment eligibility.
