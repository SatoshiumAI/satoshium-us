## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Kenya as a Nairobi-centered East African coordination jurisdiction with distributed forty-seven-county territorial continuity, combining digital-government interoperability, central-bank payment-settlement coordination, telecommunications and exchange-layer governance, electricity transmission, aviation and maritime logistics, research-network participation, and cybersecurity coordination under bounded observability conditions. The evidence layer documents eCitizen portal and Huduma service-delivery and State Department coordination with National Registration Bureau and Civil Registration digital-governance infrastructure; Central Bank of Kenya CBK National Payments System with KEPSS RTGS and EAPS and REPSS settlement interoperability; Communications Authority and KeNIC .ke registry and KIXP exchange infrastructure telecommunications governance; KETRACO Kenya Electricity Transmission Company and Kenya Power electricity coordination; Kenya National Highways Authority KeNHA and Kenya Railways SGR Standard Gauge Railway with Nairobi and Naivasha depot infrastructure; Kenya Civil Aviation Authority KCAA and Kenya Airports Authority KAA with JKIA Jomo Kenyatta International Airport; Kenya Ports Authority KPA and Port of Mombasa maritime logistics; National Disaster Operations Centre NDOC; KE-CIRT/CC Kenya Computer Incident Response Team; and KENET Kenya Education Network with eduroam research-network participation. The canonical package holds all fintech, mobile-money, Silicon Savannah, East Africa leadership, regional-power, gateway-to-Africa, and innovation-hub framings strictly inside negation and exclusion boundaries.
