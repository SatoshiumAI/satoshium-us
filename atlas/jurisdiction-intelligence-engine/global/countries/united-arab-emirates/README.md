## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records the United Arab Emirates within the Gulf Digital Finance and Connectivity Corridor as a multi-zone regulatory and infrastructure-coordination environment spanning regulatory layering continuity, CBDC experimentation continuity, sovereign compute coordination continuity, exchange-layer participation continuity, submarine landing continuity, logistics continuity infrastructure, and digital-governance modernization continuity. The evidence layer documents a layered digital-asset regulatory structure across the Central Bank of the UAE federal layer, ADGM's FSRA, VARA's Dubai virtual-asset regime, and DIFC's separate legal-regulatory structure coexisting in parallel without collapsing into a single national licensing channel; cross-border and domestic CBDC experimentation through the Digital Dirham Financial Infrastructure Transformation Programme including mBridge and bilateral CBDC bridge proof-of-concept work; national AI and compute infrastructure through G42, Core42, Khazna, and MBZUAI; exchange-layer infrastructure through UAE-IX in partnership with DE-CIX; telecommunications backbone infrastructure through e& SmartHub facilities; submarine cable landing infrastructure in Fujairah supporting multi-route Gulf-facing connectivity; port-centered intermodal logistics continuity through Jebel Ali; and federal digital identity and service modernization through UAE PASS. The metadata layer documents a multi-zone financial governance arrangement with federal, Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and DIFC surfaces operating in parallel.
