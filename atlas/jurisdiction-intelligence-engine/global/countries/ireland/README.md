## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Ireland as a Dublin-centered island-state continuity jurisdiction combining EU payment and digital interoperability, all-island electricity coordination, UK-adjacent energy and rail continuity, transatlantic cable connectivity, and maritime and aviation-supported national movement. The evidence layer documents gov.ie and MyGovID public-service administration with OGCIO Build to Share architecture across Government Cloud, Government Networks, and the Government Data Centre programme under Connecting Government 2030 principles; Central Bank of Ireland participation in SEPA and Eurosystem TARGET services; INEX exchange infrastructure with Aqua Comms transatlantic cable routes; Dublin Port unitised-freight concentration; EirGrid and ESB Networks electricity coordination with East West, Greenlink, and Celtic interconnectors and all-island Single Electricity Market arrangements jointly operated with SONI; Gas Networks Ireland national and cross-channel pipeline infrastructure; HEAnet research-network participation; and Met Éireann, NECG, NCSC, and OPW continuity layers. The signals layer reflects continuity through overlapping physical and digital systems across a capital-region concentration, island-state distribution pattern, EU interoperability, and bounded cross-border continuity. The canonical package records Ireland as not a transatlantic routing-authority classification, not a global hyperscale-ownership classification, and not an independent Atlantic-cable dominance classification beyond documented landing and exchange infrastructure.
