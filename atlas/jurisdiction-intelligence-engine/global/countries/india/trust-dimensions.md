## Institutional Continuity Dimension

- The source layers indicate institutional continuity supported through interoperable identity systems, public digital-service infrastructure, central-bank-operated settlement systems, public-sector cloud environments, registry-governance systems, research-network federation, broadband-expansion systems, and cybersecurity coordination structures operating at population scale rather than through isolated sector-specific systems.
- Aadhaar, DigiLocker, UMANG, and India Stack indicate continuity across identity, document access, service aggregation, and procedural digitization inside a shared national digital-public-infrastructure environment.
- RBI, NEFT, RTGS, NPCI, and UPI indicate continuity in payment-system governance through a layered environment combining central-bank settlement systems with interoperable retail-payment modernization.
- NIC, National Data Centres, and MeghRaj indicate continuity in public-sector digital administration through nationally coordinated ICT infrastructure, distributed hosting environments, and government cloud frameworks.
- NKN and the National Supercomputing Mission indicate continuity in research-network federation and scientific-compute coordination through nationally organized academic-network and distributed supercomputing systems.
- NIXI, the .IN Registry, and IRINN indicate continuity in registry, exchange, and numbering governance through nationally administered institutional structures.
- BharatNet, POWERGRID telecom systems, CERT-In, and NCIIPC indicate continuity across broadband expansion, state-linked communications systems, cyber coordination, and critical-infrastructure protection functions.
- India Stack Global, the UPI-PayNow linkage, and major-port logistics materials indicate that this continuity operates inside outward-facing regional and global interfaces rather than within a closed domestic perimeter.
- The documented trust characteristic is institutional continuity carried through interoperable identity systems, public digital-service infrastructure, central-bank-operated settlement systems, public-sector cloud environments, registry governance, research-network federation, broadband-expansion systems, and cybersecurity coordination structures operating at population scale.

## Financial Infrastructure and Settlement Dimension

- The source layers indicate payment-system continuity supported through central-bank-operated settlement infrastructure, nationwide electronic-transfer systems, instant-payment interoperability environments, and cross-border digital-payment interfaces.
- RBI indicates continuity through direct operation of RTGS and NEFT inside a central-bank-governed settlement and transfer environment rather than a decentralized settlement perimeter.
- RTGS indicates continuity through real-time settlement finality in the books of RBI, while NEFT indicates continuity through a nation-wide centralized transfer environment available on a continuous basis.
- NPCI and UPI indicate continuity through interoperable instant-payment infrastructure linked to multiple bank accounts rather than a payment environment limited to closed-loop systems.
- The coexistence of RTGS, NEFT, and UPI indicates a layered settlement and payment-modernization environment spanning large-value settlement, nationwide transfer, and retail instant payments.
- The India-Singapore UPI-PayNow linkage indicates continuity through an official cross-border payment interface attached to the domestic instant-payment environment.
- The documented trust characteristic is payment-system continuity supported through central-bank-operated settlement infrastructure, nationwide electronic-transfer systems, instant-payment interoperability environments, and cross-border digital-payment interfaces.

## Internet Exchange and Registry Governance Dimension

- The source layers indicate registry and exchange continuity supported through nationally administered domain-governance systems, domestic peering coordination environments, and numbering-resource administration systems.
- NIXI indicates continuity through a neutral domestic peering environment designed to retain domestic traffic within India rather than relying by default on external routing paths.
- The .IN Registry indicates continuity through formal administration of India’s country-code top-level domain under nationally delegated authority.
- IRINN indicates continuity through allocation and registration services for IP addresses and AS numbers within a structured institutional framework.
- The coexistence of NIXI, the .IN Registry, and IRINN indicates continuity across naming, peering, and numbering governance rather than fragmented administration of those functions.
- The documented trust characteristic is registry and exchange continuity supported through nationally administered domain-governance systems, domestic peering coordination environments, and numbering-resource administration systems.

## Telecommunications and Connectivity Dimension

- The source layers indicate telecommunications continuity supported through nationwide broadband-expansion systems, institutional networking environments, state-linked fiber infrastructure, and coordinated communications frameworks.
- BharatNet indicates continuity through a middle-mile broadband-expansion system extending connectivity from blocks to Gram Panchayats rather than leaving rural extension solely to fragmented last-mile arrangements.
- The documented NOFN and BharatNet scale indicates continuity through a nationally coordinated rural connectivity build-out environment spanning large numbers of districts, blocks, and Gram Panchayats.
- NKN indicates continuity through a parallel institutional networking layer serving the knowledge community across India.
- POWERGRID telecom materials indicate continuity through state-linked fiber infrastructure built on transmission-line-linked optical ground wire systems.
- UMANG’s role as a unified service interface indicates that this communications environment supports broad digital-service delivery in addition to transport connectivity.
- The documented trust characteristic is telecommunications continuity supported through nationwide broadband-expansion systems, institutional networking environments, state-linked fiber infrastructure, and coordinated communications frameworks.
