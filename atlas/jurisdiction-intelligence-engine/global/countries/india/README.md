# India

## Atlas Jurisdiction Package

India is a jurisdiction package within the Satoshium Atlas jurisdiction intelligence framework.

This package provides a structured jurisdiction record designed to support research, analysis, orientation, comparison, discovery, and future interoperability across the broader Atlas ecosystem.

---

## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records India as a New Delhi-centered large-scale South Asian digital-public-infrastructure jurisdiction combining interoperable identity systems, central-bank-operated settlement infrastructure, instant-payment modernization, public-sector cloud continuity, research-network federation, broadband expansion, registry governance, cybersecurity coordination, and globally connected digital-public-infrastructure interfaces, spanning digital public infrastructure and identity, RTGS and instant-payment modernization, public-sector cloud and procedural digitization, registry and numbering governance, research-network and distributed compute federation, telecommunications and broadband expansion, energy grid operational coordination, cybersecurity and critical infrastructure coordination, global digital public infrastructure integration, and trade and maritime connectivity frameworks. The evidence layer documents Aadhaar, DigiLocker, UMANG, and India Stack interoperability under Digital India; Reserve Bank of India RTGS, NEFT, NPCI, and UPI with cross-border payment linkage environments including UPI-PayNow; NIC, MeghRaj government cloud, and National Data Centres with distributed public-sector ICT and mixed-provider infrastructure; NKN and the National Supercomputing Mission distributed research-compute federation; NIXI, .IN Registry, IRINN, domestic peering, and DNS governance; BharatNet, NOFN, and POWERGRID telecom rural broadband expansion; NLDC and GRID-INDIA national-grid synchronization with cross-border power coordination; JNPA and major-port maritime trade-connectivity and logistics; CERT-In and NCIIPC critical-infrastructure protection and cyber-alert dissemination; and India Stack Global with UPI-PayNow cross-border remittance and international interoperability. Primary coordination cities are New Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Chennai.
