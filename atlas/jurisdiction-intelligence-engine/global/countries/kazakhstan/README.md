## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Kazakhstan as an Astana- and Almaty-centered state-coordinated Eurasian infrastructure jurisdiction combining digital-government modernization, payment-system experimentation, digital-asset regulation, terrestrial telecommunications continuity, cyber coordination, distributed exchange support, and cross-border Eurasian integration, spanning Eurasian terrestrial telecommunications, cross-border financial modernization, Central Asian and Eurasian digital-government coordination, terrestrial transit and cross-border connectivity, distributed exchange and cyber coordination, digital-asset regulatory and financial-zone coordination, and research-network and institutional connectivity frameworks. The evidence layer documents eGov Kazakhstan, NIT JSC, IIN, Digital ID, Mobile Citizens Database, biometric verification, and National Certification Authority PKI digital-governance and trust-service coordination; National Bank of Kazakhstan, NPCK, Digital Tenge CBDC pilot and Digital Tenge Hub, Open Banking and Open API, Identification Data Exchange Center, and Anti-fraud Center payment modernization; AIFC, AFSA, and AIX dual-layer digital-asset regulatory environment with the 2023 Law on Digital Assets and 2024 AFSA Digital Asset Activities Rulebook establishing mining licensing and DLT trading facility regulation; STS exchange-support infrastructure and KZ-CERT cyber-incident coordination across distributed domestic exchange environments; Kazakhtelecom National Information Superhighway with a 248-point network, 17 data centers, and cross-border terrestrial connectivity to China, Russia, Europe, and Central Asia; and KazRENA institutional research-network coordination embedded within Eurasian cross-border systems. The signals layer reflects state-coordinated continuity across digital governance, identity, payments modernization, digital-asset regulation, trust services, terrestrial telecommunications, and Eurasian systems integration.
