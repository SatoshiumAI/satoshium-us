## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Poland as a Warsaw-centered administrative coordination jurisdiction with distributed territorial continuity across 16 voivodeships and maritime cities Gdańsk and Gdynia elevated to primary coordination cities alongside Warsaw reflecting a Baltic-plus-inland duality, operating as a layered Central European coordination environment spanning digital-government interoperability, dual-currency wholesale settlement, rail and road infrastructure, energy transmission, research-network federation, and EU and NATO institutional coordination. The evidence layer documents Profil Zaufany, ePUAP, mObywatel, and eIDAS identity infrastructure; NBP SORBNET3 zloty wholesale settlement and TARGET-NBP euro settlement with KIR Elixir, Euro Elixir, and Express Elixir 24/7/365 instant clearing; NASK and CERT Polska cybersecurity coordination with PIONIER research-network infrastructure of 8,504 km fiber with DWDM and GÉANT connectivity; PSE electricity transmission across 309 lines and 16,520 km and 112 EHV substations including 450 kV DC Sweden interconnector; GAZ-SYSTEM gas transmission network with Baltic Pipe cross-border infrastructure and Świnoujście LNG terminal; PKP Polskie Linie Kolejowe and PKP Intercity rail including the Baltic Express; GDDKiA national road administration; Warsaw Chopin Airport; Government Centre for Security RCB crisis-management; TEN-T Baltic Sea-Adriatic corridor alignment; and EU membership since 1 May 2004 and Schengen since 21 December 2007. The canonical package explicitly excludes military, intelligence, geopolitical-ranking, deployment-readiness, East-West-shield, frontier-state, geopolitical-buffer, and geopolitical-corridor framings as negated-only constructions.
