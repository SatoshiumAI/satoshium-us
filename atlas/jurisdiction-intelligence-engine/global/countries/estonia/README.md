## Jurisdiction Summary

The canonical package records Estonia as a Tallinn-centered digital-governance and data-interoperability coordination environment, an Eesti Pank-linked Eurosystem settlement participant, an X-tee-based distributed data-governance environment, an EENet- and ETAIS-linked research-network federation surface, an Elering-linked energy and grid-integration coordination environment, and a NCSC-EE-linked cybersecurity coordination jurisdiction, aligned across EU digital identity interoperability, euro-area settlement integration, Baltic electricity and gas integration, Nordic-Baltic research network federation, EU and NATO cybersecurity coordination, and cross-border data continuity and interoperability frameworks. The evidence layer documents RIA, X-tee, ID-card, Mobile-ID, Smart-ID, GovSSO, TARA, eesti.ee, eIDAS, and e-Residency digital-governance and identity infrastructure; Eesti Pank Eurosystem participation and TARGET T2, TIPS, T2S settlement participation with digital euro experimentation; X-tee distributed data architecture with data-embassy cross-border continuity infrastructure through Luxembourg cooperation; EENet, NORDUnet, GÉANT, ETAIS, and LUMI HPC access research-network federation; .ee registry governance through the Estonian Internet Foundation with DNSSEC; Elering transmission, EstLink interconnection, Balticconnector gas interconnection, ENTSO-E Continental Europe synchronization, and Nord Pool electricity-market participation; NCSC-EE, CERT-EE, 24/7 cyber monitoring, international CERT cooperation, and CCDCOE presence in Tallinn; and EU, Baltic, and NATO institutional coordination participation. The signals layer reflects a multi-layer national coordination environment spanning digital governance, distributed data architecture, Eurosystem settlement participation, research-network federation, naming stewardship, energy interconnection, cybersecurity coordination, and regional institutional embedding.
