# 🧩 DAO Statutory Environments
## Comparative Topology of Legally Recognized Decentralized Governance Structures Across Jurisdictions

The **DAO Statutory Environments** comparison file defines how Atlas interprets jurisdiction-level statutory frameworks that accommodate decentralized autonomous organizations (DAOs), blockchain-native governance entities, and digitally coordinated legal structures.

This document supports **signal normalization**, **jurisdiction comparison consistency**, and **future governance-routing compatibility** within Atlas exports.

It does **not** rank jurisdictions or recommend incorporation strategy.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

This comparison layer exists to:

- distinguish statutory DAO-recognition environments
- normalize interpretation of blockchain governance signals
- support corridor classification consistency
- enable cross-jurisdiction topology mapping
- prepare machine-readable governance compatibility surfaces

Atlas compares **legal structure availability**, not jurisdictional leadership.
