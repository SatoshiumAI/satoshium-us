# 🧪 Research Corridor Alignment
## Comparative Topology of Federal Laboratory, University, and Applied Science Infrastructure Continuity Across Jurisdictions

The **Research Corridor Alignment** comparison file defines how Atlas interprets jurisdiction-level clustering of federal laboratories, university-linked research ecosystems, aerospace experimentation environments, and applied science infrastructure continuity across multi-state regions.

This document supports **signal normalization**, **corridor classification consistency**, and **future machine-readable research-topology compatibility** within Atlas exports.

It does **not** rank jurisdictions by research capability or national scientific priority.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

This comparison layer exists to:

- distinguish research infrastructure continuity structures across jurisdictions
- normalize interpretation of federal laboratory and university signals
- support corridor identification consistency
- enable cross-jurisdiction research topology mapping
- prepare agent-routing compatibility surfaces for experimentation-aware systems

Atlas compares **institutional research persistence**, not innovation leadership.
