# ⚡ Energy Topology Compatibility
## Comparative Topology of Grid Structure, Generation Alignment, and Compute-Energy Interaction Surfaces Across Jurisdictions

The **Energy Topology Compatibility** comparison file defines how Atlas interprets jurisdiction-level grid structures, generation environments, transmission coordination patterns, and compute-energy interaction signals across regions.

This document supports **signal normalization**, **corridor classification consistency**, and **future machine-readable infrastructure-routing compatibility** within Atlas exports.

It does **not** rank jurisdictions by energy capacity, pricing advantage, or deployment suitability.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

This comparison layer exists to:

- distinguish grid-structure environments across jurisdictions
- normalize interpretation of generation-alignment signals
- support corridor identification consistency
- enable cross-jurisdiction topology mapping of energy infrastructure continuity
- prepare routing compatibility surfaces for compute-energy interaction environments

Atlas compares **energy infrastructure structure**, not production leadership.
