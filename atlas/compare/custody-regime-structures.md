# 🏦 Custody Regime Structures
## Comparative Topology of Institutional Asset Custody Environments Across Jurisdictions

The **Custody Regime Structures** comparison file defines how Atlas interprets jurisdiction-level custody infrastructure continuity across financial, fiduciary, digital-asset-adjacent, and institutional trust-service environments.

This document supports **signal normalization**, **jurisdiction comparison consistency**, and **future routing compatibility** within Atlas exports.

It does **not** rank jurisdictions or assign regulatory preference.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

This comparison layer exists to:

- distinguish custody-aligned infrastructure types
- normalize interpretation of institutional trust-service signals
- support corridor classification consistency
- enable cross-jurisdiction topology mapping
- prepare machine-readable custody-routing compatibility surfaces

Atlas compares **structures**, not authority or leadership.
