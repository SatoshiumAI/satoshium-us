# 🏙 Municipal Experimentation Patterns
## Comparative Topology of City-Level Innovation, Regulatory Pilots, and Local Infrastructure Testing Environments

The **Municipal Experimentation Patterns** comparison file defines how Atlas interprets jurisdiction-level signals reflecting city-scale policy pilots, infrastructure experimentation programs, smart-city deployments, digital service innovation environments, and localized regulatory flexibility across municipalities.

This document supports **signal normalization**, **corridor classification consistency**, and **future machine-readable experimentation-topology compatibility** within Atlas exports.

It does **not** rank municipalities by innovation performance or regulatory progressiveness.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

This comparison layer exists to:

- distinguish municipal experimentation environments across jurisdictions
- normalize interpretation of city-scale pilot-program signals
- support corridor identification consistency
- enable cross-jurisdiction topology mapping of local innovation activity
- prepare agent-routing compatibility surfaces for municipal coordination environments

Atlas compares **experimentation continuity**, not innovation leadership.
