Atlas Framework Specification

Federal Overlay Model

This document defines how national-scale federal infrastructure overlays reinforce continuity interpretation across jurisdictions without altering the canonical topology contract. Federal overlays are interpretive continuity surfaces — they help Atlas describe nationally coordinated infrastructure influence without converting federal presence into canonical corridor authority.

Framework Library Overlay Participation Model Topology-Subordinate Layer v1 — Initial Specification
Core Rule

Federal overlays are continuity amplifiers, not topology origin sources. Overlays may strengthen interpretation, but may not independently assign anchor status, transit status, terminus status, completion-layer placement, or corridor-role authority. The canonical topology trio remains authoritative across every interaction in this specification.

Topology Authority

Corridor-group placement, foundation-layer classification, and completion-layer participation. Canonical and binding.

Interpretive Reinforcement

Federal overlays that strengthen plausibility, adjacency confidence, and continuity visibility without transferring authority.

Evidence-Grounded Participation

Overlay visibility must remain tied to documented federal infrastructure relevance — never inherited, never assumed.

§ 01

Purpose

Federal overlays describe national-scale infrastructure systems that reinforce corridor continuity across jurisdictions.

Federal overlays:

  • support interpretation
  • strengthen adjacency confidence
  • clarify infrastructure dependence
  • extend continuity visibility
Governing Principle Federal overlays must not independently establish anchor placement authority. They are continuity amplifiers rather than topology origin sources. They help Atlas describe nationally coordinated infrastructure influence without altering the canonical topology contract.
§ 02

Federal Overlay Definition

A federal overlay is infrastructure continuity derived from nationally coordinated systems including:

  • national laboratories
  • defense infrastructure
  • DOE transmission corridors
  • interstate transmission designation
  • federal compute systems
  • federal logistics networks

Federal overlays operate as continuity amplifiers rather than topology origin sources. They may strengthen continuity interpretation across multiple jurisdictions, but they do not independently create corridor placement authority.

§ 03

Federal Overlay Authority Limits

Federal overlays must not
  • assign anchor status
  • assign transit status
  • assign terminus status
  • override completion-layer placement
  • replace corridor-role classification
Retained Authority The topology trio remains authoritative. Federal overlays may strengthen the plausibility, continuity, or bounded stability of a reading — but they may not convert support into canonical placement authority.
§ 04

Overlay Participation Categories

Atlas recognizes the following federal overlay categories. Each category must remain independently visible.

Blending Prohibition Overlay blending is not permitted. If multiple overlays are relevant, they must remain distinguishable rather than collapsing into a generic federal-strength claim.
Equivalence Prohibition Federal overlay participation must not be interpreted as equivalence between jurisdictions that share the same overlay surface. Shared overlay visibility does not imply identical corridor-role strength, foundation participation depth, or completion-layer placement.
§ 05

National Laboratory Overlay Participation

National laboratory overlays include:

  • DOE national labs
  • federally administered research campuses
  • federal experimental infrastructure complexes

National laboratory overlays strengthen research corridor continuity, innovation adjacency interpretation, and federal infrastructure visibility.

Authority Boundary

They do not independently establish anchor placement. They may strengthen the interpretive reading of research participation, but they do not replace canonical topology or evidence requirements.

Designation overlays strengthen energy-foundation stacking interpretation, corridor continuity plausibility, and adjacency-supported transmission reasoning.

Authority Boundary

§ 09

Federal Compute Infrastructure Overlay

Federal compute overlays include:

  • national computing facilities
  • federal HPC systems
  • DOE compute backbone infrastructure
  • defense compute clusters

Federal compute overlays strengthen compute-foundation continuity interpretation.

Separation Rule Federal compute overlays must remain distinct from hyperscale private-sector compute placement signals. Federal compute presence may strengthen infrastructure continuity reading, but it must not be conflated with non-federal compute anchor logic unless canonical evidence explicitly supports that conclusion.
§ 10

Federal Logistics Continuity Overlay

Federal logistics overlays include:

  • military logistics alignment systems
  • federal emergency transport continuity infrastructure
  • interstate mobilization corridors

These overlays strengthen transport-foundation continuity reasoning.

Authority Boundary

§ 11

Overlay Interaction with Topology Trio

Federal overlays must defer to:

  • corridor-group placement
  • foundation-layer classification
  • completion-layer participation
Interaction Rule Overlay visibility strengthens interpretation clarity but does not modify placement authority. Overlay relevance may influence how a jurisdiction is interpreted — it may not alter the trio fields or their authority.
§ 12

Overlay Interaction with Corridor Roles

Federal overlays may
  • reinforce anchor stability interpretation
  • clarify transit continuity plausibility
  • strengthen support-node interpretation
  • improve expansion-edge trajectory confidence
Federal overlays must not
  • create anchor roles
  • create transit roles
  • create terminus roles
  • override role classification

Overlay presence may support a role reading. It may not generate the role independently.

§ 13

Overlay Interaction with Foundation Types

Federal overlays commonly stack with:

  • energy foundation
  • compute foundation
  • research foundation
  • transport foundation
Explicit Stacking Rule Overlay stacking must remain explicit. Implicit cross-foundation inheritance is not permitted. A federal overlay may support multiple foundations at once, but the package must preserve visibility of which foundation domains are actually implicated.
Dominance Visibility Rule When multiple federal overlays affect the same jurisdiction simultaneously, dominant overlay dependence must remain explicitly identifiable. Secondary overlay participation must not obscure the primary infrastructure continuity surface being reinforced.
§ 14

Overlay Interaction with Signals

Federal overlays may appear as:

  • continuity-support signals
  • infrastructure reinforcement indicators
  • federal participation visibility markers
Federal overlays must not
  • convert signals into evidence
  • replace corridor-role classification
  • override topology placement
Signal Taxonomy Authority Signal taxonomy remains authoritative. Overlay-related signals may indicate emerging or strengthening federal continuity relevance, but they must remain within signal authority boundaries.
§ 15

Overlay Interaction with Trust Dimensions

Federal overlays may strengthen
  • continuity confidence
  • infrastructure stability interpretation
  • adjacency reliability interpretation
Overlay presence must not create
  • deployment readiness inference
  • certification posture inference
  • corridor sufficiency inference
Non-Scoring Retention Trust dimensions remain non-scoring interpretive structures. Federal overlays may contribute to trust posture without upgrading trust into topology or lifecycle authority.
§ 16

Overlay Interaction with Builder Mode

Builder-mode interpretation may reference overlays to:

  • clarify infrastructure dependence
  • highlight federal continuity participation
  • describe reinforcement environments
Builder Mode must not

treat overlays as siting authority signals. Deployment posture remains evidence-bound. An overlay may explain why an environment is structurally connected — it may not function as a deployment authorization shortcut.

§ 17

Rendering Visibility Rules

The renderer may
  • display overlay participation layers
  • visualize federal continuity surfaces
  • highlight overlay stacking with foundations
The renderer must not
  • upgrade overlays to topology authority
  • collapse overlays into corridor roles
  • remove overlay dependence visibility
Subordination Rule Rendering layers must preserve overlay classification boundaries. Overlay presence may be surfaced visually, narratively, or through helper summaries, but it must remain visibly subordinate to the canonical topology contract.
§ 18

Overlay Compliance Definition

Compliant when
  • overlay participation remains evidence-grounded
  • overlay stacking remains explicit
  • overlay roles remain interpretation-bound
  • topology authority remains unchanged
Non-compliance occurs when
  • overlays imply anchor authority
  • overlays replace corridor roles
  • overlays override foundation classification
  • overlays produce deployment inference
