# ATLAS Federal Overlay Model v1

## 1\. Purpose

Federal overlays describe national-scale infrastructure systems that reinforce corridor continuity across jurisdictions.

Federal overlays:

* support interpretation
* strengthen adjacency confidence
* clarify infrastructure dependence
* extend continuity visibility

Federal overlays must not independently establish anchor placement authority.

Federal overlays are continuity amplifiers rather than topology origin sources.

They help Atlas describe nationally coordinated infrastructure influence without altering the canonical topology contract.

## 2\. Federal Overlay Definition

A federal overlay is infrastructure continuity derived from nationally coordinated systems including:

* national laboratories
* defense infrastructure
* DOE transmission corridors
* interstate transmission designation
* federal compute systems
* federal logistics networks

Federal overlays operate as continuity amplifiers rather than topology origin sources.

They may strengthen continuity interpretation across multiple jurisdictions, but they do not independently create corridor placement authority.

## 3\. Federal Overlay Authority Limits

Federal overlays must not:

* assign anchor status
* assign transit status
* assign terminus status
* override completion-layer placement
* replace corridor-role classification

The topology trio remains authoritative.

Federal overlays may strengthen the plausibility, continuity, or bounded stability of a reading.

They may not convert support into canonical placement authority.

## 4\. Overlay Participation Categories

Atlas recognizes the following federal overlay categories:

* national-laboratory continuity
* DOE corridor infrastructure
* defense infrastructure networks
* interstate transmission designation
* federal compute backbone
* federal logistics continuity

Each category must remain independently visible.

Overlay blending is not permitted.

If multiple overlays are relevant, they must remain distinguishable rather than collapsing into a generic federal-strength claim.

Federal overlay participation must not be interpreted as equivalence between jurisdictions that share the same overlay surface.

Shared overlay visibility does not imply identical corridor-role strength, foundation participation depth, or completion-layer placement.

## 13\. Overlay Interaction with Foundation Types

Federal overlays commonly stack with:

* energy foundation
* compute foundation
* research foundation
* transport foundation

Overlay stacking must remain explicit.

Implicit cross-foundation inheritance is not permitted.

A federal overlay may support multiple foundations at once, but the package must preserve visibility of which foundation domains are actually implicated.

When multiple federal overlays affect the same jurisdiction simultaneously, dominant overlay dependence must remain explicitly identifiable.

Secondary overlay participation must not obscure the primary infrastructure continuity surface being reinforced.

## 16\. Overlay Interaction with Builder Mode

Builder-mode interpretation may reference overlays to:

Deployment posture remains evidence-bound.

An overlay may explain why an environment is structurally connected.

It may not function as a deployment authorization shortcut.

## 17\. Rendering Visibility Rules

The renderer may:

* display overlay participation layers
* visualize federal continuity surfaces
* highlight overlay stacking with foundations

The renderer must not:

* upgrade overlays to topology authority
* collapse overlays into corridor roles
* remove overlay dependence visibility

Rendering layers must preserve overlay classification boundaries.

Overlay presence may be surfaced visually, narratively, or through helper summaries, but it must remain visibly subordinate to the canonical topology contract.

## 18\. Overlay Compliance Definition

A jurisdiction package is federal-overlay compliant when:

* overlay participation remains evidence-grounded
* overlay stacking remains explicit
* overlay roles remain interpretation-bound
* topology authority remains unchanged

Non-compliance occurs when:

* overlays imply anchor authority
* overlays replace corridor roles
* overlays override foundation classification
* overlays produce deployment inference
