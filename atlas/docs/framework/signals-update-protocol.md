
# Signals Update Protocol
## Satoshium Atlas — Jurisdiction Intelligence Engine

Defines how jurisdiction signals evolve while preserving evidence → signals derivation integrity.

This version includes downstream corridor-assignment-matrix synchronization guidance.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

Atlas signals represent evidence-derived structural observations describing jurisdiction environments affecting:

- AI infrastructure posture
- Bitcoin regulatory compatibility
- compute-energy geography
- institutional research presence
- municipal experimentation structure
- corridor adjacency potential

Signals must remain:

- evidence-derived
- jurisdiction-lens aligned
- cross-state comparable
- export-compatible

---

## 🔄 Signal Update Triggers

Signals update only when structural evidence changes.

Matrix alignment changes alone do not trigger signal updates unless supported by evidence changes.

---

## 🧭 Structural vs Observational Changes

Structural changes modify:

signals.md
trust-dimensions.md (review only)
profile.md (if topology shifts)

Observational changes modify only:

change-log.md

---

## 🧩 Corridor Assignment Matrix Synchronization

When signals change structurally, downstream consistency must be checked against:

/atlas/corridors/corridor-assignment-matrix.md

If matrix alignment changes:

- update metadata.md
- update corridor registry participation (if applicable)
- record adjustment in change-log.md

Signals remain upstream authority relative to corridor classification.

---

## 🤖 Machine-Readable Compatibility

Signals support future export formats:

signals.json
trust-dimensions.json
metadata.json
corridors.json
