
# Signals Update Protocol  
## Satoshium Atlas — Jurisdiction Intelligence Engine

Defines how jurisdiction signals evolve over time while preserving Atlas normalization integrity, evidence-derivation discipline, and cross-state structural comparability.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

Atlas signals represent **evidence-derived structural observations** describing jurisdiction environments affecting:

- AI infrastructure posture
- Bitcoin regulatory compatibility
- compute-energy geography
- institutional research presence
- municipal experimentation structure
- corridor adjacency potential

This protocol ensures signals:

- remain evidence-derived
- remain jurisdiction-lens aligned
- remain cross-state comparable
- evolve without inference drift
- remain exportable into machine-readable formats

Signals must never become:

- rankings
- deployment guidance
- optimization recommendations
- corridor leadership claims
- national positioning language

---

## 🧱 Signal Lifecycle Model

Signals follow the canonical Atlas derivation chain:

evidence → signals → trust-dimensions → profile → builder-mode

Signals may change **only** when upstream evidence changes.

Signals must never originate independently.

---

## 🔄 Signal Update Triggers

Signals update only when structural evidence changes within one of the following domains.

### Governance Signals

Examples:

- digital-asset statutory changes
- custody authorization frameworks
- DAO recognition structures
- privacy-rights legislation
- AI regulatory posture shifts

---

### Infrastructure Signals

Examples:

- interconnection corridor expansion
- hyperscale compute siting confirmation
- backbone routing topology change
- grid-capacity availability shifts

---

### Research Ecosystem Signals

Examples:

- federal laboratory designation changes
- semiconductor ecosystem expansion
- major institutional AI research investment
- aerospace infrastructure alignment

---

### Municipal Experimentation Signals

Examples:

- city-level Bitcoin policy frameworks
- zoning support for compute infrastructure
- smart-city infrastructure pilots
- sandbox regulatory experimentation programs

---

### Corridor Emergence Signals

Examples:

- multi-state infrastructure clustering
- IX density adjacency alignment
- compute-energy co-location continuity
- cross-jurisdiction research corridors

Corridor signals must remain descriptive unless supported by multi-jurisdiction structural evidence.

---

## 🧭 Structural vs Observational Changes

Atlas distinguishes between two update classes.

### Structural Changes

Structural changes modify signals.

Examples:

- statutory enactment
- infrastructure deployment
- federal designation
- custody authorization frameworks
- persistent institutional investment alignment

Structural updates may affect:

- signals.md
- trust-dimensions.md (review only)
- profile.md (if topology changes)

---

### Observational Changes

Observational changes do **not** modify signals.

Examples:

- policy proposals
- announcements
- pilot programs without permanence
- exploratory partnerships
- early-stage initiatives

These must be recorded only in:

change-log.md

---

## 📚 Evidence Requirements

Signals must always be:

- documented
- jurisdiction-specific
- lens-aligned
- structurally persistent
- non-comparative

Signals must not rely on:

- media narratives
- speculative investment claims
- ecosystem branding language
- temporary funding announcements
- corridor marketing initiatives

---

## 🧩 Lens Alignment Enforcement

Each jurisdiction operates inside its **state-specific instruction lens**.

Signal updates must remain constrained to:

- infrastructure topology
- governance posture
- research environment
- municipal experimentation structure
- compute-energy geography

Signals must not expand beyond lens scope without supporting structural evidence.

---

## 🧱 Structural Exclusions Protection

Signal updates must respect exclusions recorded in:

change-log.md

Examples:

- absence of national routing spine alignment
- absence of hyperscale corridor participation
- absence of custody-regime jurisdiction status
- absence of federal hosting corridor designation
- absence of DAO-wrapper authorization structure

Signals must not contradict exclusions unless new structural evidence emerges.

---

## 🔗 Cross-State Comparability Safeguards

Signals must remain:

- terminology-consistent
- scope-consistent
- structure-consistent

across all jurisdictions.

Example:

If IX density qualifies as a signal in one jurisdiction, identical evidence thresholds must apply elsewhere.

---

## 🕓 Update Cadence Expectations

Recommended maintenance cadence:

**Quarterly review cycle**

**Annual structural verification pass**

**Event-triggered updates when major statutory or infrastructure changes occur**

Emergency updates permitted when:

- custody legislation passes
- federal corridor designation occurs
- hyperscale infrastructure deployment confirmed
- interconnection topology materially shifts

---

## 🧾 Change-Log Interaction Rules

Every signal update must produce an entry in:

change-log.md

Entries must include:

- evidence source type
- signal category affected
- structural vs observational classification
- lens-alignment confirmation
- exclusion impact assessment

---

## 📊 Trust-Dimension Synchronization

When signals change structurally:

trust-dimensions.md must be reviewed

Trust-dimensions evaluate **stability only**.

They must never introduce:

- rankings
- deployment readiness claims
- corridor leadership language

---

## 🏗 Metadata Synchronization Expectations

Signal updates must propagate into:

metadata.md

Where applicable:

- jurisdiction tags
- corridor indicators
- governance posture markers
- research environment descriptors
- infrastructure topology markers

Metadata maintains machine-readable compatibility.

---

## 🌐 Corridor Signal Emergence Handling

Corridor signals must satisfy:

- multi-jurisdiction evidence continuity
- infrastructure adjacency persistence
- institutional alignment durability

Corridor signals must never originate from:

- branding narratives
- marketing coalitions
- temporary consortium announcements

---

## 🤖 Machine-Readable Export Compatibility

Signals must remain compatible with future export formats:

signals.json
trust-dimensions.json
metadata.json

Signal wording must remain:

- deterministic
- structured
- parseable
- corridor-aware but non-hierarchical

---

## ✅ Signal Update Integrity Checklist

Before modifying any signal:

Confirm:

- evidence exists
- jurisdiction lens alignment preserved
- structural exclusions respected
- cross-state comparability maintained
- trust-dimension stability reviewed
- metadata synchronized
- change-log updated

Only then may:

signals.md

be modified.
