# Corridor Indexing Framework  
## Satoshium Atlas — Signal-Layer Topology Activation

Defines how multi-jurisdiction corridors are identified, structured, recorded, and maintained within the Atlas jurisdiction intelligence engine.

This document activates Phase 2 of Atlas:

Signal-layer activation across jurisdictions.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

Corridors describe **persistent multi-jurisdiction structural alignments** affecting:

- compute infrastructure
- energy topology
- research ecosystems
- regulatory compatibility
- digital asset custody environments
- interconnection routing density

Corridors do **not** rank jurisdictions.

Corridors do **not** recommend deployment locations.

Corridors do **not** indicate leadership status.

Corridors exist solely to describe structural continuity across regions.

---

## 🧱 Corridor Definition

A corridor exists when **three or more adjacent jurisdictions** demonstrate:

- infrastructure continuity
- research alignment
- governance compatibility
- energy-compute adjacency
- interconnection topology linkage

Single-jurisdiction clusters are **not corridors**.

Two-state adjacency is **not sufficient** for corridor classification.

## 🔍 Corridor Identification Criteria

A corridor may be recorded only when:

- evidence exists across multiple jurisdictions
- infrastructure continuity is persistent
- adjacency is geographic or topological
- institutional presence is durable
- signals remain lens-aligned

Corridors must never originate from:

- branding narratives
- economic marketing initiatives
- investment speculation
- temporary coalitions

---

## 📚 Evidence Requirements

Corridor recognition requires:

- infrastructure confirmation
- statutory compatibility alignment
- institutional presence verification
- research continuity indicators
- grid or routing adjacency signals

Evidence must already exist inside:

evidence.md

across each participating jurisdiction.

Corridors cannot introduce new evidence.

---

## 🔗 Cross-State Signal Participation

Corridor membership derives from existing jurisdiction signals.

Example:

If three adjacent jurisdictions share:

interconnection_density_present

then corridor continuity may be recorded.

Corridors never override jurisdiction signals.

They only reference them.

---

## 🧩 Lens Alignment Enforcement

Corridors must respect each jurisdiction’s instruction lens.

Corridors cannot:

expand jurisdiction scope  
introduce national positioning  
imply optimization readiness  
modify signal meaning  

Corridors describe topology only.

---

## 🧾 Corridor Registry Structure

Corridors are recorded in:

/atlas/corridors/

Each corridor receives:

corridor-name.md

Example:

energy-compute-rocky-mountain-corridor.md

Each registry file includes:

- participating jurisdictions
- corridor type
- supporting signal categories
- structural continuity description
- exclusions (if applicable)
- evidence dependency summary

---

## 🧱 Structural Exclusions Protection

Corridors must respect exclusions recorded in jurisdiction:

change-log.md

Example exclusions:

absence of hyperscale participation  
absence of custody authorization  
absence of IX density alignment  

Corridor membership must not contradict exclusions.

---

## 🕓 Corridor Update Cadence

Recommended review cadence:

Quarterly structural verification

Annual topology reassessment

Event-triggered updates permitted when:

new infrastructure deployment confirmed  
statutory alignment changes  
federal designation shifts  
interconnection topology expansion occurs

Metadata tags remain descriptive only.

corridors.json

jurisdiction-linked

Corridors must remain compatible with:

signals.json

---

## 🌐 Corridor Emergence Handling

Emerging corridors may be recorded when:

evidence exists but continuity remains partial

These should be labeled:

emergent_corridor_candidate

Emergent corridors must not be treated as established corridors.

---

## ✅ Corridor Inclusion Checklist

Before registering a corridor confirm:

- three or more jurisdictions participate
- structural adjacency exists
- evidence already present in participating states
- exclusions respected
- signals remain unchanged
- metadata synchronized where appropriate

Only then may a corridor registry file be created.
