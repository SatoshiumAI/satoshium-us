# Atlas Corridor Index
## Satoshium Atlas — Multi‑Jurisdiction Structural Topology Map

The Corridor Index provides the entrypoint to Atlas Phase 2: signal‑layer activation across jurisdictions through evidence‑aligned corridor continuity structures.

Corridors describe **regional topology relationships**, not rankings or deployment recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

The corridor layer connects normalized jurisdiction packages into:

- infrastructure continuity regions
- governance alignment surfaces
- research ecosystem meshes
- energy‑compute adjacency zones
- interconnection routing structures

Corridors remain descriptive topology only.

They do **not** indicate:

- leadership status
- optimization guidance
- deployment readiness
- prioritization signals
- national positioning claims

---

## 🧭 Corridor Layer Architecture

Atlas corridor topology operates across three structural axes:

### 1. Corridor Group

Primary regional continuity classification shared across adjacent jurisdictions.

Examples include:

- Pacific Coastal Hyperscale Corridor
- Great Lakes Industrial Core Corridor
- Federal Interface Governance Corridor
- Central Interior Logistics Spine Corridor
- Northeast Institutional Mesh Corridor
- Pacific Northwest Coordination Corridor

Corridor Groups describe persistent multi‑state alignment structures.

---

### 2. Foundation Layer

Defines the structural infrastructure or governance substrate supporting corridor continuity.

Examples include:

- Coastal Hyperscale & Global Infrastructure Layer
- Federal Interface Governance Layer
- Interior Corridor Logistics Spine Layer
- Mountain West Research Layer
- Northeast Institutional Mesh Layer
- Pacific Northwest Coordination Layer

Foundation Layers describe base topology continuity surfaces.

---

### 3. Topology Completion Layer

Describes adjacency integration across larger regional topology structures.

Examples include:

- Pacific Edge Anchor Completion Layer
- Mid‑Atlantic Completion Layer
- Central Plains Completion Layer
- Desert–Interior Transition Layer
- Southeast Coastal Completion Layer
- Northern Interior Completion Layer

Completion Layers describe regional integration boundaries.

---

## 🧱 Corridor Registry Integration

Individual corridor definitions live within:

/atlas/corridors/<corridor-name>.md

Each registry file includes:

- participating jurisdictions
- supporting signal continuity
- infrastructure alignment description
- exclusions affecting participation
- emergence classification
- metadata synchronization notes

Registry entries must remain evidence‑derived.

## 🧩 Emergent Corridor Handling

Emergent corridors may be recorded when:

- adjacency continuity exists
- infrastructure alignment is partial
- signal continuity is developing

Such corridors are classified as:

emergent_corridor_candidate

Emergent corridors remain descriptive only until continuity stabilizes.
