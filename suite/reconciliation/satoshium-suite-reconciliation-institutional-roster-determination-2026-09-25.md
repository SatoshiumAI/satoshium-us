# Satoshium Suite Reconciliation — Institutional Roster Determination

**Date:** September 25, 2026  
**Phase:** Phase I — Establish the Reconciliation Baseline  
**Step:** 5  
**Status:** Proposed for Approval  
**Purpose:** Reconcile the formal institutional roster of the Satoshium Suite before role and object-ownership analysis begins.

---

## 1. Issue

The formal institutional roster must be established before institutional roles, authority boundaries, canonical objects, or Suite sequence can be reconciled.

The question for Step 5 is:

> Which institutions belong to the formal Satoshium Suite, and which named systems or architectural elements remain outside that roster?

This step addresses membership only.

It does not yet reconcile:

- institutional roles;
- canonical responsibilities;
- canonical object ownership;
- sequence;
- dependency;
- interoperability;
- legacy layer models.

---

## 2. Existing Architecture

The pre-reconciliation baseline identifies eight formal institutions:

1. Atlas
2. Navigator
3. Certifier
4. Registry
5. Chronicle
6. Anchor
7. Beacon
8. Attestor

All eight are Operational.

Aegis is explicitly identified as external/pre-Suite.

The governing reconciliation record prohibits silently moving Aegis into the formal Suite.

---

## 3. Roster Ambiguity Review

### 3.1 Aegis

Aegis is not part of the formal Suite roster.

Its historical and architectural relationship to Satoshium may remain relevant, but Suite membership is already excluded by the baseline.

**Roster Position:** External / pre-Suite

---

### 3.2 `SYS-*` System Registry Architecture

The existence of legacy `SYS-*` System Registry architecture does not itself establish a separate formal Suite institution.

The unresolved question concerns its relationship to the formal Registry architecture, not whether “System Registry” should automatically become a ninth institution.

**Roster Position:** Not independently admitted to the formal Suite roster at this stage.

**Related Deferred Issue:** DI-001

---

### 3.3 Canon / Governance / Verification Tooling

Legacy references to Canon, governance tooling, verification tooling, or similar architectural components do not establish formal institutional membership merely because they once occupied architectural positions, layers, or tooling roles.

Their status remains subject to later reconciliation where necessary.

**Roster Position:** Not independently admitted to the formal Suite roster at this stage.

**Related Deferred Issue:** DI-020

---

### 3.4 Legacy Layer Labels

Legacy layer or conceptual terms such as Trust, Knowledge, Intelligence, Signal, Simulation, Interface, Canon, Agent, and similar architectural labels are not treated as formal Suite institutions merely because earlier architecture used them structurally.

Their relationship to the mature Suite remains a separate reconciliation question.

**Roster Position:** Conceptual / legacy architecture unless separately established otherwise.

**Related Deferred Issue:** DI-002

---

## 4. Reconciled Roster Boundary

For purposes of the formal Satoshium Suite Reconciliation, the institutional roster is bounded as follows:

### Formal Suite Institutions

- Atlas
- Navigator
- Certifier
- Registry
- Chronicle
- Anchor
- Beacon
- Attestor

### Explicitly Outside the Formal Suite

- Aegis — external/pre-Suite

### Not Recognized as Separate Formal Institutions at This Stage

- `SYS-*` System Registry architecture
- Canon
- governance tooling
- verification tooling
- legacy layer labels
- conceptual architectural domains
- other named components not explicitly established as institutions

This boundary does not erase historical architecture.

It prevents historical or conceptual components from being silently elevated into institutional membership during reconciliation.

---

## 5. Determination

No evidence presently requires expansion or contraction of the formal institutional roster.

The reconciliation should therefore continue using the eight-institution roster already frozen in the baseline.

This Step 5 determination reconciles the roster boundary without yet making the more formal Step 6 confirmation of the eight institutions.

**Recommended Classification:** CONFIRMED

**Step 5 Status:** PENDING APPROVAL
