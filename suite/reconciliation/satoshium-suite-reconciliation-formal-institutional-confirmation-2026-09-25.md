The eight institutions above constitute the formal Suite roster for the September 25–30, 2026 Satoshium Suite Reconciliation.

In particular:

- Aegis remains external/pre-Suite.
- `SYS-*` System Registry architecture is not independently recognized as a ninth institution.
- Canon is not independently recognized as a formal Suite institution.

These items may still require architectural interpretation later, but they do not alter the formal Suite roster.

## 4. Roster Integrity Rule

Any future proposal to add, remove, merge, split, or reclassify a formal Suite institution would constitute a substantive architectural change.

Such a change cannot occur silently through documentation cleanup, terminology reconciliation, interoperability work, or historical reinterpretation.

It would require an explicit architectural decision supported by evidence and approved through the reconciliation process.
