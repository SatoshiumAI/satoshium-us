**Date:** September 29, 2026  
**Phase:** IV-B — Adversarial Consistency Review  
**Purpose:** Identify matters that belong to the later Satoshium Universe Documentation Reconciliation rather than Suite Reconciliation  
**Status:** COMPLETE — APPROVED

---

## Governing Boundary

The Suite Reconciliation is limited to the formal eight-institution Satoshium Suite.

The broader Satoshium Universe Documentation Reconciliation is reserved for documentation and architecture questions spanning:

```text
satoshium.ai
satoshium.info
satoshium.xyz
satoshium.net
satoshium.store
satoshium.link
satoshium.dev
satoshium.us
satoshium.games
```

except where a particular surface was directly necessary to resolve a Suite-level issue.

The later Universe review should therefore reconcile how the completed Suite is represented inside the wider Satoshium Universe without reopening the Suite architecture itself.

## Universe Documentation Reconciliation Candidates

### UDR-001 — Universe Mapper Reconciliation

The Universe Mapper was deliberately excluded from Suite Reconciliation.

The later review should reconcile the Mapper against the completed Suite architecture, including:

```text
Formal Suite roster
Institutional roles
Canonical objects
Authority boundaries
Relationship semantics
Operational status

```

The Mapper should reflect the reconciled Suite without converting conceptual or inferred relationships into established architectural claims.

**Disposition:** DEFERRED TO UNIVERSE DOCUMENTATION RECONCILIATION
