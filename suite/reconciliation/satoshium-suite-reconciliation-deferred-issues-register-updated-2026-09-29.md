## Purpose

This review closes the loop on the Deferred Issues Register created during Phase I.

Each carried-forward issue is classified as:

- **RESOLVED** — the Suite Reconciliation produced a governing determination;
- **PRESERVED** — the earlier construct remains valid as historical, legacy, or explicitly external architecture without becoming part of the mature formal Suite model;
- **DEFERRED** — the matter properly belongs to a later dedicated review rather than the Suite Reconciliation.

The review confirms that the major Suite-level issues were, in fact, resolved as they were encountered during Phases I–IV.
