## 1. Purpose — ESTABLISHED

The purpose of the Satoshium Suite Reconciliation is to reconcile the completed operational Satoshium Suite into one internally consistent institutional architecture.

The reconciliation examines the Suite as it now exists and identifies, evaluates, and deliberately resolves or preserves:

- contradictions;
- ambiguity;
- stale assumptions;
- terminology collisions;
- overlapping authority;
- inconsistent object definitions; and
- unresolved Suite-level architectural questions.

The objective is institutional coherence, not redesign.

---

## 2. Scope — ESTABLISHED

The reconciliation applies to the formal Satoshium Suite and its eight operational institutions:

- Atlas
- Navigator
- Certifier
- Registry
- Chronicle
- Anchor
- Beacon
- Attestor

Within that boundary, the reconciliation may address:

- institutional roles;
- authority boundaries;
- canonical responsibilities;
- canonical object ownership;
- Suite terminology;
- relationship semantics;
- lifecycle semantics;
- publication semantics;
- provenance;
- correction and supersession;
- Suite architecture;
- conceptual sequence;
- legacy architecture that conflicts with the current operational institutional architecture;
- stale current-state documentation directly affecting Suite coherence; and
- genuine unresolved Suite-level ambiguity.

---

## 3. Exclusions — ESTABLISHED

The reconciliation is not:

- a redesign of Satoshium;
- a Satoshium Universe-wide documentation review;
- the formal Interoperability Review;
- the Universe Mapper reconciliation;
- a general README cleanup;
- an opportunity to reopen settled institutional architecture without cause.

Aegis remains external/pre-Suite.

Universe-level documentation issues that are not necessary to resolve the formal Suite are deferred to:

**SATOSHIUM UNIVERSE DOCUMENTATION RECONCILIATION**

Detailed interface, implementation, handoff, compatibility, exchange-mechanics, and other primarily cross-institution technical questions are deferred to:

**INTEROPERABILITY REVIEW**

---

## 4. Methodology — ESTABLISHED

The governing reconciliation sequence is:

**CONFIRM → CLARIFY → RECONCILE → CORRECT → CHANGE**

The architecture is preserved whenever it can remain internally coherent.

A change occurs only when coherence cannot otherwise be maintained.

Substantive questions are handled one at a time.

For each material architectural issue:

1. State the issue.
2. Present existing architecture and evidence.
3. Identify the contradiction or ambiguity.
4. Present available interpretations where necessary.
5. Recommend the best reconciliation.
6. Explain the reasoning.
7. Obtain approval before establishing a material architectural decision.

Approved decisions remain established unless later evidence produces a genuine contradiction.

Routine documentation corrections implementing settled decisions may proceed without repeated approval.

---

## 5. Evidence Standard — ESTABLISHED

Reconciliation decisions are grounded in the strongest available existing Satoshium evidence.

Relevant evidence may include:

- current governing documentation;
- institutional landing pages;
- canonical object records;
- published production artifacts;
- controlled-value definitions;
- current repository documentation;
- approved prior architectural decisions;
- historical records where historical context is relevant;
- first production relationships and exercised lineage;
- explicit current institutional status.

Evidence is evaluated according to its actual authority, context, and historical position.

The reconciliation shall not silently resolve conflicting evidence.

Conflicts are identified and reconciled explicitly.

Historical material is not treated as current architecture merely because it remains published.

Current-state documentation does not automatically outrank stronger canonical or approved architectural evidence.

Reference does not transfer authority.

Production lineage demonstrates relationships but does not automatically establish a mandatory universal pipeline.

Institution-specific controlled values do not become Suite-wide terminology without explicit reconciliation.

---

## 6. Decision Classes — ESTABLISHED

The following decision classes govern reconciliation determinations:

### CONFIRMED
Existing architecture is coherent and remains authoritative without modification.

### CLARIFIED
Existing architecture remains valid, but its wording, scope, or boundary requires explanation.

### RECONCILED
Existing descriptions or architectural elements require a harmonized interpretation to remain coherent.

### CORRECTED
A current-state statement, status, label, document, or representation conflicts with already-established architecture and must be corrected.

### CHANGED
Existing functioning architecture must be modified because coherence cannot otherwise be maintained.

### RESERVED
An issue remains intentionally open because no present material contradiction requires final resolution.

### DEFERRED
The matter belongs to a later review or broader scope.

Where applicable, the destination should be identified as:

- Interoperability Review; or
- Satoshium Universe Documentation Reconciliation.

### HISTORICAL — PRESERVE
The material accurately represents an earlier state and remains preserved as historical documentation.

---

## 7. Completion Criteria — ESTABLISHED

The Satoshium Suite Reconciliation is complete only when:

- all eight institutional roles are coherent;
- canonical object ownership is coherent;
- controlled terminology is coherent;
- authority boundaries are coherent;
- relationship semantics are coherent;
- lifecycle and publication semantics are coherent;
- operational statuses are coherent;
- known Suite contradictions are resolved or explicitly reserved;
- unresolved interoperability matters are handed forward;
- Universe-wide documentation issues are excluded and handed forward; and
- no known material Suite-level contradiction remains silently unresolved.
