# Satoshium Suite Reconciliation — Aegis Boundary Preservation Record

**Date:** September 25, 2026  
**Phase:** Phase I — Establish the Reconciliation Baseline  
**Step:** 7  
**Status:** Proposed for Approval  
**Purpose:** Preserve the established boundary between Aegis and the formal Satoshium Suite.

---

## 1. Issue

The formal Suite roster has now been confirmed as eight institutions:

- Atlas
- Navigator
- Certifier
- Registry
- Chronicle
- Anchor
- Beacon
- Attestor

Aegis has historically existed within the broader Satoshium architecture but is explicitly classified as external/pre-Suite.

Step 7 therefore asks one narrow question:

> Does the current reconciliation preserve Aegis as external/pre-Suite, or is there evidence requiring its admission into the formal Suite?

This step addresses boundary preservation only.

It does not review Aegis internals, redesign Aegis, or determine Aegis interoperability.

---

## 2. Existing Baseline

The governing Suite Reconciliation framework establishes:

- Aegis is external/pre-Suite.
- Aegis is not one of the eight formal Suite institutions.
- The reconciliation must not silently move Aegis into the formal Suite.

The approved institutional roster likewise confirms exactly eight formal institutions and does not include Aegis.

---

## 3. Boundary Determination

No current evidence requires Aegis to be reclassified as a formal Suite institution.

Accordingly:

### Aegis remains external/pre-Suite.

This means:

- Aegis is not counted among the eight formal Suite institutions.
- Aegis does not receive a formal Suite canonical responsibility merely by historical association with Satoshium.
- Aegis does not acquire Suite institutional authority through documentation references, implementation history, or conceptual proximity.
- Aegis may interact with, precede, inform, or support Suite-related architecture without becoming part of the Suite roster.
- Any future proposal to admit Aegis into the formal Suite would require an explicit architectural decision.

---

## 4. Historical Preservation

This determination does not erase Aegis from Satoshium history.

Historical records describing Aegis as an early operational subsystem, governance-related mechanism, or pre-Suite architectural component should be preserved when historically accurate.

The reconciliation should distinguish between:

- historical descriptions of Aegis in the evolution of Satoshium; and
- current-state descriptions that incorrectly imply Aegis is one of the formal eight Suite institutions.

Historical accuracy should be preserved.

Current-state roster ambiguity should be corrected where necessary.

---

## 5. Documentation Rule

Where current Suite-level documentation lists or implies Aegis as a formal Suite institution, that documentation should be corrected.

Where documentation accurately describes Aegis as external, pre-Suite, historical, adjacent, or otherwise outside the formal institutional roster, it should be preserved.

Broader Aegis documentation outside the Suite scope is not reviewed merely because this boundary is being confirmed.

If a broader Universe-level page contains an Aegis description that is not necessary to resolve the Suite, it should be:

**FLAGGED FOR SATOSHIUM UNIVERSE DOCUMENTATION RECONCILIATION**

rather than pulled into this process.

---

## 6. Interoperability Rule

Any future question concerning detailed technical interaction between Aegis and formal Suite institutions belongs primarily to:

**INTEROPERABILITY REVIEW**

unless the interaction exposes a direct contradiction in Suite-level authority or institutional boundaries.

---

## 7. Determination

The Aegis external/pre-Suite boundary is preserved.

No Suite roster change is required.

No Aegis architectural redesign is required.

No historical rewrite is required.

**Recommended Classification:** CONFIRMED

**Step 7 Status:** PENDING APPROVAL
