### September 25, 2026

**Scope:** Formal Satoshium Suite only

---

## Purpose

This record establishes the canonical object ownership model for the eight formal institutions of the Satoshium Suite.

The objective of Step 9 was to determine, for each institution:

1. the institution's operational role;
2. the canonical object or objects it owns;
3. the institutional function performed around those objects;
4. the distinction between canonical objects and supporting artifacts, classifications, representations, or processes;
5. the authority boundary between the institution and referenced institutions;
6. any questions deliberately reserved for later architectural review.

This reconciliation documents the architecture that exists. It does not redesign the Suite.

---

# Governing Principles

The following principles govern this determination:

> **INSTITUTIONAL ROLE ≠ CANONICAL OBJECT ≠ OPERATIONAL FUNCTION**

> **REFERENCE DOES NOT TRANSFER AUTHORITY.**

> **CONNECTION ≠ IDENTITY.**

> **REFERENCE ≠ DERIVATION.**

> **REFERENCE ≠ SUPPORT.**

> **AUTHORITY ≠ ELIGIBILITY.**

> **ELIGIBILITY ≠ EVALUATION OUTCOME.**

> **EVALUATION OUTCOME ≠ TRUST STATEMENT.**

> **VALIDATION ≠ EVALUATION / ELIGIBILITY / CONFORMANCE.**

> **VALID ≠ PUBLISHED / TRUE / SUPPORTED.**

> **CONFORMANT ≠ PUBLISHED.**

> **CANONICAL CREATION ≠ LIFECYCLE ACTIVATION.**

> **LIFECYCLE ACTIVATION ≠ PUBLICATION.**

> **CORRECTION ≠ DELETION / VERSION / VERSIONING DECISION.**

> **SUPERSESSION ≠ MUTATION.**

> **CHANGED CONCLUSION = CHANGED CANONICAL STATEMENT.**

> **NOT-TESTED NEVER EQUALS PASS.**

**Institutional Role:** Authoritative Intelligence  
**Canonical Object:** **Jurisdiction Intelligence Record**  
**Canonical Package:** **Jurisdiction Intelligence Package**  
**Status:** Operational  
**Step 9 Determination:** **COMPLETE — APPROVED**

### Ownership

Atlas owns its **Jurisdiction Intelligence Records** and the governed package architecture through which those records are expressed and maintained.

The **Jurisdiction Intelligence Record** is the canonical Atlas intelligence object.

The **Jurisdiction Intelligence Package** is the coordinated publication and representation structure that carries the authoritative jurisdiction intelligence record.

### Canonical Package Components

A Jurisdiction Intelligence Package may include:

- canonical Markdown source layers;
- consolidated jurisdiction JSON;
- generation manifest;
- public presentation and navigation surfaces;
- Atlas-governed supporting documentation and metadata.

Supporting components such as:

- Evidence;
- Signals;
- Trust Dimensions;
- Profile;
- Metadata;
- Builder Mode;
- Change Log;

are components of the jurisdiction record/package architecture rather than independent competing canonical Atlas objects.

### Authority Boundary

Atlas remains authoritative for Atlas-governed jurisdiction intelligence.

Other Suite institutions may reference, register, preserve, integrity-reference, discover, evaluate, or orchestrate Atlas information without acquiring Atlas authority.

### Canonical Relationship

```text
Satoshium Atlas
        ↓ owns
Jurisdiction Intelligence Record
        ↓ expressed through
Jurisdiction Intelligence Package
        ├── Canonical Markdown source layers
        ├── Consolidated jurisdiction JSON
        ├── Generation manifest
        └── Public presentation / navigation
```

### Future Institutional Placement

**Reserved / not decided by Suite Reconciliation.**

This determination establishes the **Jurisdiction Intelligence Record** as Atlas's canonical object under the current eight-institution Suite architecture.

It does not determine whether Atlas must remain permanently within the formal Suite if a broader multi-engine Intelligence architecture is later established.

That question remains deferred for separate architectural review.

## 4. Satoshium Registry

**Institutional Role:** Canonical Registration / Public Catalog  
**Canonical Object:** **Satoshium Registry Entry (SREG)**  
**Classification Layer:** **Registry Record Type**  
**Referenced Object:** **Authoritative Source Record**  
**Public Layer:** **Registry Catalog**  
**Status:** Operational  
**Step 9 Determination:** **COMPLETE — APPROVED**

### Ownership

The **Satoshium Registry Entry (SREG)** is Registry's canonical operational object.

Registry owns:

- the SREG;
- the SREG identifier;
- Registry-controlled classification;
- Registry-owned metadata;
- Registry relationships;
- Registry versions;
- Registry status;
- Registry lifecycle;
- Registry publication state;
- Registry correction history;
- Registry Catalog representation.

### Canonical Registry Model

```text
Satoshium Registry
        ↓
Registry Entry (SREG)
        ↓
Registry Record Type
        ↓
Authoritative Source Record
```

### Distinctions

**Registry Record Type** is a classification applied to a SREG.  
It is not a separate canonical Registry object.

**Registry Catalog** is the public discovery and resolution layer over published SREGs.  
It does not replace the SREG or become authoritative for the underlying Source Record.

**Validation** is a Registry process applied to a SREG version.  
It does not certify the substantive truth of the Source Record.

**Publication** makes a validated SREG publicly discoverable.  
Publication does not create a separate Registry object.

**Registry Entry Version** is a governed state of the continuing SREG identity.  
A new version is not automatically a new SREG.

### Authority Boundary

The Source Institution retains authority over the **Authoritative Source Record**.

Registry may catalog, classify, validate, publish, version, correct, and publicly expose a Registry Entry without becoming the source institution.

> **Registry may catalog a source. It may not become the source merely by registering it.**

## 5. Satoshium Chronicle

**Institutional Role:** Historical Preservation  
**Canonical Object:** **Chronicle Entry**  
**Identifier:** **CHR-YYYY-NNNN**  
**Represented Subject:** **Qualifying Historical Occurrence**  
**Admission Rule:** **Preservation Eligibility**  
**Classification:** **Event Type**  
**Status:** Operational  
**Step 9 Determination:** **COMPLETE — APPROVED**

### Ownership

The **Chronicle Entry** is Chronicle's canonical historical-preservation object.

A Chronicle Entry represents one qualifying historical **Occurrence**.

The Entry is not the Occurrence itself.

### Canonical Chronicle Relationship

```text
Occurrence
    ↓
Preservation Eligibility
    ↓
Chronicle Entry
    ↓
Event Type / Provenance / Relationships / Verification / Validation
    ↓
Lifecycle / Versioning / Publication / Preservation
```

### Distinctions

**Occurrence** is the historical thing that happened.  
It is not Chronicle's canonical object.

**Preservation Eligibility** is the admission rule that determines whether a qualifying Occurrence should become a Chronicle Entry.

**Event Type** classifies a Chronicle Entry.  
It does not create a second canonical object.

**Verification** reviews Chronicle's historical representation.

**Validation** tests a Chronicle Entry Version against governing requirements.

**Publication** is a state transition.  
Validation PASS does not itself publish a Chronicle Entry.

**Chronicle Entry Version** is a governed revision beneath the stable CHR identity rather than a new Chronicle Entry identity.

### Representation Package

A published Chronicle Entry may be represented through a coordinated package including:

```text
CHR-YYYY-NNNN/
├── index.html
├── record.json
├── README.md
├── validation artifact
└── publication-gate artifact
```

Validation and Publication Gate artifacts support the Chronicle Entry but are not separate Chronicle canonical objects.

### Authority Boundary

Chronicle owns the historical-preservation representation embodied in the Chronicle Entry.

The institution that created or owns the referenced authoritative object retains substantive authority over that object.

Chronicle preserves when something happened without re-adjudicating the meaning or authority of the originating institutional object.

> **Chronicle records when. Atlas records what.**

## 8. Satoshium Attestor

**Institutional Role:** Governed Attestation & Rule-Constrained Evaluation  
**Canonical Objects:** **Attestation (ATT)** + **Trust Statement (TRST)**  
**Primary Canonical Institutional Output:** **Trust Statement**  
**Operational Function:** **Rule-Constrained Evaluation**  
**Attestation Identifier:** **ATT-YYYY-NNNN**  
**Trust Statement Identifier:** **TRST-YYYY-NNNN**  
**Status:** Operational  
**Step 9 Determination:** **COMPLETE — APPROVED**

### Ownership

Attestor deliberately owns two distinct canonical object classes:

1. **Attestation**
2. **Trust Statement**

These objects have separate canonical identities and separate identifier families.

They are not two states of one object.

### Canonical Attestor Flow

```text
Eligible Governed Inputs
        ↓
Attestation
        ↓
Rule-Constrained Evaluation
        ↓
Trust Statement
```

### Attestation

An **Attestation** is a governed, attributable assertion presented for evaluation.

It is an Attestor-owned canonical object.

### Rule-Constrained Evaluation

**Rule-Constrained Evaluation** is the governed institutional assessment performed between Attestation and Trust Statement.

It is an operational function.

It is not a third canonical Attestor object.

### Trust Statement

A **Trust Statement** is a governed, attributable, bounded Attestor conclusion.

It is an Attestor-owned canonical object and Attestor's primary canonical institutional output.

### Evaluation Outcome

Controlled evaluation outcomes may include values such as:

- supported;
- partially-supported;
- not-supported;
- contradicted;
- indeterminate.

An **Evaluation Outcome** is a controlled evaluation classification.

It is not a Trust Statement and not a separate canonical object.

### Lifecycle and Versioning

Attestations and Trust Statements each maintain their own governed lifecycle and version identity.

Permitted revisions preserve object identity.

Materially changed assertion:

```text
→ new Attestation
```

Materially changed conclusion:

```text
→ new Trust Statement
```

### Production Confirmation

The first production Attestor operation created:

- **ATT-2026-0001**
- **TRST-2026-0001**

Both are:

**Active · Published · V1.0**

The production Evaluation Outcome was:

The Trust Statement is **derived-from** the Attestation.

### Authority Boundary

Attestor owns:

- governed Attestations;
- Rule-Constrained Evaluation;
- Evaluation Outcomes;
- Trust Statements;
- Attestor lifecycle;
- versioning;
- correction;
- validation;
- conformance;
- publication.

Attestor does not independently:

- re-certify Certifier decisions;
- establish the substantive truth of Atlas intelligence;
- extend Anchor integrity beyond its defined representation;
- acquire Registry authority;
- acquire Chronicle historical authority;
- acquire Beacon discovery authority;
- replace source institutional authority.
