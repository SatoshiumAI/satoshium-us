Satoshium Suite

Satoshium Suite is the operational tool layer of the Satoshium ecosystem. It brings together systems for jurisdiction intelligence, protective services, certification, public records, historical preservation, anchoring, signaling, and attestation.

The Suite begins with tools already created and expands through a staged buildout of additional systems designed to support long-horizon infrastructure, trusted records, and decentralized intelligence services.

Suite Workflow

Satoshium Suite is designed as a coordinated sequence of tools. Each system has its own purpose, but together they create a larger operational pattern: protect, map, certify, catalog, remember, preserve, signal, and attest.

Aegis protects. Atlas maps. Certifier reviews. Registry catalogs. Chronicle remembers. Anchor preserves. Beacon signals. Attestor verifies.

Build Status

Satoshium Suite begins with Aegis and Atlas as existing operational surfaces. Certifier and Registry are partially created and scheduled for completion in July and August 2026. Chronicle, Anchor, Beacon, and Attestor are planned for staged completion from September through December 2026.
