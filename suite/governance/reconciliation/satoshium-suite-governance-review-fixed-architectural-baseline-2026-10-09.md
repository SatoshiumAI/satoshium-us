# No-Reopening Rule

The Governance Review must not reopen a settled Reconciliation decision merely because:

- an older landing page uses legacy terminology;
- a status page uses simplified shorthand;
- an institution-specific page describes an earlier conceptual model;
- implementation details are incomplete;
- interoperability hardening remains queued;
- governance mechanisms are being formalized after architecture was settled;
- a future capability could be useful;
- or a public description can be improved.

A settled architectural decision may be reconsidered only if the Governance Review identifies a genuine constitutional conflict that cannot be resolved while preserving the reconciled architecture.

Such a matter must be explicitly classified as:

```text
CONSTITUTIONAL CHANGE CANDIDATE
```

It may not be changed silently through governance documentation.
