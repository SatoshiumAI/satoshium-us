## Purpose

This record determines whether each of the eight formal Satoshium Suite institutions retains exclusive authority, within its settled institutional domain, over:

- its canonical objects;
- its institution-specific Rules;
- its lifecycle decisions;
- its Corrections;
- its Versioning;
- and its institution-specific conclusions or determinations.

This determination builds on the fixed architectural baseline and the completed Institutional Sovereignty Review.

---

# Meaning of “Exclusive Authority”

For this review, **exclusive authority** means:

> **Within the institution's settled domain, no other Suite institution may create, alter, approve, supersede, correct, version, or reinterpret an institution-owned object or institutional determination as though it were that institution's own action.**

Exclusive authority does **not** mean unlimited or unconstrained authority.

Each institution remains subject to:

- the settled Suite architecture;
- applicable Suite Standards;
- applicable Suite Methodology;
- Suite Governance;
- applicable Interoperability requirements;
- and any formally adopted cross-institution constraints.

Therefore:

```text
EXCLUSIVE INSTITUTIONAL AUTHORITY
≠
UNLIMITED AUTHORITY
```

and:

```text
SUITE CONSTRAINT
≠
SUITE OWNERSHIP
```

---

# General Authority Rule

The review adopts the following governing rule:

> **Each institution exclusively governs the objects, Rules, lifecycle decisions, Corrections, Versions, and substantive determinations that belong to its settled institutional responsibility. Other institutions may reference, evaluate, preserve, register, discover, orchestrate, or depend upon those results, but may not act as the originating authority for them.**

This rule is consistent with the completed Suite model:

```text
REFERENCE DOES NOT TRANSFER AUTHORITY

CONNECTION ≠ IDENTITY

AUTHORITY ≠ PROVENANCE

OVERSIGHT ≠ OPERATION

INTEROPERABILITY ≠ OWNERSHIP
```

Atlas maintains its own framework doctrine, methodology references, signal logic, jurisdiction structures, and rule boundaries.

**Determination:** EXCLUSIVE AUTHORITY — CONFIRMED AS ARCHITECTURAL RULE  
**Documentation maturity:** OBJECT-LEVEL LIFECYCLE MECHANISM NOT FULLY EVIDENCED ON THE SUPPLIED LANDING SURFACE

Atlas controls correction of Atlas-owned intelligence and representations.

A downstream institution may identify or reference an apparent Atlas issue but may not silently correct Atlas's authoritative source content as its own institutional action.

**Determination:** EXCLUSIVE AUTHORITY — CONFIRMED AS ARCHITECTURAL RULE  
**Documentation maturity:** DETAILED CORRECTION MECHANISM NOT ESTABLISHED BY THE SUPPLIED LANDING SURFACE

Atlas retains authority over versions or revisions of Atlas-owned intelligence packages and representations.

Downstream use may record which Atlas version was referenced but cannot create an Atlas version.

**Determination:** EXCLUSIVE AUTHORITY — CONFIRMED AS ARCHITECTURAL RULE

# Important Qualification

The review distinguishes:

```text
AUTHORITY
```

from:

```text
DOCUMENTATION MATURITY
```

Atlas, Navigator, and Beacon do not currently expose every governance mechanism on their supplied landing surfaces with the same operational depth as Registry, Chronicle, Anchor, and Attestor.

That is **not** evidence that another institution owns those functions.

It means only that the source set does not support claiming an equally mature documented mechanism.

Accordingly:

> **Where the detailed mechanism is not yet explicit, canonical ownership still determines institutional authority, but this review does not invent the missing procedure.**

---

# Suite-Level Constraint Rule

Exclusive institutional authority remains bounded by Suite Governance.

An institution may not use its exclusive domain authority to:

- change formal Suite membership;
- absorb another institution's canonical role;
- redefine Suite-wide terminology;
- alter another institution's canonical object;
- transfer Source Authority;
- contradict a binding Suite Standard;
- silently override shared Methodology;
- or reopen a settled Reconciliation decision.

If an institution proposes such a change, the matter becomes a Suite Governance issue.

---

# Governing Exclusive-Authority Rule

> **The institution that owns the canonical responsibility owns the institutional decision. The Suite constrains that authority; neighboring institutions may interact with its results; neither circumstance transfers the originating authority.**
