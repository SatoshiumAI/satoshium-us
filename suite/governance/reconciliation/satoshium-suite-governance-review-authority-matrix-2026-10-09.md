# Governance-Level Summary

## Suite / Constitutional Governance

Responsible for:

- formal Suite membership;
- institutional role boundaries;
- canonical-object ownership assignments;
- shared constitutional distinctions;
- material cross-institution authority changes;
- constitutional change.

Must not:

- operate institution-owned canonical objects;
- issue institution-specific conclusions;
- micromanage institution-specific procedures.

---

## Suite Standards Governance

Responsible for:

- ownership and stewardship of Suite Standards;
- Standards change management;
- Standards versioning;
- supersession;
- transparency;
- historical preservation of Standards.

Must not:

- operate institution-owned canonical objects;
- convert Standards into operational ownership.

---

## Suite Methodology

Responsible for:

- shared implementation principles;
- repeatable Suite-level methodological guidance.

Must not:

- replace institution-specific governance;
- replace institution-specific Procedures;
- replace institution-specific lifecycle, Validation, Conformance, or publication architecture.

---

## Suite Interoperability

Responsible for:

- compatibility;
- references;
- serialization;
- resolution;
- handoffs;
- state/version propagation;
- cross-institution exchange constraints.

Must not:

- transfer authority;
- redefine canonical ownership;
- redefine institutional roles;
- merge institutions.

---

## Institutional Governance

Responsible for:

- institution-specific Rules;
- Policies;
- Procedures;
- schemas;
- controlled values;
- object lifecycle;
- Corrections;
- Versioning;
- publication;
- institutional conclusions and determinations;
- internal delegation;
- internal accountability.

Must not:

- redefine Suite membership;
- redefine another institution's authority;
- claim another institution's canonical objects;
- override Suite-wide Standards or constitutional boundaries.

# Authority Escalation Rule

A matter remains institution-specific when:

```text
one institution
+ one settled institutional domain
+ no change to Suite membership
+ no change to role
+ no change to canonical ownership
+ no cross-institution authority impact
```

A matter escalates to Suite Governance when it affects:

```text
Suite membership
institutional role
canonical ownership
Suite-wide Standards
shared Methodology
cross-institution authority
constitutional distinctions
or settled Reconciliation decisions
```

---

# Governing Matrix Rule

> **Governance authority follows the governed subject. Suite-level subjects belong to Suite Governance. Institution-owned subjects belong to the owning institution. Interaction between levels constrains behavior but does not transfer ownership or operational authority.**
