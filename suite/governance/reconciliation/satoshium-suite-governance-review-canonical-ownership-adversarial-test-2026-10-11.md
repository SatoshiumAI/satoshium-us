# XIV. Invalid Ownership Change Rule

A claimed ownership change is invalid if:

- Constitutional review did not occur;
- the decision was misclassified as Routine or Material;
- approval authority was insufficient;
- ownership transfer is only implied;
- the change exists only in implementation;
- the change exists only in documentation;
- the transfer scope is unclear;
- historical treatment is absent.

In that case:

