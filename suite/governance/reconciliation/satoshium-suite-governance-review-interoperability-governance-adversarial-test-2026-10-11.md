## Scenario

Institution A consumes output from Institution B.

Over time, A's software begins rejecting B's output unless A's local rules are satisfied.

That operational validation is then described as:

```text
Institution A approves Institution B's output
```

### Risk

```text
consumer validation
→ becomes source authority
```

### Defense

The public interoperability model preserves independent canonical ownership and explicitly rejects authority transfer through reference. citeturn395462view0

A consuming institution may determine:

```text

```

It may not determine:

```text
whether the source institution's object is valid within the source institution's domain
```

### Determination

```text
ATTACK
→ BLOCKED
```
