## Purpose

This record adversarially tests whether the Satoshium Suite Governance model can accommodate a future **Institution #9** without requiring the constitutional governance model itself to be rewritten.

The central question is:

> **Could the Suite add Institution #9 in 2030 without rewriting the constitutional model?**

The determination is:

# YES.

The present constitutional model is **extensible by governed admission**.

A future institution would change the **constitutional state of the Suite**, but it would not require replacement of the **constitutional governance model** that governs how such a change occurs.


### Finding

This is a **wording pressure point**, not an architectural contradiction.

The intended meaning is current-state descriptive:


The enduring constitutional principles are:

```text

shared constitutional constraints
distinct canonical authority
interaction without authority transfer
```

The numeral reflects present membership.

It is not itself an immutable constitutional principle.

The doctrine should be interpreted as:

> **One Suite. Eight current sovereign institutions. Shared constitutional constraints. Distinct canonical authority. Interaction without authority transfer.**

If future membership changes:


while the rest of the doctrine survives unchanged.
