## Purpose

This register indexes the **material governance determinations** made during the Satoshium Suite Governance Review.

It implements the approved distinction:

```text
Governance Decision Record
→ authoritative record of one governance decision

Governance Decision Register
→ authoritative index of governance decisions
```

The existing review artifacts listed in this register preserve the full rationale, analysis, and operative determination for each entry.

The register does not create new substantive governance. It assigns stable decision identifiers and exposes the standing, classification, affected subjects, and decision lineage of determinations already approved during the review.

# I. Register Conventions

## Decision Identifier

Decision identifiers use:

```text
GDR-2026-NNNN
```

These identifiers are stable governance-decision identities.

## Classification

```text
MATERIAL
→ significant governance determination that does not itself reallocate constitutional role, ownership, membership, or sovereignty

CONSTITUTIONAL
→ determination governing foundational Suite architecture, authority, membership, canonical ownership, or constitutional change
```

## Status

All decisions in this register are:

```text
APPROVED
```

unless a later Governance Decision Record supersedes or corrects them.

## Effective State

Unless otherwise stated:

```text
EFFECTIVE
```

means the decision is part of the current Suite Governance Model as of October 14, 2026.
