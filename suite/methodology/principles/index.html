Methodology Principles

Methodology Principles establish the philosophical foundation that guides every Satoshium methodology. They ensure that every process is repeatable, transparent, objective, and worthy of long-term institutional trust.

Every lifecycle, workflow, evaluation, and certification process should align with these principles before it becomes part of the Satoshium Suite.

Evidence Before Assertion

Conclusions should be supported by verifiable evidence rather than opinion or assumption.

Transparency Before Authority

Processes should be understandable and reviewable rather than dependent upon hidden authority.

Repeatability Over Subjectivity

Equivalent evidence evaluated under equivalent conditions should produce equivalent outcomes.

Truth Before Trust

Trust is earned through faithful application of truth, not by declaration alone.

Consistency Across the Suite

Every subsystem should apply shared methodologies in a consistent manner.

Continuous Improvement

Methodologies should evolve through documented review while preserving historical integrity.

Methodology Role

These principles serve as the foundation beneath every Satoshium methodology. They guide how standards are interpreted, how evidence is evaluated, and how certification decisions are reached throughout the Suite.

Principles endure longer than procedures. Procedures may evolve, but they should always remain faithful to the principles from which they were derived.
Lasting methodology begins with enduring principles.
