Suite · Methodology

Certification Workflow

The Certification Workflow defines the operational sequence used to perform Certifier and certification-domain activities consistently, transparently, and repeatably. It is not a universal workflow for the Satoshium Suite.

Where the Certification Lifecycle describes the stages of a certification, the Workflow describes the work performed within those stages.

Scope Boundary

The Certification Workflow is a certification-specific methodology surface. It does not define the canonical workflow or lifecycle of every Suite institution.

Certification Workflow → Certifier / certification-domain methodology.
Navigator Workflow Definition → Navigator-owned canonical object.
Institutional Operations → governed by the institution that owns the work.

Conceptual Sequence ≠ Mandatory Pipeline.
Exercised Lineage ≠ Mandatory Architecture.
Workflow Participation ≠ Authority Transfer.

Submission Processing

Receive the certification request, establish scope, assign identifiers, and prepare the record for validation.

Validation

Verify completeness, required fields, identity, eligibility, and conformance with the selected certification standard.

Evidence Processing

Collect, organize, classify, reference, and prepare evidence for formal evaluation.

Evaluation

Apply evaluation criteria, scoring methodology, trust requirements, and certification logic to the submitted evidence.

Decision

Determine the appropriate certification outcome based upon documented standards and repeatable methodology.

Record Generation

Create structured certification records, metadata, verification identifiers, and supporting documentation.

Publication

Publish certification information under Certifier authority where governed publication is appropriate. Other Suite institutions may later register, preserve, reference, discover, or evaluate that information under their own authority.

Ongoing Maintenance

Monitor certifications for renewal, correction, suspension, revocation, expiration, or archival.

Cross-Institution Workflow Boundary

Certification activity may participate in broader Suite workflows, but downstream institutional participation is conditional and independently governed.

Certification → Registration → Preservation → Integrity → Discovery → Attestation
may describe an exercised lineage or workflow example.

It is not a mandatory universal pipeline.

Registry, Chronicle, Anchor, Beacon, Attestor, Navigator, and Atlas participate only where the applicable governed workflow or institution-specific rules require or permit it.

Navigator Boundary

Navigator owns Workflow Definition / Orchestration and the canonical Navigator Workflow Definition. The Certification Workflow is methodology, not a Navigator-owned canonical object.

Certification Workflow Methodology ≠ Navigator Workflow Definition.
Navigator Orchestration ≠ Certification Authority.

Methodology Role

The Certification Workflow transforms certification standards and methodology into repeatable Certifier practice. It defines how certification-domain work is performed without creating a universal Suite sequence or absorbing downstream institutional authority.

A lifecycle defines when certification stages occur.
A workflow defines how certification-domain work is performed within those stages.
Conceptual Sequence ≠ Mandatory Pipeline.
Repeatable workflows produce repeatable results.
