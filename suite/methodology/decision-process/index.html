Suite · Methodology

Decision Process

The Decision Process defines how Satoshium converts standards, evidence, evaluation criteria, scoring, and certification logic into official certification determinations.

Every certification decision should be understandable, repeatable, transparent, and supported by documented reasoning.

Decision Inputs

Certification decisions are based upon applicable standards, evidence, evaluation results, scores, trust assessments, and certification logic.

Decision Outcomes

Possible outcomes include: Certified Conditionally Certified Denied Suspended Revoked Expired Renewed Archived

Human Review

Methodology should support documented human review whenever professional judgment, exception handling, or appeals become necessary.

Automated Review

Where appropriate, deterministic certification logic may allow software to produce consistent certification decisions.

Appeals

Certification decisions may be reviewed when additional evidence, corrections, or procedural concerns are presented.

Decision Documentation

Every certification decision should include sufficient explanation to allow independent review of the reasoning.

Future AI Assistance

Artificial intelligence may assist with evidence review, summarization, and recommendation, while final decisions remain governed by published methodology.

Institutional Consistency

Equivalent evidence evaluated under equivalent standards should produce equivalent decisions regardless of reviewer.

Methodology Role

The Decision Process represents the final reasoning layer of the Satoshium Certification Methodology. It transforms standards, evidence, evaluation, logic, and scoring into transparent, documented, and reviewable certification outcomes.

A certification decision should never appear arbitrary. Every outcome should be explainable, reviewable, and reproducible.
Good methodology explains not only what was decided, but why it was decided.
