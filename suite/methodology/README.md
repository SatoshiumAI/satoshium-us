# Satoshium Suite — Methodology

**Path:** `/suite/methodology/`  
**Scope:** Suite-level methodology architecture  
**Canonical Page:** `index.html`

---

## Purpose

The Satoshium Methodology defines the repeatable processes used to apply Suite standards consistently, transparently, and objectively.

Standards define **what** must be achieved.

Methodology defines **how** those standards are implemented, evaluated, and maintained.

## Methodology Architecture

The Suite Methodology page organizes the following methodology areas.

### Methodology Principles

**Path:** `/suite/methodology/principles/`

Establishes the philosophical foundation guiding lifecycles, workflows, evaluations, and certification processes throughout the Suite.

### Certification Lifecycle

**Path:** `/suite/methodology/lifecycle/`

Defines the progression of a certification from submission through validation, evaluation, issuance, monitoring, renewal, retirement, and archive.

### Certification Workflow

**Path:** `/suite/methodology/workflow/`

Defines the operational sequence used to perform certification activities consistently, transparently, and repeatably.

### Evaluation Criteria

**Path:** `/suite/methodology/evaluation-criteria/`

Defines the objective measures used to assess certification targets consistently and transparently.

### Evidence Requirements

**Path:** `/suite/methodology/evidence-requirements/`

Defines the minimum information necessary to support objective, transparent, and repeatable certification decisions.

### Certification Logic

**Path:** `/suite/methodology/certification-logic/`

Defines the decision rules used to transform evidence, evaluation, and scoring into certification outcomes.

### Certification Schema

**Path:** `/suite/methodology/certification-schema/`

Defines the structured format used to represent certification records consistently throughout the Satoshium Suite.

### Decision Process

**Path:** `/suite/methodology/decision-process/`

Defines how Satoshium converts standards, evidence, evaluation criteria, scoring, and certification logic into official determinations.

## Methodology Role

The Satoshium Methodology transforms standards into repeatable practice.

Its purpose is to ensure that certification, attestation, registry-entry, and verification processes follow documented procedures that can be reviewed, reproduced, and improved over time.

> **Standards establish expectations. Methodology ensures those expectations are applied consistently.**

## Architectural Boundary

This Suite-level page is an organizational methodology surface. It does not replace the institution-specific governance, methodology, validation, conformance, lifecycle, publication, or production architecture of individual Satoshium institutions.

Where an institution has adopted more specific governed procedures, those institution-specific requirements govern that institution's operation.
