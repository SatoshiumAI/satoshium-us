Satoshium Certification Standard

The Satoshium Certification Standard defines the governed rule layer for certification expectations. Certifier implements those expectations and remains the Suite institution that exercises certification authority.

Other Suite institutions may reference or interact with certification records according to their own authority. Registration, preservation, integrity referencing, discovery, attestation, or workflow participation does not transfer Certifier authority.

Certification Authority Boundary

The Certification Standard defines certification expectations. It does not itself issue certifications or create generalized Suite-wide certification authority.

Certification Standard → defines certification expectations.
Certifier → exercises certification authority.
Certification Package → Certifier-owned canonical object.

Standard adoption ≠ authority transfer.
Reference ≠ authority transfer.
Downstream use ≠ certification ownership.

Certification Lifecycle

Defines the stages through which Certifier / certification-domain activity may progress, from submission and review through issuance, monitoring, renewal, suspension, revocation, expiration, or archival. It is not a universal Suite lifecycle.

Institutional

Cross-Institution Boundary

Certification objects may participate in broader Suite workflows without turning certification into the governing authority for Registry, Chronicle, Anchor, Beacon, Attestor, Navigator, or Atlas.

Conceptual Sequence ≠ Mandatory Pipeline.
Exercised Lineage ≠ Mandatory Architecture.
Reference ≠ Authority Transfer.

Each institution retains authority over its own canonical objects, lifecycle, publication state, correction, versioning, and institutional determinations.

Governance Boundary

The Certification Standard operates within settled Suite architecture. Changes to institutional role, canonical ownership, Suite membership, authority allocation, or constitutional dependency require Suite Governance / Architecture Reconciliation.

Certification Standard ≠ Suite Governance.
Standards Governance ≠ Constitutional Governance.

Standard Role

The Certification Standard is the governed rule surface for certification expectations. It supports consistent certification practice without turning certification into a shared authority layer across the Suite.

Standards define certification expectations.
Certification Methodology defines certification implementation.
Certifier performs certification and owns the Certification Package.
Certification becomes durable when the method is separate from the tool that applies it.
