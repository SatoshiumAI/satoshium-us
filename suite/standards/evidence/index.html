Satoshium Evidence Standard

The Satoshium Evidence Standard defines how evidence is recognized, evaluated, preserved, and used across the Satoshium Suite.

Evidence is the foundation of certification, attestation, preservation, registry records, discovery results, and public verification. The standard exists so claims can be reviewed through records rather than accepted by assertion.

Evidence Types

Defines recognized evidence categories, including primary sources, secondary sources, institutional records, self-attestations, cryptographic records, metadata, screenshots, archives, media, receipts, and witness statements.

Core

Evidence Quality

Defines how evidence is ranked by authority, independence, completeness, authenticity, timeliness, reproducibility, traceability, and resistance to alteration or misinterpretation.

Core

Evidence Requirements

Defines the minimum and recommended evidence expectations used during certification review, including required evidence, supporting evidence, disallowed evidence, and chain-of-custody concerns.

Methodology

Evidence Integrity

Defines how evidence should be preserved, referenced, hashed, time-associated, versioned, or linked to supporting records so later review can confirm what was evaluated.

Preservation

Evidence Sufficiency

Defines when evidence is sufficient to support a determination, when additional evidence is required, and when available evidence is inadequate for certification or attestation.

Review

Evidence Limitations

Defines how uncertainty, incomplete records, conflicting sources, stale evidence, unverifiable materials, and unsupported claims should be disclosed rather than hidden.

Transparency

Suite Tool Use

Defines how Certifier evaluates evidence, Anchor preserves evidence records, Attestor supports claims, Registry stores evidence references, Beacon discovers evidence-linked results, and Navigator queries them.

Interoperability

Evidence Record Structure

Defines the basic fields needed to describe evidence consistently, including source, type, authority, date, relationship to claim, preservation status, review notes, and public reference.

Schema

Standard Role

The Evidence Standard is the Suite-wide rule layer for determining whether a claim is supported, weakly supported, unsupported, contradicted, unverifiable, or preserved only as context.

Without evidence, certification becomes assertion. With evidence, certification becomes reviewable truth-work.
Satoshium begins with evidence because truth requires something that can be reviewed.
