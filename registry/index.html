Satoshium Registry

Satoshium Registry is the public record layer of the Satoshium ecosystem. It exists to identify, organize, and preserve the records created across Satoshium tools, jurisdictions, media, signals, certificates, and future attestations.

Registry does not create authority by itself. It records structure, status, and reference points so that Satoshium can preserve continuity over time.

Registry Workflow

Registry follows a simple organizational sequence: create, identify, categorize, record, catalog, and preserve. This workflow transforms scattered information into structured records that can be found, understood, and maintained later.

Atlas creates intelligence. Certifier reviews it. Registry catalogs it. Chronicle remembers it. Anchor preserves it. Attestor verifies it.
Satoshium Registry is being built as a public record layer — one reference at a time.
