Registry · Canonical Public Record Institution

Satoshium Registry

Satoshium Registry is the Suite institution responsible for canonical registration, durable SREG identity, Registry classification, provenance, validation, publication, versioning, corrections, lifecycle, public discovery, institutional history, and transparency.

Registry catalogs authoritative records without taking authority away from the institutions that created them. Its canonical operational object is the Satoshium Registry Entry, or SREG.

Institutional Position

Registry operates beneath Suite Standards, Suite Methodology, and Suite Interoperability. Those Suite-wide foundations define shared expectations, implementation principles, and institutional boundaries. Registry applies them through its own Scope, Definitions, Rules, Policies, Procedures, Governance, schemas, Controlled Values, records, and public operations.

Suite Standards → Suite Methodology → Suite Interoperability → Registry Implementation → SREG
Source Institutions retain authority over their Source Records. Registry retains authority over the SREG, its Registry Identifier, Registry classification, Registry-owned metadata, relationships, versions, status, lifecycle, publication, Catalog presentation, Corrections, and History.

Canonical Operational Hierarchy

Registry development centers on a four-layer model that preserves institutional separation while creating a durable public catalog.

Satoshium Registry → Registry Entry (SREG) → Registry Record Type → Authoritative Source Record
Registry owns the SREG and Registry-controlled representation. The Source Institution retains authority over the Authoritative Source Record.

Registry Method

Registry applies Suite Methodology through a repeatable institutional process designed for review, machine readability, interoperability, preservation, and maintenance.

Identify or receive source → confirm Source Authority → determine Scope and Registrability → assign Record Type and Registry Identifier → preserve Source-System Identifier → establish provenance and relationships → construct and validate SREG → publish → maintain versions, Status, Lifecycle, Corrections, Catalog presence, History, and Transparency.

Suite Relationship

Registry strengthens Satoshium Suite by connecting authoritative institutional objects without replacing the institutions that created them.

  • Atlas creates jurisdiction intelligence and canonical jurisdiction resources.
  • Certifier creates certifications and canonical certification artifacts.
  • Registry creates SREGs and maintains the canonical public Catalog.
  • Chronicle preserves Chronicle-created historical events and institutional chronology.
  • Anchor creates integrity references and durable verification records.
  • Beacon creates signals and discovery metadata.
  • Attestor creates attestations and trust statements.
  • Navigator coordinates workflows and cross-system operational activity.
Registry preserves discovery by preserving the path back to authority.
