Satoshium Registry

Satoshium Registry is the authoritative public catalog of institutional records created throughout the Satoshium Suite. It identifies, classifies, references, connects, and preserves the discoverability of records without taking authority away from the institutions that created them.

Registry implements Satoshium Suite Standards through documented Registry methodology, interoperable institutional boundaries, and the canonical Satoshium Registry Entry, or SREG.

Institutional Position

Registry operates within the Suite-wide constitutional hierarchy. Suite Standards define shared expectations. Suite Methodology defines repeatable implementation. Suite Interoperability preserves institutional boundaries and shared references. Registry applies those foundations through its own rules, policies, procedures, schemas, lifecycle, and public entries.

Suite Standards → Suite Methodology → Suite Interoperability → Registry Implementation → SREG
Institutions own their authoritative records. Registry creates and maintains independent SREGs that catalog those records, preserve their relationships, and provide durable public discovery.

Canonical Operational Hierarchy

Registry development centers on a four-layer operational model that preserves institutional separation while creating a durable public catalog.

Satoshium Registry → Registry Entry (SREG) → Registry Record Type → Authoritative Source Record
The source institution retains authority over the source record. Registry retains authority only over the SREG, its Registry identifier, classification, Registry status, Registry relationships, version history, and public catalog presentation.

Registry Method

Registry applies Suite Methodology Principles through a repeatable institutional process designed for public review, machine readability, interoperability, preservation, and long-term maintenance.

Identify or receive source record → confirm source authority → determine registrability → assign record type → create Registry identifier → establish source and relationship references → validate SREG schema → publish → maintain lifecycle, versions, corrections, and archival continuity.

Suite Relationship

Registry strengthens the Suite by connecting authoritative institutional objects without replacing them.

  • Atlas creates jurisdiction intelligence and canonical jurisdiction resources.
  • Certifier creates certifications and the canonical Certification Package.
  • Registry creates SREGs that catalog authoritative records.
  • Chronicle preserves historical events and institutional chronology.
  • Anchor preserves integrity references and durable verification points.
  • Beacon publishes discovery signals and discovery metadata.
  • Attestor creates trust statements and attestations.
  • Navigator coordinates workflows and cross-system operational activity.
Registry preserves discovery by preserving the path back to authority.
