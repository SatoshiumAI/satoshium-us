Registry · Records

Registry Records

Registry Records identify, classify, catalog, and preserve information across the Satoshium ecosystem.

Records are the primary operational artifacts maintained by Registry. They provide structured references that support discoverability, continuity, organization, and interoperability.

Tools

Tool records describe ecosystem applications, services, frameworks, and systems, including Atlas, Certifier, Registry, Chronicle, Anchor, and Attestor.

Jurisdictions

Jurisdiction records describe geographic and governmental entities such as countries, states, provinces, territories, regions, and future jurisdiction categories.

Media

Media records describe videos, images, thumbnails, audio resources, media pages, documents, and public-facing content.

Signals

Signal records document public signals, announcements, discovery artifacts, historical markers, and notable informational references.

Certifications

Certification records document certification activities, reports, receipts, standards, certified items, and related evidence references.

Attestations

Attestation records document future verification, validation, integrity confirmation, and attestation-related activities.

What Records Answer

Records exist to answer several important questions: what exists, what category it belongs to, what its current status is, what it is related to, and how it can be found later.

Registry is not merely a list. It is a structured reference framework designed to preserve the relationship between information, status, context, and time.

Record Lifecycle

Registry records may move through a simple lifecycle: create, classify, catalog, maintain, and preserve.

Create → Classify → Catalog → Maintain → Preserve

Records Philosophy

Records are more than entries. They are reference points that help preserve context, relationships, and continuity across time.

Registry provides the framework. Records provide the content. Together they create an organized public record system.
