Satoshium Registry

Satoshium Registry is the public record layer of Satoshium. It exists to identify, organize, and preserve the records created across Satoshium tools, jurisdictions, media, signals, certificates, and future attestations.

Registry does not create authority by itself. It records structure, status, and reference points so that Satoshium can preserve continuity over time.

Public Record Layer

Registry provides a public place for Satoshium records to be identified, organized, referenced, and preserved.

Continuity Over Time

Registry helps preserve the relationship between tools, jurisdictions, media, signals, certificates, and future attestations as the Satoshium ecosystem grows.
