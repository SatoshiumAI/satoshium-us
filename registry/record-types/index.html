Registry Record Types

Registry organizes information through defined record types. Record types establish structure, improve discoverability, and support interoperability across the Satoshium ecosystem.

Each Registry record belongs to one primary record type. Additional categories may be introduced as Registry evolves.

Tool Records

Tool Records document systems, applications, services, frameworks, and ecosystem components such as Atlas, Certifier, Registry, Chronicle, Anchor, and Attestor.

Jurisdiction Records

Jurisdiction Records document geographic and governmental entities, including countries, states, provinces, territories, regions, and future jurisdiction classes.

Media Records

Media Records document public media resources such as videos, images, thumbnails, audio resources, media pages, and future media assets.

Signal Records

Signal Records document formal public signals, announcements, discovery records, historical markers, and other notable informational artifacts.

Certification Records

Certification Records document certification activities and outputs, including reports, receipts, certified items, standards, and determinations.

Attestation Records

Attestation Records document future verification and attestation activities, including integrity confirmations, verification references, and evidence relationships.

Additional Record Types

Registry may also support Historical Records, Reference Records, Schema Records, and Policy Records.

Historical Records preserve milestones and significant ecosystem events. Reference Records connect related resources. Schema Records define record structures. Policy Records document operational guidance.

Registry is designed to evolve. Future categories may include research records, evidence records, preservation records, governance records, network records, and AI agent records.

Record Type Relationships

Record types help Registry preserve relationships between tools, jurisdictions, media, signals, certifications, attestations, and historical references.

Tools may connect to certifications, media, signals, attestations, and references. Jurisdictions may connect to media, signals, and Atlas records. Historical records may connect to Chronicle, Anchor, and Registry references.

Why Record Types Matter

Information may take many forms. Registry exists to organize those forms into understandable records.
