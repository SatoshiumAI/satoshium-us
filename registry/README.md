# Satoshium Registry

**Structure through records.**

Satoshium Registry is the public record framework of the Satoshium ecosystem.

Registry exists to identify, organize, catalog, and preserve records created across Satoshium tools, jurisdictions, media, signals, certifications, and future attestations.

While other subsystems create information, review information, preserve information, or verify information, Registry provides the structured reference layer that makes those records discoverable and maintainable over time.

---

## Purpose

Digital systems continuously create new information, documents, services, media, certifications, evidence, and historical records.

Without organization, records become difficult to locate, understand, reference, and preserve.

Registry exists to answer fundamental questions:

* What exists?
* What is its status?
* Where is it located?
* How is it categorized?
* How does it relate to other records?
* Can it be found again later?

By creating structured registry records, Registry transforms information into an organized and navigable public record system.

---

## Core Mission

The mission of Satoshium Registry is to provide a durable framework for cataloging and preserving records across the Satoshium ecosystem.

Registry seeks to increase discoverability, continuity, organization, transparency, and long-term accessibility.

## Registry Classes

Registry uses three primary record classes:

### Reference

A record identifies and references an existing resource.

### Cataloged

A record has been categorized and organized within Registry.

### Preserved

A record has been cataloged and incorporated into the broader historical structure of the ecosystem.

---

## Registry Workflow

```text
Create
  ↓
Identify
  ↓
Categorize
  ↓
Record
  ↓
Catalog
  ↓
Preserve
```

This workflow transforms information into a structured Registry record that can be referenced and discovered later.

---

## Registry Outputs

A registry event may produce:

* Registry Record
* Registry Entry
* Catalog Reference
* Classification Record
* Cross References
* Historical References

These outputs provide both human-readable and machine-readable records for long-term organization.

## Relationship to the Satoshium Ecosystem

Registry operates as one subsystem within the broader Satoshium ecosystem.

```text
Atlas
  ↓
Certifier
  ↓
Registry
  ↓
Chronicle
  ↓
Anchor
  ↓
Attestor
```

### Atlas

Creates and organizes jurisdiction intelligence and other information resources.

### Certifier

Reviews and certifies outputs against established standards.

### Registry

Catalogs records, references, classifications, and ecosystem resources.

### Chronicle

Records milestones, development history, and significant events.

### Anchor

Preserves hashes, integrity references, and permanence records.

### Attestor

Supports future independent verification and attestation activities.

## Disclaimer

Satoshium Registry is an informational record framework.

Registry entries document references, classifications, relationships, and status information based on available information at the time of record creation.

Registry records do not create authority, ownership, certification, legal rights, or verification by themselves.
