# Registry Institutional Objects

The canonical operational object of Registry is the **Satoshium Registry Entry**, or **SREG**.

A SREG identifies, classifies, references, and connects an authoritative source record.

A SREG may include Registry-owned information such as:

* Registry identifier
* Registry record type
* Registry status
* Registry lifecycle state
* Registry relationships
* Registry version
* Registry correction history
* public references
* source-system metadata
* source-record metadata

The MIT License may apply to the structure, schema, software, documentation, and Registry-authored content associated with a SREG.

The license does not automatically apply to the underlying authoritative source record referenced by that SREG.
