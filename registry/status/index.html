Satoshium Registry Status

Registry Status defines both the current development condition of the Satoshium Registry and the operational status model used to track Registry Entries over time.

The central distinction is simple: Certifier owns certification status. Registry owns registry status.

Current Status

Initial Framework Development

Registry currently exists as a developing public record framework designed to support discoverability, continuity, organization, and preservation across the Satoshium ecosystem.

Current Priorities

Core documentation, Registry Entry definitions, lifecycle states, schemas, record types, policies, organizational structures, and public Registry pages.

Current Capabilities

Registry currently supports conceptual development of record identification, classification, lifecycle planning, relationship mapping, and ecosystem integration.

Current Limitations

Registry remains in an early stage of development with evolving schemas, classifications, interoperability requirements, and operational record populations.

Status Ownership

Status values must remain attached to the system that owns their meaning. Certifier determines certification outcomes and certification status. Registry determines the status of Registry Entries.

Certification Outcome: Certified
Certification Status: Active
Registry Status: Registered

Certification Outcome

Certification Outcome describes the result of Certifier’s evaluation. It belongs to Certifier, not Registry.

Certification Status

Certification Status describes the current condition of the certification as maintained by Certifier.

Registry Status

Registry Status describes the current condition of the SREG Registry Entry created by Registry.

SREG Context

Registry may reference certification outcome and certification status, but the SREG maintains its own Registry Status.

Registry Lifecycle States

Registry uses lifecycle states to describe the institutional condition of Registry Entries over time.

Pending Registration → Registered → Active → Updated → Superseded → Revoked → Archived

Pending Registration

The entry has been identified for registration but has not yet been formally entered into the Registry.

Registered

The entry has been formally created within Registry and assigned its Registry Identifier.

Active

The entry is current, discoverable, and recognized as the active Registry representation of the referenced record.

Updated

The entry has been modified to reflect new information, corrected references, revised metadata, or Registry-maintained changes.

Superseded

The entry has been replaced by a newer Registry Entry or version while remaining preserved for historical reference.

Revoked

The entry has been withdrawn from active recognition due to invalidation, reversal, material error, loss of source authority, or another stated reason.

Archived

The entry is no longer active but remains preserved as part of the historical Registry record.

Archived does not mean deleted. It means preserved outside active operational use.

Relationship to Certifier

Registry receives the SCRD from Certifier and creates an independent SREG Registry Entry. Certifier remains the authority for certification. Registry becomes the permanent public catalog of certified records and their lifecycle state.

Certification Package → SCRD → SREG

Relationship to the Ecosystem

Registry serves as a connecting layer across the broader Satoshium ecosystem.

Atlas creates intelligence. Certifier reviews it. Registry catalogs it. Chronicle remembers it. Anchor preserves it. Attestor verifies it.

Future Milestones

Future milestones may include expanded record populations, machine-readable schemas, validation frameworks, preservation references, relationship mapping, lifecycle automation, and cross-system interoperability.

Registry is being built deliberately, with long-term continuity prioritized over rapid expansion.
