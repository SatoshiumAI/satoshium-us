Satoshium Registry Integration

Registry Integration defines how Satoshium Registry receives, catalogs, preserves, and maintains records generated by other Satoshium operational systems.

Registry does not create certification authority. It preserves public record continuity by registering certified records, maintaining authoritative references, and making those records discoverable throughout their lifecycle.

Purpose

Registry Integration establishes the relationship between Registry and the operational systems that create public records across the Satoshium Suite.

Each subsystem remains authoritative for the records it creates. Registry becomes responsible for cataloging those records and preserving their public references.

Primary Principle

Registry preserves records. It does not create their authority.

Certifier certifies. Registry registers. Chronicle remembers. Anchor preserves. Beacon discovers. Attestor verifies. Navigator orchestrates.

Certifier Integration

After Certifier completes a certification, it produces a canonical Certification Package and associated certification artifacts.

Registry receives the Certified Record (SCRD) generated from Certifier's canonical Certification Package and creates an independent Registry Entry (SREG) that catalogs and references that certification without replacing it.

Registry Responsibilities

  • Receive completed public records.
  • Assign Registry identifiers.
  • Catalog certified records.
  • Preserve record relationships.
  • Maintain lifecycle status.
  • Support public discovery.

Operational Flow

Registry receives operational outputs after they become official public records within their originating subsystem.

Atlas creates intelligence → Certifier evaluates → Certification Package becomes Certifier's canonical operational record → SCRD is published → Registry creates SREG.

What Registry Receives from Certifier

Registry references the Certification Package and related artifacts, but the primary artifact registered by Registry is the SCRD.

Certification Package = Certifier's canonical operational record SCPR = Certification Process Report SCR = Certification Receipt SCRD = Canonical Certified Record produced by Certifier SREG = Registry Entry created by Registry that catalogs the SCRD

Authority Boundaries

Registry preserves institutional boundaries by ensuring each operational system remains authoritative only for the records it creates.

Atlas owns jurisdiction intelligence. Certifier owns certification. Registry owns registration. Chronicle owns historical chronology. Anchor owns preservation references. Attestor owns verification. Navigator owns orchestration.

Integration Philosophy

Integration should strengthen institutional independence rather than merge responsibilities. Registry connects systems through shared public records while preserving provenance, authority, and continuity.

Registry is not another certification system. It preserves discoverability by cataloging certification records while leaving certification authority with Certifier.
