# Satoshium Registry Record Types

## Overview

Registry organizes information through defined record types.

Record types establish structure, improve discoverability, and support interoperability across the Satoshium ecosystem.

Each Registry record belongs to one primary record type.

Additional categories may be introduced as Registry evolves.

---

# Tool Records

Tool Records document systems, applications, services, frameworks, and ecosystem components.

Examples include:

- Atlas
- Certifier
- Registry
- Chronicle
- Anchor
- Attestor
- Future Satoshium tools

Tool Records provide structured references to operational ecosystem resources.

---

# Jurisdiction Records

Jurisdiction Records document geographic and governmental entities.

Examples include:

- Countries
- States
- Provinces
- Territories
- Regions
- Future jurisdiction classifications

Jurisdiction Records support Atlas and related intelligence resources.

---

# Media Records

Media Records document public media resources.

Examples include:

- Videos
- Thumbnails
- Images
- Audio resources
- Media pages
- Future media assets

Media Records improve discoverability and reference management.

---

# Signal Records

Signal Records document formal public signals and notable communications.

Examples include:

- Public signals
- Announcements
- Discovery records
- Historical markers
- Future signaling mechanisms

Signal Records preserve important informational references.

---

# Certification Records

Certification Records document certification activities and outputs.

Examples include:

- Certification reports
- Certification receipts
- Certified items
- Certification standards
- Certification determinations

Certification Records are expected to originate primarily from Certifier.

---

# Attestation Records

Attestation Records document future verification and attestation activities.

Examples include:

- Attestation reports
- Verification references
- Integrity confirmations
- Independent attestations

Attestation Records are expected to expand as Attestor develops.

---

# Historical Records

Historical Records preserve notable milestones and events.

Examples include:

- Launch milestones
- Major releases
- Ecosystem events
- Significant development activities

Historical Records support continuity and long-term reference.

---

# Reference Records

Reference Records provide structured links between related resources.

Examples include:

- Cross-references
- Relationship mappings
- Resource indexes
- Discovery references

Reference Records improve navigation throughout Registry.

---

# Schema Records

Schema Records define record structures used throughout Registry.

Examples include:

- Record schemas
- Metadata schemas
- Classification schemas
- Interoperability schemas

Schema Records support consistency and standardization.

---

# Policy Records

Policy Records document governance and operational guidance.

Examples include:

- Creation policies
- Update policies
- Retirement policies
- Correction policies

Policy Records establish expectations for Registry operations.

---

# Future Record Types

Registry is designed to evolve.

Additional record types may be introduced as new ecosystem requirements emerge.

Potential future categories include:

- Research Records
- Evidence Records
- Preservation Records
- Governance Records
- Network Records
- AI Agent Records

Future additions should remain consistent with Registry's organizational mission.

---

# Record Type Relationships

```text
Tools
├── Certifications
├── Media
├── Signals
├── Attestations
└── References

Jurisdictions
├── Media
├── Signals
└── References

Historical Records
├── Chronicle
├── Anchor
└── Registry References
```

These relationships help create a structured and interconnected record framework.

> Information may take many forms.
>
