# Satoshium Registry

## Overview

Satoshium Registry is the public record layer of the Satoshium ecosystem.

Its purpose is to identify, organize, classify, catalog, and preserve records across tools, jurisdictions, media, signals, certifications, attestations, and future ecosystem resources.

Registry serves as the structured reference framework that connects information across time.

---

## Mission

The mission of Registry is to provide a durable and discoverable record framework that supports continuity, organization, preservation, and interoperability throughout the Satoshium ecosystem.

Registry transforms collections of information into structured records that can be located, understood, and referenced in the future.

---

## Why Registry Exists

Information is created continuously.

Repositories expand.

Media grows.

Certifications accumulate.

Historical milestones occur.

Without structure, records become difficult to discover and maintain.

Registry exists to provide:

- Organization
- Classification
- Discoverability
- Continuity
- Preservation
- Interoperability

---

## Registry Philosophy

Registry is built upon a simple principle:

Information is more useful when it can be found.

Registry seeks to preserve relationships between records so information remains understandable long after its original creation.

---

## Core Functions

Registry performs several primary functions.

### Identification

Records establish that a resource exists.

### Classification

Records assign resources to structured categories.

### Cataloging

Records are organized into discoverable collections.

### Cross-Referencing

Relationships between records are documented.

### Preservation

References remain available for future discovery and review.

---

## Registry Workflow

```text
Create
  ↓
Identify
  ↓
Categorize
  ↓
Record
  ↓
Catalog
  ↓
Preserve
```

This workflow transforms information into a structured Registry record.

---

## Record Categories

Initial Registry categories include:

### Tools

Applications, systems, frameworks, and ecosystem services.

### Jurisdictions

Countries, states, regions, territories, and related resources.

### Media

Videos, images, thumbnails, audio, and media pages.

### Signals

Formal public signals, announcements, and informational references.

### Certifications

Certification records and certification-related resources.

### Attestations

Future verification and attestation records.

### Historical Records

Milestones, releases, and significant ecosystem events.

---

## Registry Classes

Registry currently supports three primary classes.

### Reference

A resource has been identified.

### Cataloged

A resource has been organized within Registry.

### Preserved

A resource has been incorporated into the broader historical framework.

## Long-Term Vision

The long-term objective of Registry is to provide a durable public record framework capable of supporting future generations of information, resources, references, certifications, and historical records.

Registry seeks to become the connective layer that helps maintain continuity across the broader Satoshium ecosystem.

---

## Disclaimer

Registry is an informational record framework.

Registry records information, classifications, references, and relationships.

Registry does not independently establish ownership, authority, certification, verification, legal rights, or truth.
