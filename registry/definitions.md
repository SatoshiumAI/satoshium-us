# Satoshium Registry Definitions

This document defines foundational terminology used throughout the Satoshium Registry framework.

These definitions establish a common vocabulary for records, classifications, references, and future interoperability across the Satoshium ecosystem.

---

# Registry

Registry is the public record layer of the Satoshium ecosystem.

Its purpose is to identify, organize, catalog, classify, and preserve records over time.

Registry does not create authority, ownership, certification, or verification.

Registry records information and relationships.

---

# Record

A Record is a structured entry maintained by Registry.

A record may reference:

- Tools
- Jurisdictions
- Media
- Signals
- Certifications
- Attestations
- Historical events
- Future record categories

Records serve as durable reference points.

---

# Registry Entry

A Registry Entry is the published representation of a record.

Registry entries provide discoverable references and associated metadata.

---

# Classification

Classification is the process of assigning a record to a defined category.

Classifications improve organization, discoverability, and interoperability.

---

# Category

A Category is a grouping of similar records.

Examples include:

- Tools
- Jurisdictions
- Media
- Signals
- Certifications
- Attestations

Future categories may be added over time.

---

# Reference

A Reference is a connection between a Registry record and another resource.

References may point to:

- Documents
- Pages
- Repositories
- Reports
- Certifications
- Historical records
- External resources

---

# Cross-Reference

A Cross-Reference is a relationship between two or more Registry records.

Cross-references improve navigation and contextual understanding.

---

# Schema

A Schema defines the structure of a Registry record.

Schemas establish consistency across record types and future implementations.

---

# Metadata

Metadata is descriptive information associated with a Registry record.

Examples include:

- Identifier
- Title
- Category
- Status
- Creation date
- Modification date
- Associated references

---

# Identifier

An Identifier is a unique value assigned to a Registry record.

Identifiers allow records to be referenced consistently over time.

---

# Status

Status describes the current condition of a Registry record.

Examples may include:

- Draft
- Active
- Superseded
- Retired
- Archived

---

# Historical Reference

A Historical Reference preserves a connection to prior records, versions, events, or milestones.

Historical references support continuity across time.

---

# Preservation

Preservation is the practice of maintaining records for future discovery, review, and reference.

Preservation supports continuity and institutional memory.

---

# Discoverability

Discoverability is the ability to locate, identify, and understand a record efficiently.

Registry is designed to maximize discoverability.

---

# Interoperability

Interoperability is the ability of Registry records to function alongside other Satoshium systems.

Examples include:

- Atlas
- Certifier
- Chronicle
- Anchor
- Attestor

---

# Continuity

Continuity is the preservation of relationships, history, references, and context across time.

Continuity is one of the primary objectives of Registry.

---

# Registry Principle

Registry exists to ensure that information can be found, understood, referenced, and preserved long after its original creation.
