Satoshium Registry Rules

Registry Rules establish the foundational operating principles for identifying, organizing, maintaining, correcting, preserving, and managing records across the Satoshium ecosystem.

The objective is to create a structured, discoverable, and maintainable public record framework that remains useful over time.

Records Information

Registry records information. It does not create authority, ownership, certification, verification, legal rights, or truth.

Records Must Be Identifiable

Every Registry record should be identifiable through titles, identifiers, classifications, statuses, references, or other descriptive metadata.

Records Must Be Classifiable

Each record should belong to at least one defined record category such as tools, jurisdictions, media, signals, certifications, or attestations.

Records Should Be Discoverable

Registry prioritizes discoverability. Records should be structured so users can locate, understand, and navigate information efficiently.

References Should Be Preserved

Meaningful references should be preserved whenever practical because references provide context, continuity, and long-term value.

Historical Continuity Matters

When records change, Registry seeks to preserve historical context whenever practical rather than unnecessarily erasing prior information.

Additional Principles

Registry encourages transparency, cross-references, schema consistency, interoperability, future compatibility, documentation, and preservation.

Preservation over perfection. Documentation over memory. Continuity over fragmentation. These principles help guide Registry decisions.

Registry Philosophy

Registry exists to create structure. Structure improves discovery. Discovery improves understanding. Understanding improves continuity. Continuity improves preservation.

Record what exists. Organize what is recorded. Preserve what is organized. Allow future generations to find it again.

Purpose of the Rules

Registry Rules define expectations rather than procedures. Policies define implementation. Together they create a durable framework for managing records.
