# Registry Policies

## Overview

The policies directory contains operational guidance governing how Registry records are created, maintained, corrected, updated, retired, and preserved.

Policies provide consistency across Registry activities and establish expectations for future record management.

As Registry evolves, additional policies may be introduced to support new record types, workflows, and interoperability requirements.

---

## Purpose

Policies exist to answer several important questions:

- How are records created?
- How are records updated?
- How are corrections handled?
- How are records retired?
- How is continuity preserved?
- How should future changes be managed?

Policies provide structure for answering these questions consistently.

---

This directory is expected to contain the following foundational policy documents:

```text
policies/

├── record-retirement-policy.md

```

Additional policies may be introduced over time.

---

## Policy Objectives

Registry policies are designed to support:

### Consistency

Records should be managed using predictable processes.

### Transparency

Significant changes should be understandable and traceable.

### Continuity

Historical context should be preserved whenever practical.

### Discoverability

Policies should support long-term usability of Registry records.

### Interoperability

Policies should remain compatible with the broader Satoshium ecosystem.

---

## Relationship to Registry Rules

Registry Rules establish foundational principles.

Registry Policies establish operational procedures.

In simple terms:

```text
Rules define expectations.
Policies define implementation.
```

Both are important components of Registry governance.

## Future Policy Areas

Future Registry policy categories may include:

- Metadata policies

Future additions should remain aligned with Registry's organizational mission.

## Policy Philosophy

Policies should help maintain order without creating unnecessary complexity.

The objective is to support long-term continuity, discoverability, and preservation while allowing Registry to evolve over time.

---

## Guiding Statement

> Records require structure.
>
> Structure requires consistency.
