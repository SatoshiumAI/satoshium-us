# Rules

**Establishing consistency through shared standards.**

Rules define the principles, expectations, and operational standards that guide the use of Satoshium Navigator.

Navigator is designed to support transparent exploration, retrieval, comparison, and analysis of information. Rules help ensure that these activities remain consistent, understandable, and aligned with the broader objectives of the Satoshium ecosystem.

Rules provide structure to exploration.

---

## Purpose

Information systems become more useful when consistent standards exist for how information is retrieved, organized, analyzed, and presented.

Without rules, users may encounter inconsistent methodologies, unclear expectations, fragmented outputs, or reduced transparency.

Rules exist to promote consistency, accountability, interoperability, and trust throughout Navigator.

---

## Core Mission

The mission of Rules is to establish clear and transparent standards that support reliable exploration and interpretation of information.

Rules seek to improve consistency while preserving flexibility, accessibility, and openness to inquiry.

---

## Foundational Principles

All Navigator activities should seek to uphold several foundational principles:

* Transparency over opacity
* Evidence over assumption
* Context over isolation
* Consistency over ambiguity
* Traceability over uncertainty
* Accessibility over complexity
* Understanding over confusion

These principles guide both system design and user interaction.

---

## Information Rules

Navigator seeks to operate according to the following information standards:

### Source Awareness

Information should maintain connections to underlying records whenever practical.

Users should be able to understand where information originates.

---

### Context Preservation

Information should be presented with sufficient context to support interpretation.

Context reduces the risk of misunderstanding or misrepresentation.

---

### Traceability

Information should remain connected to supporting records, references, and related resources whenever possible.

Traceability improves transparency and accountability.

---

### Documentation

Significant methodologies, classifications, and structures should be documented and accessible.

Documentation improves consistency and understanding.

---

## Query Rules

Queries should seek to:

* Retrieve information transparently
* Preserve record relationships
* Apply filters consistently
* Follow documented methodologies
* Generate understandable outputs

Queries should prioritize clarity and repeatability.

---

## Comparison Rules

Comparisons should seek to:

* Use consistent criteria
* Preserve methodological transparency
* Distinguish observations from conclusions
* Maintain traceability to source records
* Present differences fairly and accurately

Comparisons should improve understanding rather than create confusion.

---

## Output Rules

Outputs should seek to:

* Be understandable
* Be traceable
* Be appropriately contextualized
* Preserve relevant references
* Clearly communicate limitations

Outputs should help users evaluate information rather than obscure it.

---

## Interoperability Rules

Navigator should seek compatibility with other systems throughout the Satoshium ecosystem.

Shared records, metadata, references, schemas, and terminology should be used whenever practical.

Interoperability improves continuity and usability.

---

## User Expectations

Users are encouraged to:

* Review supporting information
* Evaluate evidence independently
* Consider context
* Understand limitations
* Exercise critical thinking

Navigator supports exploration, not unquestioning acceptance.

---

## Prohibited Assumptions

Navigator should avoid:

* Presenting assumptions as facts
* Concealing methodological limitations
* Ignoring relevant context
* Misrepresenting records
* Obscuring source relationships

Transparency should remain a guiding objective.

---

Atlas serves as the primary intelligence repository.

Rules help ensure that information retrieved from Atlas remains understandable, traceable, and properly contextualized.
