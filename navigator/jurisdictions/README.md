# Jurisdictions

**Understanding places through structured intelligence.**

Jurisdictions are the primary organizational units explored through Satoshium Navigator.

Navigator uses jurisdictional records to help users discover, compare, analyze, and interpret information associated with geographic, political, legal, administrative, and regulatory entities.

Jurisdictions provide the foundation upon which much of Atlas and Navigator are built.

---

## Purpose

People, businesses, researchers, and institutions frequently evaluate locations when making decisions.

These decisions may involve investment, relocation, research, governance, commerce, education, policy, technology, or other objectives.

Jurisdictions provide the framework through which information can be organized, compared, and understood.

Navigator exists to make those records accessible.

---

## Core Mission

The mission of Jurisdictions is to provide structured access to information associated with geographic and administrative entities.

Jurisdictions seek to improve understanding through transparency, comparison, context, and evidence-based exploration.

## Jurisdiction Components

A jurisdiction may include information associated with:

### Profile

General information describing the jurisdiction.

Examples may include:

* Geographic Information
* Administrative Information
* Classification Information
* Reference Data

---

### Evidence

Supporting information associated with jurisdiction records.

Evidence may provide context, references, documentation, and supporting materials.

---

### Signals

Observable indicators associated with a jurisdiction.

Examples may include:

* Economic Signals
* Governance Signals
* Technology Signals
* Media Signals
* Infrastructure Signals

---

### Trust Dimensions

Structured categories used to evaluate characteristics associated with a jurisdiction.

Examples may include:

* Stability
* Transparency
* Predictability
* Accessibility
* Consistency

---

### Metadata

Information used to classify, organize, and reference records.

Metadata improves discoverability and interoperability.

---

### Historical Records

Information associated with significant events, developments, and changes over time.

Historical context may assist interpretation and analysis.

## Relationship to Atlas
