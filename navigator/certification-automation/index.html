NAVIGATOR · CERTIFICATION AUTOMATION

Satoshium Navigator Certification Automation

Certification Automation defines how Navigator coordinates repeatable certification workflows across the Satoshium Suite without becoming the authority for certification decisions.

Navigator automates operational processes. Certifier remains responsible for certification authority.

Purpose

Certification Automation reduces repetitive operational work by coordinating repeatable certification workflows across multiple Suite systems.

Operational Role

Navigator initiates, coordinates, monitors, and tracks certification workflows while preserving the independent responsibilities of every participating system.

Authority Boundary

Automation never replaces certification authority. Every certification decision remains the responsibility of Certifier.

Repeatable Processes

Automation transforms common operational procedures into consistent, repeatable workflows that reduce manual effort.

Representative Automation Workflows

Navigator may automate a growing collection of certification-related operational workflows.

Launch certification

Launch recertification

Identify uncertified records

Schedule certification reviews

Detect expired certifications

Detect certifications approaching expiration

Request additional evidence

Initiate Registry registration

Request Anchor preservation

Publish Beacon discovery updates

Initiate Attestation generation

Workflow Triggers

Automation may begin from user requests, scheduled operations, lifecycle events, certification queries, policy rules, or future external integrations.

Workflow Execution

Navigator coordinates each step of an operational workflow while allowing every participating system to perform its own institutional responsibility.

Workflow Monitoring

Automation tracks workflow progress, identifies incomplete operations, and reports operational status without modifying authoritative records.

Future Automation

Future implementations may support scheduled automation, event-driven automation, APIs, policy engines, machine-triggered workflows, and cross-system orchestration.

Automation Sequence

Navigator coordinates operational automation while preserving institutional boundaries throughout the workflow.

Operational Question ↓ Workflow Selected ↓ Systems Coordinated ↓ Certification Executed ↓ Records Updated ↓ History Preserved ↓ Integrity Preserved ↓ Discovery Updated ↓ Trust Statements Produced

Automation Philosophy

Automation should simplify operations without transferring responsibility away from the systems that own institutional authority.
