# Definitions

**Shared language for shared understanding.**

Definitions establishes common terminology used throughout Satoshium Navigator.

Navigator relies upon consistent language to support exploration, comparison, analysis, and interpretation of information. Definitions provide a reference framework that helps ensure terms are understood consistently across records, queries, outputs, and related systems.

Definitions exist to reduce ambiguity and improve clarity.

---

## Purpose

Information systems depend upon shared understanding.

Without consistent terminology, users may interpret records, queries, methodologies, and outputs differently, leading to confusion and inconsistency.

Definitions exist to provide a common language for Navigator and its associated systems.

---

## Core Mission

The mission of Definitions is to establish and preserve clear, consistent, and accessible terminology throughout Navigator.

Definitions seek to improve communication, interpretation, transparency, and understanding.

## Foundational Terms

### Atlas

The jurisdictional intelligence framework of the Satoshium ecosystem.

Atlas organizes and maintains intelligence records, evidence, signals, profiles, and related information.

---

### Navigator

The query and exploration framework of the Satoshium ecosystem.

Navigator helps users discover, retrieve, compare, and interpret information.

---

### Query

A structured request for information.

Queries are used to retrieve records, perform comparisons, apply filters, and generate outputs.

---

### Query Type

A predefined method used to retrieve or analyze information.

Examples may include jurisdiction lookups, comparisons, signal reviews, and filtered searches.

---

### Record

A structured unit of information maintained within a system.

Records may contain evidence, metadata, classifications, references, or related information.

---

### Jurisdiction

A geographic, political, legal, administrative, or regulatory entity represented within Atlas.

Examples may include countries, states, territories, provinces, municipalities, or similar entities.

---

### Profile

A structured summary associated with a jurisdiction or subject.

Profiles may consolidate information from multiple sources and records.

---

### Evidence

Information used to support a record, conclusion, classification, assessment, or claim.

Evidence may originate from documents, sources, observations, records, or other supporting materials.

---

### Signal

An observable indicator associated with a jurisdiction, institution, policy, trend, event, or subject.

Signals may provide insight into changes, patterns, opportunities, risks, or emerging conditions.

---

### Trust Dimension

A structured category used to evaluate characteristics relevant to confidence, predictability, stability, transparency, or reliability.

Trust Dimensions may vary based upon methodology and use case.

---

### Metadata

Information describing another record.

Metadata may include classifications, dates, identifiers, categories, sources, relationships, or status information.

---

### Comparison

A structured evaluation of similarities, differences, relationships, or distinctions between records.

Comparisons provide context and analytical insight.

---

### Filter

A mechanism used to narrow or refine results based upon selected criteria.

Filters help users locate information relevant to specific interests or objectives.

---

### Output

Information produced in response to a query, comparison, analysis, or retrieval process.

Outputs may include reports, summaries, profiles, comparisons, and related results.

---

### Cross Reference

A documented relationship between two or more records.

Cross references improve navigation, context, and discoverability.

---

### Traceability

The ability to identify the origin, relationship, and history of information.

Traceability supports transparency and accountability.
