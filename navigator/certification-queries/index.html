Satoshium Navigator Certification Queries

Certification Queries define the operational questions Navigator asks across the Satoshium Suite to locate certifications, certification opportunities, lifecycle conditions, and supporting records.

Navigator does not own certification data. It orchestrates operational questions that reference authoritative systems.

Purpose

Certification Queries help operators identify work requiring certification, review, renewal, or operational attention.

Operational Questions

Navigator expresses operational intent through structured questions rather than direct ownership of certification records.

Reference-Based Queries

Queries operate by referencing Atlas, Certifier, Registry, Chronicle, Anchor, Beacon, and Attestor rather than duplicating their information.

Repeatable Operations

Every Certification Query should produce repeatable, understandable, and traceable operational results.

Query Inputs

Queries may include identifiers, certification classes, lifecycle states, jurisdictions, dates, evidence conditions, registry status, trust signals, or workflow requirements.

Query Results

Results identify operational work requiring attention while preserving references back to the originating systems.

Cross-System Queries

Navigator may execute certification queries spanning multiple Suite components without transferring ownership of their records.

Future Query Types

Future implementations may support scheduled queries, saved queries, parameterized workflows, automation triggers, APIs, and machine-readable query definitions.

Certification Query Philosophy

Navigator exists to answer operational questions that no single institutional system can answer by itself.
