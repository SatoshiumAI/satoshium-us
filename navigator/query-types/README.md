# Query Types

**Structuring exploration through defined methods.**

Query Types establish the categories and methodologies through which information is retrieved, explored, compared, filtered, and analyzed within Satoshium Navigator.

While a query represents a request for information, a query type defines how that request is processed.

Query Types provide consistency, transparency, and repeatability across Navigator workflows.

---

## Purpose

Different questions require different approaches.

A user comparing two jurisdictions may require a different methodology than a user researching historical records or reviewing technology-related signals.

Without defined query types, information retrieval may become inconsistent, difficult to interpret, or challenging to reproduce.

Query Types exist to provide structure and clarity throughout the exploration process.

---

## Core Mission

The mission of Query Types is to establish transparent and repeatable frameworks for retrieving and analyzing information.

Query Types seek to improve consistency, accessibility, comparability, and understanding across Navigator activities.

---

## Why Query Types Matter

Query Types help ensure that:

* Similar questions follow similar workflows.
* Information is retrieved consistently.
* Results remain understandable.
* Methodologies remain transparent.
* Comparisons remain repeatable.
* Users understand how outputs are generated.

These principles improve confidence and usability.

## Core Query Types

### Jurisdiction Query

Designed to retrieve information associated with a specific jurisdiction.

Examples may include:

* Country Reviews
* State Reviews
* Territory Reviews
* Regional Reviews

The objective is to provide a comprehensive view of a selected jurisdiction.

---

### Comparison Query

Designed to evaluate multiple jurisdictions or records simultaneously.

Examples may include:

* Jurisdiction Comparisons
* Signal Comparisons
* Trust Dimension Comparisons
* Historical Comparisons

The objective is to identify similarities, differences, and relationships.

---

### Signal Query

Designed to retrieve information associated with observable indicators.

Examples may include:

* Economic Signals
* Governance Signals
* Technology Signals
* Media Signals
* Infrastructure Signals

The objective is to explore patterns and conditions associated with a subject.

---

### Historical Query

Designed to examine changes across time.

Examples may include:

* Historical Reviews
* Timeline Analysis
* Development Trends
* Chronicle References

The objective is to provide historical context.

---

### Filtered Query

Designed to narrow information using selected criteria.

Examples may include:

* Geographic Filters
* Record Filters
* Signal Filters
* Time-Based Filters
* Trust Dimension Filters

The objective is to improve relevance and focus.

---

### Research Query

Designed to support broader analytical objectives.

Examples may include:

* Relocation Research
* Technology Reviews
* Media Reviews
* Tax-Oriented Research
* Regulatory Research

The objective is to support structured investigation and evaluation.

---

### Cross-Reference Query

Designed to identify relationships between records.

Examples may include:

* Related Records
* Connected Jurisdictions
* Supporting References
* Associated Resources

The objective is to improve discoverability and context.
