# Scope

**Defining the boundaries of exploration.**

Scope defines what information, records, systems, subjects, and activities fall within the operational boundaries of Satoshium Navigator.

Navigator is designed to help users discover, retrieve, compare, analyze, and understand information contained within Atlas and related systems. Scope establishes the areas Navigator is intended to support while helping distinguish what Navigator does and does not attempt to do.

A clearly defined scope improves transparency, consistency, and user expectations.

---

## Purpose

Information systems become more effective when their responsibilities are clearly defined.

Without defined boundaries, users may misunderstand capabilities, misinterpret outputs, or expect functionality beyond the intended purpose of the system.

Scope exists to establish the operational focus of Navigator and provide clarity regarding its intended use.

---

## Core Mission

The mission of Scope is to define the areas of information retrieval, exploration, analysis, and presentation supported by Navigator.

Scope seeks to improve understanding by clearly identifying the information domains Navigator is designed to address.

## Activities Outside Scope

Navigator is not intended to:

* Serve as a legal authority
* Provide legal advice
* Provide tax advice
* Provide investment advice
* Provide medical advice
* Replace professional consultation
* Determine truth by declaration

## Scope Boundaries

Navigator seeks to:

* Retrieve information
* Organize information
* Present information
* Compare information
* Improve discoverability

Navigator does not seek to:

* Control information sources
* Establish official policy
* Override authoritative sources
* Replace independent evaluation

Users remain responsible for their own conclusions and decisions.
