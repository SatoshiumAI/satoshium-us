Navigator · Scope Framework

Scope

Scope defines the boundaries, coverage, capabilities, and limitations of Satoshium Navigator.

Understanding scope helps establish what Navigator is intended to do, what information it may explore, and what activities remain outside its intended purpose.

Purpose of Scope

● Core Framework

Defined Boundaries

Scope establishes the operational boundaries that help guide Navigator's development, capabilities, and intended use.

● Expectations Framework

Clear Understanding

Scope helps users understand what Navigator includes, what it may support in the future, and what remains outside its intended role.

Included Scope

Information Retrieval

Navigator supports retrieval of information from Atlas and related systems throughout the Satoshium ecosystem.

Jurisdiction Exploration

Navigator supports exploration of countries, states, regions, territories, and related jurisdictional records.

Comparisons

Navigator supports structured comparison of jurisdictions, signals, trust dimensions, records, and related information.

Filtering

Navigator supports refinement of information through filters, categories, classifications, and analytical criteria.

Research & Analysis

Navigator supports investigation, exploration, organization, contextual review, and structured understanding.

Traceable Outputs

Navigator supports generation of outputs that remain connected to supporting records and information sources.

Excluded Scope

Legal Advice

Navigator does not provide legal advice and should not be interpreted as a substitute for qualified legal counsel.

Financial Advice

Navigator does not provide financial, investment, or wealth management advice.

Tax Advice

Navigator does not provide tax planning, tax preparation, or professional tax advice.

Medical Advice

Navigator does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or healthcare guidance.

Government Authority

Navigator is not a governmental authority, regulatory agency, court, or official decision-making body.

Truth Determination

Navigator organizes and presents information but does not determine objective truth, certainty, or final conclusions.

Disclaimer
