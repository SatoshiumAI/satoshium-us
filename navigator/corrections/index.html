Navigator · Correction Framework

Corrections

Corrections provides a structured framework for identifying, reviewing, documenting, and improving information throughout Satoshium Navigator.

The objective of Corrections is not perfection, but continual improvement through transparency, accountability, traceability, and evidence-based revision.

Correction Purpose

● Active Framework

Continuous Improvement

Corrections help improve information quality by documenting errors, omissions, updates, clarifications, and refinements.

● Transparency Process

Documented Revision

Rather than silently modifying information, Navigator seeks to preserve transparency by documenting meaningful corrections and revisions when appropriate.

Correction Categories

Factual Corrections

Address inaccuracies involving names, dates, locations, statistics, classifications, references, and related factual information.

Reference Corrections

Improve supporting references, source documentation, cross-references, citations, and associated records.

Clarifications

Provide additional explanation when information may be ambiguous, incomplete, confusing, or open to misinterpretation.

Structural Corrections

Address formatting issues, organizational inconsistencies, navigation problems, or documentation structure improvements.

Record Updates

Reflect new information, revised classifications, expanded context, or newly available supporting records.

Methodology Improvements

Refine frameworks, processes, evaluation methods, and operational procedures used throughout Navigator.

Correction Process

Identification

Potential issues may be identified through research, internal review, user feedback, new evidence, or supporting documentation.

Review

Relevant information is evaluated to determine whether a correction, clarification, update, or revision is warranted.

Documentation

Significant corrections should be documented to preserve transparency, continuity, and historical traceability.

Implementation

Approved changes are incorporated into the appropriate records, outputs, references, or supporting documentation.

Verification

Updated information may be reviewed against supporting evidence and associated records when appropriate.

Preservation

Historical correction records may be maintained to support accountability and future review.

Relationship to Atlas

Many Navigator records originate from Atlas and related systems. Corrections may reference Atlas records when inaccuracies, clarifications, or updates are identified.

Navigator corrections do not replace Atlas records. They provide a mechanism for documenting and communicating revisions when appropriate.

Disclaimer

Corrections are intended to improve the quality and reliability of information throughout Navigator.

The existence of a correction does not necessarily imply malicious intent, negligence, or misconduct. Information systems evolve, records change, and new information may become available over time.

Users remain responsible for independently evaluating information and drawing their own conclusions.
