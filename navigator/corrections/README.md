# Corrections

**Improving information through transparency.**

Corrections is the review and amendment framework within Satoshium Navigator responsible for identifying, documenting, and resolving errors, omissions, inaccuracies, and outdated information associated with Navigator outputs and related records.

Corrections exist to improve the quality, reliability, and transparency of information over time.

While information may evolve, transparency requires that changes remain visible and traceable.

---

## Purpose

Information systems are not static.

Records may change, new evidence may emerge, methodologies may improve, and previously unknown inaccuracies may be identified.

Without a structured correction process, errors may persist and confidence in information may decline.

Corrections exist to provide a transparent framework for reviewing, documenting, and resolving identified issues.

---

## Core Mission

The mission of Corrections is to support the continuous improvement of information quality through transparent review and documented amendments.

Corrections seek to improve accuracy while preserving accountability and historical traceability.

## Correction Workflow

```text
Identify Issue
      ↓
Review Information
      ↓
Evaluate Evidence
      ↓
Determine Resolution
      ↓
Apply Correction
      ↓
Document Change
```

This workflow provides a structured method for improving information while preserving transparency.

## Correction Categories

### Factual Corrections

Corrections involving inaccurate or incomplete information.

Examples may include:

* Incorrect data
* Missing information
* Outdated information
* Misidentified records

---

### Structural Corrections

Corrections involving organization, categorization, or formatting issues.

Examples may include:

* Classification errors
* Record placement issues
* Cross-reference errors
* Navigation inconsistencies

---

### Methodological Corrections

Corrections involving analytical frameworks, calculations, or evaluation methods.

Examples may include:

* Scoring revisions
* Framework updates
* Methodology improvements
* Evaluation refinements

---

### Reference Corrections

Corrections involving citations, links, references, and supporting materials.

Examples may include:

* Broken references
* Incorrect citations
* Missing supporting records
* Cross-reference updates

---

## Transparency Principles

Corrections should improve information without obscuring historical context.

Whenever practical:

* Corrections should be documented.
* Changes should be traceable.
* Historical records should be preserved.
* Supporting evidence should be retained.
* Resolution processes should remain transparent.

Transparency strengthens trust.
