# Interoperability

**Connecting systems through shared understanding.**

Interoperability is the framework within Satoshium Navigator responsible for enabling information exchange, coordination, compatibility, and interaction between Navigator and other systems throughout the Satoshium ecosystem.

Navigator does not operate in isolation.

Its value increases when information, records, intelligence, and outputs can be discovered, accessed, interpreted, and referenced across multiple systems.

Interoperability exists to support those relationships.

---

## Purpose

Modern information systems often consist of multiple specialized components.

Without interoperability, information may become fragmented, duplicated, isolated, or difficult to access across organizational boundaries.

Interoperability exists to improve coordination, accessibility, consistency, and continuity throughout the ecosystem.

---

## Core Mission

The mission of Interoperability is to establish and maintain frameworks that allow Navigator to operate effectively alongside connected systems.

Interoperability seeks to improve discoverability, compatibility, transparency, and long-term usability of information.

---

## Relationship to Atlas

Atlas serves as the primary intelligence repository.

Navigator relies upon Atlas records, profiles, evidence, signals, metadata, and trust dimensions to support exploration and analysis.

Atlas provides information. Navigator helps users interact with that information.

---

## Relationship to Certifier

Certifier evaluates information against defined standards and methodologies.

Navigator may retrieve, display, and reference Certifier outputs when relevant records are available.

---

## Relationship to Registry

Registry organizes and catalogs records throughout the ecosystem.

Navigator may retrieve Registry records, references, classifications, and related information to support discovery and navigation.

---

## Relationship to Chronicle

Chronicle preserves historical records, events, milestones, and developments.

Navigator may access Chronicle records to provide historical context and supporting information.

---

## Relationship to Anchor

Anchor preserves integrity references, permanence records, and associated verification information.

Navigator may reference Anchor records when traceability or integrity-related information is relevant.

---

## Relationship to Beacon

Beacon improves discoverability throughout the ecosystem.

Beacon helps information become easier to find.

Navigator helps information become easier to explore and understand.

The systems provide complementary capabilities.

---

## Relationship to Attestor

Attestor supports evidentiary assertions, trust-oriented outputs, and independent verification activities.

Navigator may retrieve and present Attestor records when relevant to a query or analytical workflow.

---

## Shared Components

Interoperability may rely upon shared:

### Records

Structured units of information maintained across systems.

### Metadata

Descriptive information used to organize and reference records.

### References

Connections between records, systems, and related resources.

### Terminology

Common language used throughout the ecosystem.

### Schemas

Standardized structures used to maintain consistency.

### Identifiers

Unique references used to locate and connect information.
