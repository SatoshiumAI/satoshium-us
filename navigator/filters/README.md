# Filters

**Finding relevant information through refinement.**

Filters are the refinement framework within Satoshium Navigator responsible for narrowing, organizing, and prioritizing information based upon selected criteria.

While queries retrieve information, filters help users focus on the information most relevant to their objectives.

Filters transform broad collections of records into targeted and actionable results.

---

## Purpose

Large information systems often contain significant amounts of data, records, evidence, signals, and related resources.

Without mechanisms for refinement, users may encounter information overload and difficulty identifying the records most relevant to their objectives.

Filters exist to improve discoverability by reducing unnecessary complexity and focusing attention on selected criteria.

---

## Core Mission

The mission of Filters is to provide transparent, flexible, and repeatable methods for refining information within Navigator.

Filters seek to improve accessibility, efficiency, relevance, and understanding.

---

## Filter Workflow

```text
Retrieve Records
        ↓
Apply Filters
        ↓
Refine Results
        ↓
Review Matches
        ↓
Generate Output
```

This workflow allows users to focus on information most relevant to their needs.

## Filter Categories

### Geographic Filters

Refine results based upon location.

Examples may include:

* Country
* State
* Province
* Territory
* Region
* Continent

---

### Record Filters

Refine results based upon record type.

Examples may include:

* Profiles
* Evidence
* Signals
* Metadata
* Registry Records
* Chronicle Records

---

### Signal Filters

Refine results using signal-related criteria.

Examples may include:

* Economic Signals
* Technology Signals
* Governance Signals
* Media Signals
* Infrastructure Signals

---

### Trust Dimension Filters

Refine results using trust-oriented categories.

Examples may include:

* Stability
* Transparency
* Predictability
* Accessibility
* Consistency

---

### Time-Based Filters

Refine results using temporal criteria.

Examples may include:

* Recent Records
* Historical Records
* Specific Time Periods
* Date Ranges

---

### Custom Filters

Users may combine multiple criteria to create more specific results.

Examples may include:

* Region + Signal Type
* Jurisdiction + Time Period

* Signal Category + Historical Range
