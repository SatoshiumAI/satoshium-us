# Atlas Viewer

**Exploring Atlas through Navigator.**

Atlas Viewer is the primary viewing framework of Satoshium Navigator.

Atlas Viewer provides structured access to information contained within Atlas, allowing users to discover, retrieve, compare, and interpret jurisdictional intelligence and related records.

While Atlas serves as the underlying intelligence repository, Atlas Viewer serves as the interface through which that information becomes accessible and understandable.

---

## Purpose

Atlas contains large collections of jurisdictional intelligence, evidence, signals, trust dimensions, metadata, and related records.

Without an organized viewing framework, information can become difficult to locate, navigate, compare, and understand.

Atlas Viewer exists to transform Atlas records into a structured and navigable experience.

---

## Core Mission

The mission of Atlas Viewer is to provide transparent, accessible, and understandable access to Atlas intelligence.

Atlas Viewer seeks to improve discovery, exploration, comparison, and interpretation while preserving traceability to underlying records.

## Relationship to Atlas

Atlas serves as the authoritative intelligence repository.

Atlas Viewer serves as the structured viewing layer through which Atlas information is accessed.

Atlas Viewer does not replace Atlas and does not function as the source of record.

---

## Relationship to Navigator

Navigator provides the broader exploration framework.

Atlas Viewer provides the primary interface for accessing Atlas information through Navigator.

Together they support discovery, retrieval, comparison, and analysis.
