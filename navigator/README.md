# Satoshium Navigator

**Understanding through exploration.**

Satoshium Navigator is the query and exploration framework of the Satoshium ecosystem.

Navigator exists to help users discover, retrieve, compare, analyze, and understand information contained within Atlas and other connected systems.

While Atlas organizes intelligence, Registry organizes records, Chronicle preserves history, Certifier evaluates information, Anchor preserves integrity, Beacon improves discovery, and Attestor supports evidentiary assertions, Navigator provides the mechanism through which users interact with those resources.

Navigator transforms collections of records, signals, evidence, and related information into structured, searchable, and understandable outputs.

---

## Purpose

Modern information systems generate large quantities of records, intelligence, evidence, signals, classifications, and historical information.

Even when information is available, it is often difficult to locate, compare, analyze, interpret, or understand.

Navigator exists to help answer fundamental questions:

* What information is available?
* What jurisdictions can be explored?
* What signals exist?
* How do jurisdictions compare?
* What records are connected?
* What patterns can be identified?
* What information is relevant to a specific objective?

Navigator provides a structured framework for discovering and interacting with information.

## Navigator Components

Navigator includes several core components:

### Queries

Structured requests used to retrieve and explore information.

### Query Types

Defined methodologies used to guide information retrieval and analysis.

### Jurisdictions

Geographic and administrative entities available for exploration.

### Signals

Observable indicators associated with jurisdictions, institutions, events, and trends.

### Comparisons

Frameworks used to evaluate similarities, differences, and relationships.

### Filters

Tools used to refine information and improve relevance.

### Outputs

Structured results generated from queries and analysis.

### Schemas

Standardized structures that support consistency and interoperability.

### Templates

Reusable frameworks used throughout Navigator activities.

### Examples

Illustrative query and output examples demonstrating system capabilities.
