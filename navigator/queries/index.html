Navigator · Query Framework

Queries

Queries are the primary mechanism through which users retrieve, explore, compare, and analyze information within Satoshium Navigator.

Every exploration process begins with a query. Queries transform questions, objectives, and areas of interest into structured requests for information.

Query Purpose

● Core Framework

Information Retrieval

Queries provide structured access to records, jurisdictions, signals, evidence, trust dimensions, and related resources.

● Exploration Framework

Guided Discovery

Queries help transform broad collections of information into focused exploration experiences tailored to specific objectives and questions.

Query Categories

Jurisdiction Queries

Retrieve information associated with specific countries, states, regions, territories, and related jurisdictional records.

Comparison Queries

Request structured side-by-side evaluations of multiple jurisdictions, records, signals, trust dimensions, or related subjects.

Signal Queries

Retrieve information associated with observable indicators, trends, measurements, classifications, and analytical categories.

Historical Queries

Explore historical developments, timeline information, change records, Chronicle references, and related context.

Research Queries

Support broader investigation by combining multiple records, categories, filters, and analytical frameworks.

Custom Queries

User-defined requests that combine multiple objectives, criteria, filters, and informational requirements.

Query Components

Objective

The question, goal, or informational need that initiates the query process.

Scope

The boundaries of the query, including jurisdictions, subjects, categories, timeframes, and related constraints.

Filters

Criteria used to refine, narrow, prioritize, or organize retrieved information.

Sources

Records, profiles, evidence, signals, historical references, and related resources available for retrieval.

Analysis

Processes that organize, compare, evaluate, or contextualize retrieved information.

Output

The structured presentation generated from the query process.
