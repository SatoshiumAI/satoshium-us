# Queries

**Transforming questions into understanding.**

Queries are the foundation of Satoshium Navigator.

Every exploration, comparison, review, filter, and analytical workflow begins with a query. Queries provide the structured mechanism through which users retrieve, explore, and interact with information contained within Atlas and related systems.

Navigator exists to help users ask better questions and discover meaningful answers.

---

## Purpose

Information becomes useful when it can be explored.

Large collections of records, evidence, signals, profiles, and historical information often contain valuable insights, but those insights may remain difficult to discover without effective methods of retrieval.

Queries exist to provide structured access to information throughout the Satoshium ecosystem.

---

## Core Mission

The mission of Queries is to transform user questions into transparent, repeatable, and meaningful information retrieval processes.

Queries seek to improve discoverability, accessibility, understanding, and contextual analysis.

---

## What Is a Query?

A query is a structured request for information.

Queries allow users to identify, retrieve, compare, analyze, and interpret records associated with jurisdictions and related subjects.

A query may be simple or complex depending upon the objectives of the user.

---

## Query Categories

Navigator may support several categories of queries.

### Jurisdiction Queries

Designed to retrieve information associated with a specific jurisdiction.

Examples may include:

* Country Reviews
* State Reviews
* Territory Reviews
* Regional Reviews

---

### Comparison Queries

Designed to evaluate multiple jurisdictions or records together.

Examples may include:

* Jurisdiction Comparisons
* Signal Comparisons
* Trust Dimension Comparisons
* Historical Comparisons

---

### Signal Queries

Designed to retrieve information associated with observable indicators.

Examples may include:

* Economic Signals
* Governance Signals
* Technology Signals
* Media Signals
* Infrastructure Signals

---

### Historical Queries

Designed to explore historical information and changes over time.

Examples may include:

* Historical Reviews
* Timeline Analysis
* Change Reviews
* Chronicle References

---

### Filtered Queries

Designed to narrow information using selected criteria.

Examples may include:

* Geographic Filters
* Record Filters
* Signal Filters
* Trust Dimension Filters
* Time-Based Filters

---

### Research Queries

Designed to support broader analytical objectives.

Examples may include:

* Relocation Research
* Technology Reviews
* Media Reviews
* Regulatory Reviews
* Tax-Oriented Research
