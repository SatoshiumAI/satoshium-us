# Jurisdiction Comparison Query

**Comparing jurisdictions through structured analysis.**

This example demonstrates how Satoshium Navigator may be used to compare two or more jurisdictions using information available within Atlas and related systems.

Jurisdiction comparison queries help users identify similarities, differences, strengths, weaknesses, patterns, and relationships across multiple jurisdictions.

---

## Purpose

Individual jurisdiction records provide information about a single location.

Comparison queries provide additional context by evaluating multiple jurisdictions together.

This example illustrates how Navigator may support comparative analysis.

---

## Example Question

```text
How do Texas and Florida compare?
```

---

## Example Query

```text
Query Type: Jurisdiction Comparison

Jurisdiction A: Texas
Jurisdiction B: Florida
```

---

## Query Objectives

The comparison process may seek to evaluate:

* Jurisdiction Profiles
* Signals
* Trust Dimensions
* Supporting Evidence
* Historical Context
* Related Records
* Available References

The specific scope of a comparison may vary depending upon available records and user requirements.

---

## Example Workflow

```text
Select Jurisdictions
        ↓
Retrieve Records
        ↓
Normalize Information
        ↓
Compare Attributes
        ↓
Identify Differences
        ↓
Generate Results
```

This workflow transforms jurisdiction records into a structured comparison output.

---

## Example Comparison Categories

### Profile Comparison

Review high-level characteristics associated with each jurisdiction.

Examples may include:

* Geographic Information
* Administrative Structure
* Classification Information
* Associated Metadata

---

### Signal Comparison

Evaluate observable indicators associated with each jurisdiction.

Examples may include:

* Economic Signals
* Governance Signals
* Technology Signals
* Media Signals
* Infrastructure Signals

---

### Trust Dimension Comparison

Evaluate differences across trust-related categories.

Examples may include:

* Stability
* Transparency
* Accessibility
* Predictability
* Consistency

---

### Historical Comparison

Review historical records associated with each jurisdiction.

Examples may include:

* Significant Changes
* Historical Trends
* Record Revisions
* Chronicle References
