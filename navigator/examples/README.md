# Examples

**Understanding through demonstration.**

Examples provides illustrative use cases, sample queries, reference outputs, and demonstration workflows for Satoshium Navigator.

Examples help users understand how Navigator may be used to explore, retrieve, compare, and interpret information contained within Atlas and related systems.

While documentation explains capabilities, examples demonstrate those capabilities in practice.

---

## Purpose

Information systems often become easier to understand when users can observe practical examples.

Examples provide representative scenarios that demonstrate how Navigator functions and how information may be explored through structured queries and outputs.

Examples exist to improve accessibility, understanding, and adoption.

---

## Core Mission

The mission of Examples is to provide practical demonstrations of Navigator workflows, queries, comparisons, filters, and outputs.

Examples seek to transform abstract concepts into understandable and repeatable use cases.

---

## Example: Jurisdiction Lookup

Which information is available for Texas?

### Query

```text

```

* Evidence Records
* Signals
