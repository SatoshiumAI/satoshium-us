Satoshium Navigator Status

Status provides information regarding Navigator's current development, workflow orchestration architecture, certification query model, automation posture, and operational maturity.

Navigator is being established as the workflow orchestration layer of the Satoshium Suite. It does not own data or certification authority; it coordinates operational workflows across participating systems.

Current Status

● Foundation Complete

Framework Established

Navigator's foundational documentation, workflow orchestration model, certification query architecture, automation concepts, schemas, templates, and operational concepts have been established.

● Active Development

Implementation Phase

Navigator is currently progressing through implementation planning, workflow orchestration refinement, cross-system integration, and ecosystem alignment activities.

Development Areas

Documentation

Core documentation now includes integration, certification queries, certification automation, workflow orchestration, frameworks, definitions, rules, schemas, templates, and supporting materials.

Query Frameworks

Certification Query structures, retrieval methodologies, comparison systems, operational questions, and exploration workflows are under active development and refinement.

Integration

Navigator is being designed to orchestrate workflows across Atlas, Certifier, Registry, Chronicle, Anchor, Beacon, Attestor, and future Suite systems.

Output Systems

Output formats, workflow results, automation reports, comparison outputs, and structured presentation mechanisms continue to evolve.

Schemas

Standardized schemas help support workflow definitions, certification queries, automation triggers, traceability, interoperability, and future scalability.

User Experience

Navigation structures, query workflows, workflow launch paths, automation views, documentation access, and exploration experiences remain areas of active improvement.

Relationship to Atlas

Navigator is being developed to complement Atlas by identifying records that may require certification, review, recertification, additional evidence, or operational attention.

Atlas serves as a primary intelligence repository, while Navigator focuses on workflow orchestration, operational queries, coordination, and actionability.

Atlas provides intelligence.
Navigator identifies work.
Workflows coordinate action.
