# Status

**Tracking the development and operational state of Navigator.**

The Status page provides a centralized overview of the current condition, capabilities, milestones, priorities, and progress of Satoshium Navigator.

Status exists to improve transparency by documenting what Navigator currently supports, what is actively being developed, and how the platform evolves over time.

As Navigator grows, the Status page serves as a historical and operational reference point.

---

## Purpose

Information systems evolve over time.

New capabilities are introduced, existing components mature, and priorities change as requirements and objectives develop.

Without a centralized status framework, users may have difficulty understanding the current state of the platform.

Status exists to provide visibility into Navigator's operational progress and development trajectory.

---

## Core Mission

The mission of Status is to provide transparent documentation regarding the maturity, capabilities, priorities, and evolution of Navigator.

Status seeks to improve clarity, accountability, and long-term continuity.
