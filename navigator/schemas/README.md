# Schemas

**Creating structure for consistent information.**

Schemas define the organizational frameworks used throughout Satoshium Navigator.

They establish standardized structures for queries, records, outputs, comparisons, filters, references, and related information, helping ensure consistency across retrieval, analysis, and presentation activities.

Schemas provide the foundation upon which Navigator operates.

---

## Purpose

Information becomes more useful when it follows consistent structures.

Without standardized frameworks, records may be difficult to retrieve, compare, interpret, or exchange between systems.

Schemas exist to create common formats that improve organization, interoperability, transparency, and long-term usability.

---

## Core Mission

The mission of Schemas is to provide clear and repeatable structures for information throughout Navigator.

Schemas seek to improve consistency, compatibility, accessibility, and understanding while supporting interoperability across the broader Satoshium ecosystem.

---

## Why Schemas Matter

Schemas help ensure that:

* Information is organized consistently.
* Records remain comparable.
* Outputs remain understandable.
* Queries follow defined structures.
* Metadata remains standardized.
* Systems remain interoperable.

These benefits improve both usability and reliability.
