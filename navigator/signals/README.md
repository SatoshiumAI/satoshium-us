# Signals

**Observing information through measurable indicators.**

Signals are observable indicators associated with jurisdictions, institutions, events, policies, trends, and related subjects within Satoshium Navigator.

Signals help transform large collections of information into identifiable patterns that can be explored, compared, analyzed, and understood.

While records provide information, signals help reveal what that information may indicate.

---

## Purpose

Information often becomes more valuable when patterns can be identified.

Individual records may provide isolated facts, but signals help reveal relationships, trends, developments, and conditions that may otherwise remain difficult to recognize.

Signals exist to support structured observation and analysis across jurisdictions and related subjects.

---

## Core Mission

The mission of Signals is to provide transparent and organized access to observable indicators that may assist understanding, exploration, comparison, and research.

Signals seek to improve context, discoverability, and interpretation while preserving traceability to supporting records.

---

## What Is a Signal?

A signal is an observable indicator associated with a subject, jurisdiction, event, institution, trend, or condition.

Signals do not inherently determine conclusions.

Instead, signals provide information that may contribute to broader analysis and understanding.

Signals should be considered alongside supporting evidence, historical context, and related records.

## Signal Components

A signal record may include:

### Signal Classification

Identifies the category and nature of the signal.

### Signal Description

Describes the observed indicator.

### Supporting Evidence

References information associated with the signal.

### Historical Context

Provides background and related developments.

### Related Records

Connects the signal to other information resources.

### References

Preserves traceability and supporting documentation.
