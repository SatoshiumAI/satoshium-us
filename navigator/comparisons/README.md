# Comparisons

**Understanding through comparison.**

Comparisons is the analytical framework within Satoshium Navigator responsible for evaluating similarities, differences, relationships, and distinctions between jurisdictions, records, signals, and related resources.

While individual records provide information about a single subject, Comparisons provide context by examining multiple subjects together.

Comparisons transform isolated information into comparative understanding.

---

## Purpose

Information often becomes more valuable when viewed alongside other information.

A jurisdiction may appear attractive in isolation but may present different characteristics when compared against neighboring jurisdictions, peer jurisdictions, or historical alternatives.

Comparisons exist to help users identify distinctions, patterns, strengths, weaknesses, relationships, and trends across multiple records.

---

## Core Mission

The mission of Comparisons is to provide structured, transparent, and repeatable methods for evaluating information across multiple records.

Comparisons seek to improve understanding by placing information within a broader context.

## Comparison Workflow

```text
Select Records
      ↓
Retrieve Data
      ↓
Normalize Information
      ↓
Compare Attributes
      ↓
Identify Differences
      ↓
Present Results
```

This workflow provides a structured approach for evaluating information across multiple records.
