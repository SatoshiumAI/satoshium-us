Beacon · Purpose

Purpose

Beacon exists to help users discover relevant information.

Its purpose is to surface signals, sources, records, and relationships that may otherwise remain difficult to locate, navigate, or understand.

Discovery

Beacon helps users locate information that may be relevant to a question, investigation, decision, observation, or area of interest.

Visibility

Information has limited value if it cannot be found. Beacon seeks to improve visibility across the Satoshium ecosystem.

Navigation

Beacon assists users in navigating records, sources, signals, historical information, and related discoveries.

Transparency

Discovery should preserve attribution and source visibility whenever possible.

Accessibility

Beacon seeks to reduce information friction and improve access to meaningful information.

Exploration

Discovery supports investigation, learning, research, and exploration without dictating conclusions.
