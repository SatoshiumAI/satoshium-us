Beacon · Purpose

Purpose

Beacon exists as the Satoshium Suite institution for discovery and signals.

Its purpose is to publish Discovery Signals and discovery metadata that help users and Suite workflows locate relevant sources, canonical objects, and relationships while preserving attribution, provenance, traceability, context, and institutional authority.

Discovery

Beacon helps users and Suite workflows locate information relevant to a question, investigation, decision, observation, workflow, or area of interest.

Visibility

Information and authoritative Suite objects have limited utility if they cannot be found. Beacon improves their discoverability without assuming authority over them.

Navigation

Beacon assists users and workflows in navigating sources, Discovery Signals, discovery metadata, and references to canonical objects maintained throughout the Suite.

Transparency

Discovery should preserve attribution, provenance, stable references, source visibility, and institutional ownership.

Accessibility

Beacon seeks to reduce information friction and improve access to meaningful information through reviewable, traceable discovery structures.

Exploration

Discovery supports investigation, learning, research, exploration, and Suite workflows without dictating conclusions or transferring authority from the originating institution.

Purpose Statement

Modern information environments contain more information than any individual can reasonably process.

Help users find the signal.

Beacon owns the Discovery Signals and discovery metadata it publishes. When those objects reference authoritative objects maintained by another Suite institution, that institution retains authority over the referenced object.

Reference does not transfer authority.

That purpose remains at the center of Beacon's mission and architecture as it advances through Suite alignment and production preparation.
