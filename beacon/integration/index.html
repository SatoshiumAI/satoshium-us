Beacon · Integration

Integration

Beacon Integration defines how Beacon connects to the Satoshium ecosystem by publishing discovery signals without becoming the authority for the records those signals reference.

Integration answers one fundamental question: what should the world discover?

Purpose

Beacon Integration establishes the institutional boundary between discovery and authority. Beacon helps information become discoverable without changing its meaning or ownership.

Core Distinction

Certifier determines certification. Registry catalogs records. Chronicle preserves history. Anchor preserves integrity. Beacon publishes discovery signals.

Discovery Layer

Beacon operates as the public discovery layer of the Suite. It helps users, applications, and future services locate information that already exists within authoritative systems.

Signal Layer

Beacon publishes signals that summarize the discoverable state of important artifacts while preserving references to their authoritative sources.

Integration Relationship

Beacon integrates with every major Suite component by surfacing discovery signals rather than reproducing records.

Atlas creates intelligence.
Certifier creates authority.
Registry catalogs records.
Chronicle preserves history.
Anchor preserves integrity.
Beacon publishes discovery.
Attestor evaluates trust.

Relationship to Certifier

Beacon may publish signals indicating certification status while always referencing Certifier as the authoritative source.

Relationship to Registry

Beacon may surface Registry Entries and Registry metadata to improve discoverability while preserving Registry's ownership of public cataloging.

Relationship to Chronicle

Beacon may surface historical events preserved by Chronicle, allowing users to discover meaningful changes without replacing historical records.

Relationship to Anchor

Beacon may expose integrity-related signals that reference Anchor while leaving integrity preservation and verification to Anchor itself.

Discovery Philosophy

Beacon exists to make important information easier to discover. Discovery does not replace certification, cataloging, preservation, or verification. It simply helps users find them.

Beacon never certifies.
Beacon never verifies.
Beacon never determines trust.
Beacon publishes discovery signals.

Future Interoperability

Future Beacon implementations may publish machine-readable discovery signals, indexes, APIs, metadata feeds, search interfaces, notification services, and interoperability standards while preserving authoritative references back to the originating systems.
