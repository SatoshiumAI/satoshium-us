Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Beacon, the discovery layer of the Satoshium Suite.

Beacon helps users find signals, sources, records, and relationships across the broader Satoshium ecosystem.

What is Beacon?

Beacon is the discovery layer of the Satoshium Suite. Its purpose is to help users locate and explore relevant information.

What does Beacon do?

Beacon helps surface signals, sources, records, historical information, identity references, and related discoveries.

Does Beacon verify information?

No. Verification is the responsibility of Certifier. Beacon helps users discover information but does not determine whether it is correct.

Does Beacon establish trust?

No. Trust-related assessments belong to Attestor. Beacon may surface trust-related information but does not determine trust.

What is a signal?

A signal is an observation, event, update, record, or relationship that may warrant attention or further investigation.

What is discovery?

Discovery is the process of locating and surfacing information that may be relevant to a user, question, or objective.
