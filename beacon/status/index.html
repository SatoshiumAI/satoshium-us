Satoshium Beacon Status

Beacon is currently defining the discovery and signaling architecture of the Satoshium ecosystem.

Core documentation, discovery signals, discovery metadata, interoperability models, and foundational schemas have been established. Operational discovery systems and signal distribution capabilities remain under development.
