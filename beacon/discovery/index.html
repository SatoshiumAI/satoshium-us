Discovery Flow

Discovery connects user intent to visible information. Queries initiate the process. Discovery surfaces what may matter. Results present what was found.

Query → Discovery → Results

Discovery Boundaries

Beacon does not create information, certify information, determine truth, establish authority, or replace human judgment.

Beacon helps users discover what already exists.

Discovery Philosophy

Relevant information should be easier to find. Important signals should be distinguishable from background noise. Sources should remain visible.

Beacon improves visibility while preserving transparency, attribution, and discovery context.
