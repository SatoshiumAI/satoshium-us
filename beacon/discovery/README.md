# Discovery

## Purpose

Discovery is the process of locating, surfacing, organizing, and presenting relevant information within the Satoshium ecosystem.

Beacon exists to help users find meaningful signals across jurisdictions, media, records, certifications, historical entries, identities, and future attestations. Discovery provides the mechanisms through which those signals become visible.

Beacon does not create information.

Beacon does not certify information.

Beacon does not determine truth.

Beacon helps users discover what already exists.

---

## What Discovery Does

Discovery enables users to:

* Locate relevant records
* Surface meaningful signals
* Explore related sources
* Identify patterns and relationships
* Navigate large information collections
* Reduce information friction
* Improve visibility of important data

---

## What Discovery Does Not Do

Discovery does not:

* Verify accuracy
* Certify claims
* Establish authority
* Create trust
* Modify source information
* Replace human judgment

Those responsibilities belong to other components of the Satoshium Suite.

---

## Discovery Layers

Beacon discovery may operate across multiple layers.

### Signal Discovery

Identification of noteworthy events, updates, records, changes, or observations.

### Source Discovery

Identification of original information sources relevant to a query.

### Record Discovery

Identification of structured records maintained by the Registry.

### Historical Discovery

Identification of relevant historical events preserved by the Chronicle.

### Identity Discovery

Identification of relevant entities, organizations, jurisdictions, or individuals represented through Anchor.

### Future Discovery

Identification of emerging developments, trends, and signals that may warrant future observation.

---

## Discovery Principles

### Visibility Over Obscurity

Relevant information should be easier to find.

### Signal Over Noise

Important information should be distinguishable from background activity.

### Transparency Over Black Boxes

Users should understand where information originated.

### Relevance Over Volume

More information is not necessarily better information.

### Navigation Over Control

Discovery systems should help users navigate information, not dictate conclusions.

---

## Relationship to Beacon

Discovery is the operational function of Beacon.

Signals provide information.

Sources provide origin.

Indexes provide organization.

Queries provide intent.

Results provide output.

Discovery connects these components into a unified process.

---

## Relationship to the Satoshium Suite

Discovery relies upon and interacts with other Satoshium systems.

* Atlas provides jurisdictional and media data.
* Navigator provides query and exploration capabilities.
* Certifier provides verification.
* Registry provides records.
* Chronicle provides historical context.
* Anchor provides identity context.
* Attestor provides trust assessments.

Beacon helps users discover information across these systems.

---

## Status

Beacon Discovery is currently under development.

Standards, schemas, discovery methods, and interoperability rules may evolve as the Beacon system matures.
