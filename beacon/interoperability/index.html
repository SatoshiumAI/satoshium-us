Beacon · Interoperability

Interoperability

Beacon is designed to operate across multiple systems, helping users discover information while preserving attribution, transparency, and context.

Interoperability allows Beacon to surface signals, sources, records, and relationships from throughout the broader Satoshium ecosystem.

Atlas Integration

Beacon may discover jurisdiction intelligence, media records, and related information originating from Atlas.

Navigator Integration

Navigator may formulate and refine queries while Beacon assists with discovery and information retrieval.

Certifier Integration

Beacon may surface certifications, evidence records, and verification-related information maintained by Certifier.

Registry Integration

Beacon may discover structured records and metadata maintained within Registry.

Chronicle Integration

Beacon may help users locate historical entries, events, evidence, and preserved historical context.

Anchor & Attestor

Beacon may discover identity records, attestations, trust-related information, and supporting references maintained by Anchor and Attestor.

Interoperability Goals

Transparency
Attribution
Traceability
Compatibility
Discovery

Interoperability seeks to improve discovery without removing responsibility from the systems that maintain underlying records and information.

Ecosystem Relationships

Atlas → Data
Navigator → Query
Beacon → Discovery
Certifier → Verification
Registry → Records
Chronicle → History
Anchor → Identity
Attestor → Trust

Beacon serves as a discovery layer that helps users navigate information across the broader ecosystem.

Interoperability Philosophy

Beacon does not replace other systems. It helps users discover information that already exists within those systems.

Discovery works best when information remains connected to its source.
