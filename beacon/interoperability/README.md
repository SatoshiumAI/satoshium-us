# Interoperability

## Purpose

Interoperability defines how Beacon interacts with other systems, records, services, standards, and components within the Satoshium ecosystem.

Beacon is designed as a discovery layer.

Its value increases as it becomes capable of locating and surfacing information from multiple systems while preserving transparency, traceability, and source integrity.

---

## Core Principle

Beacon does not replace other systems.

Beacon helps users discover information that exists within those systems.

Each system maintains responsibility for its own functions, records, standards, and operations.

---

## Interoperability Goals

Beacon seeks to:

* Improve information discovery
* Reduce information fragmentation
* Increase visibility of relevant records
* Support navigation across systems
* Preserve source attribution
* Maintain transparent information pathways

---

## Relationship to Atlas

Atlas provides jurisdictional and media intelligence.

Beacon may discover:

* Jurisdictions
* Jurisdiction profiles
* Media records
* Country records
* State records
* Atlas-derived information

Atlas remains the authoritative source for Atlas records.

---

## Relationship to Navigator

Navigator provides query and exploration capabilities.

Beacon provides discovery capabilities.

Navigator may submit queries.

Beacon may surface signals, sources, records, and results relevant to those queries.

Navigator and Beacon are intended to operate as complementary systems.

---

## Relationship to Certifier

Certifier provides verification and certification functions.

Beacon may discover:

* Certifications
* Verification reports
* Evidence records
* Certification metadata

Beacon does not perform verification.

Certifier remains responsible for certification activities.

---

## Relationship to Registry

Registry provides structured record management.

Beacon may discover:

* Registry entries
* Record metadata
* Record relationships
* Record updates

Registry remains the authoritative source for registered records.

---

## Relationship to Chronicle

Chronicle preserves historical records and events.

Beacon may discover:

* Historical entries
* Event records
* Supporting sources
* Historical evidence

Chronicle remains the authoritative source for historical preservation.

---

## Relationship to Anchor

Anchor provides identity structures.

Beacon may discover:

* Identity records
* Claims
* Attestations
* Reputation information
* Governance references

Anchor remains responsible for identity-related functions.

---

## Relationship to Attestor

Attestor provides trust-related assessments and attestations.

Beacon may discover:

* Trust records
* Confidence assessments
* Attestations
* Validation outcomes

Beacon does not determine trust.

Attestor remains responsible for trust-related activities.

---

## Information Flow

A simplified information flow may resemble:

Atlas → Registry → Chronicle → Anchor → Attestor

Beacon may assist users in discovering information across any stage of that process.

Navigator may help users formulate and refine the queries used to locate that information.

---

## Source Integrity

Beacon should preserve source attribution whenever possible.

Discovery should not obscure:

* Origin
* Context
* Ownership
* Record history
* Verification status

Users should be able to understand where information originated.

---

## Open Integration

Beacon may support future integrations with:

* Public datasets
* Government records
* Research repositories
* News archives
* Open-source projects
* Third-party information systems

Future integrations should prioritize transparency and traceability.

---

## Interoperability Principles

### Transparency

Information pathways should remain visible.

### Attribution

Sources should remain identifiable.

### Compatibility

Systems should be capable of working together.

### Traceability

Users should be able to follow information back to its origin.

### Modularity

Each system should maintain independent responsibilities.

---

## Future Development

Future Beacon interoperability standards may include:

* Shared schemas
* Discovery protocols
* Signal exchange formats
* Record linking standards
* Query standards
* Metadata standards

Specific implementations may evolve as the Satoshium ecosystem matures.

---

## Status

Beacon interoperability standards are currently under development.

This document defines conceptual relationships and guiding principles rather than finalized technical specifications.
